# Investigation Evidence Rules

## Purpose
Make fraud investigations reproducible, evidence-based, and distinguish facts from inference.

## Scope
Case analysis, root-cause investigation, fraud-ring analysis, and escalations.

## MUST
- Investigations MUST preserve relevant timestamps, identifiers, decision versions, source evidence, and uncertainty.
- Conclusions MUST distinguish observed facts, derived evidence, hypotheses, and unresolved questions.
- Material claims MUST be traceable to source records or reproducible analysis.
- Evidence handling MUST preserve required access controls and auditability.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT alter source evidence to fit a hypothesis.
- MUST NOT present correlation or model confidence as proof of intent.
- MUST NOT expose sensitive investigation methods beyond authorized audiences.

## SHOULD
- Investigators SHOULD test plausible competing hypotheses before recommending broad corrective action.

## Exceptions
Time-critical containment may precede full root cause but requires explicit uncertainty and follow-up.

## Verification
Review case records, query reproducibility, evidence links, access logs, timelines, and peer review of material conclusions.
