# False Positive Rules

## Purpose
Treat legitimate-user harm as a first-class engineering risk in fraud controls.

## Scope
Declines, holds, challenges, reviews, account restrictions, and other interventions.

## MUST
- Material controls MUST measure legitimate-user impact using appropriate confirmed or sampled outcomes.
- False-positive metrics MUST be segmented enough to reveal concentrated harm where lawful and feasible.
- Severe customer-impact cases MUST have an escalation and remediation path.
- Changes that materially increase friction MUST have explicit benefit-versus-harm evidence.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT report fraud capture without corresponding customer-impact context for high-impact controls.
- MUST NOT suppress false-positive evidence to preserve a favorable control metric.

## SHOULD
- Controls SHOULD prefer step-up verification over irreversible denial when risk and product constraints permit.

## Exceptions
Temporary emergency controls require bounded duration, approval, monitoring, and retrospective harm assessment.

## Verification
Review decline/challenge outcomes, complaints, sampled cases, segment metrics, appeal results, and experiment reports.
