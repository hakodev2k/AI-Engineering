# Funnel Analysis Rules

## Purpose
Make funnel diagnosis evidence-based and resistant to misleading aggregation.

## Scope
Acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, referral, and monetization funnels.

## MUST
- Define funnel entry, ordered steps, conversion windows, identity rules, and eligible populations before analysis.
- Segment material changes by relevant dimensions and investigate mix shifts before attributing causality.
- Validate major drop-offs against raw or sampled user journeys where practical.

## MUST NOT
- Treat correlation between a release and funnel movement as proof of causation.
- Compare funnel rates with materially different definitions without disclosing the difference.

## SHOULD
- Quantify both relative and absolute impact and include uncertainty where relevant.

## Exceptions
Rapid incident triage may use preliminary funnel evidence if conclusions are explicitly provisional.

## Verification
Reproduce calculations from source data, inspect cohort definitions, compare baselines, and reconcile totals across funnel stages.
