# Decision Evidence Rules

## Purpose
Ensure Senior growth decisions are explicit about evidence, uncertainty, trade-offs, and authority.

## Scope
Experiment decisions, prioritization, launches, rollbacks, investments, and strategic growth recommendations.

## MUST
- Separate observed facts, assumptions, interpretations, and recommendations in material decisions.
- State expected upside, downside, uncertainty, affected users, reversibility, and decision owner for high-impact changes.
- Preserve enough evidence that another reviewer can reproduce or challenge the conclusion.

## MUST NOT
- Treat agent confidence, stakeholder seniority, or dashboard movement alone as proof of causality.
- Execute actions beyond granted authority merely because they are technically possible.

## SHOULD
- Prefer reversible decisions when evidence is weak and learning can be obtained safely.

## Exceptions
Time-critical decisions may use incomplete evidence when uncertainty and risk are explicit and follow-up validation is assigned.

## Verification
Review decision records, source queries, experiment evidence, approvals, assumptions, alternatives, and post-decision outcomes.
