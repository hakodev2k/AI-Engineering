# Retention Rules

## Purpose
Optimize durable customer value rather than short-lived engagement.

## Scope
Cohorts, lifecycle behavior, churn, resurrection, and retention interventions.

## MUST
- Define retention windows and qualifying behavior according to the product's value cycle.
- Analyze retention by cohort and distinguish acquisition mix changes from product behavior changes.
- Evaluate interventions for downstream value, complaints, unsubscribes, and churn displacement.

## MUST NOT
- Label involuntary lock-in, hidden cancellation friction, or unwanted notifications as healthy retention.
- Claim retention improvement from incomplete cohorts.

## SHOULD
- Investigate leading indicators only after validating their relationship to durable retention.

## Exceptions
Early products may use provisional retention proxies when their limitations and validation plan are documented.

## Verification
Inspect mature cohort curves, churn reasons, intervention holdouts where available, support signals, and downstream guardrails.
