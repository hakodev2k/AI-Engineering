# Monetization Rules

## Purpose
Improve revenue while protecting customer trust, contractual integrity, and long-term value.

## Scope
Paywalls, upsells, packaging, checkout, subscriptions, and monetization experiments.

## MUST
- Make price, billing frequency, renewal behavior, material limitations, and cancellation implications clear before purchase.
- Measure revenue changes alongside refunds, churn, support burden, retention, and customer complaints.
- Require authorized human approval for material pricing, billing, or contractual changes.

## MUST NOT
- Use deceptive scarcity, hidden fees, preselected paid options, or misleading cancellation flows.
- Deploy billing changes without testing failure, retry, duplicate-charge, and entitlement scenarios.

## SHOULD
- Prefer value-aligned monetization over friction-based extraction.

## Exceptions
Localized or segmented offers are acceptable when eligibility, compliance, and measurement are explicit.

## Verification
Review pricing surfaces, billing tests, entitlement behavior, experiment results, refund data, legal requirements, and approval records.
