# Activation Rules

## Purpose
Improve time-to-value without manufacturing superficial engagement.

## Scope
Onboarding, first-run experiences, setup, education, and activation milestones.

## MUST
- Define activation from evidence that it predicts meaningful customer value or retention.
- Measure time-to-value, completion, failure, abandonment, and downstream quality.
- Preserve recovery paths for interrupted or partially completed onboarding.

## MUST NOT
- Optimize activation by forcing irrelevant actions or hiding required choices.
- Remove essential consent, security, or configuration steps solely to improve conversion.

## SHOULD
- Reduce unnecessary steps and defer optional configuration until it becomes useful.

## Exceptions
Mandatory legal, safety, or security steps may add friction and must not be bypassed for growth.

## Verification
Review cohort retention by activation state, funnel evidence, usability results, failure telemetry, and guardrail metrics.
