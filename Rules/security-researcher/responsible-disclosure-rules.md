# Responsible Disclosure Rules

## Purpose
Coordinate vulnerability disclosure so affected parties can reduce risk while findings remain accurate, confidential, and actionable.

## Scope
Applies to internally discovered and externally reportable vulnerabilities, coordinated disclosure, embargoes, advisories, and public research communication.

## MUST
- Disclosure MUST identify an appropriate recipient or coordination channel before sensitive technical details are distributed broadly.
- Reports MUST provide sufficient evidence for the affected party to validate the issue without unnecessary weaponization.
- Agreed embargoes, confidentiality terms, and coordination timelines MUST be honored unless superseded by legal or explicit safety authority.
- Material changes in exploitability, active exploitation, remediation status, or affected scope MUST be communicated to the relevant coordinator.
- Public statements MUST distinguish verified facts, affected versions, remediation guidance, residual uncertainty, and researcher inference.
- Sensitive attachments MUST use approved secure transfer mechanisms.
- Disclosure records MUST preserve dates, material communications, decisions, and acknowledgments.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT disclose secrets, customer data, unrelated vulnerabilities, or operational details unnecessary to understanding the issue.
- MUST NOT represent a vendor or owner as notified, affected, fixed, or unresponsive without evidence.
- MUST NOT publish exploit-enabling detail ahead of an agreed embargo without authorized escalation.
- MUST NOT use disclosure timing to coerce payment, access, or unrelated concessions.

## SHOULD
- Reports SHOULD include affected conditions, minimal reproduction evidence, impact, likely root cause, mitigation options, and verification guidance.
- Researchers SHOULD provide reasonable opportunities for clarification and fix validation.
- Public advisories SHOULD prioritize defensive value over novelty or sensationalism.

## Exceptions
Early or broader disclosure requires documented justification, risk analysis, applicable legal or program authority, consideration of affected users, and human approval by the responsible disclosure owner.

## Verification
Review communication logs, embargo terms, recipients, timestamps, public drafts, redactions, and remediation evidence. Confirm claims match the latest validated state before release.
