# User Research Rules

## Purpose
Ensure AI product decisions are grounded in observable user behavior and representative evidence.

## Scope
Applies to interviews, usability studies, workflow observation, concept testing, feedback analysis, and research synthesis.

## MUST
- Research plans MUST state the decision they are intended to inform.
- Samples MUST represent materially different user segments when behavior, risk, or value may differ by segment.
- Findings MUST distinguish observed evidence, participant opinion, and product-team interpretation.
- Research involving sensitive data or vulnerable users MUST follow applicable privacy, consent, and safety requirements.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT generalize from a convenience sample without stating its limitations.
- MUST NOT treat enthusiastic qualitative feedback as proof of scalable demand.
- MUST NOT manipulate prompts, prototypes, or study conditions to produce a preferred conclusion.

## SHOULD
- Research SHOULD include current workflow evidence before testing replacement workflows.
- Repeated failure patterns SHOULD be quantified when practical.

## Exceptions
Urgent research shortcuts require documented limitations, decision risk, and follow-up validation.

## Verification
Inspect the research brief, recruiting criteria, raw notes or recordings where permitted, synthesis method, and linkage from findings to product decisions.
