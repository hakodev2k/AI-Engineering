# Post-Incident Review Rules

## Purpose
Convert AI incidents into durable improvements without blame-driven analysis.

## Scope
Applies after stabilization of material AI incidents and significant near misses.

## MUST
- Reviews MUST document impact, timeline, detection, containment, causes, contributing factors, recovery, and residual risk.
- Reviews MUST identify why existing controls, tests, evaluations, or monitoring did not prevent or detect the incident sooner when applicable.
- Corrective actions MUST have owner, priority, measurable completion criteria, and due-date expectations appropriate to risk.
- Systemic causes MUST receive systemic corrective actions rather than only operator reminders.
- Near misses with credible severe potential MUST be reviewed proportionately even when no confirmed harm occurred.
- Lessons MUST be fed into runbooks, tests, monitoring, architecture, or policy where relevant.

## MUST NOT
- Reviews MUST NOT substitute individual blame for analysis of system and process conditions.
- Action items MUST NOT be vague commitments such as "be more careful."
- Incident closure MUST NOT erase unresolved high-risk follow-up work.

## SHOULD
- Review action effectiveness after completion.
- Share sanitized lessons across teams that operate similar AI systems.

## Exceptions
Low-severity incidents may use a lightweight review format, but material causes and corrective actions must still be captured.

## Verification
Inspect review document, action tracker, ownership, completed evidence, and subsequent control changes.
