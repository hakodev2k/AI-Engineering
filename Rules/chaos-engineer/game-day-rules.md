# Game Day Rules
## Purpose
Coordinate multi-team resilience exercises safely.
## Scope
Game days, simulations, role coordination, and communications.
## MUST
- Define objectives, participants, authority, timeline, communication channel, safety limits, and recovery ownership.
- Ensure affected teams know whether the exercise is announced or intentionally blind under approved policy.
## MUST NOT
- Run a surprise production game day without authorized leadership and safety controls.
- Confuse exercise traffic with a real incident without clear control procedures.
## SHOULD
- Include realistic decision-making and communication, not only fault injection.
## Exceptions
Security exercises may restrict disclosure according to approved rules of engagement.
## Verification
Review game-day plan, approvals, timeline, communications, and outcomes.
