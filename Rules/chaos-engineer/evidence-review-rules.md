# Evidence and Review Rules
## Purpose
Turn experiment outcomes into defensible resilience conclusions.
## Scope
Results, hypotheses, measurements, limitations, and reviews.
## MUST
- Record whether the hypothesis was supported, falsified, or inconclusive.
- Preserve relevant before/during/after evidence and known limitations.
- Distinguish observed facts from interpretation.
## MUST NOT
- Report resilience improvement without comparable evidence.
- Discard failed experiments because results are inconvenient.
## SHOULD
- Have high-impact conclusions reviewed by service owners.
## Exceptions
Exploratory experiments may use lighter documentation when no production decision depends on them.
## Verification
Review result reports, telemetry links, raw measurements, and reviewer sign-off.
