# Continuous Resilience Rules
## Purpose
Convert chaos findings into sustained reliability improvement.
## Scope
Regression experiments, remediation, ownership, and resilience programs.
## MUST
- Assign owners and due criteria to material resilience gaps.
- Re-run critical experiments after relevant fixes or architecture changes.
- Track recurring failures and unresolved risk explicitly.
## MUST NOT
- Treat a successful one-time experiment as permanent proof of resilience.
- Accumulate known critical resilience gaps without escalation or risk acceptance.
## SHOULD
- Automate stable low-risk experiments in continuous verification.
## Exceptions
High-risk experiments may remain periodic manual exercises.
## Verification
Inspect remediation backlog, repeat-run history, architecture changes, and accepted risks.
