# Compliance and Audit Rules
## Purpose
Make cloud controls demonstrable and sustainable rather than documentation-only.
## Scope
Regulatory requirements, control mappings, audit evidence, retention, policy exceptions, and attestations.
## MUST
- Applicable requirements MUST map to concrete technical or procedural controls with accountable owners.
- Audit evidence MUST be reproducible, attributable, and retained according to applicable requirements.
- Control exceptions MUST record scope, risk, compensating controls, approval, and review or expiry date.
## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT claim compliance from architecture intent when deployed configuration contradicts it.
- MUST NOT fabricate, alter, or selectively omit evidence to satisfy an audit.
## SHOULD
- Automate continuous evidence collection and control checks where reliable.
## Exceptions
Alternative controls require documented equivalence and authorized acceptance.
## Verification
Inspect control mappings, deployed settings, policy reports, audit logs, evidence records, exceptions, and remediation tracking.
