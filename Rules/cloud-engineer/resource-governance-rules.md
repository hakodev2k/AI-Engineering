# Resource Governance Rules
## Purpose
Keep cloud estates controlled, attributable, and policy-compliant.
## Scope
Accounts, subscriptions, projects, resource hierarchy, naming, tagging, policy, quotas, and ownership.
## MUST
- Resource hierarchy MUST reflect ownership, isolation, billing, and policy boundaries.
- Production resources MUST have accountable owners and lifecycle expectations.
- Preventive or detective policies MUST enforce critical organizational guardrails where feasible.
## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT create unmanaged production resources outside approved governance boundaries.
- MUST NOT disable organization-level guardrails without explicit authority and impact review.
## SHOULD
- Standardize metadata needed for ownership, environment, data classification, and cost allocation.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require scope, justification, expiry or review date, risk, and approval.
## Verification
Inspect resource inventories, policy compliance, hierarchy, tags, ownership records, quota settings, and exception registers.
