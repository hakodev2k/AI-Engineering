# Social Engineering Rules

## Purpose
Control human-targeted security testing so objectives can be measured without unnecessary deception, privacy harm, or coercion.

## Scope
Covers phishing simulations, pretexting, voice or messaging tests, physical approaches, and credential-capture simulations.

## MUST
- MUST obtain explicit authorization defining target populations, permitted channels, prohibited themes, data collection, escalation, and debrief expectations.
- MUST minimize personal data collection and use synthetic or non-reusable credential capture where possible.
- MUST define immediate stop conditions for distress, safety concerns, legal concerns, or unintended third-party involvement.
- MUST separate measurement objectives from punitive employee evaluation unless governance explicitly requires otherwise.
- MUST securely delete collected interaction data according to the engagement retention plan.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use threats, harassment, medical emergencies, bereavement, illegal coercion, or similarly harmful pretexts.
- MUST NOT request or retain real passwords when a safe simulation can measure the same control.
- MUST NOT target excluded individuals or vulnerable populations.
- MUST NOT publicly shame participants.

## SHOULD
- SHOULD design scenarios around realistic organizational threats and measurable defensive outcomes.
- SHOULD coordinate debrief and remediation with appropriate stakeholders.

## Exceptions
Any expansion of targets, channels, or sensitive pretexts requires explicit governance and legal approval before execution.

## Verification
Review authorization, target lists, templates, data fields collected, stop events, retention/deletion evidence, metrics, and debrief records.
