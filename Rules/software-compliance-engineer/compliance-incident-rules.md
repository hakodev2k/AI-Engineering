# Compliance Incident Rules

## Purpose
Ensure suspected compliance failures are contained, investigated, escalated, and documented with evidence.

## Scope
Applies to control failures, unauthorized data handling, policy violations, audit-log gaps, licensing issues, and other material nonconformities.

## MUST
- Material incidents MUST have a documented owner, severity, scope, timeline, and escalation path.
- Investigation MUST preserve relevant evidence and distinguish confirmed facts from hypotheses.
- Containment and remediation decisions MUST consider legal, contractual, operational, and customer impact.
- Required notification decisions MUST be routed to authorized legal, compliance, or leadership owners.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT conceal, delete, or modify evidence to reduce apparent impact.
- MUST NOT make external notification commitments without authorized approval.

## SHOULD
- Convert confirmed failure modes into stronger controls, tests, monitoring, or training.

## Exceptions
Emergency containment may precede ordinary review when authorized by incident policy, but actions MUST be documented afterward.

## Verification
Review incident records, timelines, evidence preservation, escalation history, corrective actions, and follow-up validation.
