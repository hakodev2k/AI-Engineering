# Exception Governance Rules

## Purpose
Ensure deviations from compliance controls are explicit, bounded, owned, and periodically reconsidered.

## Scope
Applies to waivers, risk acceptances, temporary bypasses, compensating controls, and approved nonconformities.

## MUST
- Every exception MUST identify the affected requirement or control, reason, scope, owner, residual risk, and approval authority.
- Time-limited exceptions MUST have an expiration or mandatory review date.
- Compensating controls MUST be specific and verifiable.
- Expired exceptions MUST not remain effective without renewed review and approval.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use informal chat, undocumented verbal approval, or implementation convenience as sufficient exception authority.
- MUST NOT allow exceptions to silently become permanent architecture.

## SHOULD
- Maintain a centralized exception register with aging and renewal reporting.

## Exceptions
This domain has no self-exemption; alternative governance requires equivalent traceability and approval.

## Verification
Review exception records, approvals, dates, compensating-control evidence, renewal history, and open aging reports.
