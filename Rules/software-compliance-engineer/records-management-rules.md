# Records Management Rules

## Purpose
Ensure compliance-relevant records are complete, attributable, retained, and disposed of according to defined policy.

## Scope
Applies to approvals, assessments, control evidence, exceptions, release records, audit responses, remediation, and governance decisions.

## MUST
- Required records MUST identify owner, date, scope, and the decision or evidence they represent.
- Record retention MUST align with applicable policy, legal, contractual, and audit needs.
- Records MUST be stored in systems that preserve integrity and authorized access.
- Superseded records MUST remain distinguishable from current authoritative records where history is required.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use transient chat or personal storage as the sole record for material compliance decisions.
- MUST NOT delete records subject to preservation requirements.

## SHOULD
- Use structured metadata and stable identifiers to improve search and auditability.

## Exceptions
Alternative storage requires documented integrity, accessibility, retention, ownership, and approval.

## Verification
Inspect record repositories, retention settings, access controls, metadata, preservation rules, and sampled decision trails.
