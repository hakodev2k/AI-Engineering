# Audit Readiness Rules

## Purpose
Keep compliance evidence and ownership continuously reviewable so audits do not depend on last-minute reconstruction.

## Scope
Applies to control inventories, evidence, owners, exceptions, remediation, system boundaries, and auditor-facing records.

## MUST
- Control ownership and evidence locations MUST be current before an audit request arrives.
- Material assertions MUST be supportable by evidence that can be reproduced or independently inspected.
- Known gaps and open remediation MUST be disclosed through the approved governance process.
- Audit responses MUST distinguish verified fact from interpretation or planned remediation.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT manufacture, backdate, or alter evidence to satisfy an audit request.
- MUST NOT provide unsupported assurances when evidence is incomplete.

## SHOULD
- Perform periodic internal readiness reviews for high-impact control sets.

## Exceptions
Alternative evidence packaging is acceptable when provenance and control coverage remain clear.

## Verification
Review evidence repositories, owner assignments, open gaps, sample audit requests, response records, and internal review findings.
