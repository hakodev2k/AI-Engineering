# Escalation and Incident Rules

## Purpose
Ensure urgent program issues receive timely ownership, escalation, and coordinated response.

## Scope
Critical blockers, production incidents related to program work, severe delivery issues, and cross-team escalations.

## MUST
- Escalations MUST state the issue, impact, owner, decision or support needed, and required timing.
- Program-related incidents MUST have clear coordination with the operational incident owner.
- Critical blockers MUST remain visible until resolved, accepted, or replanned.
- Follow-up actions from major incidents MUST be tracked to accountable owners.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT escalate without first clarifying the requested decision or assistance.
- MUST NOT override incident-command authority during active operational response.

## SHOULD
- Escalations SHOULD be evidence-based and proportionate to impact.
- Recurring incidents SHOULD trigger systemic corrective actions.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, alternate coordination path, and named owner.

## Verification
Review escalation records, incident links, decision outcomes, and follow-up action completion.
