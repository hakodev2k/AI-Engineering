# Stakeholder Alignment Rules

## Purpose
Maintain shared understanding across technical and business stakeholders.

## Scope
Sponsors, engineering teams, product teams, operations, security, vendors, and dependent groups.

## MUST
- Stakeholders MUST understand program outcomes, major commitments, material risks, and decision rights relevant to them.
- Conflicting expectations MUST be surfaced and resolved explicitly.
- Material changes MUST be communicated to affected stakeholders promptly.
- Status communication MUST distinguish fact, forecast, assumption, and decision request.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT report only positive progress while omitting material blockers.
- MUST NOT assume silence means alignment.

## SHOULD
- Communication SHOULD be tailored to stakeholder decision needs.
- Repeated misunderstandings SHOULD trigger clearer artifacts or governance changes.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale and an alternate communication path.

## Verification
Inspect stakeholder maps, status reports, decision requests, meeting outcomes, and change communications.
