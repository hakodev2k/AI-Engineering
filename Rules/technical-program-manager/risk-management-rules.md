# Risk Management Rules

## Purpose
Ensure material program risks are identified early, owned, and managed with evidence.

## Scope
Delivery, architecture, security, quality, operational, vendor, and organizational risks.

## MUST
- Material risks MUST have likelihood, impact, owner, mitigation, and review date.
- Risks MUST be escalated when exposure exceeds agreed tolerance or threatens critical outcomes.
- Mitigations MUST have measurable completion criteria.
- Closed risks MUST include evidence that exposure was reduced or accepted.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT classify known issues as risks merely to avoid escalation.
- MUST NOT close risks based only on confidence or optimism.

## SHOULD
- Risk reviews SHOULD focus on trend and residual exposure.
- High-impact risks SHOULD have contingencies.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented rationale, residual exposure, owner, and approval.

## Verification
Inspect risk registers, mitigation evidence, escalation records, and review cadence.
