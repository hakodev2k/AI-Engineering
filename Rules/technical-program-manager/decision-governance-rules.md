# Decision Governance Rules

## Purpose
Make important program decisions explicit, timely, and traceable.

## Scope
Architecture, scope, priority, sequencing, funding, ownership, and launch decisions.

## MUST
- Material decisions MUST identify the decision owner, options considered, rationale, date, and downstream impact.
- Decision deadlines MUST reflect the cost of delay.
- Reopened decisions MUST state what new evidence or changed constraint justifies reconsideration.
- Decisions affecting multiple teams MUST be communicated to all impacted owners.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT allow unresolved decisions to remain hidden inside meeting notes.
- MUST NOT treat consensus as mandatory when a named decision owner is accountable.

## SHOULD
- Decisions SHOULD prefer evidence and reversible options when uncertainty is high.
- Decision logs SHOULD remain concise and searchable.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale and an explicit temporary decision owner.

## Verification
Review decision logs, timestamps, owner assignments, rationale, and linked plan changes.
