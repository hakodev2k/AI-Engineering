# Resource and Capacity Rules

## Purpose
Align program commitments with realistic team capacity and critical skills.

## Scope
Engineering capacity, specialized expertise, shared teams, staffing assumptions, and allocation conflicts.

## MUST
- Critical work MUST identify required skills and accountable teams.
- Capacity assumptions used for commitments MUST be validated with the responsible owners.
- Material allocation conflicts MUST be surfaced before they invalidate delivery plans.
- Staffing changes affecting milestones MUST update forecasts and risks.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT assume people are fully available without validating competing commitments.
- MUST NOT hide skill gaps behind aggregate headcount numbers.

## SHOULD
- Programs SHOULD reduce single-person dependencies for critical knowledge.
- Capacity planning SHOULD include review, integration, and support work.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, impact, temporary mitigation, owner, and review date.

## Verification
Inspect capacity plans, team acknowledgements, staffing assumptions, allocation conflicts, and forecast updates.
