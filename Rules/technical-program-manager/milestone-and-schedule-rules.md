# Milestone and Schedule Rules

## Purpose
Create realistic schedules that expose uncertainty and protect critical outcomes.

## Scope
Milestones, sequencing, estimates, critical paths, and delivery commitments.

## MUST
- Milestones MUST represent verifiable outcomes rather than activity completion alone.
- Schedule commitments MUST identify assumptions, dependencies, and confidence where uncertainty is material.
- Critical-path changes MUST be reflected in forecasts promptly.
- Slippage on critical milestones MUST trigger impact analysis and recovery options.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT conceal forecast movement to preserve an outdated target date.
- MUST NOT create false precision where estimates are uncertain.

## SHOULD
- Plans SHOULD include integration, validation, rollout, and stabilization time.
- Buffers SHOULD be risk-based rather than arbitrary.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, affected commitments, impact, and accountable approval.

## Verification
Inspect milestone definitions, schedule history, dependency links, forecast changes, and recovery plans.
