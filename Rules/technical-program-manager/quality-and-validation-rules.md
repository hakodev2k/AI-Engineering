# Quality and Validation Rules

## Purpose
Ensure program delivery includes explicit quality gates and evidence before acceptance or launch.

## Scope
Testing, validation, acceptance, readiness, defects, and quality criteria across program deliverables.

## MUST
- Critical outcomes MUST have explicit validation criteria and accountable owners.
- Quality gates MUST be defined before the work reaches the corresponding decision point.
- Known material defects MUST be visible in launch or acceptance decisions.
- Acceptance MUST rely on evidence appropriate to the risk of the change.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT declare readiness solely because implementation is complete.
- MUST NOT waive critical validation without documented approval and residual risk.

## SHOULD
- Programs SHOULD validate integration and operational behavior, not only component completion.
- Quality trends SHOULD inform schedule and scope decisions.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, residual risk, compensating checks, owner, and approval.

## Verification
Inspect validation plans, test evidence, acceptance records, defect status, and waiver decisions.
