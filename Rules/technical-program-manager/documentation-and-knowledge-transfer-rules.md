# Documentation and Knowledge Transfer Rules

## Purpose
Preserve critical program knowledge and ensure ownership transitions are durable.

## Scope
Program decisions, operational knowledge, handoffs, onboarding, architecture context, and post-launch ownership.

## MUST
- Critical decisions, ownership, assumptions, and operating knowledge MUST be recorded in durable artifacts.
- Knowledge transfer MUST identify the receiving owner and required evidence of readiness.
- Handoffs MUST include unresolved risks, dependencies, and support expectations.
- Superseded artifacts MUST be clearly marked or replaced to avoid conflicting guidance.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT rely on a single individual as the only source of critical program knowledge.
- MUST NOT treat document existence as proof that a handoff was understood.

## SHOULD
- Documentation SHOULD be concise, current, searchable, and linked from authoritative program records.
- Complex transitions SHOULD include walkthroughs or exercises.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, temporary knowledge owner, mitigation, and follow-up date.

## Verification
Review authoritative artifacts, handoff checklists, owner acknowledgements, version history, and knowledge-transfer evidence.
