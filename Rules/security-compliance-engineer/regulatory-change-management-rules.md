# Regulatory Change Management Rules

## Purpose
Ensure new or changed legal, regulatory, contractual, and framework requirements are identified, assessed, and converted into controlled implementation work.

## Scope
Applies to requirement changes that can affect security controls, evidence, system design, data handling, operations, or reporting.

## MUST
- Material requirement changes MUST be assessed for applicability, effective date, impacted controls, systems, owners, and implementation work.
- Ambiguous requirements MUST be escalated to appropriate legal, privacy, compliance, or subject-matter authority rather than interpreted silently.
- Required control changes MUST be tracked to verified completion before applicable deadlines or formally risk accepted where permitted.
- Control documentation and evidence procedures MUST be updated when obligations change.

## MUST NOT
- Regulatory change MUST NOT be considered complete merely because a policy document was updated.
- Compliance engineers MUST NOT invent legal conclusions beyond their delegated authority.
- Known deadline risk MUST NOT be hidden until the effective date.

## SHOULD
- Maintain a structured change register with source, applicability decision, impact, owner, milestones, and evidence.
- Review upstream standards and regulator guidance when they materially clarify obligations.

## Exceptions
Delayed implementation requires documented authority, rationale, residual risk, interim controls, target date, and approval where legally permissible.

## Verification
Inspect the change register, source requirements, applicability decisions, implementation records, control updates, approvals, and completion evidence.
