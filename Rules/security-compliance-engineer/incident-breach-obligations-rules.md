# Incident and Breach Obligation Rules

## Purpose
Ensure security incidents trigger the correct compliance, legal, contractual, and notification processes.

## Scope
Applies to suspected and confirmed incidents affecting regulated systems, protected data, control evidence, or contractual obligations.

## MUST
- Incident procedures MUST identify escalation paths for legal, privacy, compliance, security, and business stakeholders.
- Potential notification obligations MUST be assessed using documented facts, affected scope, jurisdictions, contracts, and timelines.
- Decision records MUST preserve what was known, when it was known, who decided, and supporting evidence.
- Evidence preservation and chain-of-custody requirements MUST be followed when relevant.

## MUST NOT
- Notification decisions MUST NOT be made solely from unverified assumptions.
- Incident severity MUST NOT be downgraded to avoid escalation or reporting requirements.
- Required evidence MUST NOT be altered or destroyed during remediation.

## SHOULD
- Predefine jurisdictional and contractual notification matrices.
- Exercise notification workflows with realistic scenarios.

## Exceptions
Any deviation from required response or notification process requires documented legal or risk authority and rationale.

## Verification
Review incident records, escalation timestamps, legal/compliance assessments, evidence handling, notification decisions, and exercise results.
