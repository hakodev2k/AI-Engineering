# Audit Readiness Rules

## Purpose
Maintain defensible compliance posture continuously instead of assembling unsupported evidence immediately before an audit.

## Scope
Applies to internal reviews, external audits, customer assurance, certifications, and regulatory examinations.

## MUST
- Control owners MUST know their control objective, operating procedure, evidence source, test method, and unresolved findings.
- Audit evidence MUST be collected from authoritative sources and scoped to the relevant period and population.
- Known deficiencies MUST be disclosed accurately to authorized reviewers and tracked to resolution or accepted risk.
- Requests and responses MUST preserve a traceable record of what evidence was supplied.

## MUST NOT
- Evidence MUST NOT be fabricated, backdated, selectively altered, or created to imply historical operation that did not occur.
- Teams MUST NOT remediate a control only temporarily for an audit while knowingly allowing immediate regression afterward.
- Unsupported statements MUST NOT be presented as verified control operation.

## SHOULD
- Run periodic readiness reviews using representative sampling before formal assessments.
- Keep evidence repositories organized by control, period, source, and owner.

## Exceptions
Unavailable historical evidence must be reported transparently with the reason, alternative evidence, residual uncertainty, and reviewer approval where needed.

## Verification
Perform mock sampling, trace evidence to source systems, reconcile open findings, and confirm owner readiness through walkthroughs.
