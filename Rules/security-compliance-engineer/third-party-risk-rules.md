# Third-Party Risk Rules

## Purpose
Ensure vendors and external service providers do not create unmanaged security or compliance exposure.

## Scope
Applies to suppliers, SaaS providers, subprocessors, contractors, managed services, and other third parties that access systems, process protected data, or support material controls.

## MUST
- Third parties MUST be risk-tiered based on access, data sensitivity, service criticality, and control dependency.
- Due diligence MUST verify relevant security capabilities rather than rely only on marketing claims.
- Contractual security obligations, breach notification expectations, data handling, and termination requirements MUST be identified where applicable.
- Material third-party findings MUST have accountable owners and remediation or acceptance decisions.

## MUST NOT
- A third-party certification MUST NOT automatically substitute for evaluating project-specific risk.
- High-risk vendors MUST NOT be onboarded without required security review and approval.
- Vendor access MUST NOT persist after the business relationship or need ends.

## SHOULD
- Reassess critical vendors periodically and after material incidents or service changes.
- Track concentration and dependency risk for critical providers.

## Exceptions
Urgent onboarding requires documented business necessity, interim safeguards, explicit risk acceptance, and a deadline for full review.

## Verification
Inspect vendor inventory, risk tiering, assessment evidence, contracts, findings, access records, reassessment dates, and termination samples.
