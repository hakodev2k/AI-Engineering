# Brand Governance Rules

## Purpose
Protect brand consistency, trust, and controlled evolution across marketing execution.

## Scope
Applies to visual identity, tone, naming, claims, sponsorships, co-marketing, and public-facing materials.

## MUST
- Marketing assets MUST follow approved brand standards unless an approved exception exists.
- Material brand changes MUST document rationale, intended effect, affected touchpoints, and rollout plan.
- Co-branded work MUST define ownership, review rights, and usage boundaries.
- Public corrections MUST be coordinated when material brand misinformation is discovered.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT create unauthorized brand variants that can confuse identity or ownership.
- MUST NOT use third-party marks without appropriate rights.
- MUST NOT sacrifice factual accuracy to preserve a preferred narrative.

## SHOULD
- Brand guidance SHOULD define principles rather than only static examples.
- High-visibility deviations SHOULD receive explicit brand-owner review.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require owner, scope, rationale, risk, duration, and approval.

## Verification
Review asset samples, approvals, licensing evidence, brand documentation, and exception records.
