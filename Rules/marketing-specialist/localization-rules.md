# Localization Rules

## Purpose
Adapt marketing for local markets while preserving meaning, compliance, and brand integrity.

## Scope
Applies to translated and localized messaging, offers, campaigns, assets, and market-specific adaptations.

## MUST
- Localization MUST account for language, cultural context, local product availability, pricing, and applicable legal constraints.
- Material claims MUST remain semantically equivalent to the approved source or receive fresh approval.
- Localized assets MUST be reviewed by qualified reviewers for high-risk or high-visibility campaigns.
- Market-specific deviations MUST be documented and owned.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT rely on literal translation when it changes customer meaning or legal effect.
- MUST NOT publish offers unavailable in the target market.
- MUST NOT assume one market's consent, disclosure, or promotional rules apply globally.

## SHOULD
- Reusable terminology SHOULD be governed through approved glossaries where scale warrants it.
- Local performance SHOULD be evaluated independently when market behavior differs materially.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, local risk review, owner, and approval.

## Verification
Inspect source-to-local comparisons, approvals, local availability, legal requirements, and market QA records.
