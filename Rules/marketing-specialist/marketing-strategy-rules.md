# Marketing Strategy Rules

## Purpose
Ensure marketing strategy is evidence-based, commercially aligned, and operationally executable.

## Scope
Applies to campaign, channel, audience, positioning, and growth planning decisions.

## MUST
- Strategy MUST define target audience, business objective, success metrics, constraints, and decision horizon.
- Major recommendations MUST connect expected customer behavior to measurable business outcomes.
- Assumptions MUST be documented and distinguish facts from hypotheses.
- Channel and budget choices MUST be justified using available performance, audience, and cost evidence.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT optimize isolated vanity metrics while ignoring business impact.
- MUST NOT present unvalidated assumptions as established customer truth.
- MUST NOT commit material spend without measurable objectives and accountability.

## SHOULD
- Strategy SHOULD include a primary path, alternatives considered, and trigger conditions for reassessment.
- Plans SHOULD favor reversible experiments before irreversible commitments when uncertainty is high.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented rationale, risk, evidence gaps, owner, and review date.

## Verification
Review strategy briefs, KPI definitions, budget rationale, source evidence, and decision logs.
