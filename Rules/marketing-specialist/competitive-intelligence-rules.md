# Competitive Intelligence Rules

## Purpose
Use competitor information lawfully, accurately, and in ways that improve strategic decisions.

## Scope
Applies to competitor monitoring, comparative messaging, battlecards, pricing observations, and market tracking.

## MUST
- Competitive claims MUST be based on dated, attributable evidence.
- Material comparisons MUST identify the dimensions being compared and known limitations.
- Intelligence collection MUST use lawful and approved methods.
- Rapidly changing competitor facts MUST be revalidated before high-visibility use.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT misrepresent competitor capabilities to strengthen internal narratives.
- MUST NOT use confidential information obtained improperly.
- MUST NOT treat competitor activity as proof that the same action is appropriate for the current business.

## SHOULD
- Competitive analysis SHOULD focus on customer-relevant differentiation rather than exhaustive feature counting.
- Important findings SHOULD include strategic implications and uncertainty.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require source limitations, risk, owner, and approval for public comparative claims.

## Verification
Inspect cited sources, timestamps, comparison logic, legal review where required, and update history.
