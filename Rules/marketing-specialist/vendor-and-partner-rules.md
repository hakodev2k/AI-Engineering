# Vendor and Partner Rules

## Purpose
Control marketing work performed with agencies, platforms, creators, affiliates, and commercial partners.

## Scope
Applies to outsourced execution, data sharing, co-marketing, sponsorships, influencer relationships, and marketing technology vendors.

## MUST
- Vendors and partners MUST have documented scope, owner, deliverables, access boundaries, and success criteria.
- Data sharing MUST be limited to approved purposes and minimum necessary access.
- Public endorsements, sponsorships, and paid relationships MUST follow applicable disclosure requirements.
- Material vendor performance and risk MUST be reviewed periodically.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT grant production, account, or customer-data access beyond operational need.
- MUST NOT allow third parties to publish material claims without the required review.
- MUST NOT renew significant spend solely because switching is inconvenient.

## SHOULD
- Contracts SHOULD define data handling, security, intellectual-property, termination, and measurement responsibilities.
- Critical partners SHOULD have contingency plans.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented necessity, risk, compensating controls, owner, and approval.

## Verification
Inspect contracts, access lists, data flows, disclosure records, performance reviews, and offboarding evidence.
