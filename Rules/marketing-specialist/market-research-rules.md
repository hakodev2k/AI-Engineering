# Market Research Rules

## Purpose
Ensure market research is representative enough for the decision and transparent about uncertainty.

## Scope
Applies to surveys, interviews, panels, desk research, customer research, and market sizing inputs.

## MUST
- Research MUST define the decision question, target population, method, sample, and known limitations.
- Findings MUST distinguish observed evidence from interpretation.
- Incentives, recruitment, and question wording MUST avoid avoidable bias when decision risk is material.
- Market estimates MUST document source assumptions and calculation logic.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT generalize small or convenience samples beyond their support without caveat.
- MUST NOT suppress contradictory findings.
- MUST NOT present directional research as precise population truth.

## SHOULD
- Important decisions SHOULD triangulate multiple evidence sources.
- Research instruments SHOULD be piloted when ambiguity could materially distort results.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, known bias, decision risk, owner, and review criteria.

## Verification
Inspect research plans, questionnaires, sample composition, source citations, calculations, and synthesis notes.
