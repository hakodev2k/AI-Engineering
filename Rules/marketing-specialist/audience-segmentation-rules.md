# Audience Segmentation Rules

## Purpose
Create actionable audience segments without overfitting, stereotyping, or unsupported precision.

## Scope
Applies to prospect, customer, lifecycle, behavioral, firmographic, and demographic segmentation.

## MUST
- Segments MUST be tied to a marketing decision, treatment, or measurement purpose.
- Segment definitions MUST use reliable data and documented inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- Sensitive attributes MUST be handled according to applicable privacy, legal, and policy constraints.
- Segment performance MUST be periodically revalidated against current data.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT create segments solely because data is available.
- MUST NOT infer sensitive traits without legitimate, approved grounds.
- MUST NOT claim causal differences from descriptive segment performance alone.

## SHOULD
- Segments SHOULD be mutually understandable, operationally addressable, and sufficiently stable for the intended use.
- Small segments SHOULD be merged or suppressed when statistical reliability or privacy risk is poor.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require purpose, evidence, risk assessment, approval, and a review date.

## Verification
Inspect segment definitions, source fields, sample sizes, privacy review, activation logic, and revalidation evidence.
