# Privacy and Consent Rules

## Purpose
Protect customer privacy and ensure marketing data use respects consent, purpose, and policy constraints.

## Scope
Applies to contact data, tracking, targeting, profiling, enrichment, remarketing, and audience activation.

## MUST
- Marketing data use MUST have a documented purpose and permitted basis under applicable policy and law.
- Consent and preference signals MUST propagate to relevant marketing systems within defined service levels.
- Data collection MUST be limited to fields necessary for the stated marketing purpose.
- Third-party audience or enrichment data MUST be reviewed for provenance and permitted use.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT bypass opt-out, suppression, or consent controls to improve campaign reach.
- MUST NOT export personal data to unapproved tools or vendors.
- MUST NOT retain marketing data indefinitely without a defined retention basis.

## SHOULD
- Campaign design SHOULD minimize personal data exposure and favor aggregated measurement where practical.
- Privacy review SHOULD occur before introducing materially new tracking or profiling.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require privacy/legal review, documented necessity, safeguards, owner, and expiry.

## Verification
Inspect consent flows, suppression propagation, data inventories, vendor terms, retention settings, and campaign configurations.
