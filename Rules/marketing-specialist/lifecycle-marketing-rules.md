# Lifecycle Marketing Rules

## Purpose
Manage customer communications across lifecycle stages without over-messaging, inconsistency, or loss of consent.

## Scope
Applies to onboarding, nurture, retention, reactivation, upsell, cross-sell, and win-back programs.

## MUST
- Lifecycle programs MUST define entry, exit, suppression, and frequency rules.
- Messages MUST reflect current customer state and applicable consent or preference settings.
- Automation triggers MUST be tested for duplicate, stale, and contradictory communications.
- Lifecycle performance MUST be evaluated beyond open or click rates when business outcomes are available.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT continue contacting recipients who have validly opted out where opt-out applies.
- MUST NOT send mutually contradictory journeys because systems disagree on customer state.
- MUST NOT use urgency or scarcity claims that are not genuine.

## SHOULD
- Programs SHOULD prioritize customer relevance and long-term value over short-term contact volume.
- Suppression rules SHOULD protect support, incident, and sensitive customer states.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, legal/privacy compatibility, risk review, owner, and expiry.

## Verification
Inspect journey logic, suppression tests, consent handling, frequency controls, and outcome reporting.
