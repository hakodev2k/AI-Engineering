# Attribution Rules

## Purpose
Prevent overclaiming marketing impact and make attribution assumptions explicit.

## Scope
Applies to channel, campaign, conversion, pipeline, revenue, and multi-touch attribution.

## MUST
- Attribution models MUST document scope, lookback window, conversion definition, and known blind spots.
- Reported attributed value MUST be labeled according to the model used.
- Material model changes MUST be versioned and explained before trend comparisons.
- Incrementality claims MUST require evidence beyond attribution alone.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT treat attributed revenue as proven causal lift.
- MUST NOT combine incompatible attribution models without disclosure.
- MUST NOT hide unattributed or dark-funnel limitations when they affect interpretation.

## SHOULD
- High-spend decisions SHOULD use incrementality tests or triangulation where practical.
- Reports SHOULD separate model output from business interpretation.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented limitations, rationale, owner, and review date.

## Verification
Inspect model configuration, conversion definitions, lookback windows, version history, and experiment evidence.
