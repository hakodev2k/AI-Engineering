# Budget and Spend Rules

## Purpose
Control marketing spend using explicit economics, risk limits, and accountability.

## Scope
Applies to paid media, sponsorships, events, agencies, tools, promotions, and campaign budgets.

## MUST
- Material spend MUST have an approved budget, owner, objective, and measurement plan.
- Budget reallocations MUST be based on current performance, strategic need, or documented learning goals.
- Forecasts MUST distinguish committed, planned, and discretionary spend.
- High-risk or unusually large commitments MUST follow the required approval path.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT exceed approved spend authority without approval.
- MUST NOT scale spend using incomplete tracking as if results were reliable.
- MUST NOT hide sunk costs when evaluating continuation decisions.

## SHOULD
- Budgets SHOULD reserve capacity for controlled experimentation when strategically useful.
- Spend reviews SHOULD include marginal returns, not only average historical performance.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require business rationale, owner, amount, residual risk, and approval.

## Verification
Inspect purchase records, media plans, approvals, forecasts, spend dashboards, and reallocation logs.
