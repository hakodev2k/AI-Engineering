# Channel Strategy Rules

## Purpose
Select and manage channels according to audience behavior, economics, and strategic fit.

## Scope
Applies to paid, owned, earned, partner, lifecycle, event, and community channels.

## MUST
- Channel selection MUST be justified by audience reach, expected economics, measurement feasibility, and strategic role.
- Each active channel MUST have defined KPIs and an owner.
- Channel performance MUST be reviewed using comparable time periods and relevant cost context.
- Material channel concentration risk MUST be recognized and monitored.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT continue a channel solely because it is historically familiar.
- MUST NOT compare channels using incompatible attribution windows without disclosure.
- MUST NOT scale spend when measurement integrity is materially broken.

## SHOULD
- Channel portfolios SHOULD balance near-term efficiency with learning and strategic resilience.
- New channels SHOULD begin with bounded tests and explicit stop criteria.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented strategic rationale, measurement limitations, risk, and review date.

## Verification
Review media plans, channel scorecards, attribution assumptions, spend trends, and decision logs.
