# Campaign Planning Rules

## Purpose
Ensure campaigns are scoped, measurable, coordinated, and safe to execute.

## Scope
Applies to integrated campaigns, launches, promotions, and channel activations.

## MUST
- Campaigns MUST define objective, audience, offer, message, channels, timeline, budget, owner, and success criteria before launch.
- Dependencies and launch blockers MUST be identified before execution.
- Tracking and attribution requirements MUST be validated before paid or high-impact launch.
- Material campaign risks MUST have mitigation and rollback or pause criteria.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT launch without an accountable owner.
- MUST NOT use unapproved assets or claims in regulated or high-risk contexts.
- MUST NOT change core campaign variables mid-flight without recording the change when it affects interpretation.

## SHOULD
- Campaigns SHOULD include pre-launch QA and post-launch review.
- High-uncertainty campaigns SHOULD use staged rollout or capped spend.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, owner, residual risk, and approval appropriate to spend and exposure.

## Verification
Inspect campaign briefs, approvals, tracking QA, budgets, launch checklists, and post-launch evidence.
