# Content Governance Rules

## Purpose
Ensure marketing content is accurate, consistent, reviewable, and maintainable.

## Scope
Applies to web, email, social, downloadable assets, scripts, enablement materials, and campaign content.

## MUST
- Content MUST have an accountable owner and intended audience.
- Factual, pricing, product, legal, or comparative claims MUST be sourced and current.
- High-risk content MUST follow the required review path before publication.
- Published assets MUST have a process for correction or retirement when claims become stale.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT knowingly publish outdated or contradicted claims.
- MUST NOT bypass mandatory brand, legal, privacy, or product review.
- MUST NOT retain obsolete assets in active distribution without clear archival controls.

## SHOULD
- Content SHOULD use reusable source-of-truth facts and approved messaging where available.
- Material evergreen assets SHOULD have periodic freshness reviews.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented rationale, risk, owner, expiry or review date, and required approvals.

## Verification
Inspect content inventories, approval records, cited evidence, version history, and retirement controls.
