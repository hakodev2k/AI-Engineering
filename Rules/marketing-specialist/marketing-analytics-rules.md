# Marketing Analytics Rules

## Purpose
Ensure marketing analysis is reproducible, decision-relevant, and statistically responsible.

## Scope
Applies to campaign reporting, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, dashboards, and performance interpretation.

## MUST
- Analyses MUST define metric logic, source data, time window, exclusions, and comparison basis.
- Material conclusions MUST be supported by reproducible evidence.
- Data quality limitations MUST be disclosed when they can change decisions.
- Analysts MUST distinguish correlation, contribution, and causal evidence.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT cherry-pick periods or segments to support a preferred narrative.
- MUST NOT silently change metric definitions across reports.
- MUST NOT present statistically unstable small samples as reliable trends.

## SHOULD
- Analyses SHOULD include uncertainty and sensitivity where material.
- Repeated reporting SHOULD use governed metric definitions.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require rationale, known limitations, owner, and review criteria.

## Verification
Inspect queries, dashboard definitions, source lineage, metric dictionaries, sample sizes, and reproducibility.
