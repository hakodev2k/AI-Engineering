# Positioning and Messaging Rules

## Purpose
Protect clarity, differentiation, credibility, and consistency in market-facing claims.

## Scope
Applies to positioning, value propositions, campaign messages, landing pages, sales enablement, and brand narratives.

## MUST
- Positioning MUST identify the intended audience, problem, differentiated value, and credible proof.
- Material product or performance claims MUST be supportable by current evidence.
- Messaging MUST remain consistent with approved product capabilities, pricing, and legal constraints.
- Changes to core positioning MUST document the customer or market evidence motivating them.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use deceptive, unverifiable, or materially misleading claims.
- MUST NOT imply guarantees where outcomes depend on customer context.
- MUST NOT copy competitor positioning without independent validation of fit.

## SHOULD
- Messaging SHOULD prioritize customer-relevant outcomes over internal jargon.
- Variants SHOULD be tested when material uncertainty exists.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented evidence, owner, risk, and approval when claims create legal or reputational exposure.

## Verification
Review source evidence, product documentation, legal approvals, message hierarchy, and experiment results.
