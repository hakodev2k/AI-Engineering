# Exploratory Testing Rules
## Purpose
Use disciplined investigation to discover risks not efficiently captured by scripted checks.
## Scope
Exploratory sessions, charters, heuristics, evidence, and follow-up testing.
## MUST
- Define a mission or risk focus for material exploratory sessions.
- Capture significant observations, environment, data, defects, and follow-up questions.
- Convert recurring critical discoveries into durable regression protection where appropriate.
## MUST NOT
- Use unstructured clicking as a substitute for deliberate exploratory testing.
- Report a defect without enough evidence to identify the observed failure context.
## SHOULD
- Vary personas, states, sequences, inputs, integrations, and interruption patterns.
## Exceptions
Very short investigations may use lightweight notes if evidence remains reproducible.
## Verification
Review charters, session notes, defect yield, follow-up coverage, and risk areas explored.
