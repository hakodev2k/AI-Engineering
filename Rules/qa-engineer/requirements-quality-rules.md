# Requirements Quality Rules
## Purpose
Prevent defects caused by ambiguous, contradictory, or untestable requirements.
## Scope
Stories, specifications, acceptance criteria, business rules, and non-functional requirements.
## MUST
- Challenge ambiguity, missing boundary conditions, conflicting rules, and unverifiable acceptance statements before execution.
- Identify assumptions and unresolved questions separately from confirmed requirements.
- Ensure critical behavior has observable acceptance evidence.
## MUST NOT
- Invent expected behavior when a material requirement is unclear.
- Mark a requirement testable when its outcome cannot be objectively observed.
## SHOULD
- Participate early in refinement to prevent defects rather than only detect them later.
## Exceptions
Exploratory work may proceed with explicit hypotheses and validation goals.
## Verification
Inspect requirement reviews, open-question logs, acceptance criteria, and traceability to tests.
