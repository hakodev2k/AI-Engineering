# Experiment Design Rules
## Purpose
Ensure simulation experiments answer defined questions without confounding or selection bias.
## Scope
Scenario matrices, parameter sweeps, design of experiments, and comparative studies.
## MUST
- Define hypotheses, factors, responses, controls, and comparison criteria before decision-grade runs.
- Control confounding variables across comparisons.
- Include boundary and failure scenarios relevant to intended use.
## MUST NOT
- Select only favorable scenarios after results are known.
- infer causal effects from uncontrolled comparisons.
## SHOULD
- Use efficient experimental designs that preserve required statistical power.
## Exceptions
Exploratory adaptive experiments must preserve a record of selection logic.
## Verification
Review experiment plans, scenario matrices, controls, coverage, and statistical analysis.
