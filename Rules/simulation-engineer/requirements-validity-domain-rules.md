# Requirements and Validity Domain Rules
## Purpose
Bind simulation claims to explicit stakeholder questions and operating conditions.
## Scope
Simulation requirements, acceptance criteria, and intended use.
## MUST
- Convert decision questions into measurable outputs, scenarios, tolerances, and acceptance criteria.
- Define parameter ranges and conditions for which results are considered valid.
- Record unresolved requirements as risks before model approval.
## MUST NOT
- Treat an unspecified scenario as implicitly supported.
- change acceptance criteria after seeing results without documenting the change.
## SHOULD
- Maintain traceability from requirements to model components, experiments, and evidence.
## Exceptions
Emergency exploratory work may proceed with provisional criteria if clearly labeled and not used for irreversible decisions.
## Verification
Inspect requirement traceability, scenario coverage, acceptance criteria, and review records.
