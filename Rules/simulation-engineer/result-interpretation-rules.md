# Result Interpretation Rules
## Purpose
Prevent simulation outputs from being overstated or misused.
## Scope
Analysis, visualization, reports, recommendations, and downstream decisions.
## MUST
- Report assumptions, uncertainty, validity limits, and material failed scenarios alongside conclusions.
- Distinguish simulated predictions from observed facts.
- Preserve enough context that plotted or aggregated results cannot be materially misinterpreted.
## MUST NOT
- extrapolate beyond validated conditions without explicit qualification.
- cherry-pick metrics, time windows, or scenarios to support a preferred conclusion.
## SHOULD
- Present sensitivity and alternative explanations for high-impact decisions.
## Exceptions
Executive summaries may compress detail only when traceable to full evidence.
## Verification
Independent review of claims against run evidence, uncertainty analysis, and validity domain.
