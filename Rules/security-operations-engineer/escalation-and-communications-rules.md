# Escalation and Communications Rules

## Purpose
Ensure incidents are escalated and communicated with accurate, decision-useful information.

## Scope
Operational escalation, executive updates, legal or compliance coordination, technical handoffs, and stakeholder communications.

## MUST
- Escalation criteria MUST be documented for severity, uncertainty, affected scope, business impact, and regulatory sensitivity.
- Updates MUST distinguish confirmed facts, working hypotheses, unknowns, decisions needed, and next checkpoints.
- Material changes in scope or impact MUST be communicated promptly to accountable stakeholders.
- Sensitive incident information MUST be shared only with authorized recipients using approved channels.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT present speculation as fact.
- MUST NOT delay escalation merely to obtain a complete root cause when immediate decisions are required.

## SHOULD
- Communications SHOULD be concise, timestamped, audience-appropriate, and consistent across handoffs.

## Exceptions
Restricted disclosure requires documented legal, privacy, or investigative justification.

## Verification
Review incident updates, timestamps, stakeholder lists, escalation decisions, channel usage, and handoff records.
