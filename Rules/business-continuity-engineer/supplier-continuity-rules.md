# Supplier Continuity Rules

## Purpose
Ensure third-party dependencies do not create unmanaged continuity risk for critical capabilities.

## Scope
Applies to vendors, outsourcers, cloud and managed service providers, logistics partners, utilities, and other external dependencies.

## MUST
- Critical suppliers MUST be identified, assigned an accountable internal owner, and assessed against the recovery requirements of dependent services.
- Contracts or equivalent commitments for critical suppliers MUST address relevant availability, recovery, notification, data, security, and termination requirements where controllable.
- Concentration, geographic, subcontractor, and common-provider risks MUST be considered for critical dependencies.
- Material supplier continuity gaps MUST have documented contingency or risk acceptance.
- Supplier failure scenarios MUST be represented in continuity exercises when the dependency is material.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT equate a contractual SLA with proven end-to-end business continuity.
- MUST NOT assume a supplier's continuity certification proves that the specific contracted service meets required recovery objectives.

## SHOULD
- Maintain alternatives, substitution procedures, or exit strategies for high-impact supplier dependencies where feasible.
- Request evidence proportionate to criticality rather than generic assurances.

## Exceptions
Accepted supplier gaps require documented business impact, alternatives considered, compensating controls, accountable approval, and review date.

## Verification
Inspect supplier registers, assessments, contracts, assurance evidence, concentration analysis, contingency plans, exercises, and open remediation actions.
