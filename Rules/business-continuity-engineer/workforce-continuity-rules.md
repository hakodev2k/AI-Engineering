# Workforce Continuity Rules

## Purpose
Ensure critical operations can continue when normal staffing, access, or workplace arrangements are disrupted.

## Scope
Applies to critical roles, staffing dependencies, succession, remote work, cross-training, welfare, and workforce availability assumptions.

## MUST
- Critical activities MUST identify minimum staffing, required competencies, decision authorities, and alternates.
- Single-person dependencies for critical operations MUST be identified and either mitigated or explicitly accepted.
- Continuity plans MUST address plausible simultaneous staff unavailability and restricted site access.
- Alternate work arrangements MUST preserve required security, privacy, safety, and regulatory controls.
- Workforce assumptions MUST be tested during continuity exercises.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT assume all named staff will be available during a regional or prolonged disruption.
- MUST NOT bypass identity, access, or safety controls merely to restore staffing capacity.

## SHOULD
- Cross-train critical roles and maintain accessible operating procedures.
- Define sustainable shift, handover, and fatigue-management practices for prolonged events.

## Exceptions
Unmitigated staffing constraints require documented impact, interim coverage, owner, risk acceptance, and remediation plan.

## Verification
Inspect critical-role matrices, succession coverage, training records, alternate-work tests, access controls, exercise findings, and staffing assumptions.
