# Crisis Management Rules

## Purpose
Provide controlled decision-making when disruption exceeds normal incident management boundaries.

## Scope
Applies to crisis activation, command structure, decision rights, prioritization, escalation, and stabilization of critical operations.

## MUST
- Crisis criteria MUST define when normal operational processes escalate to crisis governance.
- Crisis roles MUST have explicit authority, alternates, and succession arrangements.
- Material decisions MUST record the decision owner, available evidence, assumptions, risks, and intended review point.
- Life safety, legal obligations, security, and critical customer impact MUST be considered before convenience or schedule pressure.
- The crisis team MUST maintain a current operating picture and agreed priorities throughout activation.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT permit ambiguous command authority during an active crisis.
- MUST NOT execute irreversible high-impact actions without authorized approval unless an established emergency authority explicitly permits them.

## SHOULD
- Use predefined decision thresholds and concise situation reports.
- Assign coordination roles separately from technical execution where scale requires it.

## Exceptions
Emergency deviations require the highest available authorized decision-maker, contemporaneous rationale when possible, and retrospective review.

## Verification
Inspect crisis procedures, authority matrices, exercise records, decision logs, escalation evidence, and post-event actions.
