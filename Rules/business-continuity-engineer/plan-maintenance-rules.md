# Plan Maintenance Rules

## Purpose
Keep continuity plans accurate as services, organizations, suppliers, risks, and recovery capabilities change.

## Scope
Applies to all approved business continuity plans, supporting procedures, contact data, dependency records, and recovery references.

## MUST
- Every continuity plan MUST have an accountable owner, version, approval status, and review cadence.
- Material organizational, technology, supplier, facility, process, or regulatory changes MUST trigger targeted plan review.
- Exercise and incident findings that invalidate plan assumptions MUST be incorporated or formally risk-accepted.
- Superseded plans MUST be clearly distinguished from current approved versions.
- Critical plans MUST remain accessible through an alternate method if normal systems are unavailable.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT treat annual review as sufficient when material changes have occurred since the last review.
- MUST NOT retain obsolete contacts, dependencies, or procedures in an approved plan when known corrections are available.

## SHOULD
- Automate reminders and reconciliation against authoritative inventories where practical.
- Minimize duplicated volatile information across plans.

## Exceptions
Deferred updates require documented impact, temporary guidance, owner, approval, and completion date.

## Verification
Inspect plan metadata, revision history, change-trigger records, exercise remediation, approval evidence, alternate access, and sampled contact/dependency accuracy.
