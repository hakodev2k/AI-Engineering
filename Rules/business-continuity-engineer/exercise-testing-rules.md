# Exercise and Testing Rules

## Purpose
Provide objective evidence that continuity arrangements work under realistic disruption conditions.

## Scope
Applies to walkthroughs, tabletop exercises, simulations, technical recovery tests, supplier exercises, and integrated business continuity exercises.

## MUST
- Critical capabilities MUST be exercised at a cadence proportionate to criticality, change rate, and risk.
- Exercises MUST define objectives, scenario, scope, participants, success criteria, safety boundaries, and evidence to collect before execution.
- Results MUST distinguish demonstrated capability from assumptions, untested steps, and simulated outcomes.
- Material findings MUST receive an owner, severity, target date, and closure evidence.
- Exercises that could affect production, safety, customer data, or security controls MUST have explicit authorization and abort criteria.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT report an exercise as successful solely because participants completed the planned script.
- MUST NOT conceal failed objectives or materially narrow success criteria after execution.

## SHOULD
- Progress from component exercises to integrated scenarios as maturity increases.
- Include unavailable personnel, degraded communications, supplier failure, or concurrent disruptions where relevant.

## Exceptions
Deferred exercises require documented risk, reason, accountable approval, compensating assurance, and a new due date.

## Verification
Review approved exercise plans, evidence logs, actual versus target outcomes, findings, remediation records, and independent observations where available.
