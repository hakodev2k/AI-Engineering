# Regulatory and Compliance Continuity Rules

## Purpose
Ensure continuity arrangements preserve applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and records obligations during disruption.

## Scope
Applies to continuity requirements arising from regulation, law, contracts, licenses, internal policy, and mandatory reporting obligations.

## MUST
- Critical capabilities MUST identify continuity-related obligations that materially affect recovery priorities, communications, data handling, or operational controls.
- Plans MUST identify applicable notification deadlines, approval authorities, evidence requirements, and record-retention expectations.
- Continuity workarounds MUST preserve mandatory security, privacy, safety, financial, and recordkeeping controls unless an authorized emergency provision explicitly permits otherwise.
- Changes in material obligations MUST trigger review of affected plans and recovery strategies.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT assume normal obligations are suspended merely because operations are disrupted.
- MUST NOT make regulatory or contractual notifications without authorized review when such review is required and available.

## SHOULD
- Maintain traceability between obligations, critical services, controls, plans, and exercise scenarios.
- Validate interpretation with appropriate legal, compliance, security, or risk owners where obligations are ambiguous.

## Exceptions
Any temporary deviation requires documented legal or policy basis, accountable approval, risk assessment, compensating measures, and evidence of subsequent normalization.

## Verification
Review obligation registers, plan mappings, notification procedures, approvals, exercise evidence, audit findings, and documented treatment of identified gaps.
