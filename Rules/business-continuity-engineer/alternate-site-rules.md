# Alternate Site Rules

## Purpose
Ensure alternate operating locations are viable recovery capabilities rather than untested assumptions.

## Scope
Applies to alternate offices, command locations, operations centers, technical sites, remote-work arrangements, and contracted recovery facilities.

## MUST
- Alternate sites MUST be mapped to the critical activities they are intended to support and the capacity required within recovery objectives.
- Access, connectivity, equipment, utilities, security, health and safety, and staffing assumptions MUST be validated periodically.
- Shared-site and regional concentration risks MUST be assessed.
- Activation procedures MUST define authority, prioritization when capacity is constrained, and transition back to normal operations.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT count nominal seats or floor space as usable recovery capacity without required systems, connectivity, access, and personnel arrangements.
- MUST NOT assume an alternate site remains unaffected by the same geographic or supplier event as the primary site.

## SHOULD
- Exercise alternate sites under realistic staffing and workload conditions.
- Prefer flexible arrangements that support multiple plausible disruption scenarios when controls remain adequate.

## Exceptions
Unverified alternate-site assumptions require documented risk, fallback, accountable approval, and a scheduled validation date.

## Verification
Inspect site assessments, capacity records, access tests, network validation, supplier evidence, exercise outcomes, and activation procedures.
