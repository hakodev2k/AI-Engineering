# Facility Continuity Rules

## Purpose
Protect critical operations from loss or degradation of normal workplaces, sites, utilities, and physical access.

## Scope
Applies to offices, operations centers, technical facilities, utilities, physical security, alternate workplaces, and site-dependent critical processes.

## MUST
- Critical site-dependent activities MUST identify minimum facility, utility, connectivity, equipment, and access requirements.
- Plans MUST define alternatives for credible loss of site, power, environmental controls, transportation, or physical access.
- Alternate facilities MUST have capacity and access assumptions validated before being treated as available recovery options.
- Life-safety and physical-security requirements MUST remain authoritative during continuity operations.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT direct personnel to unsafe or unauthorized locations to preserve service continuity.
- MUST NOT assume alternate premises are usable without considering regional events, shared dependencies, and occupancy constraints.

## SHOULD
- Reduce concentration risk for critical capabilities where business impact justifies it.
- Coordinate facility recovery assumptions with workforce, technology, supplier, and emergency-management plans.

## Exceptions
Facility gaps require documented risk, temporary workaround, responsible owner, required approvals, and a remediation or review date.

## Verification
Review facility dependency records, alternate-site assessments, utility resilience evidence, access tests, exercise outcomes, and safety/security approvals.
