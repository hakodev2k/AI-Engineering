# SEO Audit Rules
## Purpose
Produce audits that identify material causes and decisions rather than unprioritized tool output.
## Scope
Technical, content, authority, international, local, and measurement audits.
## MUST
- Define audit scope, business context, representative URL populations, evidence sources, and known limitations.
- Reproduce material issues manually or with independent evidence before escalating them.
- Group findings by root cause and affected template/system where possible.
- Attach impact, confidence, risk, owner, verification method, and remediation direction to priority findings.
## MUST NOT
- Present crawler warnings or vendor scores as confirmed business problems without validation.
- Recommend changes whose likely side effects were not considered.
## SHOULD
- Sample both successful and failing pages to identify discriminating factors.
## Exceptions
Rapid incident audits may prioritize severe hypotheses first, with complete audit documentation afterward.
## Verification
Reproducible crawl/config evidence, sampled URLs, prioritization rationale, and remediation validation.
