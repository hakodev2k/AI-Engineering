# Link Acquisition Rules
## Purpose
Earn legitimate references without creating search-manipulation, legal, or reputation risk.
## Scope
Digital PR, outreach, partnerships, sponsorships, and backlink initiatives.
## MUST
- Evaluate link initiatives for editorial legitimacy, audience value, disclosure requirements, and search-engine policies.
- Keep paid, sponsored, affiliate, or otherwise compensated relationships appropriately qualified.
- Document high-risk outreach or acquisition tactics before approval.
## MUST NOT
- Buy ranking links, operate deceptive link schemes, automate spam outreach, or create undisclosed compensated endorsements.
- Guarantee ranking outcomes from backlink counts.
## SHOULD
- Prefer assets, research, tools, expertise, and stories that earn citations because they are useful.
## Exceptions
Legitimate advertising and sponsorships are allowed when links and disclosures are handled appropriately.
## Verification
Sample acquired links, agreements, relationship disclosures, anchor patterns, referral quality, and manual-risk indicators.
