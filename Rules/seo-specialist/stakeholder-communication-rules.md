# Stakeholder Communication Rules
## Purpose
Make SEO decisions understandable, evidence-based, and actionable across product, engineering, content, legal, and leadership teams.
## Scope
Recommendations, reporting, escalations, reviews, and executive communication.
## MUST
- State the problem, evidence, affected users/pages, expected impact, uncertainty, trade-offs, owner, and requested decision for material recommendations.
- Separate observed facts from hypotheses and forecasts.
- Translate search metrics into business outcomes without overstating causality.
- Escalate material security, legal, policy, migration, or revenue risks to appropriate owners.
## MUST NOT
- Use unexplained SEO jargon to conceal uncertainty or weak evidence.
- Promise rankings, traffic, or revenue outcomes outside reasonable control.
## SHOULD
- Tailor detail to the decision maker while preserving access to underlying evidence.
## Exceptions
Incident communications may be concise initially but must be followed by a complete evidence-based record.
## Verification
Review decision documents, reports, assumptions, linked evidence, owners, and follow-up outcomes.
