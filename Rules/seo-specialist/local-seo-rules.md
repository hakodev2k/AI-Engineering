# Local SEO Rules
## Purpose
Maintain accurate local discovery signals for organizations serving geographic markets.
## Scope
Business profiles, location pages, citations, reviews, and local landing pages.
## MUST
- Keep public business name, address/service area, phone, hours, and category information accurate across authoritative properties.
- Create location pages only where a real location or distinct service-area experience can be represented usefully.
- Follow platform policies for business representation and review solicitation.
## MUST NOT
- Create fake locations, virtual-office representations, fabricated reviews, or misleading local pages.
- Stuff business names with keywords not part of the represented name.
## SHOULD
- Connect location content to genuine local services, staff, inventory, policies, or evidence.
## Exceptions
Service-area businesses may omit public addresses when platform rules and business reality support it.
## Verification
Profile audits, first-party business records, citation sampling, location-page review, and local performance reporting.
