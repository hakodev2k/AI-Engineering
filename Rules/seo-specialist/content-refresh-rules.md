# Content Refresh Rules
## Purpose
Keep important search content accurate and useful without destructive freshness theater.
## Scope
Updates, consolidation, pruning, archival, and evergreen maintenance.
## MUST
- Refresh content when facts, user needs, product behavior, regulations, or search intent materially change.
- Preserve or intentionally redirect valuable URLs when consolidating content.
- Review historical performance and inbound links before deleting or radically repurposing established pages.
## MUST NOT
- Change publication dates merely to simulate freshness.
- Delete underperforming pages solely because recent traffic is low without considering purpose, seasonality, links, conversions, and alternatives.
## SHOULD
- Maintain review cadences based on factual volatility and business risk.
## Exceptions
Obsolete or harmful content may be removed urgently when user, legal, or security risk outweighs search value.
## Verification
Change history, source validation, performance trends, backlink checks, redirect tests, and post-refresh monitoring.
