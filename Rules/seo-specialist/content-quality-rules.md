# Content Quality Rules
## Purpose
Protect usefulness, originality, trust, and search performance of indexable content.
## Scope
New content, refreshes, programmatic pages, and AI-assisted content.
## MUST
- Ensure indexable pages provide a clear primary purpose and materially useful information for the intended audience.
- Verify factual claims that can affect user decisions against authoritative evidence.
- Review AI-assisted content for accuracy, originality, usefulness, and brand/legal requirements before publication.
## MUST NOT
- Publish scaled low-value pages whose primary purpose is search manipulation.
- Add unsupported expertise, experience, reviews, statistics, or citations.
## SHOULD
- Include first-party evidence, examples, analysis, or experience when it materially improves the answer.
## Exceptions
Templated content is acceptable when each page has legitimate unique value and data quality controls.
## Verification
Human editorial review, source checks, duplicate-content analysis, engagement signals, and post-launch search performance.
