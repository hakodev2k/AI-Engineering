# Search Policy Rules
## Purpose
Prevent tactics that create spam, deception, policy, or long-term reputation risk.
## Scope
Content, links, redirects, structured data, automation, reputation, and search-manipulation tactics.
## MUST
- Evaluate material SEO tactics against current search-engine spam policies and applicable legal/brand constraints.
- Escalate tactics with unclear policy status or significant manual-action risk before execution.
- Prefer durable user value over loopholes whose benefit depends on search-engine misunderstanding.
## MUST NOT
- Use cloaking, doorway abuse, hidden content, hacked content, reputation abuse, deceptive redirects, or other prohibited manipulation.
- Misrepresent third-party content as first-party expertise.
## SHOULD
- Document rejected high-risk tactics so teams do not repeatedly reconsider them without new evidence.
## Exceptions
None for deceptive or prohibited practices; policy ambiguities require review rather than silent execution.
## Verification
Policy review, crawl/render comparison, link/content audits, manual-action reports, and implementation inspection.
