# Programmatic SEO Rules
## Purpose
Scale search landing pages only when data and templates produce legitimate unique user value.
## Scope
Template-generated pages, large catalogs, directories, integrations, and automated content systems.
## MUST
- Define the unique user value, authoritative data source, indexability criteria, and quality threshold before scaling page generation.
- Validate representative edge cases and prevent publication when required source data is missing or invalid.
- Monitor indexation, duplication, engagement, crawl load, and conversion quality after launch.
## MUST NOT
- Generate large numbers of thin pages by swapping keywords or locations without meaningful differentiated value.
- Fabricate data or use unverified generated claims to make pages appear unique.
## SHOULD
- Start with a controlled cohort and expand only after quality and search evidence support scaling.
## Exceptions
Low-search-volume pages may still be valuable when they serve real users and belong naturally in the product information architecture.
## Verification
Template QA, data validation, duplicate analysis, crawl/index reports, user metrics, and sampled human review.
