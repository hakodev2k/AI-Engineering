# Keyword Research Rules
## Purpose
Build defensible search-demand models that guide useful work.
## Scope
Keyword discovery, clustering, prioritization, and mapping.
## MUST
- Combine multiple evidence sources when material decisions depend on demand estimates.
- Record intent, topic, target page, business relevance, and uncertainty for priority query groups.
- Distinguish branded, non-branded, navigational, informational, commercial, and transactional demand where relevant.
## MUST NOT
- Present third-party volume estimates as exact traffic forecasts.
- Create separate pages solely for trivial keyword variants.
## SHOULD
- Prefer topic coverage and user need over mechanical exact-match repetition.
## Exceptions
Low-volume queries may be prioritized when conversion value, strategic importance, or customer evidence justifies them.
## Verification
Audit keyword sources, clustering logic, page mapping, and assumptions against Search Console or equivalent first-party data when available.
