# Site Migration Rules
## Purpose
Preserve discoverability, relevance, authority signals, and measurement during high-risk site changes.
## Scope
Domain, protocol, platform, URL, architecture, and major redesign migrations.
## MUST
- Inventory valuable legacy URLs and map changed URLs to the closest valid destinations before launch.
- Define redirect, canonical, sitemap, robots, analytics, monitoring, rollback, and ownership plans.
- Baseline traffic, rankings, indexation, crawl errors, and key conversions before migration.
- Require explicit approval for broad URL or domain migrations after risk review.
## MUST NOT
- Redirect unrelated legacy URLs indiscriminately to a homepage or category root.
- combine avoidable major migrations without documenting compounded risk.
## SHOULD
- Preserve stable URLs when change provides no material user or platform benefit.
## Exceptions
Emergency platform moves may compress preparation but still require minimum redirect, crawl, monitoring, and rollback controls.
## Verification
Pre/post crawls, redirect tests, log analysis, index reports, analytics, rankings, and conversion comparisons.
