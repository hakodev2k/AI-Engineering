# SEO Prioritization Rules
## Purpose
Direct limited engineering and content capacity toward the highest expected business value and risk reduction.
## Scope
Backlogs, roadmaps, audits, recommendations, and incident prevention.
## MUST
- Prioritize material work using documented impact, confidence, effort, dependency, risk, and strategic relevance.
- Distinguish defects, opportunities, experiments, and compliance risks because they require different decisions.
- Reprioritize when evidence, business priorities, or platform conditions materially change.
## MUST NOT
- Rank work solely by keyword volume, audit issue count, or tool severity labels.
- Present speculative traffic upside as guaranteed value.
## SHOULD
- Prefer fixes that remove systemic template-level causes over repeated URL-by-URL remediation.
## Exceptions
Mandatory legal, security, or platform-policy work may override normal opportunity scoring.
## Verification
Backlog rationale, supporting evidence, dependency review, decision records, and realized-impact review.
