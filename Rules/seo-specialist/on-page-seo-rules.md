# On-Page SEO Rules
## Purpose
Make page meaning, hierarchy, and search presentation clear without degrading user experience.
## Scope
Titles, headings, copy, internal context, images, and metadata.
## MUST
- Keep each indexable page aligned to one coherent primary purpose.
- Write unique, descriptive titles and headings that accurately represent visible content.
- Keep important explanatory content available in crawlable rendered output.
## MUST NOT
- Stuff keywords into titles, headings, alt text, or body copy.
- Hide text or links for ranking manipulation.
- use metadata that promises content the page does not provide.
## SHOULD
- Optimize snippets for clarity and qualified clicks rather than click-through rate alone.
## Exceptions
Repeated boilerplate is acceptable where product or legal constraints require it, provided unique page purpose remains clear.
## Verification
Rendered-page review, crawler inspection, metadata uniqueness checks, and query/page relevance review.
