# Competitive Analysis Rules
## Purpose
Use competitor evidence to understand search markets without blindly copying tactics.
## Scope
SERP competitors, content gaps, links, features, and market benchmarking.
## MUST
- Distinguish search competitors from commercial competitors and identify why each is relevant.
- Compare competitors on intent satisfaction, content value, authority signals, technical experience, and SERP features using observable evidence.
- Validate whether a competitor tactic fits the organization's users, capabilities, and risk tolerance before recommending it.
## MUST NOT
- Assume correlation between a competitor tactic and rankings proves causation.
- Copy protected content, deceptive tactics, or policy-risk behavior.
## SHOULD
- Seek unmet user needs and differentiation rather than feature parity alone.
## Exceptions
Rapid benchmarking may use smaller samples when clearly labeled and sufficient for a reversible decision.
## Verification
SERP samples, competitor-page evidence, link/source data, documented assumptions, and recommendation rationale.
