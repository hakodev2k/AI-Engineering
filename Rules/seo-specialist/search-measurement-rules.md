# Search Measurement Rules
## Purpose
Make SEO decisions from reproducible, appropriately scoped evidence.
## Scope
Search Console, analytics, rank tracking, dashboards, and experiments.
## MUST
- Define metric, population, date range, comparison method, and known data limitations for material conclusions.
- Separate impressions, clicks, sessions, conversions, revenue, and ranking metrics rather than treating them as interchangeable.
- Annotate major releases, migrations, tracking changes, seasonality, and external events where relevant.
## MUST NOT
- Attribute causality to SEO changes from simple before/after correlation alone.
- Hide unfavorable segments or uncertainty when presenting outcomes.
## SHOULD
- Use matched controls, experiments, or triangulated evidence when causal confidence matters.
## Exceptions
Directional reporting is acceptable when limitations are explicit and decisions are reversible.
## Verification
Dashboard definitions, raw-source reconciliation, tracking tests, statistical review where applicable, and reproducible queries.
