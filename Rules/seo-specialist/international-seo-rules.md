# International SEO Rules
## Purpose
Serve the correct language or regional experience without creating conflicting search signals.
## Scope
Localized URLs, hreflang, language targeting, regional content, and redirects.
## MUST
- Give independently indexable localized pages stable URLs and correct self/cross references where hreflang is used.
- Keep language/region annotations reciprocal and aligned with canonical strategy.
- Validate localization quality and market relevance, not only translation completeness.
## MUST NOT
- Force users or crawlers to a locale solely from IP when it prevents access to alternate versions.
- Use hreflang to compensate for fundamentally incorrect canonicals.
## SHOULD
- Provide visible locale switching and preserve user choice.
## Exceptions
A single global page may serve multiple markets when content, offer, legal terms, and language genuinely do not require variants.
## Verification
Crawler hreflang reports, URL inspection, locale tests, canonical checks, and market-specific performance data.
