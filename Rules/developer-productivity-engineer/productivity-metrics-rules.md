# Productivity Metrics Rules
## Purpose
Use engineering metrics as evidence without creating harmful incentives.
## Scope
Lead time, build time, review time, failure rate, adoption, satisfaction, and developer-flow metrics.
## MUST
- Every metric MUST have a defined question, population, calculation, and known limitations.
- Productivity conclusions MUST triangulate quantitative evidence with workflow or user evidence when causality is uncertain.
- Metric changes MUST preserve comparability or explicitly mark discontinuities.
- Team-level decisions MUST consider quality, reliability, and safety alongside speed.
## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use commit count, lines changed, or similar activity measures as standalone individual performance proxies.
- MUST NOT claim causation from correlation alone.
## SHOULD
- Metrics SHOULD emphasize friction removed and outcomes improved.
## Exceptions
Experimental metrics require clear labeling and limited decision authority.
## Verification
Review definitions, dashboards, sampling, confounders, trend breaks, and decision records.
