# Compliance and Audit

## Purpose
Provide defensible evidence that protection controls meet applicable obligations.

## Scope
Policies, retention, access, encryption, restore testing, legal holds, evidence, and control attestations.

## MUST
- Compliance claims MUST map to specific controls and evidence rather than product capability statements.
- Evidence MUST be retained with integrity and for the period required by applicable policy or obligation.
- Control exceptions MUST identify affected assets, risk, owner, approval, and remediation/review date.
- Audit access MUST protect backup confidentiality and privileged information.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT claim compliance solely because a backup product supports a feature that is not configured or verified.
- MUST NOT alter or fabricate evidence.
- MUST NOT expose sensitive backup contents unnecessarily for audit purposes.

## SHOULD
- Evidence collection SHOULD be automated where it improves completeness and repeatability.
- Controls SHOULD be mapped once to multiple applicable frameworks where semantics genuinely align.

## Exceptions
Alternative evidence requires documented equivalence and accountable compliance/control-owner approval.

## Verification
Sample control mappings, configuration evidence, access reviews, restore records, retention reports, exception registers, and audit trails.
