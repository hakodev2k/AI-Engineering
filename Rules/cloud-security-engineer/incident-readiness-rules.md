# Incident Readiness

## Purpose
Prepare cloud environments for safe and effective security incident handling.

## Scope
Runbooks, evidence access, emergency identities, isolation methods, contacts, and exercises.

## MUST
- Critical cloud services MUST have incident procedures for credential compromise, public exposure, malicious change, and data-access investigation where applicable.
- Responders MUST know how to preserve relevant logs and snapshots without unnecessarily altering evidence.
- Emergency access MUST be strongly protected, monitored, tested, and limited to authorized use.
- Response actions with destructive or irreversible production impact MUST follow approved emergency authority.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT improvise broad destructive containment when a safer bounded action is available.
- MUST NOT depend on a compromised identity path as the only means of response.

## SHOULD
- Exercise cross-team cloud incidents and record control gaps.
- Pre-stage forensic queries and isolation patterns.

## Exceptions
Document readiness gap, impact, interim procedure, owner, remediation date, and approval.

## Verification
Review runbooks, access tests, tabletop or simulation evidence, emergency-account monitoring, forensic data availability, and remediation tracking.
