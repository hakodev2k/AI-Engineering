# Developer Support Rules

## Purpose
Provide technically sound guidance while maintaining clear boundaries between advocacy, support, and engineering ownership.

## Scope
Applies to public Q&A, escalations, office hours, community support, and developer troubleshooting.

## MUST
- Troubleshooting guidance MUST separate verified facts from hypotheses.
- Reproduction steps, environment details, errors, and relevant versions MUST be captured before broad conclusions are made.
- Issues indicating product defects MUST be routed to the accountable engineering or support channel with sufficient evidence.
- Workarounds that carry security, data-loss, compatibility, or operational risk MUST disclose that risk explicitly.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT invent undocumented fixes when evidence is insufficient.
- MUST NOT request secrets or unnecessary sensitive data from developers.
- MUST NOT represent an unresolved issue as fixed without verification.

## SHOULD
- Common support patterns SHOULD feed documentation, tooling, or product improvements.
- Responses SHOULD prefer reproducible diagnostics over guesswork.

## Exceptions
Urgent incidents may use abbreviated intake when speed matters, but missing evidence must be gathered as soon as practical.

## Verification
Review support threads, escalation payloads, reproduction evidence, risk disclosures, and whether resolved cases were actually validated.
