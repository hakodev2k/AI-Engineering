# Content Strategy Rules

## Purpose
Ensure developer content investments align with measurable audience and product needs.

## Scope
Applies to editorial planning, topic selection, channel choice, campaign sequencing, and content maintenance.

## MUST
- Major content initiatives MUST identify target audience, developer problem, intended action, success measure, owner, and review date.
- Topic prioritization MUST consider evidence of developer demand, product readiness, support burden, and strategic relevance.
- Evergreen content MUST have an ownership and freshness strategy.
- Content tied to unreleased features MUST follow approved release and disclosure boundaries.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT publish unsupported promises about future product behavior.
- MUST NOT measure success solely by impressions when adoption, comprehension, or activation is the actual objective.
- MUST NOT create duplicate content that fragments canonical guidance without a clear reason.

## SHOULD
- Content portfolios SHOULD balance discovery, onboarding, depth, migration, and operational guidance.
- High-cost formats SHOULD be justified by expected audience value.

## Exceptions
Exploratory content may use lighter planning when scope, uncertainty, and evaluation criteria are explicit.

## Verification
Inspect briefs, audience evidence, editorial calendars, canonical links, freshness owners, release approvals, and outcome metrics.
