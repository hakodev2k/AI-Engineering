# Community Engagement Rules

## Purpose
Maintain technically useful, respectful, and trustworthy engagement with developer communities.

## Scope
Applies to forums, chat communities, social platforms, meetups, user groups, and direct community interactions.

## MUST
- Official participation MUST clearly distinguish verified facts, personal interpretation, and unresolved questions.
- Material product issues raised by developers MUST be routed to an accountable owner with sufficient reproduction context.
- Community moderation and participation MUST follow published conduct and safety policies.
- Public commitments made on behalf of the product or organization MUST stay within delegated authority.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT dismiss repeated developer reports without evidence-based investigation or routing.
- MUST NOT disclose private roadmap, customer, security, or incident information.
- MUST NOT manipulate community consensus through undisclosed coordinated advocacy.

## SHOULD
- Responses SHOULD explain constraints and workarounds when a direct solution is unavailable.
- Recurring questions SHOULD inform documentation, product feedback, or tooling improvements.

## Exceptions
Sensitive reports may move to private channels when public discussion could expose user data, security details, or confidential information.

## Verification
Review interaction logs, escalation records, conduct compliance, public commitments, and whether recurring issues resulted in accountable follow-up.
