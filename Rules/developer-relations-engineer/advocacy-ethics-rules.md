# Advocacy Ethics Rules

## Purpose
Preserve developer trust by keeping advocacy transparent, accurate, and free from deceptive influence.

## Scope
Applies to endorsements, comparisons, partnerships, sponsorships, testimonials, community programs, and public representation.

## MUST
- Material sponsorships, incentives, affiliations, and conflicts of interest MUST be disclosed where relevant.
- Competitive claims MUST be supportable by current evidence and scoped to the tested conditions.
- Testimonials and community stories MUST preserve the contributor's intended meaning and required permissions.
- Public advocacy MUST respect applicable advertising, disclosure, privacy, and platform requirements.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT fabricate developer sentiment, testimonials, benchmarks, or adoption claims.
- MUST NOT conceal material limitations while presenting comparative claims.
- MUST NOT pressure community members to provide positive public statements as a condition of support.

## SHOULD
- Comparisons SHOULD explain methodology and material limitations.
- Advocacy SHOULD favor verifiable developer value over hype.

## Exceptions
None for fabrication or undisclosed material conflicts. Other exceptions require documented legal or policy review where applicable.

## Verification
Inspect disclosure language, permissions, benchmark evidence, source records, and whether public claims can be independently substantiated.
