# Partner Integration Rules

## Purpose
Ensure joint developer programs and integrations remain technically accurate, supportable, and aligned with user interests.

## Scope
Applies to technology partners, integrations, co-marketing, joint samples, and ecosystem enablement.

## MUST
- Joint technical claims MUST be verified by both accountable sides before publication.
- Integration examples MUST document version, authentication, data-flow, and failure assumptions that materially affect users.
- Ownership for support, maintenance, and deprecation MUST be explicit for public joint assets.
- Shared developer data MUST follow approved privacy and access boundaries.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT imply official support beyond the actual support agreement.
- MUST NOT share confidential roadmap or customer information without authorization.
- MUST NOT publish partner-provided code without technical and licensing review.

## SHOULD
- Joint content SHOULD identify the canonical owner for each dependency.
- Integration guidance SHOULD include realistic failure and compatibility considerations.

## Exceptions
Experimental partner prototypes may use lighter support commitments when clearly labeled and isolated from production guidance.

## Verification
Review approvals, support ownership, dependency versions, privacy handling, licensing, and reproducibility of the integration.
