# Feedback Triage Rules

## Purpose
Convert developer feedback into traceable, evidence-based product and documentation signals.

## Scope
Applies to feature requests, pain points, bug reports, adoption blockers, support themes, and qualitative feedback.

## MUST
- Feedback records MUST capture source context, affected workflow, frequency evidence when available, and user impact.
- Similar reports MUST be deduplicated without losing distinct use cases or severity differences.
- Critical defects, security-sensitive reports, and broad adoption blockers MUST follow the appropriate escalation path.
- Product recommendations derived from feedback MUST distinguish observed evidence from interpretation.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT prioritize solely by loudness, follower count, or internal preference.
- MUST NOT expose reporter identity beyond the access needed for follow-up.
- MUST NOT promise implementation dates without authorized product commitment.

## SHOULD
- Recurring themes SHOULD be reviewed across support, telemetry, community, and research sources.
- Closed feedback loops SHOULD tell reporters when meaningful outcomes can be shared.

## Exceptions
Anonymous or incomplete feedback may be retained when potentially valuable, but its evidentiary limits must be explicit.

## Verification
Inspect triage records, deduplication links, escalation history, privacy handling, and the evidence supporting prioritization recommendations.
