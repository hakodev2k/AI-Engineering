# Audience Research Rules

## Purpose
Ensure developer-relations programs are based on evidence about real developer needs rather than internal assumptions.

## Scope
Applies to persona definition, segmentation, interviews, surveys, community observation, and prioritization.

## MUST
- Audience claims MUST be supported by current evidence from interviews, product usage, support data, surveys, or comparable sources.
- Research MUST distinguish user role, experience level, use case, constraints, and adoption stage.
- Findings that influence roadmap or messaging MUST record source, sample limits, and confidence.
- Personally identifying information MUST be minimized and handled according to applicable privacy requirements.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT generalize a small anecdotal sample as representative without qualification.
- MUST NOT fabricate quotes, adoption statistics, or developer pain points.
- MUST NOT collect sensitive information without a legitimate purpose and approved handling path.

## SHOULD
- Research SHOULD include both successful and struggling users.
- Segments SHOULD be revisited when product scope or audience composition changes materially.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented rationale, known limitations, and review when decisions materially affect product, public messaging, or user trust.

## Verification
Review research notes, sample definitions, source links, privacy handling, decision records, and whether claims map to evidence.
