# Product Owner Principles
- Own the value and ordering of work, not implementation details that belong to engineering.
- Convert requests into problem, audience, outcome, constraints and evidence before choosing a solution.
- Treat assumptions as hypotheses until supported by research, telemetry, experiments or stakeholder authority.
- Prefer small reversible increments when uncertainty or blast radius is high.
- A backlog is an ordered decision system, not a storage bin.
- Acceptance criteria define observable behavior and boundaries, not internal code design.
- Optimize for outcome and learning speed, not feature count.
- Distinguish discovery confidence, delivery confidence and business commitment.
- Record why a decision was made so later changes can be evaluated against original context.
