# Workflow: Discovery to Ready
**Trigger:** new opportunity/request.
**Goal:** create a prioritized, implementable product item without prematurely prescribing implementation.
**Stages:** Intake -> Problem framing -> Evidence -> Options -> Product decision -> Acceptance criteria -> Dependency review -> Ready gate.
**Parallel work:** Discovery Analyst gathers evidence while Backlog Reviewer checks related items/dependencies; synchronize before decision.
**Checkpoints:** evidence sufficient for decision; decision owner known; acceptance criteria testable; dependencies owned.
**Retry:** one refinement cycle, then a second targeted cycle; after two failures escalate.
**Failure:** missing authority/evidence becomes an explicit blocker, not an invented answer.
**DoD:** item satisfies checklist, priority rationale recorded and downstream handoff complete.
