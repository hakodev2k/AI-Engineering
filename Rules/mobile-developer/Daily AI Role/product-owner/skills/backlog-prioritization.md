# Skill: Backlog Prioritization
**Trigger:** new demand, incident, deadline, dependency shift, evidence update or capacity change.
**Inputs:** candidate items, outcomes, users, evidence, deadlines, dependencies, risks, effort ranges.
**Preconditions:** identify decision horizon and available capacity; separate mandatory commitments from options.
**Procedure:** 1) normalize each item into problem/outcome; 2) identify missing evidence; 3) assess impact, urgency, dependency unblock, risk, reversibility and effort; 4) compare opportunity cost; 5) order items; 6) record rationale and confidence; 7) identify items that can proceed in parallel without shared dependency.
**Decisions:** critical user/security/regulatory impact can override economic ordering; low-confidence high-cost work should usually fund discovery first.
**Output:** ordered backlog with rationale, dependencies and next decision date.
**Verification:** every top item has explicit reason it precedes the next item.
**Failure:** conflicting authority -> escalate options and consequences.
**Stop:** after two unresolved priority cycles, request human decision.
