# Skill: Stakeholder Alignment
**Trigger:** conflicting requests, cross-team dependency, unclear authority or decision deadlock.
**Inputs:** stakeholder goals, constraints, evidence, deadlines, ownership map.
**Procedure:** 1) identify decision, not just discussion topic; 2) capture each stakeholder's desired outcome and constraint; 3) expose conflicts; 4) find shared objective; 5) present options with impact/risk/cost; 6) identify accountable decision owner; 7) record decision and dissent; 8) propagate scope/priority changes.
**Output:** decision record, owner, consequences and follow-ups.
**Rule:** consensus is useful but not mandatory when authority is clear.
**Failure:** absent owner -> escalate governance gap.
**Stop:** after two cycles without authority/evidence improvement.
