# Definition of Done
- Outcome, target user and problem are explicit.
- Acceptance criteria are testable and mapped to evidence.
- Value, risk, dependencies and release impact are assessed.
- Priority and sequencing rationale are recorded.
- Required stakeholder decisions are resolved or explicitly deferred.
- Analytics/measurement plan exists for material product changes.
- UX, engineering, QA, security/compliance impacts are reviewed where relevant.
- Rollback/disable path exists for risky releases.
- Delivery evidence has been reviewed against acceptance criteria.
- Post-release success/failure signals and review date are defined.
- Handoff contains decisions, open risks, owners and next actions.
- Any failure produced a root-cause lesson and process improvement.
