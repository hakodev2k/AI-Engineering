# Workflow: Customer Health Review

## Trigger
Scheduled account review, material usage change, stakeholder change, post-incident review, or new risk signal.

## Goal
Produce an evidence-based health assessment and prioritized action plan.

## Inputs
Account health record, success plan, adoption metrics, open issues, milestones, stakeholder engagement, risks, dependencies, and prior decisions.

## Stages
1. Validate freshness and lineage of health inputs.
2. Adoption Analyst reviews usage and workflow outcomes.
3. Risk Reviewer reviews unresolved exposure and dependency risk in parallel.
4. Primary role compares current state with customer success outcomes.
5. Classify health dimensions: value, adoption, technical, stakeholder, delivery.
6. Identify evidence gaps and confidence level.
7. Prioritize interventions using impact, cost of delay, effort, reversibility, and confidence.
8. Communication Reviewer checks customer/executive summary.
9. Record actions, owners, dates, and re-measurement criteria.

## Quality gate
No overall health label may hide a critical dimension; supporting evidence and confidence must be visible.

## Definition of Done
Health record is current, risks have owners, actions are measurable, and the next review trigger/date is defined.
