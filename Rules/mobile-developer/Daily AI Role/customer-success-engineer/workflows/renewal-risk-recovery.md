# Workflow: Renewal-Risk Recovery

## Trigger
Material risk that the customer will not realize enough value to continue or expand, identified through adoption, unresolved technical issues, stakeholder loss, deadline failure, or explicit concern.

## Goal
Reduce value risk through a factual recovery plan without making unauthorized commercial promises.

## Stages
1. Confirm the risk signal and evidence; distinguish commercial information from technical/value evidence.
2. Reconstruct original desired outcomes and current gaps.
3. Adoption Analyst quantifies behavior/value gaps while Risk Reviewer maps technical, stakeholder, and dependency exposure in parallel.
4. Identify recoverable causes versus constraints outside role authority.
5. Prioritize a small number of interventions with owners, dates, expected signal, and cost of delay.
6. Escalate commercial terms, roadmap commitments, credits, contract interpretation, or executive decisions to accountable humans.
7. Run intervention checkpoints and re-measure.
8. Communicate progress with evidence and unresolved risk, not optimism alone.
9. Close only when risk is materially reduced or transferred with explicit owner/decision.

## Definition of Done
Root risk drivers are documented, recovery actions have evidence criteria, unauthorized promises are absent, accountable owners accept decisions, and the final risk state is explicit.
