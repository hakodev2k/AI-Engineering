# Workflow: Technical RFP/RFI

**Trigger:** Formal questionnaire or technical due diligence request.
**Goal:** Deliver consistent, evidence-backed answers by deadline.

## Stages
1. Parse questions and classify ownership.
2. Establish shared terminology and target product/version.
3. Parallelize product, architecture, security/privacy, and operations sections.
4. Reuse approved answers only after checking source freshness and scope.
5. Consolidate duplicate/contradictory answers.
6. Route legal/commercial/security commitments to accountable owners.
7. Final reviewer verifies evidence, exceptions, and unanswered questions.
8. Submit approved answer set and archive source mapping.

**Retry:** One revision cycle after reviewer findings; unresolved authority-bound items remain explicitly pending.
**DoD:** Every submitted material answer has source/owner; no contradictory or unapproved commitment remains.
