# Skill: Technical Discovery

**Purpose:** Build an evidence-based model of the customer problem and evaluation context.

**Trigger:** New qualified opportunity, unclear requirements, or material change in scope.

**Inputs:** Account context, customer stakeholders, business outcome, existing notes.

**Preconditions:** Customer contact or reliable source exists; discovery objective is known.

**Procedure:**
1. Confirm outcome, urgency, owner, and decision process.
2. Map current workflow and systems.
3. Gather requirements across identity, data, integration, scale, reliability, security, operations, migration, and support as relevant.
4. Separate must-have from preference and assumption.
5. Identify unknowns with highest decision risk.
6. Produce fit-gap hypotheses and next evidence needed.

**Outputs:** Discovery record, requirement list, unknowns, risks, decision criteria, next actions.

**Quality:** No invented requirements; material unknowns are explicit.

**Failure/stop:** Stop and escalate when customer objective cannot be established, contradictory requirements lack an owner, or requested activity exceeds authority.
