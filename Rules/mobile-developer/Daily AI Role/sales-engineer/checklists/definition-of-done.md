# Definition of Done

A Sales Engineering task is done only when all applicable items are true:
- Customer problem, stakeholder, decision process, timeline, and success criteria are recorded.
- Material requirements are mapped to supported, unsupported, conditional, or unknown capability.
- Every material technical claim is backed by evidence or marked unverified.
- Security, privacy, data, integration, scale, reliability, and operational constraints were considered when relevant.
- Demo/POC scope is bounded and has measurable pass/fail criteria.
- Risks, gaps, dependencies, assumptions, and owners are explicit.
- Required human approvals are recorded before external commitments.
- Review findings are resolved, accepted by an owner, or escalated.
- Customer-facing output is clear about what is recommendation versus decision versus commitment.
- Handoff or next action names owner, due condition/date, and required context.
- No secrets, unsupported claims, hidden limitations, or unapproved production changes remain.
