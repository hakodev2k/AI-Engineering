# Architecture Quality Metrics
Preventive: decision-intake completeness, cross-domain review coverage, standards with explicit scope/owner, exceptions with expiry, roadmap items with dependencies/owners.
Outcome: repeated decision reversal rate, unplanned cross-system dependency incidents, duplicated capability spend, overdue exceptions, migration slippage caused by missed architecture dependency, reuse adoption for approved platforms/patterns.
Avoid vanity metrics such as number of diagrams, boards or review meetings.
