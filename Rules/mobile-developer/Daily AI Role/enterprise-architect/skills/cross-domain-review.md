# Cross-Domain Review
Purpose: detect architecture conflicts across business, data, application, integration, security and technology domains.
Inputs: proposal, standards, current/target views, specialist findings.
Steps: check outcome alignment; boundary ownership; data authority; integration coupling; identity/security; resilience/operability; platform compatibility; cost/lock-in; migration impact; exceptions.
Decision rules: local optimization cannot override enterprise risk without explicit exception approval.
Outputs: findings by severity, required changes, approvals and residual risks.
Quality: evidence-linked findings with accountable owner.
Retry: max 2 review/fix cycles before escalation.
