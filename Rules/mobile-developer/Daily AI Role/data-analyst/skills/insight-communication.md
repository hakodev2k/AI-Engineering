# Skill: Insight Communication

**Purpose:** make evidence understandable and actionable without overstating certainty.

**Trigger:** analysis is ready for stakeholder consumption.

**Steps:**
1. Lead with decision context and one-sentence answer.
2. Present 3–5 load-bearing observations with metric/window/population context.
3. Separate interpretation from fact.
4. State confidence, caveats, and strongest alternative explanation.
5. Recommend the smallest sensible action and next evidence checkpoint.
6. Tailor depth to executive, product, engineering, or analyst audience while preserving definitions.
7. Include reproducibility path: query/report/source owner and refresh timestamp.

**Quality:** charts must have labeled units, denominators, periods, and comparable scales. Avoid decorative charts.

**Output:** decision brief/report/dashboard annotation plus handoff.
