# Skill: Metric and Segmentation Analysis

**Purpose:** explain movement in business metrics without confusing composition and within-segment change.

**Trigger:** KPI movement, funnel drop, retention change, cohort difference, or segment request.

**Steps:**
1. Lock metric contract and baseline comparison.
2. Decompose numerator and denominator separately.
3. Compare absolute and relative movement.
4. Check population/composition shift before within-segment conclusions.
5. Evaluate pre-specified dimensions; limit exploratory slicing to avoid false narratives.
6. Test contradictory segments and Simpson's-paradox risk.
7. Quantify contribution where possible and separate largest segment from largest driver.
8. Perform sensitivity checks on window, exclusions, or alternative defensible metric definitions.

**Output:** evidence table, ranked drivers with confidence, unresolved alternatives, and next test.

**Stop:** do not continue slicing until a story appears; mark exploratory findings as hypotheses.
