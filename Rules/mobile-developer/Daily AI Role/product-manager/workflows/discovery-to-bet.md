# Workflow: Discovery to Bet

## Trigger
New signal, strategic theme, KPI issue, or stakeholder opportunity.

## Goal
Convert uncertain signal into an approved, evidence-backed product bet or a clear stop decision.

## Stages
1. Intake and problem framing.
2. Existing-evidence review.
3. Parallel lanes when independent: Customer Signal Analyst, Market Researcher, Metrics Analyst.
4. Consolidate evidence into canonical opportunity record.
5. Risk and Assumption Reviewer challenges evidence and unknowns.
6. Product Manager sizes opportunity and checks strategic fit.
7. Select cheapest next learning step or proposed bet.
8. Obtain human approvals for high-risk/irreversible commitments.
9. Record decision and handoff.

## Checkpoints
Evidence quality, confidence, priority trade-off, dependency readiness, success metric, stop condition.

## Retry
At most two retries after a specific research/validation defect is corrected.

## Definition of Done
A decision exists: proceed, discover more, park, or reject; rationale and next owner are recorded.
