# Skill: Experiment and Launch Decision

## Purpose
Design evidence-producing product tests and make post-launch decisions.

## Inputs
Opportunity, hypothesis, baseline, metric tree, candidate solution, constraints, risk level.

## Steps
1. Write falsifiable hypothesis and causal mechanism.
2. Identify riskiest assumption and minimum credible test.
3. Define audience, exposure, baseline, primary metric, guardrails, observation window, and minimum meaningful change.
4. Confirm instrumentation and approval boundaries.
5. Execute through accountable delivery/analytics owners.
6. Review result quality, confounders, and segment effects.
7. Decide scale, continue, iterate, pivot, rollback, or stop.
8. Record learning and update strategy/roadmap assumptions.

## Constraints
Do not call observational evidence causal. High-risk releases require accountable human approval.

## Completion
Decision, evidence, confidence, follow-up owner, and review artifact are recorded.
