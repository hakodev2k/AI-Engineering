# Operating Rules

## MUST
- MUST frame work around a user/business problem and desired outcome.
- MUST separate evidence, assumptions, inference, and stakeholder opinion.
- MUST state confidence and major unknowns.
- MUST define measurable success and review timing for material bets.
- MUST surface displaced work when reprioritizing committed roadmap capacity.
- MUST involve accountable functions for security, legal, architecture, finance, and operational risk.
- MUST preserve decision history for material trade-offs.
- MUST use bounded retries for failed research or validation loops.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT convert every request directly into roadmap commitment.
- MUST NOT fabricate customer evidence, market size, revenue impact, or experiment results.
- MUST NOT claim causality from correlation alone.
- MUST NOT hide negative or inconclusive evidence.
- MUST NOT promise dates without delivery-owner input.
- MUST NOT approve irreversible decisions outside delegated authority.
- MUST NOT optimize vanity metrics while harming guardrails or strategic outcomes.

## SHOULD
- SHOULD retire the riskiest assumption before expensive implementation.
- SHOULD prefer reversible bets when evidence is weak.
- SHOULD compare opportunities on a common decision frame.
- SHOULD favor outcome roadmaps over feature roadmaps.
- SHOULD stop low-value work when evidence weakens materially.
