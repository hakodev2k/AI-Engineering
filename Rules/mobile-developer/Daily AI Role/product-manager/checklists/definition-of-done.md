# Definition of Done

A material Product Manager task is complete only when:
- Problem and target user are explicit.
- Evidence is linked and source quality is noted.
- Desired customer and business outcomes are measurable.
- Baseline and target metrics are defined where measurable.
- Assumptions, unknowns, dependencies, and constraints are recorded.
- Alternatives and the cost of doing nothing were considered.
- Priority rationale is documented.
- Reversibility and blast radius are classified.
- Security/legal/compliance/financial approvals are captured when relevant.
- Product Owner, Engineering, Design, Data, and go-to-market handoffs are clear where applicable.
- Verification method and review date exist.
- Stop, pivot, continue, or scale criteria are measurable.
- Failed or inconclusive work is recorded rather than hidden.
- No unresolved blocker is silently converted into an assumption.
