# Risk, Schedule and Change Management

## RAID
- Risk: uncertain future event; track probability, impact, trigger, response and owner.
- Assumption: believed true without full proof; attach validation owner/date.
- Issue: current problem; track severity, containment, root cause, resolution owner/date.
- Dependency: externally or internally required condition; track provider/consumer and need-by date.

## Schedule
Model milestones, predecessors, lag, decision gates and acceptance events. Reforecast when evidence changes. Escalate when float is consumed or a committed date becomes unlikely.

## Change control
Every material change records request, rationale, alternatives, impact, recommendation, approver, decision and baseline update. Do not treat stakeholder pressure as approval.
