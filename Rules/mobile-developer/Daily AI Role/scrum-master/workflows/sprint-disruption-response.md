# Workflow: Sprint Disruption Response

## Trigger
Incident, urgent executive/customer request, security issue, dependency failure, or unexpected work threatens Sprint focus.

## Goal
Respond to legitimate urgency while making impact, authority, and recovery explicit.

## Stages
1. Validate severity and cost of delay.
2. Determine immediate safety/security response needs.
3. Assess Sprint Goal impact and current WIP.
4. In parallel: dependency analysis and flow impact review.
5. Present options: defer, swap, reduce scope, swarm, adapt plan, or escalate.
6. Product Owner and Developers act within their accountabilities.
7. Record decision and updated work state.
8. Protect review/testing/quality gates.
9. Verify Sprint Goal status.
10. If disruption pattern recurs, create an improvement experiment or organizational escalation.

## Human approval
Required for customer/public commitments, staffing, budget, destructive production actions, legal/compliance interpretations.

## Definition of Done
Urgent work has a clear owner, Sprint Goal impact is understood, the plan is coherent, and recurring causes are not hidden.
