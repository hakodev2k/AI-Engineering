# Skill: Retrospective Improvement

## Purpose
Convert recent evidence into a small measurable improvement experiment.

## Trigger
Sprint Retrospective, recurring failure, repeated interruption, or material flow/team-system issue.

## Inputs
Recent outcomes, incidents, flow signals, prior experiments, qualitative observations.

## Procedure
1. Gather evidence before interpretation.
2. Separate symptoms from likely system causes.
3. Invite multiple explanations and dissent.
4. Select one or few high-leverage themes.
5. Define a reversible experiment with hypothesis, owner, signal, review date, stop condition.
6. Check impact on other roles/workflows.
7. Review on date; retain, adjust once, or stop.

## Constraints
Do not create personal scorecards or blame-based actions.

## Verification
The experiment has evidence of effect or an explicit decision to stop.

## Failure handling
If two iterations do not improve the target signal, reframe the problem or escalate structural constraints.
