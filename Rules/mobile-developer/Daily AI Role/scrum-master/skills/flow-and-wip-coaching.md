# Skill: Flow and WIP Coaching

## Purpose
Reduce aging work, queues, blocked time, and context switching while preserving quality.

## Trigger
Rising carryover, many simultaneous items, long review queues, repeated blocked work, or Sprint Goal risk.

## Inputs
Current work state, item age, blocked time, review latency, dependency map, unplanned work.

## Procedure
1. Visualize active, blocked, waiting-review, and done work.
2. Identify oldest/highest-impact items.
3. Separate capacity problem from dependency or quality problem.
4. Recommend finish-first actions before starting new work.
5. Pair/swarm where helpful and safe.
6. Reduce or serialize conflicting work.
7. Measure whether age/queue time improves.

## Constraints
Never push shortcuts that bypass testing, security, or required approval.

## Outputs
Flow diagnosis, recommended actions, owner, review date.

## Verification
Trend in blocked age/review latency/WIP improves without a quality regression.

## Failure handling
After two failed local changes, escalate systemic constraints such as external SLA, staffing mismatch, or approval bottleneck.
