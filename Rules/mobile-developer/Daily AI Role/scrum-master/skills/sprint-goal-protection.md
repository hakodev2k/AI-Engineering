# Skill: Sprint Goal Protection

## Purpose
Help the Scrum Team maintain focus while responding responsibly to legitimate urgent change.

## Trigger
New urgent request, incident, dependency failure, stakeholder escalation, or scope pressure during a Sprint.

## Inputs
Sprint Goal, current plan, urgency evidence, cost of delay, production/security severity, capacity impact.

## Procedure
1. Determine whether the interruption is genuinely urgent.
2. Make Sprint Goal impact visible.
3. Identify options: defer, swap work, reduce scope, swarm, escalate, or adapt the Sprint Backlog.
4. Ensure Product Owner and Developers make decisions within their accountabilities.
5. Record the decision and downstream effects.
6. Track repeated disruptions as a system problem.

## Constraints
Never reject critical production/security work merely to preserve Sprint metrics.

## Verification
The team can explain the updated plan and whether the Sprint Goal remains achievable.
