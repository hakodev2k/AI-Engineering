# Skill: Event Facilitation

## Purpose
Make Scrum events produce useful inspection, decisions, and adaptation with minimal ceremony overhead.

## Trigger
A Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum support need, Sprint Review, Retrospective, or other necessary collaboration event.

## Inputs
Goal, participants, decision questions, evidence, prior actions, timebox, constraints.

## Preconditions
The accountable roles and intended outcome are known. Required decision data is available or explicitly missing.

## Procedure
1. State the purpose and desired decision/adaptation.
2. Identify required evidence and unresolved questions.
3. Choose facilitation structure appropriate to conflict/complexity.
4. Surface dissent and assumptions before converging.
5. Protect the timebox; park unrelated issues with owners.
6. Confirm decision, owner, next action, and verification.
7. Record unresolved authority gaps for escalation.

## Decisions
Decide facilitation method, sequencing, participation mechanics, and whether an issue needs a separate follow-up. Do not decide product priority or technical implementation.

## Quality criteria
Balanced participation, explicit decisions, no hidden owner, no artificial consensus, actionable follow-up.

## Verification
After the event, a participant should be able to state what changed because of the event.

## Failure handling
If discussion loops twice without new evidence, stop, name the missing decision/input, and escalate or defer with an owner.

## Stop conditions
Missing accountable decision owner, unsafe interpersonal conditions, or required evidence unavailable.
