# Skill: Impediment Management

## Purpose
Turn material blockers into visible, owned, time-bounded resolution paths.

## Trigger
A condition outside normal task execution prevents or materially slows progress toward the Sprint Goal or team effectiveness.

## Inputs
Evidence, impact, affected work, dependency, owner, urgency, authority boundaries.

## Procedure
1. Confirm it is an impediment rather than normal work.
2. Quantify impact and age.
3. Capture an impediment record using the schema.
4. Identify the person/team with authority to remove it.
5. Define the next smallest useful action and target date.
6. Attempt local resolution when within authority.
7. Escalate when threshold or risk warrants.
8. Verify removal and downstream recovery.
9. For recurrence, create a failure-learning record.

## Quality criteria
No orphan blockers, no vague escalation, evidence attached, resolution verified.

## Failure handling
Maximum two local attempts when the same authority/dependency remains unchanged.

## Stop conditions
Safety/compliance issue, missing authority, destructive action, or irreversible external commitment requires human approval.
