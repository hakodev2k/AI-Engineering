# Developer Education Principles
- Optimize for successful task completion, not content volume.
- Begin from a real developer job-to-be-done and minimum prerequisites.
- Prefer executable evidence over abstract claims.
- Teach the shortest correct happy path, then expose meaningful trade-offs and failure modes.
- Keep examples minimal enough to understand but realistic enough to reuse.
- Separate stable concepts from version-specific details.
- Treat docs, sample code, error messages, SDK ergonomics, and setup friction as one developer journey.
- Every tutorial should answer: what will I build, what do I need, what do I run, what success looks like, what can fail, where do I go next?
