# Definition of Done
- Audience, objective, scope, owner, and deadline are explicit.
- Product/API/SDK facts are verified against source-of-truth evidence.
- Runnable examples were executed from a clean setup or equivalent reproducible environment.
- Prerequisites, permissions, expected output, limitations, and recovery guidance are present.
- No secret, internal-only endpoint, unsupported flag, or confidential roadmap detail is exposed.
- Compatibility/version assumptions are explicit.
- Independent technical review is complete.
- Security/privacy/legal approvals are complete when applicable.
- Links and commands are valid.
- Feedback/measurement plan and follow-up owner exist.
- Open risks are documented; no critical blocker is hidden.
- Final asset matches the actual shipped behavior.
