# Workflow: Telemetry Gap Remediation
Trigger: incident, support case, failed investigation or audit reveals missing/misleading evidence.
Stages: capture unanswered question; reproduce or reconstruct evidence; classify gap as absent, wrong semantics, missing correlation, delayed/dropped pipeline or discoverability problem; prioritize by user/incident impact; design minimal fix; review privacy/cardinality/cost; implement and verify; update contract/dashboard/runbook; record lesson.
Dependencies: do not add a signal until the exact evidence gap is stated.
Escalation: source-system ownership or compliance conflict -> accountable owner.
DoD: original investigation question is answerable with verified evidence.
