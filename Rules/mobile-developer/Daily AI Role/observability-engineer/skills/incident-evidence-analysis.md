# Skill: Incident Evidence Analysis
Trigger: active incident or post-incident telemetry gap.
Inputs: timeline, alerts, metrics, traces, logs, deployment events and topology.
Procedure: establish impact window; verify telemetry health; correlate service/dependency/deployment signals; separate observed facts from hypotheses; identify missing evidence; provide investigation pivots; preserve timestamps/query assumptions; after stabilization convert gaps into improvement work.
Output: evidence summary, confidence, gaps and next probes.
Constraint: do not claim root cause from temporal correlation alone.
