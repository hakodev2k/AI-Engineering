# Gap and Impact Analysis

## Purpose
Assess what must change and who is affected before a requirement or policy change is approved.

## Trigger
Scope change, policy change, migration, integration change, new compliance rule, or production behavior adjustment.

## Inputs
Current state, proposed state, stakeholder map, process map, systems/interfaces, data model, operational procedures, commitments.

## Procedure
1. Define the exact delta from baseline.
2. Identify affected personas, teams, processes, systems, data, reports, interfaces, permissions, training, and support.
3. Classify impacts as direct, indirect, transitional, or unknown.
4. Map dependencies and sequencing constraints.
5. Estimate reversibility and blast radius qualitatively or quantitatively when evidence exists.
6. Identify backward-compatibility and historical-data implications.
7. Record risks, mitigations, decisions, and owner for each unknown.
8. Produce implementation and communication implications without prescribing unsupported technical design.

## Outputs
Gap matrix, impact register, dependency map, risk register, rollout considerations, decision requests.

## Quality criteria
No high-impact stakeholder or system boundary remains unassessed without being explicitly marked unknown.

## Stop conditions
Stop when decision-makers can see the consequence of approve, defer, or reject.
