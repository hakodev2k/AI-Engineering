# Definition of Done

- [ ] Business objective and measurable outcome are explicit.
- [ ] In-scope and out-of-scope boundaries are stated.
- [ ] Stakeholders and decision owner are identified.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, decisions, questions, and recommendations are separated.
- [ ] Business rules are unambiguous and testable.
- [ ] Normal, alternative, exception, invalid, permission, and timing cases are covered where applicable.
- [ ] Process ownership and handoffs are defined where impacted.
- [ ] Data/history/audit implications are assessed where relevant.
- [ ] Dependencies and sequencing constraints have owners.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria permit objective pass/fail verification.
- [ ] Conflicts are resolved or explicitly blocked/escalated.
- [ ] High-risk decisions have required human approval.
- [ ] Requirement → decision → acceptance traceability is complete.
- [ ] Change impact has been reviewed for baselined requirement changes.
- [ ] Independent acceptance/traceability review has no blocking findings.
- [ ] Handoff identifies remaining non-blocking risks and open questions.
- [ ] No required artifact contains placeholder implementation text.
