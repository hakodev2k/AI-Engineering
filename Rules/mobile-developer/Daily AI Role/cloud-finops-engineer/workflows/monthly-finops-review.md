# Workflow: Monthly FinOps Review

## Trigger
Monthly billing period closes or material management cadence begins.

## Stages
1. Reconcile billing scope and adjustments.
2. Review allocation coverage and unresolved ownership.
3. Compare actual vs forecast/budget.
4. Decompose material variance.
5. Review unit-cost trends.
6. Review anomalies and unresolved leaks.
7. In parallel: commitment health, optimization backlog, exception expiry, shared-cost review.
8. Consolidate decisions and owners.
9. Separate estimated from realized savings.
10. Publish executive and engineering views.
11. Record actions with deadlines and verification.

## Checkpoints
Finance reconciliation before reporting; owner validation for material variance; human approval for commercial/destructive actions.

## Retry
At most two correction cycles for data/reconciliation gaps, then escalate.

## Done
Numbers reconcile, material variance has explanation/owner, actions are assigned, and decisions are traceable.
