# Workflow: Optimization to Realization

## Goal
Convert optimization ideas into verified financial outcomes.

## Stages
1. Intake and baseline.
2. Technical/economic analysis.
3. Parallel allocation and risk review when relevant.
4. Rank alternatives.
5. Human approval gate for material change.
6. Pilot or staged rollout.
7. Observe reliability/performance and cost.
8. Normalize for demand changes.
9. Update savings ledger: identified → approved → implemented → realized.
10. Close or rollback.

## Stop conditions
Stop if SLO/security/compliance guardrail is violated, assumptions are invalidated, or expected value becomes negative.

## Bounded retries
Maximum two remediation/retest cycles before escalation to service owner.
