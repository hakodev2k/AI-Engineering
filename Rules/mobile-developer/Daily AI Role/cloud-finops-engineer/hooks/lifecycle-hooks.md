# Lifecycle Hooks

Hooks are deterministic gates, not autonomous side-effect actions.

## intake
Verify: objective, owner, scope, time window, evidence source, currency, materiality, deadline.
Fail closed if the requested financial decision has no accountable owner.

## pre-analysis
Check billing data freshness, account scope, duplicate credits/refunds, currency normalization, and known contract effects.

## pre-recommendation
Require baseline, formula, confidence, operational constraints, alternatives, and approval classification.

## pre-change
Block destructive cleanup, commitment purchase, production capacity reduction, chargeback-policy changes, or control exceptions without human approval.

## post-change
Require observation window, cost normalization method, service health check, and realized-savings update.

## exception-expiry
Flag expired exceptions for renewal or closure; never auto-renew.

## failure-close
Require root cause, lesson, process improvement, prevention owner, and follow-up date.
