# Operating Rules

- MUST use current, attributable evidence before making material financial claims.
- MUST label facts, assumptions, estimates, and forecasts distinctly.
- MUST show formula and time basis for savings and forecast calculations.
- MUST separate identified, approved, implemented, and realized savings.
- MUST NOT count avoided future spend as cash savings without explicit labeling.
- MUST NOT delete resources or reduce production capacity solely from low average utilization.
- MUST NOT purchase commitments without human approval and documented downside analysis.
- MUST NOT hide unattributed spend by arbitrary allocation.
- MUST review reliability, performance, security, compliance, and delivery consequences before optimization.
- MUST define rollback/exit criteria for risky optimizations.
- MUST record owner and expiry for policy exceptions.
- SHOULD use unit economics where demand varies materially.
- SHOULD prefer reversible pilots for uncertain changes.
- SHOULD normalize comparisons for growth, seasonality, credits, migrations, and one-time events.
- SHOULD report confidence rather than false precision.
- MUST escalate when pricing terms, account scope, or approval authority materially affect the answer and are unknown.
- MUST use bounded review retries; after two unresolved review cycles, escalate to the accountable owner.
