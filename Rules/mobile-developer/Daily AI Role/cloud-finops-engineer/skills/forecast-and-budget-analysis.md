# Skill: Forecast and Budget Analysis

## Inputs
Historical normalized spend, commitments, known roadmap, seasonality, growth drivers, migrations, contracts, budgets.

## Procedure
1. Reconcile historical actuals.
2. Remove or annotate one-time effects.
3. Identify structural drivers: traffic, users, storage, environments, launches, migrations.
4. Build baseline forecast with explicit assumptions.
5. Add known step changes.
6. Produce at least base and downside/upside scenarios when uncertainty is material.
7. Compare to budget and quantify variance.
8. Attribute variance to owner/driver.
9. Review forecast confidence and decision sensitivity.
10. Record forecast error after actuals arrive.

## Quality
Never imply a precise forecast when the underlying demand or commercial assumptions are uncertain. Publish ranges/confidence when appropriate.
