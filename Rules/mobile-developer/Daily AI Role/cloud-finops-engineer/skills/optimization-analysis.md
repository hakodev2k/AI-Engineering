# Skill: Optimization Analysis

## Purpose
Produce implementable, economically justified optimization proposals.

## Procedure
1. Define baseline and business/service context.
2. Gather multi-signal usage and reliability evidence.
3. Generate alternatives: rightsizing, scheduling, lifecycle, architecture, demand shaping, commercial option.
4. Quantify gross savings and implementation/operational cost.
5. Evaluate performance, availability, security, compliance, migration, and engineering-effort risk.
6. Compute payback where effort/cost is meaningful.
7. Rank by value, confidence, reversibility, and cost of delay.
8. Route risk review.
9. Obtain approval for material production change.
10. Pilot where uncertainty is meaningful.
11. Measure realized savings after change.

## Common mistakes
Average-utilization-only rightsizing, double-counting savings, assuming list price, ignoring growth, and claiming savings before observation.
