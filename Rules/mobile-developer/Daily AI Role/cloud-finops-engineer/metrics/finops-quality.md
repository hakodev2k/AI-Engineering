# FinOps Quality Metrics

Track outcomes, not vanity activity:
- Allocation coverage percentage.
- Unallocated spend value and age.
- Forecast error by budget owner.
- Cost anomaly mean time to detect and resolve.
- Unit-cost trend for material products/services.
- Commitment utilization and coverage with risk bands.
- Identified vs approved vs implemented vs realized savings.
- Savings realization rate.
- Optimization rollback rate caused by service impact.
- Exception count, value, age, and expiry compliance.
- Percentage of recommendations with reproducible evidence and owner.

Do not count recommendation volume as success. A high number of recommendations can indicate weak platform defaults.
