# Workflow: New Platform Capability

**Trigger:** validated recurring developer need or strategic platform requirement.

**Goal:** deliver a useful self-service capability with explicit contract and measurable outcome.

**Stages:**
1. Intake and validate consumer/problem.
2. Discovery: evidence, existing capability, constraints, ownership.
3. In parallel: DX analysis, dependency mapping, security/reliability review.
4. Design golden path and contract.
5. Human approval for restricted policy/permission/spend decisions.
6. Implement smallest usable automation and documentation.
7. Validate happy/failure/retry/rollback/permissions.
8. Pilot with representative consumers.
9. Review evidence; bounded fix/retest cycles, maximum configured retries before escalation/replan.
10. Progressive rollout.
11. Measure adoption, SLO, task time, support demand.
12. Handoff ongoing ownership and backlog follow-ups.

**Definition of Done:** contract, self-service, evidence, support, metrics, approvals, documentation, rollback, ownership.
