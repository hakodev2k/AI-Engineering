# Workflow: Platform Incident Response

**Trigger:** platform failure materially blocks or harms consumers.

**Goal:** restore safe consumer capability, communicate clearly, and prevent recurrence.

**Stages:** detect/confirm impact -> identify affected journeys and blast radius -> assign incident authority -> parallelize dependency/reliability evidence -> contain or roll back with approval where required -> restore/verify from consumer perspective -> communicate state and workarounds -> capture failure record -> prioritize systemic prevention.

Do not experiment destructively during active impact without authority. Bound automated retries. Escalate when dependencies or permissions exceed platform ownership.

**Done:** consumer path verified, residual risk explicit, owners assigned, incident evidence preserved, prevention action reviewed.
