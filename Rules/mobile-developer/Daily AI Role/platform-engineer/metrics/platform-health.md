# Platform Metrics

Measure system and product outcomes, not individual developer productivity.

Recommended signals:
- self-service success rate;
- p50/p90 task completion time for common journeys;
- manual ticket avoidance rate;
- platform SLO availability/error budget;
- deployment or provisioning failure rate attributable to platform;
- golden-path adoption by eligible workloads;
- support requests per active consumer and repeated-request rate;
- migration/deprecation completion rate;
- mean time to recover from platform incidents;
- platform change failure rate;
- cost per supported workload/capability where meaningful;
- developer satisfaction/task-effort pulse;
- time from platform request to usable capability.

Pair metrics: speed with reliability, adoption with satisfaction, standardization with exception rate, and automation with support burden. Never rank individual developers by platform telemetry.
