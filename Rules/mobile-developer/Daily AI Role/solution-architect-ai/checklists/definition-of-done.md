# Solution Architecture Definition of Done

- [ ] Objective, scope, decision owner, stakeholders, and success criteria are explicit.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, constraints, and open questions are separated.
- [ ] Functional requirements and measurable critical NFRs are traceable.
- [ ] Context, trust boundaries, data flows, integrations, and ownership are visible.
- [ ] Credible alternatives and their trade-offs were compared proportionately to impact.
- [ ] Security, privacy, reliability, performance, scalability, operability, maintainability, delivery, and cost were considered.
- [ ] Failure modes, migration, rollback or roll-forward, observability, and verification are actionable.
- [ ] Risks and dependencies have severity, mitigation, owner, and decision status.
- [ ] Independent review is complete for high-impact or hard-to-reverse decisions.
- [ ] Required human approvals are recorded.
- [ ] Blocking findings are resolved or explicitly accepted by an authorized owner.
- [ ] Implementation and verification owners acknowledge the handoff.
- [ ] Residual risks, review triggers, and follow-up dates are documented.
