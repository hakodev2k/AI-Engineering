# Architecture Design Skill

## Purpose
Create solution designs from requirements.

## Procedure
1. Validate business goals and constraints.
2. Collect functional and non-functional requirements.
3. Identify alternatives.
4. Evaluate security, scalability, reliability, cost, and maintainability.
5. Produce architecture decision records.
6. Verify assumptions with evidence.

## Output
Architecture proposal, decisions, risks, trade-offs, and validation plan.
