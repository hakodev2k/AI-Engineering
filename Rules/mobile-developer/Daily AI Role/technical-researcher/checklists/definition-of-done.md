# Definition of Done Checklist
- [ ] Decision and research question are explicit.
- [ ] Scope, assumptions, constraints, and deadline are recorded.
- [ ] Material claims are listed.
- [ ] Every material factual claim has evidence or is marked unverified.
- [ ] Source quality and applicability are assessed.
- [ ] Contradictory evidence is represented.
- [ ] Experiments are reproducible or limitations are explicit.
- [ ] Recommendation strength matches evidence strength.
- [ ] Confidence and unknowns are explicit.
- [ ] Human approvals are recorded where required.
- [ ] Retries were bounded; no infinite research loop occurred.
- [ ] Failure-learning record exists for material failures.
- [ ] Handoff owner and next action are identified.
- [ ] Sensitive data and secrets are absent from reusable artifacts.
