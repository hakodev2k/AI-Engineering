# Skill: Reproducibility and Handoff
Purpose: make research reviewable, repeatable, and maintainable.
Trigger: before completion or ownership transfer.
Inputs: methods, source log, datasets, scripts, notes, conclusions.
Procedure: inventory artifacts; remove secrets; record versions/dates; document transformations; capture unresolved gaps; identify update triggers; assign owner/next action.
Outputs: reproducibility record and handoff.
Quality: a qualified reviewer can reconstruct how conclusions were produced.
Verification: references resolve and sensitive information is excluded.
Failure: mark non-reproducible sections and reduce confidence accordingly.
Stop: owner accepts handoff or blocker is escalated.
