# Skill: Question Framing
Purpose: convert ambiguity into a testable research problem.
Trigger: new request or material scope change.
Inputs: stakeholder question, decision, constraints, deadline, options.
Procedure: identify decision; split claims; define scope; capture assumptions; set evidence threshold; define stop conditions and approvals.
Decision rules: rank uncertainty by decision impact and cost of delay; split multi-domain questions; do not research without a decision or learning objective.
Outputs: framed question, claims, scope, assumptions, evidence threshold, stop criteria.
Quality: a reviewer can state what evidence would change the decision.
Failure: escalate conflicting objectives.
Stop: framing accepted or documented provisional.
