# Release Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Objective, scope, exclusions, release owner, and decision owner are explicit.
- [ ] Artifact name, version, digest, provenance, and immutability are verified.
- [ ] Target environments, dependencies, compatibility, and migration sequencing are understood.
- [ ] Required build, test, security, data, and operational evidence is current and passing.
- [ ] Required approvals and exceptions have accountable owners and evidence.
- [ ] Deployment stages, ownership, checkpoints, and stop criteria are documented.
- [ ] Rollback or roll-forward trigger, owner, feasibility, and rehearsal evidence are recorded.
- [ ] Health checks, thresholds, observation window, and decision owner are explicit.
- [ ] Support and stakeholder communications are prepared and owned.
- [ ] High-impact/irreversible actions remain human approval-gated.
- [ ] Independent readiness review has no unresolved blocking finding.
- [ ] Outcome evidence, residual risk, follow-up, and release-system learning are captured.
