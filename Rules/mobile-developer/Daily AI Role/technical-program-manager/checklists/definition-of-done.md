# Technical Program Definition of Done

- [ ] Sponsor, program owner, decision owners, scope, exclusions, and authority are explicit.
- [ ] Intended outcomes and measurable success evidence are accepted.
- [ ] Workstreams, milestones, interfaces, dependencies, and critical path have accountable owners.
- [ ] Material risks, issues, assumptions, and decisions are current and evidence-based.
- [ ] Architecture, security, quality, release, support, recovery, and operational gates pass where applicable.
- [ ] Commitments and baseline changes have accountable approval.
- [ ] Milestone and outcome status is supported by evidence rather than activity alone.
- [ ] Blocking findings and unresolved dependencies are resolved or explicitly escalated.
- [ ] Operational ownership and knowledge transfer are acknowledged.
- [ ] Residual actions have owners and dates.
- [ ] Stakeholders receive final status and the system of record is updated.
- [ ] Material failures produce a recorded cause, lesson, and process improvement.
