# Cloud Engineering Quality Metrics

Track trends, not vanity numbers:
- percentage of production resources managed by approved IaC
- unauthorized/public exposure findings
- privileged access exceptions and age
- deployment rollback/change-failure rate
- mean time to restore for Azure-caused incidents
- backup restore test success
- quota headroom for critical resources
- drift findings and time to reconcile
- cost variance against approved baseline
- percentage of critical workloads with tested RTO/RPO evidence

Metrics require context; never optimize one metric by degrading reliability, security, or business outcomes.
