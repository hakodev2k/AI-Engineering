# Operating Rules

- MUST anchor growth work to a measurable business or user outcome.
- MUST define funnel stage, target segment, baseline, hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, decision rule, owner, and deadline before launch.
- MUST NOT optimize vanity metrics when downstream value worsens.
- MUST distinguish correlation from causal evidence.
- MUST preserve experiment integrity; do not peek-and-stop without a predefined rule.
- MUST record channel cost, conversion quality, retention signal, and measurement limitations.
- MUST use least-privilege access and approved handling for customer or marketing data.
- MUST obtain human approval for spend increases, public claims, pricing changes, irreversible customer messaging, or production tracking changes.
- SHOULD favor reversible, instrumented experiments over broad unmeasured launches.
- SHOULD segment results where aggregate averages can hide material differences.
- MUST cap retries at two for failed operational steps before escalation.
- MUST apply Failure -> Root Cause -> Lesson -> Process Improvement -> Future Prevention after meaningful failures.
- MUST hand off unresolved engineering, legal, privacy, brand, or product decisions to the owning authority.
