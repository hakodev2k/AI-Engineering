# Lifecycle Campaign Workflow
Trigger: validated lifecycle friction or behavior-triggered opportunity.
Goal: improve retained value or reactivation without consent, fatigue or quality regressions.
Inputs: lifecycle state, audience rules, trigger, message/experience, guardrails.
Stages: define incremental objective; validate audience and consent; define holdout or comparison; create variants; independent lifecycle review; dry-run suppression/trigger logic; human approval for irreversible broad sends; launch; monitor delivery/complaints/unsubscribes; analyze incremental outcome; archive learning.
Parallelism: creative, audience QA and measurement can proceed after shared brief freezes.
Retries: max two operational retries; duplicate-send risk causes immediate stop.
Failure: isolate, suppress, root-cause, repair, prevention.
DoD: eligible audience evidenced, guardrails acceptable, incrementality assessed, next action owned.
