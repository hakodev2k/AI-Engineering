# Channel Allocation Review
Trigger: weekly/monthly budget review, saturation signal, CAC/payback deterioration.
Goal: allocate effort/spend toward sustainable marginal growth.
Inputs: channel spend, funnel quality, cohort value, attribution caveats, capacity and budget constraints.
Stages: validate data; normalize economics; segment; model marginal response; identify saturation/quality shifts; review with economics reviewer; rank hold/test/scale/cut actions; request approval where required; schedule measurement checkpoint.
Parallelism: per-channel analysis may run independently using one shared metric contract; final consolidation is owned by Growth Marketer.
Dependencies: no scaling recommendation before downstream quality and attribution caveats are known.
Retries: at most two data-repair cycles.
Human gate: any material spend increase or contractual commitment.
DoD: allocation action, expected outcome, downside guardrail, owner and review date recorded.
