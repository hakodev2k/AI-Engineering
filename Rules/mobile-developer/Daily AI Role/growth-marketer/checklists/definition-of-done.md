# Definition of Done
A growth work item is complete only when:
- Objective, funnel stage, segment, owner and deadline are explicit.
- Baseline and source-of-truth metric definitions are recorded.
- Hypothesis/mechanism and expected decision are stated where experimentation applies.
- Primary metric and guardrails are unambiguous.
- Instrumentation/data quality is verified or limitations are explicit.
- Dependencies and approvals are resolved or accepted by named owners.
- Independent review appropriate to measurement, experiment, channel or lifecycle risk is complete.
- Outcome is measured against the predefined decision rule.
- Downstream quality and economic effects are considered before scaling.
- Rollout, rollback or stop action has an owner.
- Failures include root cause, lesson, process improvement and prevention.
- Handoff artifacts contain evidence, uncertainty and next action.
