# Retention and Lifecycle
Purpose: improve durable value realization after activation.
Trigger: churn, cohort decay, re-engagement opportunity.
Inputs: retention cohorts, lifecycle states, product usage, support/research evidence, communication eligibility.
Procedure: define meaningful retained behavior; compare cohorts; identify drop-off moments and causes; segment by value/risk; select product or lifecycle intervention; enforce consent/frequency constraints; test incremental lift; inspect long-term quality and unsubscribe/complaint guardrails.
Decisions: do not use messaging to mask unresolved product failure; favor behavior-triggered relevance over volume.
Output: lifecycle map, intervention, experiment, measured lift and learning.
Quality: incremental effect separated from naturally returning users where possible.
Failure: fatigue or negative guardrail -> stop and rollback.
Stop: outcome is measured and next action recorded.
