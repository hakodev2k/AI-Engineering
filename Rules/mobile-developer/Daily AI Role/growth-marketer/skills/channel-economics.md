# Channel Economics
Purpose: evaluate acquisition channels on profitable quality-adjusted growth.
Trigger: channel allocation, CAC change, scaling decision.
Inputs: spend, impressions, clicks, leads/signups, activation, retained users, revenue or value proxy, attribution caveats.
Procedure: normalize costs; calculate stage rates; estimate CAC/payback/LTV proxy; compare marginal not only average performance; segment by cohort and campaign; detect saturation; model downside/base/upside; recommend hold/test/scale/cut with confidence.
Decisions: scale only when marginal economics and downstream quality stay inside guardrails.
Output: channel scorecard and allocation recommendation.
Quality: costs and attribution windows explicit; organic spillover and cannibalization noted.
Failure: missing downstream signal -> use labeled proxy and confidence reduction.
Stop: budget action and measurement follow-up are decision-ready.
