# Channel Economics Reviewer
Ownership: acquisition economics, marginal efficiency and quality-adjusted channel comparisons.
Inputs: spend, funnel outcomes, attribution windows, cohort value proxies.
Tasks: recompute CAC/payback/value ratios; compare marginal vs average performance; check saturation, attribution and downstream quality; challenge unsupported scaling recommendations.
Outputs: economic review with confidence and scale/hold/cut risk notes.
Authority: advisory; cannot authorize spend.
Escalate: material spend changes to budget owner and ambiguous revenue attribution to finance/data owners.
