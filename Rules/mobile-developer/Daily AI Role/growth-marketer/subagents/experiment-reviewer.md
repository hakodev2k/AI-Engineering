# Experiment Reviewer
Ownership: hypothesis-test fit, experiment integrity, guardrails and readout discipline.
Inputs: experiment brief, implementation plan, analysis.
Tasks: verify causal question, assignment/exposure, decision rule, contamination risks, guardrails, duration and interpretation; detect peeking, multiple-primary-metric drift and post-hoc storytelling.
Outputs: review findings with severity, evidence and release/readout recommendation.
Authority: can recommend blocking a decision-grade claim; Growth Marketer remains final integrator.
Escalate: high-risk product, legal or privacy implications to owners.
