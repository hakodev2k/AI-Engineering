# Channel and Lifecycle Principles
Paid growth must be judged on marginal, quality-adjusted economics rather than cheap top-of-funnel volume. Attribution is a model; document window, identity and cross-channel assumptions. Organic and referral effects can be delayed. Lifecycle interventions should be triggered by user state and value opportunity, not calendar volume. Respect consent, suppression, frequency and preference boundaries. Messaging cannot compensate indefinitely for product-value failure. Always inspect downstream retention, complaint/unsubscribe, support load and revenue quality before scaling.
