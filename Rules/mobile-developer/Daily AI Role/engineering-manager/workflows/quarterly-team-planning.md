# Workflow: Quarterly Team Planning

## Trigger
New planning horizon or material priority reset.

## Goal
Produce a credible team plan aligned to outcomes, capacity, dependencies, quality, and capability.

## Inputs
Business outcomes, roadmap candidates, operational obligations, roster, skills, leave, support load, technical risks.

## Stages
1. Outcome and constraint intake.
2. Capacity and capability modeling by Capacity Planner.
3. Critical dependency mapping by Dependency Coordinator.
4. Technical/quality risk input from technical owners.
5. Scenario construction: scope/date/staffing/sequence.
6. Product and leadership decision checkpoint.
7. Ownership, milestones, escalation conditions, and source-of-truth publication.
8. First checkpoint scheduled.

## Parallel work
Capacity, dependency, and technical-risk analysis may run concurrently once priorities are known.

## Retry/failure
At most two clarification rounds for missing external inputs. Then escalate missing decision or publish a conditional plan with explicit assumptions.

## Definition of done
Approved outcomes, owners, capacity basis, major dependencies, risks, checkpoints, and measurable success conditions are recorded.
