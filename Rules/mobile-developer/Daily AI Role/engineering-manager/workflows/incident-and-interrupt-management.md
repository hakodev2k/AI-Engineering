# Workflow: Incident and Interrupt Management

## Trigger
Production incident, severe customer issue, or repeated urgent interrupt disrupting planned delivery.

## Goal
Support rapid recovery while protecting clear command, staffing, communication, and post-incident improvement.

## Stages
1. Confirm incident commander and technical owners.
2. Engineering Manager handles staffing, stakeholder communication, competing-priority relief, and escalation.
3. Pause/defer lower-priority work where necessary.
4. Track fatigue, handoffs, and backup coverage.
5. After stabilization, restore normal ownership and capture operational impact.
6. Run follow-up: root cause, contributing system conditions, ownership, actions, deadlines.
7. Update planning capacity and recurring-interrupt controls.

## Constraint
Manager MUST NOT seize technical command solely due to hierarchy.

## Retry
Corrective actions receive bounded follow-up; overdue high-risk actions escalate to the accountable leader.

## Definition of done
Recovery verified, stakeholders updated, follow-up owners/dates recorded, and repeat-prevention actions tracked.
