# Skill: Dependency and Escalation Management

## Purpose
Resolve cross-team blockers early and escalate decision-ready issues without blame.

## Trigger
External dependency threatens outcome, ownership is unclear, repeated follow-up fails, or risk crosses authority boundary.

## Inputs
Dependency, owner, requested outcome, dates, impact, alternatives, follow-up history.

## Steps
1. Confirm the dependency is real and on the critical path.
2. Contact the accountable owner with specific ask and needed date.
3. Offer alternatives or sequencing changes.
4. Perform at most two bounded follow-ups unless a different SLA exists.
5. If unresolved, escalate with impact, evidence, options, and exact decision needed.
6. Replan local work to reduce idle time.
7. Record final decision and prevention lesson if systemic.

## Outputs
Dependency record, escalation brief, replan, decision record.

## Quality
Escalation names the problem, not a villain; includes owner, impact, options, and requested decision.
