# Skill: UX Problem Framing

**Purpose:** define the right user problem before solution design.
**Trigger:** new feature request, redesign request, complaint cluster, funnel issue, or ambiguous stakeholder request.
**Inputs:** request, user/context evidence, business objective, constraints, deadlines, current behavior.
**Preconditions:** accountable decision owner is identifiable.
**Context/tools:** product artifacts, analytics, support evidence, research, stakeholder notes.

## Steps
1. Translate the request into user, situation, task, friction, consequence, and desired outcome.
2. Separate evidence from assumptions and stakeholder opinions.
3. Identify affected segments and exclusion risks.
4. Define current-state behavior and why it matters.
5. Write measurable/observable success criteria.
6. State non-goals, constraints, dependencies, and decision boundaries.
7. Rank unknowns by impact × uncertainty × cost of being wrong.
8. Select validation for the riskiest unknown first.

## Decisions
Do not begin detailed interaction design while a critical problem assumption remains both high-impact and cheap to test.

## Constraints
Do not invent missing user needs.

## Output
Problem frame, assumption/risk register, validation plan.

## Quality/verification
Stakeholders can distinguish problem, proposed solution, assumptions, and evidence.

## Failure/stop
After two attempts to resolve a blocking ambiguity, escalate with explicit options and consequences.
