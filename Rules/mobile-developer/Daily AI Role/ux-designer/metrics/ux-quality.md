# UX Quality Metrics

Use metrics as decision evidence, not vanity scores.

- Task success rate for critical journeys.
- Completion time or effort where speed matters.
- Error, retry, abandonment, and recovery rates.
- Support-contact rate attributable to interaction confusion.
- Accessibility defects by severity and recurrence.
- Usability findings by severity and resolution status.
- Percentage of material decisions linked to evidence or explicit assumptions.
- Rework caused by missing states or ambiguous handoff.
- Validation coverage of high-risk assumptions.
- Design-review lead time and blocker age.

Choose only metrics relevant to the stated outcome; define denominator, population, time window, and known caveats.
