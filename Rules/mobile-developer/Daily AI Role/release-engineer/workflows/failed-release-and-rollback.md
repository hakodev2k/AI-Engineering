# Workflow: Failed Release and Rollback
**Trigger:** release verification fails or stop condition is hit. **Goal:** restore safe service and preserve evidence. Freeze further promotion; record failure state; assess rollback feasibility; obtain required approval for destructive/data-impacting action; execute rollback or alternate recovery; verify service and business health; communicate status; preserve logs and timestamps; complete Failure -> Root Cause -> Lesson -> Process Improvement -> Future Prevention. Do not retry failed release until cause is understood enough to change conditions.
