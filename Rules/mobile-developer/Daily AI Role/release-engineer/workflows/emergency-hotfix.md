# Workflow: Emergency Hotfix
**Trigger:** urgent production risk requiring accelerated release. **Goal:** reduce harm without abandoning traceability. Capture incident reason, minimal scope, exact artifact, fast risk assessment, rollback plan, and named approver. Run evidence/provenance checks in parallel where time allows. Human approval is mandatory for bypassed controls. Promote in smallest safe scope, verify immediately, stop on adverse signals. Create follow-up work for every bypassed control. Bounded retry: 1 unless incident commander authorizes another attempt with changed conditions.
