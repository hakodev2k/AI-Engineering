# Content Strategy Cycle

## Trigger
New planning period, new market/product area, major repositioning, or material performance gap.

## Goal
Produce an evidence-backed prioritized content system and execution backlog.

## Inputs
Business objectives, audience evidence, positioning, inventory, analytics, resource constraints, deadlines.

## Preconditions
Objective owner identified; approved positioning source available or explicitly marked as a blocker.

## Stages
1. **Frame** — Content Strategist defines decisions, constraints, outputs, and success measures.
2. **Research** — Audience Researcher gathers audience/problem evidence while Performance Analyst audits current performance in parallel.
3. **Synthesize** — Strategist consolidates facts, hypotheses, contradictions, and confidence.
4. **Architect** — map journeys, topic territories, canonical assets, derivatives, taxonomy, and content gaps.
5. **Prioritize** — rank backlog by impact, user value, strategic fit, dependency, deadline, risk, cost of delay, effort, reversibility, and confidence.
6. **Review** — Claims Verifier checks material factual premises; stakeholders review decisions within authority.
7. **Commit** — assign owners, acceptance criteria, measurement, dependencies, and checkpoints.

## Dependencies and Parallelism
Research and performance audit may run concurrently after framing. Architecture waits for blocking audience/positioning questions. Final prioritization waits for synthesis.

## Checkpoints
Research sufficiency; architecture consistency; approval for material positioning change.

## Retry Policy
At most two transient tool retries and three strategy revision cycles. Repeated disagreement becomes an explicit escalation with alternatives.

## Failure Path
Missing objective → stop. Conflicting positioning → escalate. Weak evidence → reduce confidence or commission bounded research.

## Definition of Done
Strategy map exists; prioritized backlog has owners, outcomes, dependencies, evidence, measurement, risks, and approvals; no blocking contradiction is hidden.
