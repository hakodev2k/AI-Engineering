# Definition of Done

A major content task is complete only when every applicable condition is satisfied.

## Strategy and scope
- [ ] Audience, problem/job, objective, desired action, owner, deadline, and priority are recorded.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, hypotheses, decisions, evidence, open questions, and risks are distinguishable.
- [ ] Canonical versus derivative status is clear.

## Content quality
- [ ] Brief acceptance criteria are met.
- [ ] Material claims are verified against approved evidence.
- [ ] Terminology and positioning match canonical sources.
- [ ] Information hierarchy, examples, CTA, metadata, links, and channel constraints are reviewed.
- [ ] Applicable accessibility and localization requirements are addressed.

## Risk and approval
- [ ] Legal, regulatory, security, privacy, contractual, confidentiality, brand, and public-impact risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Required human approvals exist and are attributable.
- [ ] No unresolved blocker remains hidden.

## Verification
- [ ] Final asset/version exists in its intended destination.
- [ ] Publication or handoff has been verified, not merely attempted.
- [ ] Measurement instrumentation and primary/guardrail metric definitions are verified.
- [ ] Evidence and review decisions are retained.

## Operations
- [ ] Upstream sources and downstream derivatives are traceable.
- [ ] Volatile claims have freshness/expiry handling.
- [ ] Next measurement or freshness checkpoint has owner/date.
- [ ] A receiver can continue from the handoff without rediscovering context.

Publication alone is never sufficient evidence of completion.
