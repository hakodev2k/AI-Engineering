# Audience and Problem Research

## Purpose
Turn ambiguous audience assumptions into an evidence-backed problem model that can guide messaging and editorial priorities.

## Trigger
New market, segment, campaign, product area, content gap, or conflicting stakeholder assumptions.

## Inputs
Business objective, target segment, existing research, support/sales notes, product analytics, search/community signals, constraints, deadline.

## Procedure
1. State the decision the research must inform and the minimum evidence needed.
2. Separate known facts from hypotheses and unknowns.
3. Gather representative evidence from approved first-party sources before broadening outward.
4. Cluster recurring jobs, pains, triggers, objections, vocabulary, alternatives, and desired outcomes.
5. Note contradictory evidence and sample bias.
6. Rank problems by frequency, severity, strategic fit, evidence confidence, and addressability.
7. Convert findings into audience/problem statements with citations or source references.
8. Identify research gaps that materially block strategy; do not over-research non-blocking uncertainty.

## Decisions
Proceed when evidence is sufficient to discriminate among content choices. Escalate when segmentation, legal constraints, or business objective is unresolved.

## Outputs
Audience evidence map, prioritized problems, vocabulary, objections, assumptions, open questions, confidence levels.

## Quality and Verification
A reviewer can trace each material claim to evidence; contradictory signals are represented; no persona detail is invented.

## Stop Conditions
Stop after the decision threshold is met, the research budget/deadline is reached, or a blocking dependency requires human resolution.
