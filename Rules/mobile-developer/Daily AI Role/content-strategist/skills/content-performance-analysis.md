# Content Performance Analysis

## Purpose
Determine whether published content produced the intended audience and business outcome, why, and what to change next.

## Trigger
Measurement checkpoint, campaign review, refresh cycle, unusual metric movement, or strategic planning.

## Inputs
Objective, metric definitions, baseline, distribution data, analytics, qualitative feedback, attribution limits, publication history, content changes.

## Procedure
1. Reconfirm the intended outcome and primary/guardrail metrics before reading results.
2. Validate instrumentation, time window, denominator, segmentation, and comparable baseline.
3. Separate exposure, engagement, progression, conversion, retention/support impact, and business outcome.
4. Compare by audience, channel, topic, format, age, distribution intensity, and meaningful cohorts.
5. Avoid causal claims from correlation alone; record plausible confounders.
6. Combine quantitative movement with qualitative evidence and content-level review.
7. Classify action: continue, scale, adapt, test, refresh, consolidate, redistribute, or retire.
8. Turn validated lessons into updated rules, briefs, architecture, or knowledge only when evidence is sufficient.

## Outputs
Performance diagnosis, confidence, evidence, decision, next action, owner, next checkpoint.

## Quality
Metric definitions are explicit, baseline is valid, attribution uncertainty is visible, and recommendations follow evidence rather than vanity metrics.
