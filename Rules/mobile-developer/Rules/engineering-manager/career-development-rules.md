# Career Development Rules
## Purpose
Support credible growth through role-relevant opportunities and evidence.
## Scope
Growth plans, mentoring, promotion readiness, succession, and development assignments.
## MUST
- Connect development goals to observable competencies and meaningful work opportunities.
- Give employees actionable evidence about readiness gaps before promotion decisions where possible.
- Distribute growth opportunities deliberately rather than only to the most visible people.
## MUST NOT
- Promise promotion outcomes outside the manager’s authority.
- Require unsustainable overtime as evidence of advancement readiness.
## SHOULD
- Build succession and delegation paths for critical responsibilities.
## Exceptions
Confidential organizational constraints may limit disclosure but must not justify misleading commitments.
## Verification
Review growth plans, opportunity allocation, feedback, promotion evidence, and succession coverage.
