# Reliability Rules
## Purpose
Balance feature delivery with measurable service reliability.
## Scope
Availability, SLOs, error budgets, resilience work, and operational ownership.
## MUST
- Define reliability expectations for critical services with measurable indicators where practical.
- Prioritize corrective work when reliability breaches create unacceptable user or business risk.
- Assign clear operational ownership for production services.
## MUST NOT
- Claim reliability improvement without operational evidence.
- Repeatedly defer known critical resilience gaps without explicit risk acceptance.
## SHOULD
- Use SLOs and error-budget style signals to guide delivery trade-offs where appropriate.
## Exceptions
Alternative reliability frameworks are acceptable when measurable and reviewed.
## Verification
Inspect SLOs, incidents, alerts, operational metrics, backlog decisions, and risk acceptance.
