# Organizational Design Rules
## Purpose
Shape team boundaries and ownership to support clear accountability and effective delivery.
## Scope
Team topology, ownership, interfaces, staffing, and structural changes.
## MUST
- Define durable ownership for critical systems and responsibilities.
- Evaluate reorganizations against expected outcomes, transition cost, dependency changes, and continuity risk.
- Provide explicit handoff plans when ownership moves.
## MUST NOT
- Reorganize solely to imitate another organization or solve an unverified process problem.
- Leave production systems without an accountable owning team.
## SHOULD
- Align team boundaries with stable product or domain responsibilities where practical.
## Exceptions
Temporary task forces require scope, decision authority, duration, and exit criteria.
## Verification
Inspect ownership maps, handoff records, dependency patterns, staffing plans, and post-change outcomes.
