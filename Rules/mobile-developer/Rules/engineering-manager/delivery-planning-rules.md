# Delivery Planning Rules
## Purpose
Create credible delivery commitments without sacrificing quality or safety.
## Scope
Planning, milestones, dependencies, capacity, and delivery forecasts.
## MUST
- Base commitments on explicit scope, dependencies, capacity, uncertainty, and operational work.
- Surface material schedule risks early with impact and mitigation options.
- Re-plan when evidence invalidates assumptions rather than preserving obsolete dates.
## MUST NOT
- Convert estimates into guarantees without acknowledging uncertainty.
- Hide maintenance, incident, support, or leave capacity from plans.
## SHOULD
- Use ranges or confidence levels for uncertain work.
## Exceptions
Fixed commitments require documented constraints, accepted trade-offs, and accountable approval.
## Verification
Compare plans with capacity, dependency records, risk logs, and delivery retrospectives.
