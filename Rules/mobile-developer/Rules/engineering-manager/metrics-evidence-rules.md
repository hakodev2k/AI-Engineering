# Metrics and Evidence Rules
## Purpose
Use evidence to improve decisions without incentivizing harmful metric gaming.
## Scope
Engineering metrics, productivity signals, delivery data, quality, and operational evidence.
## MUST
- Define what a metric measures, what it does not measure, and the decision it informs.
- Combine quantitative signals with context before consequential people or process decisions.
- Validate claims of improvement with before/after evidence where practical.
## MUST NOT
- Use lines of code, commit count, ticket count, or similar activity metrics as standalone individual performance measures.
- Treat agent confidence or anecdote as sufficient evidence for material conclusions.
## SHOULD
- Prefer outcome, flow, quality, reliability, and user-impact measures.
## Exceptions
Proxy metrics require documented limitations and review.
## Verification
Inspect metric definitions, dashboards, decision records, baselines, and unintended incentives.
