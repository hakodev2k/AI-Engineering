# Stakeholder Communication Rules
## Purpose
Provide accurate, decision-useful communication about engineering outcomes and risk.
## Scope
Status, estimates, incidents, trade-offs, escalations, and executive communication.
## MUST
- Separate facts, assumptions, forecasts, and decisions in material updates.
- Communicate material risk early enough for stakeholders to act.
- Translate technical consequences into user, business, cost, or operational impact.
## MUST NOT
- Hide uncertainty to make a status appear more confident.
- Report activity as outcome when the distinction affects decisions.
## SHOULD
- Tailor detail to audience while preserving material caveats.
## Exceptions
Confidential matters may restrict detail but must not create materially false impressions.
## Verification
Review status reports, risk logs, decision records, incident communications, and stakeholder feedback.
