# Cost and Capacity Rules
## Purpose
Manage engineering capacity and technology cost as constrained resources.
## Scope
Headcount, cloud spend, vendor cost, operational load, and investment allocation.
## MUST
- Make material capacity decisions using priorities, mandatory work, support load, and realistic availability.
- Investigate significant cost changes with usage and architectural evidence.
- Require accountable approval for material recurring cost commitments.
## MUST NOT
- Optimize cost by removing necessary reliability, security, or recovery controls without risk approval.
- Assume nominal headcount equals fully available delivery capacity.
## SHOULD
- Prefer unit-cost and cost-to-outcome measures over raw spend alone.
## Exceptions
Urgent capacity or cost actions require documented rationale and later review.
## Verification
Inspect capacity plans, cost dashboards, contracts, utilization evidence, and approval records.
