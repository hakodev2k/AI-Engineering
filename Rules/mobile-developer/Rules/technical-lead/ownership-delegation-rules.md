# Ownership and Delegation Rules
## Purpose
Create clear technical ownership without making the lead a bottleneck.
## Scope
Decision ownership, implementation delegation, review, escalation, and mentoring.
## MUST
- Critical areas MUST have identifiable ownership and escalation paths.
- Delegated high-risk work MUST define decision boundaries and required review points.
- The lead MUST escalate risks exceeding their authority rather than silently accepting them.
## MUST NOT
- Centralize routine decisions that competent owners can safely make.
- Delegate accountability without giving sufficient context, authority, or support.
## SHOULD
- Use delegation to grow team capability while retaining controls proportional to risk.
## Exceptions
Incident command may temporarily centralize decisions when speed and coordination require it.
## Verification
Review ownership records, PR reviewers, decision history, escalation behavior, and recurring bottlenecks.
