# Prioritization Rules
## Purpose
Allocate limited product capacity to the highest-value work using explicit evidence and trade-offs.
## Scope
Backlog ordering, portfolio choices, sequencing, interrupts, and investment decisions.
## MUST
- Prioritize using stated criteria such as customer impact, strategic fit, risk, effort, urgency, and confidence.
- Make opportunity cost visible for major additions or scope changes.
- Reassess priorities when material evidence or constraints change.
## MUST NOT
- Prioritize solely by stakeholder seniority or request volume.
- Hide mandatory compliance, reliability, or security work behind feature scoring.
## SHOULD
- Separate committed work from candidate opportunities and discovery items.
## Exceptions
True incidents, legal deadlines, or critical security issues may bypass normal scoring with documented rationale.
## Verification
Inspect prioritization criteria, decision records, roadmap changes, evidence, and treatment of risk-reduction work.
