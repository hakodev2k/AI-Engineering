# Roadmap Rules
## Purpose
Represent strategic intent and sequencing without turning uncertainty into false commitments.
## Scope
Product roadmaps, themes, milestones, dependencies, and communication of timing.
## MUST
- Tie roadmap items to outcomes, evidence, dependencies, and confidence.
- Distinguish committed dates from directional sequencing and discovery work.
- Update stakeholders when assumptions or delivery confidence materially change.
## MUST NOT
- Present speculative dates as guaranteed commitments.
- Keep obsolete roadmap items solely to avoid difficult conversations.
## SHOULD
- Prefer outcome or problem-oriented roadmap items over solution inventories.
## Exceptions
Contractual dates require explicit dependency, risk, and contingency tracking.
## Verification
Inspect roadmap metadata, decision logs, dependency reviews, confidence labels, and change communication.
