# Product Strategy Rules
## Purpose
Ensure product direction is evidence-based, coherent, and tied to measurable outcomes.
## Scope
Vision, positioning, strategic choices, investment themes, and outcome ownership.
## MUST
- Define target users, problem space, strategic objective, constraints, and success metrics before committing major investment.
- Make trade-offs explicit when choosing where to compete and where not to invest.
- Revisit strategy when material market, customer, regulatory, or technical evidence changes.
## MUST NOT
- Treat feature volume as strategy.
- Present unsupported assumptions as validated facts.
## SHOULD
- Use a small set of durable strategic principles to guide prioritization.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented context, evidence, risk, and approval from the accountable decision-maker.
## Verification
Inspect strategy documents, decision records, metric definitions, roadmap alignment, and evidence supporting major bets.
