# Decision Documentation Rules
## Purpose
Preserve context, rationale, and accountability for consequential product decisions.
## Scope
Major scope, strategy, roadmap, pricing, policy, launch, deprecation, and risk decisions.
## MUST
- Record decision, owner, date, context, evidence, alternatives considered, trade-offs, risks, and follow-up criteria for material decisions.
- Update or supersede records when later evidence changes the decision.
- Link decisions to affected requirements, roadmap items, or operational actions when practical.
## MUST NOT
- Rewrite historical rationale to make past decisions appear more certain than they were.
- Rely on private chat as the sole record for consequential decisions.
## SHOULD
- Keep records concise enough to remain usable while preserving material context.
## Exceptions
Incident-time decisions may be documented immediately afterward if delay would increase harm.
## Verification
Inspect decision logs, linked evidence, ownership, alternatives, risk notes, and superseding records.
