# Requirements Quality Rules
## Purpose
Make requirements clear enough to build, test, operate, and evaluate without hiding critical assumptions.
## Scope
Product requirements, acceptance criteria, constraints, edge cases, dependencies, and non-functional expectations.
## MUST
- Describe intended outcome, users, scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and known risks for significant work.
- Clarify ambiguous business rules before implementation reaches an irreversible stage.
- Include relevant security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, and performance expectations.
## MUST NOT
- Use implementation detail as a substitute for user or business intent.
- Leave material edge cases intentionally undefined to accelerate delivery.
## SHOULD
- Keep requirements outcome-oriented while allowing engineering design freedom.
## Exceptions
Exploratory spikes may use lighter requirements when purpose, time box, and decision goal are explicit.
## Verification
Review requirement artifacts, acceptance criteria, open questions, dependency mapping, and evidence of cross-functional review.
