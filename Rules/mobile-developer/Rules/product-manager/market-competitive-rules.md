# Market and Competitive Rules
## Purpose
Use market evidence without copying competitors or confusing visibility with customer value.
## Scope
Market sizing, segmentation, competitor analysis, positioning, and trend assessment.
## MUST
- Identify evidence source, recency, assumptions, and uncertainty for material market claims.
- Evaluate competitors by customer problem, segment, trade-offs, business model, and switching context rather than feature count alone.
- Reassess positioning when market evidence materially changes.
## MUST NOT
- Copy competitor features without validating relevance to target users and strategy.
- Present speculative market size as established fact.
## SHOULD
- Include substitutes and non-consumption when assessing competition.
## Exceptions
Early directional analysis may use rough estimates when clearly labeled and excluded from irreversible investment decisions.
## Verification
Inspect market evidence, assumptions, segmentation, competitor analysis, and links from findings to product decisions.
