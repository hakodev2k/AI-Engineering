# Security and Abuse Rules
## Purpose
Ensure product choices account for misuse, fraud, authorization, and security impact.
## Scope
Sensitive workflows, permissions, abuse vectors, account actions, integrations, and trust controls.
## MUST
- Identify material abuse cases and security-sensitive user actions during product definition.
- Require appropriate authentication, authorization, auditability, rate or abuse controls where risk warrants them.
- Escalate requests to weaken security controls to accountable security and business owners.
## MUST NOT
- Disable or bypass a security control solely to improve conversion or reduce friction without approved risk acceptance.
- Treat absence of reported abuse as evidence that abuse risk is negligible.
## SHOULD
- Prefer designs that reduce privilege and blast radius by default.
## Exceptions
Emergency mitigations require defined authority, limited scope, monitoring, and follow-up review.
## Verification
Inspect threat or abuse reviews, requirements, approvals, audit behavior, monitoring, and incident evidence.
