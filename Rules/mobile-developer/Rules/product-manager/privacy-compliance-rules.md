# Privacy and Compliance Rules
## Purpose
Prevent product decisions from creating avoidable legal, privacy, or regulatory exposure.
## Scope
Personal data, consent, retention, regulated workflows, customer rights, and compliance-sensitive features.
## MUST
- Identify personal or regulated data affected by material product changes and involve accountable privacy, legal, or compliance reviewers when required.
- Define collection purpose, minimum necessary data, retention, access, deletion, and user-control expectations where applicable.
- Track mandatory obligations separately from discretionary product scope.
## MUST NOT
- Collect sensitive data merely because it may become useful later.
- Bypass required consent, disclosure, retention, or deletion controls to accelerate delivery.
## SHOULD
- Prefer privacy-preserving defaults and data minimization.
## Exceptions
Emergency legal or safety obligations require documented authority, scope, and follow-up review.
## Verification
Inspect data-flow reviews, requirements, approvals, consent behavior, retention rules, deletion paths, and compliance evidence.
