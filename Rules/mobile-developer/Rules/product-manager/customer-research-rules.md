# Customer Research Rules
## Purpose
Make customer insight representative, traceable, and ethically collected.
## Scope
Interviews, surveys, observation, support feedback, advisory sessions, and research synthesis.
## MUST
- Define research questions and participant criteria before collecting evidence.
- Record source context and distinguish direct evidence from interpretation.
- Protect personal and sensitive information according to applicable policy.
## MUST NOT
- Lead participants toward a predetermined answer.
- Generalize findings beyond the sample without stating limitations.
## SHOULD
- Triangulate qualitative findings with behavioral or market data where possible.
## Exceptions
Informal feedback may inform hypotheses but MUST NOT be labeled validated research.
## Verification
Inspect research plans, participant criteria, notes, synthesis, consent/privacy handling, and stated limitations.
