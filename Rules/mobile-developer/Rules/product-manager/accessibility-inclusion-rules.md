# Accessibility and Inclusion Rules
## Purpose
Ensure product decisions do not systematically exclude users who rely on accessible or inclusive experiences.
## Scope
User journeys, content, interaction models, localization, assistive technology, and inclusive design requirements.
## MUST
- Include accessibility requirements in relevant product scope and acceptance criteria.
- Treat known critical accessibility blockers as product quality risks, not optional polish.
- Consider affected user groups when changing essential workflows.
## MUST NOT
- Remove accessible alternatives solely to simplify implementation without approved risk acceptance.
- Assume one research sample represents all users.
## SHOULD
- Validate critical journeys with applicable accessibility standards, tooling, and representative users where practical.
## Exceptions
Temporary limitations require documented impact, workaround, remediation owner, target date, and approval.
## Verification
Inspect requirements, design reviews, accessibility test evidence, issue severity, remediation plans, and user feedback.
