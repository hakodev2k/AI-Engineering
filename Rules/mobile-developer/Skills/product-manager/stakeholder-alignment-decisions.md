# Stakeholder Alignment and Decision Making

## Purpose
Create durable alignment around product decisions without replacing evidence with consensus or allowing unclear ownership to stall progress.

## When to use
Use for contested priorities, strategic decisions, cross-functional dependencies, executive reviews, and material scope changes.

## Inputs
Decision statement, options, evidence, constraints, stakeholder interests, risks, and decision authority.

## Context to inspect
Inspect prior commitments, organizational incentives, affected teams, customer impact, financial implications, and irreversible consequences.

## Core knowledge
Alignment means stakeholders understand the decision, rationale, and implications; it does not require unanimous preference. Decision rights should be explicit.

## Procedure
1. Frame one concrete decision and deadline.
2. Identify decision owner, contributors, and affected stakeholders.
3. Gather evidence and constraints before advocating an option.
4. Present viable alternatives and trade-offs.
5. Surface disagreements and underlying assumptions.
6. Resolve factual uncertainty where practical.
7. Ask the authorized owner to decide when value judgments remain.
8. Document decision, rationale, assumptions, dissent, and revisit triggers.
9. Communicate implications to affected teams.

## Decision points
Seek consensus when reversibility and coordination benefit outweigh delay. Use clear owner judgment when time, accountability, or irreducible trade-offs require it.

## Common failure patterns
Endless meetings, stakeholder voting, hidden vetoes, socializing a predetermined answer, and reopening decisions without new evidence.

## Verification
Participants can state the decision and owner; objections and assumptions are recorded; execution implications are assigned.

## Expected output
A documented decision with evidence, trade-offs, ownership, communication, and revisit conditions.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when decision authority is disputed, legal or safety approval is required, or critical evidence is intentionally withheld.
