# Product Operations and Feedback Systems

## Purpose
Create repeatable systems that turn customer feedback, support signals, sales input, analytics, and product decisions into a usable learning loop.

## When to use
Use when feedback is fragmented, roadmap intake is noisy, teams repeat research, or decision history is difficult to recover.

## Inputs
Feedback channels, support taxonomy, CRM themes, research repository, analytics, decision records, roadmap process, and team cadence.

## Context to inspect
Inspect who submits feedback, sampling bias, duplicate channels, tagging quality, response expectations, and how insights currently reach decisions.

## Core knowledge
Feedback volume is not priority. Product operations should improve signal quality, traceability, and decision speed without creating bureaucracy.

## Procedure
1. Inventory feedback and decision channels.
2. Define a common taxonomy around problems, segments, and outcomes.
3. Establish ingestion and deduplication rules.
4. Preserve source, context, and customer importance.
5. Combine qualitative themes with behavioral evidence.
6. Create regular synthesis tied to product decisions.
7. Record decisions and rationale back into the system.
8. Close loops with customer-facing teams where appropriate.
9. Audit bias, stale tags, and unused reports.
10. Automate repetitive operations without automating judgment.

## Decision points
Centralize shared taxonomy and evidence; keep specialized workflows local when centralization would slow teams without improving decisions.

## Common failure patterns
Voting portals as prioritization, counting requests without segment context, duplicate repositories, dashboards nobody uses, and promising delivery for submitted feedback.

## Verification
Teams can trace important decisions to evidence, retrieve prior learning, and distinguish recurring problems from loud isolated requests.

## Expected output
A lightweight product-operations system for intake, synthesis, decision traceability, and feedback closure.

## Stop conditions
Stop when proposed collection violates privacy commitments or operational overhead exceeds the decision value it creates.
