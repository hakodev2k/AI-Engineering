# Sprint Planning Product Readiness

## Purpose
Prepare product context and ordered work so planning can focus on a realistic Sprint Goal and delivery strategy rather than requirement discovery from scratch.

## When to use
Use before and during sprint planning in Scrum or similar short-cycle delivery models.

## Inputs
Product goal, ordered backlog, recent outcomes, team capacity signals, dependencies, and near-term refined items.

## Context to inspect
Inspect unfinished work, production issues, dependency status, recent velocity only as context, and whether top items support a coherent outcome.

## Core knowledge
The Product Owner optimizes value and clarity; the Developers determine how much work is feasible. A Sprint Goal is more valuable than maximizing ticket count.

## Procedure
1. Review progress toward the Product Goal.
2. Reorder the top backlog using current evidence.
3. Confirm critical dependencies and decisions are ready.
4. Propose a coherent Sprint Goal.
5. Explain value and acceptance context for candidate items.
6. Let the team assess feasibility and technical approach.
7. Adjust scope while protecting the Sprint Goal.
8. Clarify unresolved product questions immediately when possible.
9. Record assumptions and external commitments.
10. Communicate the resulting goal to stakeholders.

## Decision points
Prefer fewer coherent items over unrelated capacity filling. Defer an unclear item when clarification risk threatens the sprint unless discovery itself is the goal.

## Common failure patterns
Product Owner assigning capacity, treating planning as ticket distribution, forcing prior commitments despite new evidence, and creating a sprint with no shared goal.

## Verification
The team understands the Sprint Goal, selected items support it, dependencies are visible, and feasibility was determined collaboratively.

## Expected output
A clear Sprint Goal and an executable, evidence-based selection of backlog work.

## Stop conditions
Stop when top work lacks essential decisions or external dependencies make the proposed Sprint Goal unrealistic.
