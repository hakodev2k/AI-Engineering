# Backlog Refinement

## Purpose
Create shared understanding of upcoming work early enough to expose uncertainty, dependencies, and risks without turning refinement into premature design.

## When to use
Use continuously for near-term backlog items and before planning when items are unclear or oversized.

## Inputs
Ordered backlog, product goals, user evidence, designs, technical findings, defects, and dependencies.

## Context to inspect
Inspect related functionality, prior decisions, unresolved questions, cross-team dependencies, acceptance standards, and recent production evidence.

## Core knowledge
Refinement is collaborative risk reduction. The Product Owner brings outcome and business context; engineers and QA contribute feasibility, edge cases, and implementation risk.

## Procedure
1. Select only items likely to matter soon.
2. Restate the problem and intended outcome.
3. Present evidence and constraints.
4. Invite questions before discussing solutions.
5. Surface business rules and edge cases.
6. Identify dependencies and external decisions.
7. Slice oversized work.
8. Define or improve acceptance criteria.
9. Capture unresolved questions with owners.
10. Reorder the backlog based on new information.

## Decision points
Refine further when uncertainty threatens planning; defer detail when the item is unlikely to be selected soon. Use spikes for uncertainty that cannot be resolved by discussion.

## Common failure patterns
Reading tickets aloud, refining the entire backlog, estimating before understanding, Product Owner dictating implementation, and treating refinement as a sign-off gate.

## Verification
Near-term items have shared purpose, manageable scope, visible dependencies, testable outcomes, and no critical unanswered question hidden from the team.

## Expected output
A smaller set of better-understood, appropriately sliced upcoming backlog items.

## Stop conditions
Stop refinement when a required stakeholder, policy decision, or technical investigation is missing and assign the next action explicitly.
