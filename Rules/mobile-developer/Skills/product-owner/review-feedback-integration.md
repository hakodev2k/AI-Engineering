# Review and Feedback Integration

## Purpose
Turn sprint reviews, demos, customer feedback, analytics, and stakeholder observations into evidence-based product decisions rather than an uncontrolled list of requests.

## When to use
Use after reviews, releases, pilots, usability sessions, or significant stakeholder feedback.

## Inputs
Demonstrated increment, product metrics, feedback, support signals, product goal, and current backlog.

## Context to inspect
Inspect who provided feedback, observed behavior versus opinion, affected segment, frequency, strategic fit, and existing evidence.

## Core knowledge
Feedback is input, not automatically a requirement. Senior Product Owners identify the underlying problem, weigh representativeness, and update assumptions and priorities deliberately.

## Procedure
1. Capture feedback with source and context.
2. Separate observations, requests, and interpretations.
3. Identify the underlying user or business problem.
4. Compare with analytics and other evidence.
5. Determine affected segments and prevalence.
6. Assess strategic relevance and urgency.
7. Decide whether to act, investigate, defer, or reject.
8. Update backlog or discovery hypotheses accordingly.
9. Communicate decisions to important contributors.
10. Track whether resulting changes improve the intended outcome.

## Decision points
Act quickly on high-impact verified issues; investigate conflicting or low-confidence feedback; reject requests that do not support goals despite stakeholder enthusiasm.

## Common failure patterns
Adding every suggestion to the backlog, treating demo approval as user validation, overweighting senior stakeholders, and losing the original feedback context.

## Verification
Accepted changes have evidence and rationale; rejected or deferred feedback remains traceable where necessary; priorities change only when justified.

## Expected output
A set of explicit product decisions derived from feedback and evidence.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when feedback reveals contractual, regulatory, safety, or strategic conflicts outside Product Owner authority.
