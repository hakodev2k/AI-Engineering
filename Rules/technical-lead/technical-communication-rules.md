# Technical Communication Rules
## Purpose
Make technical information decision-ready for engineers and stakeholders.
## Scope
Design communication, risk reporting, handoffs, technical proposals, and stakeholder updates.
## MUST
- Communication of material technical risk MUST state impact, evidence, uncertainty, options, and required decision when applicable.
- Handoffs for critical work MUST include current state, assumptions, unresolved risks, and ownership.
- Technical explanations MUST distinguish facts, estimates, and recommendations.
## MUST NOT
- Conceal uncertainty behind unjustified certainty or jargon.
- Present a preferred solution while omitting a material known downside relevant to the decision.
## SHOULD
- Adapt depth to the audience while preserving decision-critical facts.
## Exceptions
Incident updates may be terse but must remain accurate and identify uncertainty.
## Verification
Review design notes, incident updates, decision records, handoffs, and stakeholder feedback.
