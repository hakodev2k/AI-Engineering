# Technical Direction Rules
## Purpose
Keep engineering decisions aligned with product outcomes, system constraints, and long-term maintainability.
## Scope
Technical direction, architecture alignment, standards, and cross-team decisions.
## MUST
- Ensure major technical decisions identify business objective, constraints, owners, risks, and measurable success criteria.
- Require significant irreversible decisions to document alternatives and trade-offs.
- Resolve or escalate conflicting technical direction before teams make incompatible commitments.
## MUST NOT
- Mandate technology based only on personal preference or novelty.
- Bypass accountable technical owners on architecture decisions.
## SHOULD
- Prefer reversible decisions and standards that reduce unnecessary cognitive load.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented context, risk, evidence, owner, and review date.
## Verification
Review decision records, roadmap dependencies, architecture reviews, and follow-up outcomes.
