# Team Health Rules
## Purpose
Protect sustainable delivery, psychological safety, and healthy operating conditions.
## Scope
Workload, collaboration, burnout risk, conflict, and team climate.
## MUST
- Treat repeated overtime, overload, attrition signals, and unresolved conflict as management risks requiring action.
- Provide safe channels for raising concerns and respond without retaliation.
- Address systemic causes when recurring delivery pressure damages team health.
## MUST NOT
- Normalize chronic overtime as a planning strategy.
- Penalize respectful escalation of quality, safety, or workload risks.
## SHOULD
- Monitor qualitative and quantitative health signals without turning them into surveillance.
## Exceptions
Short emergency periods require explicit duration, recovery plan, and leadership awareness.
## Verification
Review workload trends, incident load, retention signals, escalation handling, and corrective actions.
