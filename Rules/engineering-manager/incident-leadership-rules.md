# Incident Leadership Rules
## Purpose
Ensure incidents are managed safely, clearly, and without blame-driven decision making.
## Scope
Major incidents, escalation, communication, staffing, and follow-up.
## MUST
- Establish clear incident ownership, severity, communication cadence, and decision authority.
- Prioritize containment and user impact reduction before nonessential investigation.
- Require post-incident actions to have owners and follow-up dates.
## MUST NOT
- Make unreviewed destructive production changes solely to accelerate recovery.
- use incident review to assign blame instead of identifying contributing conditions.
## SHOULD
- Rotate responders and protect recovery time during prolonged incidents.
## Exceptions
Immediate safety actions may precede normal process when delay creates greater harm; actions must be recorded afterward.
## Verification
Inspect incident timeline, approvals, communications, action items, and post-incident review.
