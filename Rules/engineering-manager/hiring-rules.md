# Hiring Rules
## Purpose
Make repeatable, fair hiring decisions based on role requirements and evidence.
## Scope
Role definition, interviews, evaluation, selection, and hiring risk.
## MUST
- Define required competencies and evaluation criteria before interviewing candidates.
- Collect independent evidence against consistent criteria before final decisions.
- Follow applicable privacy, equal-opportunity, and organizational hiring policies.
## MUST NOT
- Ask for protected or irrelevant personal information.
- Hire or reject primarily on intuition, pedigree, similarity, or one interviewer’s preference.
## SHOULD
- Use structured interviews and work-relevant assessment methods.
## Exceptions
Any process deviation requires documented reason and recruiting or HR approval where applicable.
## Verification
Inspect scorecards, interview plans, evidence, decision rationale, and policy compliance.
