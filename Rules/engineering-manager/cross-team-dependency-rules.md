# Cross-Team Dependency Rules
## Purpose
Prevent hidden dependencies from becoming delivery and ownership failures.
## Scope
Shared systems, interfaces, sequencing, ownership, and cross-team commitments.
## MUST
- Identify material dependencies with owner, required outcome, timing, and failure impact.
- Confirm commitments with the accountable team rather than assuming availability.
- Escalate unresolved dependencies before they become critical-path surprises.
## MUST NOT
- Commit another team’s capacity without agreement.
- Resolve ownership ambiguity by silently absorbing indefinite responsibility.
## SHOULD
- Reduce recurring coordination through stable contracts and clear ownership boundaries.
## Exceptions
Emergency temporary ownership requires explicit scope and handoff plan.
## Verification
Inspect dependency trackers, interface agreements, roadmap links, ownership records, and escalation history.
