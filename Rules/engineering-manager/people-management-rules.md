# People Management Rules
## Purpose
Provide fair, consistent, evidence-based management that enables sustainable performance.
## Scope
Expectations, feedback, support, accountability, and manager responsibilities.
## MUST
- Set role expectations and priorities clearly enough that performance can be evaluated fairly.
- Give material feedback promptly, privately, specifically, and with actionable examples.
- Separate observed behavior and outcomes from assumptions about intent.
## MUST NOT
- Surprise employees with material performance concerns that were reasonably knowable earlier.
- Disclose confidential personnel information beyond authorized need-to-know boundaries.
## SHOULD
- Adapt support and communication while applying standards consistently.
## Exceptions
Legal, safety, or HR-sensitive situations may require restricted communication and formal process.
## Verification
Review expectation records, feedback cadence, documented examples, and required HR process evidence.
