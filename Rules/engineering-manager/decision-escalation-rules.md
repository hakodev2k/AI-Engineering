# Decision and Escalation Rules
## Purpose
Make consequential decisions at the right level with clear evidence and accountability.
## Scope
Trade-offs, risk acceptance, deadlocks, escalation, and reversibility.
## MUST
- Identify decision owner, deadline, constraints, alternatives, and material risks for consequential decisions.
- Escalate when risk exceeds delegated authority or cross-team deadlock threatens outcomes.
- Record irreversible or high-impact decisions and their rationale.
## MUST NOT
- Delay escalation merely to avoid disagreement.
- Override domain experts without addressing their evidence and risks.
## SHOULD
- Delegate reversible decisions to the lowest accountable level with sufficient context.
## Exceptions
Emergency decisions may compress consultation but require later documentation and review.
## Verification
Inspect decision records, escalation timelines, approval boundaries, and resulting outcomes.
