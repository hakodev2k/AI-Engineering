# Performance Management Rules
## Purpose
Evaluate performance consistently using role-relevant evidence and fair process.
## Scope
Performance assessment, improvement, recognition, and escalation.
## MUST
- Evaluate sustained outcomes, behaviors, scope, and role expectations using concrete evidence.
- Document material gaps, expected improvement, support offered, and review checkpoints.
- Involve required HR or management partners before formal adverse actions.
## MUST NOT
- Base ratings on a single recent event, visibility, favoritism, or unsupported impressions.
- Change expectations retroactively to justify an outcome.
## SHOULD
- Calibrate evaluations across comparable roles where organizational process permits.
## Exceptions
Urgent misconduct or safety matters follow applicable formal procedures.
## Verification
Inspect evidence, expectation history, calibration records, improvement plans, and approvals.
