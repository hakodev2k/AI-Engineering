# Support and Troubleshooting Rules
## Purpose
Resolve developer-tooling failures through evidence while converting recurring incidents into systemic improvements.
## Scope
Support requests, diagnostics, escalation, known issues, incident handoff, and root-cause investigation.
## MUST
- Investigations MUST capture symptom, environment, reproduction status, relevant changes, and available evidence.
- Broad corrective changes MUST be based on identified or bounded root cause.
- Recurrent high-impact issues MUST have ownership beyond one-off support responses.
- Sensitive diagnostics MUST be handled according to data classification and access rules.
## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT ask users to share secrets or disable security controls as routine troubleshooting.
- MUST NOT present speculation as established root cause.
- MUST NOT close systemic issues solely because a local workaround exists.
## SHOULD
- Troubleshooting SHOULD progress from least invasive evidence collection to targeted experiments.
- Known fixes SHOULD become automation, diagnostics, or documentation when reusable.
## Exceptions
Emergency mitigations require risk, scope, owner, rollback, and follow-up root-cause work.
## Verification
Review tickets, reproduction artifacts, logs/traces, hypotheses, corrective-action tracking, recurrence rates, and documentation/tooling updates.
