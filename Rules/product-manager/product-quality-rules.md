# Product Quality Rules
## Purpose
Protect customer value by treating quality, reliability, and usability as product outcomes.
## Scope
Defects, regressions, usability failures, service quality, and release quality thresholds.
## MUST
- Define quality expectations for critical user journeys and monitor material regressions after release.
- Prioritize severe defects and recurring failure patterns according to customer impact and operational risk.
- Require evidence before declaring a material quality problem resolved.
## MUST NOT
- Reclassify serious defects as acceptable solely to meet a date.
- Measure product quality only by number of closed tickets.
## SHOULD
- Track recurring defect themes and invest in systemic prevention when justified.
## Exceptions
Known limitations require documented impact, workaround, acceptance owner, and remediation or review plan.
## Verification
Inspect defect severity, user-impact evidence, test or monitoring results, post-release metrics, and risk acceptance records.
