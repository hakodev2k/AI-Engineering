# Stakeholder Alignment Rules
## Purpose
Keep product decisions transparent, accountable, and aligned across functions.
## Scope
Engineering, design, sales, support, legal, operations, executives, and external stakeholders.
## MUST
- Identify accountable decision-makers, consulted parties, and affected teams for material product decisions.
- Surface disagreements, assumptions, dependencies, and unresolved risks before irreversible commitments.
- Communicate decision rationale and changes to affected stakeholders.
## MUST NOT
- Manufacture consensus by omitting known objections.
- Treat stakeholder alignment as unanimous approval when decision rights are already defined.
## SHOULD
- Resolve disputes using shared outcomes, evidence, and explicit trade-offs.
## Exceptions
Urgent incident decisions may use abbreviated consultation when authority and follow-up documentation are clear.
## Verification
Inspect decision records, stakeholder mapping, meeting outcomes, unresolved risks, and change communication.
