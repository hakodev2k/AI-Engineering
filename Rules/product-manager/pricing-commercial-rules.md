# Pricing and Commercial Rules
## Purpose
Ensure pricing and packaging decisions are evidence-based, transparent, and operationally supportable.
## Scope
Pricing, packaging, entitlements, trials, discounts, monetization changes, and commercial commitments.
## MUST
- Define target segment, value hypothesis, commercial objective, operational impact, and success metrics before material pricing changes.
- Review billing, contract, support, migration, and customer-communication implications.
- Obtain required approval for changes that alter contractual or financial obligations.
## MUST NOT
- Introduce hidden fees or misleading packaging.
- Change paid entitlements without assessing existing customer commitments.
## SHOULD
- Test willingness-to-pay and packaging hypotheses with multiple evidence sources.
## Exceptions
Temporary experiments require clear eligibility, duration, rollback, and legal/commercial review where applicable.
## Verification
Inspect pricing rationale, approval records, contract impact, rollout plan, customer communication, and post-change metrics.
