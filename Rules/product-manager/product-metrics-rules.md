# Product Metrics Rules
## Purpose
Ensure product decisions use meaningful, interpretable, and non-gameable measures.
## Scope
North-star metrics, KPIs, guardrails, funnels, retention, quality, and business outcomes.
## MUST
- Define metric owner, formula, source, population, time window, and intended decision use.
- Pair growth metrics with relevant quality, risk, or user-value guardrails.
- Investigate material metric shifts before declaring causality.
## MUST NOT
- Change metric definitions silently.
- Optimize a proxy when evidence shows it harms the underlying user or business outcome.
## SHOULD
- Track leading and lagging indicators when both improve decision quality.
## Exceptions
Exploratory metrics may be provisional when clearly labeled and excluded from formal commitments.
## Verification
Review metric definitions, dashboards, source lineage, guardrails, anomaly investigations, and decision records.
