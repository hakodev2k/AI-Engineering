# Product Incident Response Rules
## Purpose
Ensure customer-impacting product incidents receive clear product ownership and evidence-based decisions.
## Scope
Outages, harmful behavior, major regressions, data or trust incidents, and emergency product decisions.
## MUST
- Join incident response when customer behavior, product policy, communication, rollback, or feature-disable decisions require product judgment.
- Use logs, metrics, support evidence, and incident facts rather than assumptions when assessing impact.
- Record temporary product decisions, affected users, risk, owner, and follow-up actions.
## MUST NOT
- Block a technically necessary rollback solely to preserve a launch or metric target.
- Publicly speculate about incident cause before evidence is established.
## SHOULD
- Convert significant incident lessons into roadmap, requirement, or control improvements.
## Exceptions
The designated incident commander may make time-critical decisions within defined authority.
## Verification
Inspect incident timeline, decision records, customer-impact analysis, communications, corrective actions, and follow-up ownership.
