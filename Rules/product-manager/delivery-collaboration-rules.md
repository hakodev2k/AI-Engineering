# Delivery Collaboration Rules
## Purpose
Keep product intent clear during delivery without overriding engineering ownership.
## Scope
Execution planning, scope negotiation, delivery decisions, and cross-functional collaboration.
## MUST
- Preserve agreed outcomes and critical acceptance criteria when negotiating scope.
- Escalate material risks to customer value, safety, compliance, reliability, or commitments.
- Distinguish product decisions from engineering implementation decisions.
## MUST NOT
- Micromanage technical design without accountable engineering ownership.
- Hide scope cuts that materially alter expected value or contractual behavior.
## SHOULD
- Favor reversible scope reduction over low-quality delivery when timelines are constrained.
## Exceptions
Incident response may temporarily centralize decisions under the designated incident authority.
## Verification
Review scope-change records, acceptance criteria, delivery notes, risk escalation, and ownership boundaries.
