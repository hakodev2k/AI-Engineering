# SaaS and License Cost Rules

## Purpose
Control software and license spend using entitlement, utilization, contractual, and business-value evidence.

## Scope
SaaS subscriptions, seats, enterprise licenses, marketplace software, support plans, and license-infrastructure interactions.

## MUST
- Maintain ownership, contract term, renewal date, unit pricing, entitlements, and measurable utilization for material agreements.
- Identify inactive, duplicate, over-provisioned, and orphaned licenses before renewal.
- Validate license changes against legal, security, operational, and vendor terms.
- Require authorized approval before material renewals, expansions, or early termination commitments.

## MUST NOT
- Revoke access solely from inactivity metrics when regulatory, emergency, or operational access requirements exist.
- Count negotiated discounts as savings if purchased quantity or total cost increased without normalization.
- Ignore termination, minimum-spend, audit, or true-up clauses.

## SHOULD
- Align renewal analysis with product roadmaps, headcount forecasts, and consolidation opportunities.

## Exceptions
Strategic standby licenses may remain unused when the requirement and owner are documented.

## Verification
Review contracts, entitlement inventory, identity/activity data, renewal decisions, approval records, and realized spend.
