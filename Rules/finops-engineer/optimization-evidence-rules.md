# Optimization Evidence Rules

## Purpose
Ensure savings claims and optimization decisions are supported by reproducible evidence.

## Scope
Rightsizing, scheduling, deletion, storage tiering, architecture changes, discounts, licensing, and cost avoidance.

## MUST
- Establish a before baseline, measurement window, scope, normalization method, and expected impact before claiming savings.
- Separate realized savings, projected savings, cost avoidance, negotiated discounts, and demand reduction.
- Account for workload volume, seasonality, pricing changes, credits, and migrations when material.
- Verify that required reliability, security, performance, and product outcomes remain acceptable.

## MUST NOT
- Double-count the same savings across initiatives.
- Claim savings from recommendations that were not implemented.
- Treat lower spend caused by reduced business activity as engineering efficiency without qualification.

## SHOULD
- Prefer invoice- or usage-backed realized savings after a stable observation period.

## Exceptions
Early forecasts may report projected savings when clearly labeled and later reconciled.

## Verification
Review baselines, implementation evidence, normalized before/after data, service metrics, and initiative-level savings reconciliation.
