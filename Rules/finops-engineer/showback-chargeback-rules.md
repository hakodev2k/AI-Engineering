# Showback and Chargeback Rules

## Purpose
Provide fair, explainable cost accountability to consuming teams.

## Scope
Internal showback, chargeback, shared-service pricing, cost-center reporting, and internal invoices.

## MUST
- Publish allocation methodology, included cost categories, shared-cost treatment, timing, and dispute process.
- Reconcile distributed amounts to authoritative totals.
- Give owners enough detail to reproduce or challenge material charges.
- Version material methodology changes and communicate impact before they take effect.

## MUST NOT
- Use opaque markups or allocation formulas without governance.
- Change historical chargeback merely to improve current performance metrics.
- Penalize teams for costs they cannot influence without making that limitation visible.

## SHOULD
- Separate controllable and non-controllable costs where it improves decision quality.
- Favor stable, understandable methods over mathematically complex models with little decision value.

## Exceptions
Simplified showback may precede formal chargeback while data maturity develops, if limitations are explicit.

## Verification
Recalculate samples, reconcile totals, inspect methodology versions, dispute records, owner acknowledgements, and unexplained variances.
