# Stakeholder Accountability Rules

## Purpose
Create durable ownership for cost decisions across engineering, finance, product, procurement, and leadership.

## Scope
Cost reviews, optimization initiatives, budgets, commitments, forecasts, disputes, and escalations.

## MUST
- Assign accountable owners to material cost domains and optimization actions.
- Present financial information with definitions, assumptions, uncertainty, and decision required.
- Record material decisions, rejected recommendations, accepted risks, and due dates.
- Escalate unresolved material cost risk to the appropriate authority rather than silently accepting it.

## MUST NOT
- Use cost dashboards as a substitute for named ownership and action.
- Attribute overspend to teams without validating allocation and controllability.
- Conceal uncertainty to make recommendations appear more decisive.

## SHOULD
- Tailor communication to engineering, finance, and executive audiences while preserving consistent underlying facts.

## Exceptions
Temporary ownership may be assigned during reorganizations if transfer and review dates are explicit.

## Verification
Review ownership mappings, decision logs, action aging, escalation records, meeting outcomes, and reconciliation of communicated metrics to source data.
