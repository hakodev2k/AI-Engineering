# Cost Reporting Rules

## Purpose
Ensure cost reports are consistent, decision-useful, and resistant to misleading interpretation.

## Scope
Dashboards, executive reports, engineering reports, monthly close, variance reporting, and optimization summaries.

## MUST
- Define cost basis, currency, period, scope, exclusions, allocation method, and freshness for material reports.
- Reconcile published totals to authoritative billing data within documented tolerance.
- Explain material variances using evidence and distinguish price, usage, volume, allocation, and one-time effects.
- Preserve historical metric definitions or clearly mark restatements.

## MUST NOT
- Mix incompatible cost bases in the same trend without normalization.
- Cherry-pick periods or scopes to exaggerate savings or hide growth.
- Present stale or provisional data as final without labeling it.

## SHOULD
- Show both absolute spend and relevant normalized/unit metrics when they answer different decisions.

## Exceptions
Exploratory analysis may use provisional data if its limitations are visible and it is not represented as official reporting.

## Verification
Trace report values to source datasets, review definitions, reconciliation results, freshness indicators, variance evidence, and restatement history.
