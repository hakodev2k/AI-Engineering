# Commitment and Discount Rules

## Purpose
Manage reservations, savings plans, committed-use discounts, and negotiated commitments without creating avoidable lock-in.

## Scope
Provider commitments, reserved capacity, enterprise discounts, private pricing, and similar financial instruments.

## MUST
- Model eligible baseline demand, utilization risk, coverage, term, payment options, break-even point, and portability before purchase.
- Separate demand uncertainty from price-discount opportunity.
- Obtain authorized financial approval before creating material or long-term commitments.
- Monitor utilization and coverage throughout the commitment lifecycle.
- Record expiration, renewal, ownership, and exit implications.

## MUST NOT
- Buy commitments from short-lived spikes or unvalidated forecasts.
- Report headline discount percentage as realized savings without utilization evidence.
- Automatically renew material commitments without current demand analysis and approval.

## SHOULD
- Preserve flexibility when migrations, architecture changes, acquisitions, or demand shifts are plausible.

## Exceptions
Short-term tactical commitments may use simplified analysis when financial exposure is bounded and documented.

## Verification
Inspect demand models, approval records, commitment inventory, realized utilization, effective rates, expiration calendar, and post-purchase savings.
