# Privacy Governance Rules
## Purpose
Embed privacy obligations into governed data lifecycles.
## Scope
Personal, sensitive, inferred, and linkable data used across systems and analytics.
## MUST
- Personal data processing MUST have documented purpose, lawful/authorized basis where applicable, and lifecycle controls.
- Collection and sharing MUST be limited to data necessary for the approved purpose.
- Privacy-relevant lineage and third-party disclosures MUST be traceable.
## MUST NOT
- Data collected for one purpose MUST NOT be silently repurposed when new use requires review or authorization.
- De-identification MUST NOT be claimed without evaluating realistic re-identification risk.
## SHOULD
- Privacy-preserving transformations SHOULD be applied before broader analytical use when full identifiers are unnecessary.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require privacy/legal review when applicable, risk evidence, scope, expiry, and approval.
## Verification
Review processing records, purpose mappings, minimization evidence, disclosure records, and privacy control tests.
