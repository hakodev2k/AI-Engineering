# Data Incident and Remediation Rules
## Purpose
Contain, investigate, and correct governance-related data failures.
## Scope
Quality, privacy, access, lineage, classification, integrity, and policy incidents.
## MUST
- Material incidents MUST record scope, affected assets, timeline, impact, containment, owner, and evidence.
- Root cause MUST be identified or bounded by evidence before broad corrective changes are declared complete.
- Remediation MUST address systemic causes when recurrence risk exists.
## MUST NOT
- Evidence MUST NOT be destroyed or altered during investigation.
- Incidents MUST NOT be closed solely because symptoms disappeared.
## SHOULD
- Significant incidents SHOULD produce lessons, control improvements, and recurrence monitoring.
## Exceptions
When root cause cannot be proven, the uncertainty, tested hypotheses, residual risk, and monitoring plan must be documented.
## Verification
Review incident records, evidence chain, root-cause analysis, corrective actions, validation tests, and recurrence metrics.
