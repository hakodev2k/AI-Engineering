# Data Ownership Rules
## Purpose
Ensure every governed data asset has accountable business and technical ownership.
## Scope
Datasets, data products, reports, models, reference data, and shared schemas.
## MUST
- Every material data asset MUST have a named accountable owner and operational steward.
- Ownership MUST define decision rights for access, quality, retention, classification, and schema changes.
- Ownership transfers MUST be recorded before the prior owner leaves responsibility.
## MUST NOT
- Critical data MUST NOT remain ownerless or rely only on tribal knowledge.
- Platform teams MUST NOT be treated as business owners solely because they host data.
## SHOULD
- Ownership SHOULD align with the domain that creates or is accountable for the business meaning of the data.
## Exceptions
Temporary ownership requires an expiry date, risk statement, and accountable approver.
## Verification
Inspect the data catalog, ownership records, escalation routes, and a sample of recent ownership changes.
