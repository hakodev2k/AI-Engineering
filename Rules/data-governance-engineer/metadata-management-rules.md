# Metadata Management Rules
## Purpose
Keep governed data understandable, discoverable, and operationally traceable.
## Scope
Business, technical, operational, and governance metadata.
## MUST
- Material assets MUST record business meaning, owner, source, classification, lifecycle status, and key dependencies.
- Metadata changes MUST preserve provenance and effective dates where meaning can change over time.
- Required metadata fields MUST be validated before an asset is certified or broadly shared.
## MUST NOT
- Ambiguous names MUST NOT substitute for definitions of critical business concepts.
- Stale metadata MUST NOT remain certified after known structural or semantic changes.
## SHOULD
- Metadata SHOULD be harvested automatically from authoritative systems where practical.
## Exceptions
Missing metadata requires a documented remediation owner and deadline.
## Verification
Review catalog completeness metrics, freshness, certification records, and samples against source systems.
