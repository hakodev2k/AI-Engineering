# Third-Party Data Rules
## Purpose
Govern data acquired from or provided to external parties across its full lifecycle.
## Scope
Vendors, partners, public sources, purchased datasets, processors, and downstream recipients.
## MUST
- Third-party data MUST have documented provenance, permitted uses, restrictions, owner, and termination obligations.
- Due diligence MUST assess security, privacy, quality, licensing, residency, and continuity risks as applicable.
- Contract termination MUST trigger access revocation and required return or deletion controls.
## MUST NOT
- Data MUST NOT be used beyond licensed, consented, or contractually permitted purposes.
- Public availability MUST NOT be assumed to grant unrestricted reuse rights.
## SHOULD
- Critical providers SHOULD have contingency plans and periodic reassessment.
## Exceptions
Temporary use requires bounded purpose, qualified review, approval, and expiry.
## Verification
Inspect contracts, provenance, due-diligence evidence, usage mappings, access, deletion, and provider review records.
