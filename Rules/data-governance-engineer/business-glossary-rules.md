# Business Glossary Rules
## Purpose
Establish controlled, shared meanings for important business data concepts.
## Scope
Business terms, metrics, dimensions, critical data elements, and policy concepts.
## MUST
- Governed terms MUST have clear definitions, accountable owners, status, and effective dates.
- Conflicting definitions MUST be resolved or explicitly scoped by context.
- Material metric definitions MUST specify calculation semantics and inclusion/exclusion rules.
## MUST NOT
- Different meanings MUST NOT share one certified term without explicit contextual qualification.
- Proposed definitions MUST NOT be represented as approved.
## SHOULD
- Terms SHOULD link to physical data assets, policies, lineage, and quality controls.
## Exceptions
Local terminology requires documented mapping to enterprise or domain concepts where interoperability matters.
## Verification
Review glossary workflow, duplicate/conflict reports, term-to-asset links, and sampled metric implementations.
