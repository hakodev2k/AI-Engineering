# Governance Risk Rules
## Purpose
Prioritize governance effort according to measurable data risk and business impact.
## Scope
Data domains, assets, controls, exceptions, projects, and remediation portfolios.
## MUST
- Material governance risks MUST document likelihood, impact, affected assets, owner, treatment, and target date.
- Risk ratings MUST use defined criteria and evidence rather than intuition alone.
- Accepted high risks MUST have explicit accountable approval and review dates.
## MUST NOT
- Risks MUST NOT be closed without evidence that treatment or acceptance criteria were met.
- Repeated control failures MUST NOT be treated as isolated events without systemic assessment.
## SHOULD
- Risk prioritization SHOULD consider data criticality, exposure, change velocity, dependency breadth, and control strength.
## Exceptions
Alternative scoring methods require documented rationale and comparable decision evidence.
## Verification
Inspect risk registers, scoring evidence, approvals, overdue treatments, recurrence patterns, and closure artifacts.
