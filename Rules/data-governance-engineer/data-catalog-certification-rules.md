# Data Catalog Certification Rules
## Purpose
Ensure certified assets meet explicit trust criteria.
## Scope
Cataloged datasets, data products, reports, metrics, and reference assets.
## MUST
- Certification MUST require defined minimum metadata, ownership, quality, lineage, classification, and lifecycle evidence appropriate to risk.
- Certification status MUST identify approver and review/expiry conditions.
- Material control failures MUST trigger reassessment or suspension of certification.
## MUST NOT
- Popularity or usage volume MUST NOT substitute for certification evidence.
- Assets with known critical defects MUST NOT remain certified without visible accepted-risk status.
## SHOULD
- Certification SHOULD be tiered by criticality and automated where evidence is deterministic.
## Exceptions
Conditional certification requires documented gaps, compensating controls, owner, expiry, and approval.
## Verification
Sample certified assets against criteria, inspect approval history, expiry handling, and defect-triggered status changes.
