# Policy and Control Rules
## Purpose
Translate governance intent into enforceable, owned controls.
## Scope
Data policies, standards, control objectives, procedures, and technical enforcement.
## MUST
- Policies MUST identify scope, accountable owner, mandatory requirements, exceptions, and review cadence.
- Mandatory requirements MUST map to preventive, detective, or corrective controls with evidence sources.
- Control failures MUST have severity, owner, remediation target, and escalation path.
## MUST NOT
- Aspirational guidance MUST NOT be represented as an enforced control.
- Controls MUST NOT rely solely on undocumented manual knowledge for critical risks.
## SHOULD
- High-volume controls SHOULD be automated when automation improves consistency and evidence quality.
## Exceptions
Policy exceptions require rationale, risk, compensating controls, approval, expiry, and periodic review.
## Verification
Trace sampled policies to controls, execution evidence, failure handling, exception records, and review history.
