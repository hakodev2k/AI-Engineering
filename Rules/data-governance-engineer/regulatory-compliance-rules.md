# Regulatory Compliance Rules
## Purpose
Make data-related obligations traceable to governed assets and controls.
## Scope
Applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, standards, and audit commitments.
## MUST
- Applicable obligations MUST be mapped to affected data domains, processes, owners, and controls.
- Compliance evidence MUST be reproducible and retained for the required period.
- Material regulatory changes MUST trigger impact assessment and control updates.
## MUST NOT
- Compliance MUST NOT be inferred from policy existence without control evidence.
- Unresolved high-risk compliance gaps MUST NOT be hidden from accountable decision-makers.
## SHOULD
- Obligation mappings SHOULD distinguish legal requirements from internal policy choices.
## Exceptions
Deviations require qualified review, documented risk, remediation plan, and authorized acceptance where legally permissible.
## Verification
Inspect obligation registers, mappings, control evidence, audit findings, remediation status, and change assessments.
