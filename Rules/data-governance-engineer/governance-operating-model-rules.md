# Governance Operating Model Rules
## Purpose
Define decision rights, forums, stewardship, escalation, and accountability for data governance.
## Scope
Enterprise and domain governance bodies, owners, stewards, custodians, and control functions.
## MUST
- Governance responsibilities MUST distinguish accountability, stewardship, technical custody, risk review, and approval authority.
- Decision forums MUST have defined scope, quorum or authority, escalation path, and recorded outcomes.
- Cross-domain conflicts MUST have a named mechanism for resolution.
## MUST NOT
- Committees MUST NOT replace named individual accountability for material decisions.
- Governance MUST NOT impose controls without identifying who operates and verifies them.
## SHOULD
- Decision rights SHOULD be delegated to the lowest competent level consistent with risk.
## Exceptions
Temporary delegation requires scope, duration, delegated authority, and recorded acceptance.
## Verification
Review charters, RACI/decision-right records, meeting decisions, escalations, and sampled control ownership.
