# Statistical Reasoning Rules

## Purpose
Keep statistical conclusions proportionate to the evidence.

## Scope
Sampling, estimation, hypothesis tests, correlations, confidence intervals, and uncertainty analysis.

## MUST
- Match statistical methods to data type, design, and assumptions.
- Report uncertainty when point estimates alone can mislead.
- Check practical significance as well as statistical significance.
- Account for multiple comparisons when repeated testing materially inflates false-positive risk.
- Distinguish population facts from sample estimates.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT equate correlation with causation.
- MUST NOT claim absence of effect from a non-significant result without considering power and uncertainty.

## SHOULD
- Prefer effect sizes and confidence intervals over binary significance framing.

## Exceptions
Purely descriptive reporting may omit inferential statistics when no inference is being made.

## Verification
Review method assumptions, sample sizes, uncertainty intervals, effect sizes, and the wording of conclusions.
