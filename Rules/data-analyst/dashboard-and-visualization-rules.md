# Dashboard and Visualization Rules

## Purpose
Make analytical displays accurate, interpretable, and decision-oriented.

## Scope
Dashboards, charts, scorecards, and recurring visual reporting.

## MUST
- Choose visual encodings appropriate to the metric and comparison.
- Label units, denominators, time ranges, filters, and material caveats.
- Preserve truthful scales and baselines unless deviation is explicit and justified.
- Make default filters and refresh timestamps visible.
- Design dashboards around decisions and monitoring needs, not chart count.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use truncated or inconsistent axes to exaggerate changes without clear disclosure.
- MUST NOT encode the same concept inconsistently across a governed dashboard.

## SHOULD
- Prefer direct labels, accessible contrast, and low cognitive load.

## Exceptions
Specialized analytical visuals may require expert interpretation when the intended audience is explicitly technical.

## Verification
Review chart semantics, labels, filters, accessibility, refresh behavior, and sample values against source data.
