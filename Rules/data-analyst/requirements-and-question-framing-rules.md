# Requirements and Question Framing Rules

## Purpose
Ensure analysis answers the actual decision or business question.

## Scope
All analytical requests, ad hoc studies, dashboards, and recurring reporting.

## MUST
- Define the decision, audience, metric, grain, time window, and success criteria before analysis begins.
- Resolve ambiguous terms with documented working definitions.
- State material assumptions and constraints.
- Separate descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and causal questions.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT infer business intent from a metric name alone.
- MUST NOT present an answer to a materially different question as if it satisfies the request.

## SHOULD
- Translate stakeholder questions into explicit analytical hypotheses or decision criteria.

## Exceptions
Urgent exploratory work may proceed with provisional assumptions if clearly labeled and later validated.

## Verification
Review the analysis brief, metric definitions, assumptions, and final conclusions for traceability to the original question.
