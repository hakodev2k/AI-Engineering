# Stakeholder Communication Rules

## Purpose
Communicate analytical conclusions with the precision required for decisions.

## Scope
Readouts, written analyses, dashboards, recommendations, and executive summaries.

## MUST
- Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, and recommendations.
- State material uncertainty, limitations, and conflicting evidence.
- Quantify impact and decision relevance where practical.
- Tailor detail to the audience without changing the underlying conclusion.
- Correct materially misleading published analysis promptly.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT overstate confidence beyond the evidence.
- MUST NOT hide adverse findings because they conflict with stakeholder preference.

## SHOULD
- Lead with the decision-relevant conclusion and supporting evidence.

## Exceptions
Early exploratory discussions may be less formal if provisional status is explicit.

## Verification
Compare source evidence to claims, check caveat visibility, and have a reviewer inspect high-impact communications.
