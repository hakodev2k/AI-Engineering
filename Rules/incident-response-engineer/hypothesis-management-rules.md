# Hypothesis Management Rules

## Purpose
Drive investigation through falsifiable hypotheses rather than intuition-driven changes.

## Scope
Diagnosis of unknown or partially understood production failures.

## MUST
- State material hypotheses in testable terms with expected evidence if true and evidence that would weaken them.
- Rank investigation by impact, likelihood, test cost, reversibility, and information value.
- Update or discard hypotheses when evidence contradicts them.
- Keep causal certainty proportional to evidence.

## MUST NOT
- Repeat failed experiments without a new rationale or changed conditions.
- Present correlation, temporal proximity, or a recent deployment alone as proof of causation.
- Allow authority or confidence to substitute for validation.

## SHOULD
- Run the safest discriminating test that can eliminate the most uncertainty first.

## Exceptions
When immediate mitigation is required, a plausible hypothesis may justify a reversible action before confirmation if expected benefit and risk are explicit.

## Verification
Incident notes should show hypotheses, tests, results, confidence changes, and the evidence supporting the final causal model.
