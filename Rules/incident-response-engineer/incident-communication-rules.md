# Incident Communication Rules

## Purpose
Provide accurate, timely, audience-appropriate incident information without speculation.

## Scope
Internal updates, leadership briefings, support coordination, customer status updates, and handoffs.

## MUST
- Communicate confirmed impact, current mitigation state, known uncertainty, and the next expected update time.
- Separate facts, hypotheses, and decisions clearly.
- Keep externally communicated claims consistent with verified operational evidence and approved communication policy.
- Correct materially wrong prior statements promptly.
- Protect sensitive security, personal, contractual, and investigative information.

## MUST NOT
- Publish speculative root causes as facts.
- Promise recovery times without sufficient evidence and authorized ownership.
- Hide material customer impact to make status appear healthier.

## SHOULD
- Use stable update templates and a single authoritative incident channel or record.

## Exceptions
Legal, security, privacy, or contractual restrictions may limit detail; the restriction and appropriate communication owner SHOULD be recorded.

## Verification
Review timestamps, status messages, incident notes, and approvals for accuracy, consistency, confidentiality, and cadence.
