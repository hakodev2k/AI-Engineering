# Responder Handoff Rules

## Purpose
Preserve situational awareness and decision quality across shifts, teams, and escalations.

## Scope
Incident commander transfer, specialist handoff, regional handoff, and extended incidents.

## MUST
- Handoff current impact, severity, hypotheses, evidence, mitigations, active risks, pending actions, owners, approvals, and next decision points.
- Confirm the receiving responder acknowledges ownership before the previous owner disengages.
- Identify assumptions and unresolved uncertainty explicitly.
- Preserve links or references to authoritative incident records and dashboards.

## MUST NOT
- Transfer responsibility through an unacknowledged message for a critical active incident.
- Rely on verbal context alone when material decisions or risks exist.

## SHOULD
- Use a standardized handoff format and overlap responders for complex or high-severity incidents.

## Exceptions
Immediate personal safety or availability emergencies may force abrupt transfer; another response leader MUST reconstruct and confirm ownership as soon as possible.

## Verification
Review handoff records for acknowledgement, complete state transfer, ownership continuity, and unresolved-risk visibility.
