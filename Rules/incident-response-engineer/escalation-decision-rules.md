# Escalation and Decision Rules

## Purpose
Ensure high-risk decisions reach the right authority without unnecessary delay.

## Scope
Technical escalation, executive escalation, vendor escalation, risk acceptance, and dangerous actions.

## MUST
- Escalate when required expertise, authority, capacity, or risk ownership is missing.
- State the decision needed, deadline, available evidence, alternatives, risks, and recommendation when escalating.
- Identify who owns acceptance of residual customer, security, data, compliance, or business risk.
- Record approvals for actions that require human authorization.

## MUST NOT
- Interpret silence as approval for irreversible or high-risk action.
- Continue repeated low-value investigation when an escalation threshold has been met.
- Conceal uncertainty to obtain faster approval.

## SHOULD
- Predefine escalation thresholds and decision owners for common critical scenarios.

## Exceptions
Explicit emergency authority may permit action before normal approval where policy defines it; the authority basis and subsequent review MUST be recorded.

## Verification
Review escalation timestamps, decision records, approvers, evidence packages, and whether thresholds were followed.
