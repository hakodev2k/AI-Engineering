# Post-Incident Review Rules

## Purpose
Convert incidents into durable learning and risk reduction through evidence-based review.

## Scope
Postmortems, review meetings, stakeholder follow-up, and learning distribution.

## MUST
- Document impact, detection, timeline, response, recovery, causal analysis, what worked, what failed, and unresolved uncertainty.
- Conduct reviews without blame while retaining accountability for system, process, and decision improvements.
- Invite owners of affected systems and materially relevant perspectives.
- Track corrective actions separately from narrative conclusions.
- Review repeated incidents for systemic patterns.

## MUST NOT
- Use a postmortem to assign personal blame or suppress uncomfortable evidence.
- Mark the review complete while critical unknowns are presented as facts.

## SHOULD
- Share reusable lessons beyond the directly affected team when confidentiality permits.

## Exceptions
Security, privacy, legal, or personnel constraints may restrict distribution; an appropriately sanitized learning artifact SHOULD still be produced when possible.

## Verification
Check the review artifact against incident evidence, participant feedback, causal claims, and linked follow-up actions.
