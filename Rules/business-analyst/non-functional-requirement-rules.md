# Non-Functional Requirement Rules

## Purpose
Prevent functional scope from hiding quality and operational constraints.
## Scope
Performance, availability, security, privacy, accessibility, compliance, scalability, and supportability.
## MUST
- Identify relevant non-functional needs early and express material ones with measurable thresholds or acceptance evidence.
- Trace each critical NFR to a business, risk, regulatory, or operational driver.
- Validate feasibility and trade-offs with accountable technical owners.
## MUST NOT
- Use vague NFRs such as fast or secure without acceptance meaning.
- Defer known critical NFRs until after implementation without explicit risk acceptance.
## SHOULD
- Rank NFRs by business impact and criticality.
## Exceptions
Exploratory prototypes may relax NFRs when they are isolated from production use.
## Verification
Review NFR definitions, rationale, technical review, test evidence, and risk acceptance.
