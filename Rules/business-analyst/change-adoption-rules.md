# Change Adoption Rules

## Purpose
Ensure approved changes can be adopted safely by affected users and operations.
## Scope
Training, communications, procedure changes, support readiness, migration, and adoption monitoring.
## MUST
- Identify affected user groups, workflow changes, training needs, communication needs, support impacts, and adoption risks.
- Define adoption and readiness evidence for material business changes.
- Ensure operational owners receive updated procedures before go-live when required.
## MUST NOT
- Treat deployment as proof of successful adoption.
- Leave material process or policy changes undocumented for affected users.
## SHOULD
- Monitor usage, support demand, error patterns, and feedback after launch.
## Exceptions
Emergency changes may defer training or documentation only with temporary guidance and a named follow-up owner.
## Verification
Inspect readiness plans, communications, training evidence, updated procedures, support preparation, and adoption metrics.
