# Requirement Quality Rules

## Purpose
Make requirements precise enough to design, implement, test, and accept without hidden interpretation.

## Scope
Functional requirements, business rules, constraints, acceptance criteria, and non-functional needs.

## MUST
- Write requirements with clear actor/context, behavior or outcome, conditions, and measurable acceptance evidence where applicable.
- Define ambiguous business terms in a shared glossary or authoritative domain source.
- Mark assumptions, dependencies, exclusions, and open questions distinctly from approved requirements.
- Detect contradictions, duplicates, and unverifiable statements before approval.

## MUST NOT
- Use vague qualifiers such as fast, easy, secure, or user-friendly as acceptance criteria without measurable meaning.
- Hide business rules only inside examples or meeting notes.

## SHOULD
- Prefer outcome-oriented requirements over unnecessary implementation prescriptions.

## Exceptions
Exploratory hypotheses may remain intentionally uncertain when labeled as hypotheses with a validation plan.

## Verification
Peer-review requirements against clarity, completeness, consistency, feasibility, traceability, and testability criteria.
