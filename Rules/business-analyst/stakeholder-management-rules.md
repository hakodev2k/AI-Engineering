# Stakeholder Management Rules

## Purpose
Ensure stakeholder needs, authority, conflicts, and communication are handled explicitly.

## Scope
Stakeholder identification, engagement, decision rights, consultation, and expectation management.

## MUST
- Identify accountable decision-makers, impacted groups, subject-matter experts, and operational owners for material analysis.
- Record decision rights and escalation paths for unresolved conflicts.
- Communicate requirement changes and impacts to affected stakeholders before commitment where practical.

## MUST NOT
- Equate stakeholder seniority with requirement correctness.
- Hide dissent, unresolved conflicts, or known impacts to create artificial consensus.

## SHOULD
- Tailor communication depth and format to stakeholder role and decision need.

## Exceptions
Urgent incidents may use abbreviated consultation under defined incident authority.

## Verification
Inspect stakeholder maps, decision records, conflict logs, approvals, and change communications.
