# Risk Analysis Rules

## Purpose
Make business and delivery risks visible before decisions are made.
## Scope
Requirement, operational, compliance, dependency, adoption, and delivery risks.
## MUST
- Record material risk, cause, impact, likelihood, owner, mitigation, and trigger for review.
- Escalate risks that exceed agreed tolerance.
- Reassess risks when assumptions, scope, or dependencies change.
## MUST NOT
- Hide known risks to preserve a delivery commitment.
- Treat unquantified optimism as mitigation.
## SHOULD
- Prefer prevention and reversible decisions where practical.
## Exceptions
Accepted risks require accountable approval and documented rationale.
## Verification
Inspect risk register, owners, mitigations, escalation evidence, and review history.
