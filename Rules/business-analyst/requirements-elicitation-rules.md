# Requirements Elicitation Rules

## Purpose
Ensure requirements are discovered from evidence and relevant stakeholders before commitments are made.

## Scope
Discovery interviews, workshops, observation, document analysis, and requirement intake.

## MUST
- Identify decision owners, affected users, upstream/downstream stakeholders, constraints, assumptions, and unresolved questions.
- Trace material requirements to an observable business need or obligation.
- Record conflicting stakeholder needs explicitly and drive them to a named decision owner.
- Separate stated solutions from underlying needs before recommending implementation.

## MUST NOT
- Treat a single stakeholder statement as complete requirements when other affected parties exist.
- Invent missing requirements or silently convert assumptions into facts.

## SHOULD
- Combine interviews with operational evidence, data, process observation, or existing-system behavior where practical.

## Exceptions
Time-critical discovery may be abbreviated only when gaps, risks, owner, and follow-up validation are documented.

## Verification
Review the requirement set for stakeholder coverage, source traceability, assumptions, conflicts, and unresolved decisions.
