# Acceptance Criteria Rules

## Purpose
Define objective evidence for deciding whether requested behavior is acceptable.
## Scope
Stories, requirements, business scenarios, edge cases, and release acceptance.
## MUST
- Define observable outcomes for normal, boundary, error, and permission-sensitive scenarios where relevant.
- Align acceptance criteria with approved business rules and non-functional constraints.
- Make unresolved acceptance questions visible before implementation completion.
## MUST NOT
- Use implementation tasks as acceptance criteria.
- Declare acceptance solely because a developer demo succeeds.
## SHOULD
- Use examples that expose important business boundaries without replacing the underlying rule.
## Exceptions
Research spikes may define learning criteria instead of production acceptance criteria.
## Verification
Review criteria for testability, rule coverage, edge cases, and linkage to approved requirements.
