# Decision Governance Rules

## Purpose
Ensure significant business decisions are made by the correct authority with visible rationale.
## Scope
Requirement approval, policy interpretation, priority trade-offs, exceptions, and go-live decisions.
## MUST
- Identify the accountable decision owner for material decisions.
- Record options, evidence, trade-offs, risks, and outcome for consequential decisions.
- Escalate decisions outside analyst authority rather than silently deciding them.
## MUST NOT
- Infer approval from silence.
- Present recommendations as approved decisions.
## SHOULD
- Prefer decision criteria agreed before evaluating contentious options.
## Exceptions
Emergency authority must be predefined, auditable, and reviewed afterward.
## Verification
Inspect decision logs, named owners, approvals, evidence, and escalation records.
