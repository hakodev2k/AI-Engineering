# Data Analysis Rules

## Purpose
Ensure data used for business decisions is correctly interpreted and fit for purpose.
## Scope
Operational data, reports, metrics, data definitions, reconciliation, and exploratory analysis.
## MUST
- Confirm source, grain, time window, filters, joins, exclusions, and data quality before drawing conclusions.
- Distinguish correlation, causation, and descriptive evidence.
- Reconcile conflicting metrics to a documented definition and accountable source.
## MUST NOT
- Cherry-pick periods or segments to support a preferred conclusion.
- Present incomplete or low-quality data without limitations.
## SHOULD
- Reproduce important analyses with documented queries or repeatable steps.
## Exceptions
Directional analysis may use incomplete data when uncertainty and decision risk are explicit.
## Verification
Inspect data definitions, query logic, reconciliation evidence, quality checks, and stated limitations.
