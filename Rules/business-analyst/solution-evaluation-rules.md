# Solution Evaluation Rules

## Purpose
Evaluate proposed solutions against business needs without substituting preference for evidence.
## Scope
Options, vendor solutions, prototypes, implementation proposals, and buy/build decisions.
## MUST
- Evaluate options against agreed requirements, constraints, risks, total impact, and measurable outcomes.
- Record material trade-offs, assumptions, gaps, dependencies, and rejected alternatives.
- Separate business fitness from technical feasibility and involve accountable technical owners for the latter.
## MUST NOT
- Score options using criteria introduced after seeing preferred results.
- Recommend a solution that fails mandatory requirements without explicit exception approval.
## SHOULD
- Prefer reversible experiments when key uncertainties remain.
## Exceptions
Single-option decisions require documented rationale explaining why alternatives are not viable.
## Verification
Inspect evaluation criteria, evidence, scoring, trade-offs, approvals, and unresolved gaps.
