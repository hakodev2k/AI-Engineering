# Scope Management Rules

## Purpose
Protect agreed outcomes and prevent uncontrolled scope expansion.
## Scope
In-scope work, exclusions, change requests, dependencies, and release boundaries.
## MUST
- Define scope boundaries, exclusions, assumptions, and decision owner for material initiatives.
- Assess value, impact, effort, risk, and dependency implications before approving scope changes.
- Keep committed scope distinct from optional or future work.
## MUST NOT
- Add material scope through informal conversation without updating the authoritative requirement set.
- Hide scope reduction that changes expected business outcome.
## SHOULD
- Prefer explicit trade-offs over silent accumulation of work.
## Exceptions
Emergency changes may bypass normal intake under predefined authority with follow-up documentation.
## Verification
Review baselines, change records, approvals, exclusions, and release scope.
