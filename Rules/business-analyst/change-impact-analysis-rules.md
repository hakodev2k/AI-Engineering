# Change Impact Analysis Rules

## Purpose
Expose consequences of proposed changes before commitment or release.
## Scope
Processes, users, systems, data, controls, integrations, training, support, and operations.
## MUST
- Identify upstream, downstream, user, process, data, operational, compliance, and integration impacts for material changes.
- Record affected owners, migration needs, dependencies, and rollback considerations.
- Reassess impact when scope materially changes.
## MUST NOT
- Assume unchanged interfaces imply no business impact.
- Ignore manual procedures or reporting dependencies.
## SHOULD
- Prioritize impacts by severity, likelihood, and reversibility.
## Exceptions
Low-risk isolated changes may use a lightweight checklist with explicit boundaries.
## Verification
Inspect impact matrix, dependency evidence, owner review, migration plan, and unresolved risks.
