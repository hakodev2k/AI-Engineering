# Business Process Analysis Rules

## Purpose
Ensure current and future processes are understood before change is specified.
## Scope
Process discovery, workflow modeling, handoffs, controls, exceptions, and bottlenecks.
## MUST
- Model actors, triggers, inputs, outputs, decisions, handoffs, exceptions, controls, and measurable pain points.
- Distinguish current-state facts from proposed future-state changes.
- Validate critical process models with operational owners.
## MUST NOT
- Optimize one step while ignoring downstream impact.
- Treat undocumented manual workarounds as nonexistent.
## SHOULD
- Use process evidence such as timestamps, volumes, defect data, or observation where available.
## Exceptions
Lightweight mapping is acceptable for low-risk, local changes with explicit scope.
## Verification
Review process maps, exception paths, validation records, bottleneck evidence, and ownership.
