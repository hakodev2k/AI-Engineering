# Compliance Analysis Rules

## Purpose
Ensure regulatory, contractual, policy, and audit obligations are translated into verifiable requirements.
## Scope
Regulations, contracts, internal policy, audit findings, and mandatory controls.
## MUST
- Trace each material obligation to an authoritative source and accountable interpreter.
- Distinguish mandatory controls from recommendations and local preferences.
- Define evidence needed to demonstrate compliance where applicable.
## MUST NOT
- Invent legal interpretations.
- Relax mandatory obligations without authorized approval.
## SHOULD
- Involve legal, compliance, security, or privacy specialists when interpretation exceeds analyst expertise.
## Exceptions
Temporary compensating controls require documented authority, risk, duration, and review date.
## Verification
Inspect source references, requirement mapping, approvals, control evidence, and exception records.
