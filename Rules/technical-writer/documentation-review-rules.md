# Documentation Review Rules
## Purpose
Apply proportionate expert review before documentation can create user or operational risk.
## Scope
Editorial, technical, security, legal/compliance, accessibility, and operational review.
## MUST
- Assign review based on risk: technical claims to qualified SMEs, security-sensitive content to security reviewers, regulated claims to authorized owners, and high-risk operations to operational owners.
- Resolve or explicitly disposition material review findings before publication.
- Re-review affected content after substantive technical changes.
- Preserve evidence of required approvals for high-risk content.
## MUST NOT
- Let authors self-approve content where policy requires independent review.
- Treat copy editing as a substitute for technical validation.
## SHOULD
- Use checklists and ownership rules so review expectations are predictable.
## Exceptions
Emergency publication may use expedited review only under an approved process with follow-up review and documented risk.
## Verification
Inspect review records, approvals, unresolved findings, change diffs, and required reviewer coverage.
