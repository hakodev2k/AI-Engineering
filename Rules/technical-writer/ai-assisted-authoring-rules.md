# AI-Assisted Authoring Rules
## Purpose
Use generative AI without lowering factual, security, copyright, or accountability standards.
## Scope
AI-generated drafts, summaries, examples, transformations, translations, and documentation analysis.
## MUST
- Treat AI output as unverified until checked against authoritative sources and applicable project requirements.
- Require human review for material technical claims and for security-, legal-, privacy-, or production-sensitive content.
- Verify generated code, commands, links, versions, citations, and configuration before publication.
- Follow data-handling policy before sending proprietary, personal, secret, or restricted material to an AI service.
## MUST NOT
- Treat model confidence or fluent prose as evidence.
- Publish fabricated citations, APIs, behavior, or test results.
- Delegate approval authority to an AI system where human approval is required.
## SHOULD
- Use AI for bounded transformations and gap detection when outputs can be efficiently verified.
## Exceptions
Low-risk mechanical edits may use lighter review when automated checks and established editorial controls cover the risk.
## Verification
Source comparison, code/procedure testing, citation checks, sensitive-data review, and accountable human approval where required.
