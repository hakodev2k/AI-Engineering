# Source Validation Rules
## Purpose
Prevent documentation from presenting unverified behavior as fact.
## Scope
Technical claims, product behavior, configuration, limits, compatibility, and operational guidance.
## MUST
- Trace material technical claims to current code, tests, schemas, specifications, approved design records, product behavior, or accountable subject-matter experts.
- Record uncertainty when authoritative evidence is unavailable.
- Revalidate volatile facts such as defaults, limits, versions, endpoints, and platform behavior before publication.
## MUST NOT
- Treat memory, agent confidence, old screenshots, or unverified secondary content as authoritative evidence.
- Invent missing technical details to complete a narrative.
## SHOULD
- Prefer machine-verifiable or primary sources over informal descriptions.
## Exceptions
Clearly labeled conceptual examples may simplify behavior when the simplification cannot mislead implementation.
## Verification
Review source links or evidence, reproduce critical behavior where practical, and require SME approval for unresolved high-impact claims.
