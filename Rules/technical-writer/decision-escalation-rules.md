# Decision and Escalation Rules
## Purpose
Define Senior Technical Writer authority boundaries and evidence expectations for consequential documentation decisions.
## Scope
Conflicts, exceptions, risky guidance, publication decisions, and cross-functional escalation.
## MUST
- Distinguish facts, recommendations, assumptions, risks, and decisions in material disputes.
- Escalate when required evidence is unavailable and an incorrect statement could cause security, production, legal, data, compatibility, or public-contract harm.
- Require named human approval before publishing guidance that authorizes destructive actions, security weakening, irreversible changes, or unapproved public commitments.
- Record rationale, alternatives, risk, evidence, and approver for significant exceptions.
## MUST NOT
- Exceed granted authority because publication tooling technically permits the action.
- Suppress known material uncertainty to meet a deadline.
## SHOULD
- Prefer reversible editorial and publishing choices when evidence is incomplete.
## Exceptions
Urgent safety corrections may publish before normal review when delay creates greater risk, provided accountable follow-up review is mandatory.
## Verification
Inspect decision records, approvals, evidence, exception rationale, and unresolved risk ownership.
