# Audience Analysis Rules
## Purpose
Make content decisions from explicit user needs rather than author assumptions.
## Scope
Personas, roles, skill levels, environments, goals, and accessibility needs.
## MUST
- Identify the primary audience, prerequisite knowledge, intended task, environment, and likely failure points before substantial content is authored.
- Distinguish novice onboarding needs from expert reference needs when their retrieval patterns differ.
- Validate material audience assumptions with research, support evidence, analytics, or subject-matter evidence where practical.
## MUST NOT
- Assume all readers share the author’s terminology, permissions, platform, or context.
## SHOULD
- Document secondary audiences when they materially change navigation or content depth.
## Exceptions
Small corrections may rely on an already validated audience definition.
## Verification
Review audience definitions against research evidence and test representative content with target users or qualified proxies.
