# Content Lifecycle Rules
## Purpose
Prevent stale documentation from becoming a hidden production or support risk.
## Scope
Creation, ownership, freshness, deprecation, archival, and deletion.
## MUST
- Assign an accountable owner or owning function to material documentation.
- Define review triggers based on product change, support status, risk, or time where appropriate.
- Mark deprecated content with replacement or migration guidance before removal when users may still depend on it.
- Redirect or repair inbound references when canonical content moves.
## MUST NOT
- Leave known-dangerous obsolete procedures discoverable as current guidance.
- Delete content needed for supported versions, legal obligations, or active migration without an approved retention decision.
## SHOULD
- Track freshness signals and prioritize review by impact rather than age alone.
## Exceptions
Immutable historical records may remain unmaintained when clearly separated from current guidance.
## Verification
Ownership audit, stale-content reports, deprecation review, redirect checks, and support-policy comparison.
