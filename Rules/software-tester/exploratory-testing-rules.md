# Exploratory Testing Rules

## Purpose
Use skilled investigation to discover risks that scripted checks may miss.
## Scope
Session-based and unscripted product exploration.
## MUST
- Define a charter, target risk, timebox, environment, and evidence capture approach.
- Record meaningful observations, defects, questions, coverage, and follow-up ideas.
- Adapt exploration based on discoveries while preserving enough notes to explain conclusions.
## MUST NOT
- Present random clicking as exploratory testing.
- Claim an area was validated when evidence cannot show what was explored.
## SHOULD
- Use heuristics, tours, production incidents, user behavior, and change history to guide exploration.
## Exceptions
Rapid incident exploration may defer formal notes until immediate risk is contained.
## Verification
Review session notes for charter alignment, evidence, findings, coverage, and follow-up actions.
