# Risk and Prioritization Rules

## Purpose
Direct finite testing effort toward failures with the greatest expected impact.
## Scope
Test prioritization, scope negotiation, residual risk, and time-constrained testing.
## MUST
- Evaluate risk using impact, likelihood, detectability, change exposure, and recovery cost where relevant.
- Make omitted or deferred material coverage visible to decision makers.
- Reprioritize when new defects, incidents, telemetry, or scope changes alter risk.
## MUST NOT
- Equate stakeholder urgency automatically with defect severity or test risk.
- Hide untested high-risk areas behind aggregate pass metrics.
## SHOULD
- Use explicit risk categories or scoring when portfolios are large enough to benefit.
## Exceptions
Business owners may accept residual risk when acceptance and rationale are recorded.
## Verification
Review risk register, test order, deferred coverage, defect trends, and release decisions.
