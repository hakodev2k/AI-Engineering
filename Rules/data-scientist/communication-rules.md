# Analytical Communication Rules
## Purpose
Communicate evidence without overstating certainty or hiding decision risk.
## Scope
Reports, dashboards, presentations, recommendations, and model documentation.
## MUST
- Separate observed facts, assumptions, estimates, uncertainty, interpretation, and recommendations.
- State material limitations, affected populations, and conditions under which conclusions may fail.
- Present effect magnitude and decision impact, not only technical metrics.
## MUST NOT
- Use visual scales, selective windows, or omitted denominators that materially mislead.
- Present model output as certainty when uncertainty is decision-relevant.
## SHOULD
- Lead with the decision and evidence most relevant to the audience.
## Exceptions
Executive brevity may reduce detail only when linked evidence remains available.
## Verification
Peer-review claims against source analysis, charts, uncertainty, and documented limitations.
