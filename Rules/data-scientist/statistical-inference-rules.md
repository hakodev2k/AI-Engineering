# Statistical Inference Rules
## Purpose
Keep uncertainty and statistical claims defensible.
## Scope
Estimation, hypothesis tests, intervals, and statistical comparisons.
## MUST
- Report effect sizes and uncertainty appropriate to the design, not significance alone.
- Validate assumptions or use methods robust to their violation.
- Correct or explicitly account for multiplicity when many hypotheses are tested.
## MUST NOT
- Interpret statistical significance as practical importance.
- Treat failure to reject as proof of no effect.
- Select tests solely because they produce preferred results.
## SHOULD
- Use sensitivity analysis for consequential assumptions.
## Exceptions
Approximate methods require documented limitations and evidence that conclusions remain decision-useful.
## Verification
Review assumptions, estimands, intervals, tests, multiplicity handling, and sensitivity results.
