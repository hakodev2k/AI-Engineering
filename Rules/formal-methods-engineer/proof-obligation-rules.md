# Proof Obligation Rules

## Purpose
Ensure all material correctness claims are decomposed into explicit obligations with visible status and ownership.

## Scope
Applies to invariants, refinement, pre/postconditions, termination, totality, safety, security, and implementation-conformance obligations.

## MUST
- Enumerate proof obligations generated by each significant specification or refinement step.
- Track every obligation as proved, disproved, pending, intentionally excluded, or dependent on an external assumption.
- Prevent unresolved critical obligations from being represented as completed assurance.
- Record dependencies between obligations and foundational lemmas or assumptions.
- Reassess impacted obligations when shared definitions change.

## MUST NOT
- Hide failed or pending obligations in generated output or tool filters.
- Merge an unresolved critical obligation solely because adjacent obligations pass.
- Treat a manually asserted lemma as mechanically proved evidence.

## SHOULD
- Keep obligations small enough that reviewers can understand the reasoning boundary.
- Automate status reporting and regression detection for proof suites.

## Exceptions
A deferred obligation requires documented rationale, consequence, expiry or remediation path, and explicit approval when it affects a release or assurance claim.

## Verification
Inspect proof reports, dependency graphs, admitted goals, CI results, generated verification conditions, and change-impact records.
