# Ethical Design Rules
## Purpose
Prevent deceptive, coercive, discriminatory, or predictably harmful behavior.
## Scope
Choice architecture, monetization, engagement, consent, and vulnerable users.
## MUST
- Identify foreseeable harms and affected groups for consequential decisions.
- Make material costs, commitments, and consequences clear before action.
- Escalate vulnerable-user, sensitive-decision, addictive-mechanic, or discrimination risks.
## MUST NOT
- Use deception, obstruction, forced continuity, disguised advertising, or confirm-shaming.
- Make informed choice harder merely to optimize a metric.
## SHOULD
- Pair business metrics with user-outcome and harm signals.
## Exceptions
None for deceptive practices.
## Verification
Run dark-pattern review, harm assessment, content review, and required legal or ethics approval.
