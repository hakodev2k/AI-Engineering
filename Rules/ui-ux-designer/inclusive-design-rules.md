# Inclusive Design Rules
## Purpose
Prevent exclusion caused by assumptions about identity, ability, language, culture, device, or context.
## Scope
Personas, defaults, imagery, fields, workflows, and research.
## MUST
- Identify exclusion risks for consequential journeys.
- Challenge unnecessary demographic, cultural, or ability assumptions.
- Support relevant variation in names, identities, addresses, and formats.
## MUST NOT
- Require sensitive personal data without necessity and authorization.
- Use stereotypes as design shortcuts.
## SHOULD
- Prefer flexible patterns over multiplying special cases.
## Exceptions
Legally required classifications must be minimized to their required purpose.
## Verification
Review assumptions, research coverage, field constraints, content, and edge cases.
