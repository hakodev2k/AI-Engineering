# Usability Testing Rules
## Purpose
Validate whether representative users can complete important tasks and understand the experience.
## Scope
Moderated, unmoderated, benchmark, and prototype evaluation.
## MUST
- Define tasks, participants, success criteria, severity model, and evidence capture before testing.
- Test realistic scenarios without coaching the intended path.
- Separate preference from observed usability failure.
- Prioritize findings by impact, frequency, criticality, and confidence.
## MUST NOT
- Declare usability solely from participant preference.
- Suppress contradictory findings.
## SHOULD
- Retest fixes for severe findings.
## Exceptions
Expert review may temporarily substitute when limitations and follow-up validation are recorded.
## Verification
Inspect protocol, participant fit, evidence, severity rationale, and retest results.
