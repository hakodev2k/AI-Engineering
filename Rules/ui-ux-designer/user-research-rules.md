# User Research Rules

## Purpose
Ensure design decisions are grounded in representative user evidence.
## Scope
Interviews, observation, surveys, field studies, and evaluative research.
## MUST
- Define the research question, target participants, method, and decision the study will inform before recruiting.
- Separate observed evidence, participant statements, researcher interpretation, and recommendations.
- Record material sampling limitations and contradictory findings.
- Obtain required consent and follow applicable privacy rules before collecting personal data.
## MUST NOT
- Present convenience-sample feedback as representative without qualification.
- Lead participants toward a preferred design or fabricate missing evidence.
## SHOULD
- Triangulate consequential findings with another method or data source.
## Exceptions
Rapid research may reduce sample size when uncertainty and limitations are documented.
## Verification
Review the plan, consent evidence, notes, synthesis traceability, and stated limitations.
