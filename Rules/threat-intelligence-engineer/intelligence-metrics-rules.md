# Intelligence Metrics
## Purpose
Measure whether intelligence improves decisions and defensive outcomes.
## Scope
Program, product, collection, and consumer metrics.
## MUST
- Measure outcomes tied to requirements, decisions, timeliness, accuracy, or defensive improvement.
- Separate activity metrics from effectiveness metrics.
- Review false positives, retractions, stale intelligence, and unmet requirements.
## MUST NOT
- Use IOC count, report count, or feed volume as standalone success measures.
- Optimize analyst behavior around metrics that reward noise.
## SHOULD
- Collect structured consumer feedback and evidence of action taken.
## Exceptions
New programs may use provisional leading indicators with a defined transition to outcome metrics.
## Verification
Review metric definitions, data lineage, targets, consumer feedback, and corrective actions.
