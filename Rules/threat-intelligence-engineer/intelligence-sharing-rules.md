# Intelligence Sharing
## Purpose
Deliver useful intelligence without violating handling or trust boundaries.
## Scope
Internal, partner, customer, community, and executive distribution.
## MUST
- Verify audience authorization and handling restrictions before sharing.
- Include confidence, relevant context, timestamps, and actionability.
- Remove source-sensitive or unnecessary personal data unless explicitly required.
## MUST NOT
- Forward third-party intelligence beyond its permitted distribution.
- Strip caveats in ways that materially change meaning.
## SHOULD
- Tailor technical depth and actions to the recipient's decision role.
## Exceptions
Emergency distribution may use an abbreviated format but not bypass authorization.
## Verification
Review distribution lists, handling labels, redaction, source terms, and delivered artifacts.
