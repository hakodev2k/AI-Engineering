# Incident Support
## Purpose
Provide intelligence that accelerates incident decisions without contaminating evidence or exceeding authority.
## Scope
Active incidents, scoping, enrichment, hypotheses, and response recommendations.
## MUST
- Preserve the distinction between incident facts and external intelligence.
- Timestamp fast-changing enrichment and communicate confidence.
- Coordinate recommendations with the incident commander or designated authority.
## MUST NOT
- Alter production systems, delete evidence, block infrastructure, or contact suspected actors without explicit authorization.
- Let external actor attribution override incident evidence.
## SHOULD
- Prioritize intelligence that changes containment, scoping, or recovery decisions.
## Exceptions
None for authority boundaries.
## Verification
Review incident timeline, evidence references, approvals, intelligence updates, and decision impact.
