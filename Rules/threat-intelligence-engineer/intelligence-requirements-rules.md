# Intelligence Requirements
## Purpose
Ensure collection and analysis answer explicit security decisions.
## Scope
Threat-intelligence planning and prioritization.
## MUST
- Define each requirement as a decision, question, owner, priority, and review date.
- Trace collection and finished intelligence to an active requirement.
- Reassess requirements after material business or threat changes.
## MUST NOT
- Treat feed volume, IOC count, or novelty as intelligence value.
- Collect sensitive data without a documented need and lawful basis.
## SHOULD
- Rank requirements by exposure, consequence, uncertainty, and decision urgency.
## Exceptions
Urgent incident-driven requirements may be provisional but must be documented during stabilization.
## Verification
Review requirement register, stakeholder acceptance, collection mapping, and review history.
