# Strategic Intelligence
## Purpose
Support longer-horizon security and business decisions with defensible threat assessments.
## Scope
Trends, actors, geopolitical drivers, technology shifts, and business exposure.
## MUST
- Tie strategic assessments to explicit decisions and time horizons.
- Separate structural drivers from short-lived events.
- Document assumptions, indicators that would change the assessment, and confidence.
## MUST NOT
- Present geopolitical speculation as technical evidence.
- Extrapolate a single incident into a trend without supporting data.
## SHOULD
- Use multiple independent evidence classes and scenario analysis.
## Exceptions
Early warnings may rely on sparse evidence when uncertainty and decision stakes are explicit.
## Verification
Review assumptions, trend evidence, alternative scenarios, confidence, and decision traceability.
