# Analytic Confidence
## Purpose
Make uncertainty explicit and defensible.
## Scope
Assessments, estimates, warnings, and briefings.
## MUST
- Distinguish observed facts, assumptions, analytic judgments, and forecasts.
- Assign confidence using defined criteria tied to evidence quality, consistency, and gaps.
- State material alternatives when evidence supports more than one explanation.
## MUST NOT
- Convert probability or confidence into false certainty.
- Hide contradictory evidence.
## SHOULD
- Use calibrated probability language consistently.
## Exceptions
Short alerts may compress rationale but must preserve confidence and key caveats.
## Verification
Review evidence links, assumptions, alternatives, confidence rationale, and contradictory reporting.
