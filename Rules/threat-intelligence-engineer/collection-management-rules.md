# Collection Management
## Purpose
Collect the minimum evidence needed to satisfy intelligence requirements safely.
## Scope
Collection plans, tasking, gaps, and retention.
## MUST
- Map collection tasks to requirements and documented gaps.
- Define cadence, owner, permitted methods, retention, and stop conditions.
- Prefer lawful, authorized, least-invasive collection methods.
## MUST NOT
- Expand collection scope merely because data is available.
- Bypass access controls or authorization boundaries.
## SHOULD
- Remove low-yield sources and duplicate collection paths.
## Exceptions
Incident response may accelerate approved collection while preserving authorization boundaries.
## Verification
Review collection plan, permissions, gap register, retention controls, and yield metrics.
