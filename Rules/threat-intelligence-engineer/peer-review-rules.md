# Analytic Peer Review
## Purpose
Catch reasoning, evidence, and communication failures before consequential intelligence is used.
## Scope
High-impact assessments and material updates.
## MUST
- Review evidence sufficiency, alternative hypotheses, confidence, scope, and source restrictions.
- Require an independent reviewer for high-consequence attribution or strategic judgments when time permits.
- Record material reviewer disagreements and their resolution.
## MUST NOT
- Reduce peer review to grammar or formatting checks.
- Suppress dissent merely to produce consensus.
## SHOULD
- Use structured analytic challenge for judgments vulnerable to confirmation bias.
## Exceptions
Urgent alerts may publish before full review but must receive retrospective review.
## Verification
Inspect review records, resolved comments, dissent, and post-publication corrections.
