# Threat Model Alignment
## Purpose
Connect external intelligence to actual organizational exposure.
## Scope
Assets, attack surfaces, dependencies, adversaries, and scenarios.
## MUST
- Evaluate intelligence against relevant assets, identities, technologies, regions, and business processes.
- Distinguish theoretical capability from plausible exposure.
- Update threat scenarios when material intelligence changes likelihood or impact.
## MUST NOT
- Rank threats by media attention alone.
- Claim relevance without an explicit exposure path or justified strategic rationale.
## SHOULD
- Prioritize scenarios that combine capability, intent, opportunity, and consequence.
## Exceptions
Emerging threats may be tracked before exposure is confirmed if uncertainty is stated.
## Verification
Review traceability from intelligence to assets, scenarios, risk decisions, and control changes.
