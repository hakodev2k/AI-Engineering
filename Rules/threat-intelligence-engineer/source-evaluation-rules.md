# Source Evaluation
## Purpose
Control trust placed in intelligence sources.
## Scope
Commercial, community, internal, partner, and open sources.
## MUST
- Record source provenance, access constraints, reliability history, and known bias.
- Separate source reliability from information credibility.
- Corroborate high-impact claims when independent evidence is reasonably available.
## MUST NOT
- Present anonymous or unverifiable claims as confirmed facts.
- Remove provenance needed to assess confidence.
## SHOULD
- Track source performance against later-confirmed outcomes.
## Exceptions
Single-source reporting is acceptable when time-critical if uncertainty is explicit.
## Verification
Inspect source records, corroboration evidence, confidence rationale, and retrospective accuracy.
