# Intelligence Reporting
## Purpose
Produce concise, traceable intelligence products that support decisions.
## Scope
Alerts, assessments, briefs, reports, and executive products.
## MUST
- State the key judgment, relevance, confidence, evidence basis, and recommended decision or action.
- Date time-sensitive claims and identify material intelligence gaps.
- Preserve traceability from consequential judgments to supporting evidence.
## MUST NOT
- Bury critical caveats where decision-makers are unlikely to see them.
- Mix facts and judgments without clear distinction.
## SHOULD
- Put decision-relevant content before background detail.
## Exceptions
Machine-readable products may omit prose sections if equivalent semantics are explicit.
## Verification
Peer review for traceability, confidence, timeliness, audience fit, and actionability.
