# OSINT Collection
## Purpose
Ensure public-source research is lawful, minimal, and reproducible.
## Scope
Public publications, websites, repositories, and datasets.
## MUST
- Record source reference, retrieval time, and relevant context.
- Follow applicable authorization, privacy, retention, and source-use requirements.
- Corroborate consequential claims when independent evidence is available.
## MUST NOT
- Collect data outside approved research boundaries.
- Retain or redistribute unnecessary personal information.
## SHOULD
- Preserve permitted evidence needed for later verification.
## Exceptions
Ephemeral evidence may be described without retention when policy prohibits storage.
## Verification
Inspect provenance, research scope, retention controls, and corroboration records.
