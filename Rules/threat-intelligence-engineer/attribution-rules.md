# Attribution
## Purpose
Prevent unsupported actor attribution and its operational consequences.
## Scope
Campaign, cluster, malware, infrastructure, and actor attribution.
## MUST
- Base attribution on multiple discriminating evidence classes where feasible.
- Document competing hypotheses, deception risk, and confidence.
- Separate activity clustering from identity attribution.
## MUST NOT
- Attribute solely from shared tooling, geography, language, or infrastructure.
- Present vendor naming equivalence as proven identity.
## SHOULD
- Prefer stable internal activity clusters until identity evidence is sufficient.
## Exceptions
Time-critical working attribution must be explicitly provisional.
## Verification
Inspect evidence matrix, alternatives, confidence statement, and peer review.
