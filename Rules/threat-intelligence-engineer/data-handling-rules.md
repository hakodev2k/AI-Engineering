# Intelligence Data Handling
## Purpose
Protect sensitive intelligence throughout its lifecycle.
## Scope
Raw collection, enrichment, analysis, storage, sharing, and disposal.
## MUST
- Classify intelligence according to sensitivity and sharing restrictions.
- Apply least-privilege access and retention limits.
- Protect source-identifying, personal, credential, and incident-sensitive data.
## MUST NOT
- Place secrets or authentication material in intelligence reports.
- Share restricted intelligence beyond authorized recipients.
## SHOULD
- Minimize sensitive fields before downstream distribution.
## Exceptions
Broader disclosure requires documented purpose, risk review, and authorized approval.
## Verification
Inspect access controls, labels, audit records, retention, redaction, and sharing history.
