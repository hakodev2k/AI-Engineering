# Operational Intelligence
## Purpose
Support campaign-level defensive decisions with timely contextual intelligence.
## Scope
Campaigns, targeting, infrastructure, capabilities, and likely next actions.
## MUST
- Define the affected population, observation window, and evidence basis.
- Connect campaign assessment to concrete defensive decisions.
- Update or retract assessments when material evidence changes.
## MUST NOT
- Assume all activity sharing an indicator belongs to one campaign.
- Let urgency remove confidence or scope qualifiers.
## SHOULD
- Identify observable changes that would confirm or falsify the working assessment.
## Exceptions
Incident-time assessments may be provisional and must be marked accordingly.
## Verification
Inspect campaign clustering evidence, timelines, decision outputs, updates, and retractions.
