# Issue and Escalation Rules
## Purpose
Drive active problems to accountable resolution without hiding impact.
## Scope
Blockers, incidents affecting delivery, unresolved decisions, disputes, and escalations.
## MUST
- Record material issues with impact, evidence, owner, target resolution, actions, and escalation threshold.
- Separate symptom, known facts, hypotheses, and decisions when reporting complex issues.
- Escalate when authority, time, risk, or cross-team coordination exceeds the current owner's ability to resolve.
- Track agreed actions to closure with evidence.
## MUST NOT
- Delay escalation to preserve a favorable status report.
- Declare resolution when only a workaround exists unless residual risk is explicitly accepted.
## SHOULD
- Escalate with options and consequences rather than forwarding raw problems alone.
## Exceptions
Immediate safety or production incidents may bypass normal escalation paths according to incident procedures.
## Verification
Review issue age, actions, decision records, escalation timing, and closure evidence.
