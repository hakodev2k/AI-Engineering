# Security and Compliance Governance Rules
## Purpose
Ensure project delivery incorporates mandatory security, privacy, legal, and compliance obligations.
## Scope
Security reviews, privacy, regulatory controls, audit requirements, sensitive data, access, and compliance approvals.
## MUST
- Identify applicable obligations and accountable control owners during planning, not only before release.
- Include required reviews, evidence, remediation, and approvals in schedule and dependencies.
- Escalate unresolved high-risk findings before release decisions.
- Protect sensitive project information according to its classification.
## MUST NOT
- Approve weakening security or compliance controls merely to preserve scope or dates.
- Claim compliance based on project confidence without required evidence or authorized review.
## SHOULD
- Engage security, privacy, legal, and compliance specialists early when impact is plausible.
## Exceptions
Only authorized control owners may approve documented exceptions with scope, duration, compensating controls, and residual risk.
## Verification
Inspect obligation mapping, review results, remediation, exceptions, evidence, and approval records.
