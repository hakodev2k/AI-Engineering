# Evidence and Status Rules
## Purpose
Base project conclusions on verifiable evidence rather than optimism or reporting pressure.
## Scope
Progress, milestone completion, risk claims, readiness, performance, quality, and project health.
## MUST
- Define evidence required to claim completion of material milestones and deliverables.
- Reconcile status with source artifacts, accepted outcomes, tests, financials, dependencies, or operational evidence as applicable.
- State uncertainty and data limitations when evidence is incomplete.
- Correct material reporting errors promptly and preserve the correction trail.
## MUST NOT
- Treat agent confidence, meeting consensus, or activity volume as proof of completion.
- Suppress adverse evidence to maintain a preferred status.
## SHOULD
- Automate objective status signals where reliable source systems exist.
## Exceptions
Qualitative assessments are acceptable when quantitative evidence is unavailable, provided basis and confidence are explicit.
## Verification
Sample reported claims against authoritative artifacts, acceptance records, metrics, logs, approvals, and source-system data.
