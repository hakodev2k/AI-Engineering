# Operational Readiness Rules
## Purpose
Ensure delivered capabilities can be operated, supported, monitored, and recovered after project handoff.
## Scope
Support ownership, runbooks, monitoring, service levels, training, continuity, access, and handover.
## MUST
- Identify operational owners and readiness criteria before go-live.
- Confirm support procedures, monitoring or business controls, access, documentation, training, and escalation paths appropriate to the delivered capability.
- Resolve or formally accept material operational gaps before handoff.
- Define post-release validation and stabilization responsibilities.
## MUST NOT
- Close a project while critical operational ownership remains unassigned.
- Assume delivery documentation alone constitutes operational readiness.
## SHOULD
- Involve operators early enough to influence maintainability and supportability.
## Exceptions
Temporary project-team support may bridge a handover only with a named end date and transition plan.
## Verification
Review readiness evidence, owner acceptance, support artifacts, access, training, monitoring, open risks, and handover sign-off.
