# Assumption and Constraint Rules
## Purpose
Prevent hidden assumptions and constraints from undermining commitments and decisions.
## Scope
Planning assumptions, external constraints, policy limits, technical boundaries, availability, dates, and dependencies.
## MUST
- Record material assumptions with owner, validation method, due date or trigger, and impact if false.
- Distinguish externally imposed constraints from chosen preferences.
- Reassess affected estimates, risks, scope, and schedule when an assumption fails or constraint changes.
- Validate high-impact assumptions before irreversible commitments where practical.
## MUST NOT
- Convert an unverified assumption into a baseline fact without evidence.
- Use a self-imposed target as an immutable external constraint to avoid trade-off analysis.
## SHOULD
- Prioritize early validation of assumptions with high uncertainty and high impact.
## Exceptions
Low-impact assumptions may be embedded in planning notes if their effect remains bounded.
## Verification
Review assumption log, validation evidence, expired assumptions, changed constraints, and linked plan updates.
