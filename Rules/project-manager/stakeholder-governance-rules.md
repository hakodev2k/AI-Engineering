# Stakeholder Governance Rules
## Purpose
Align decision makers and affected groups through explicit ownership and engagement.
## Scope
Sponsors, customers, users, delivery teams, operations, vendors, governance bodies, and impacted functions.
## MUST
- Identify stakeholders by decision authority, impact, influence, information need, and required engagement.
- Assign accountable owners for material project decisions.
- Surface unresolved stakeholder conflicts with options and consequences.
- Reassess stakeholder impact after major scope or organizational changes.
## MUST NOT
- Treat meeting attendance as stakeholder alignment.
- Allow the loudest stakeholder to override approved governance without authority.
## SHOULD
- Tailor engagement frequency and detail to stakeholder impact and decision role.
## Exceptions
Confidential initiatives may restrict participation, but required legal, security, compliance, and authorization roles must still be engaged.
## Verification
Review stakeholder map, decision ownership, approvals, unresolved conflicts, and engagement evidence.
