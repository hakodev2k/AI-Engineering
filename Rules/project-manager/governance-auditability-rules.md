# Governance and Auditability Rules
## Purpose
Maintain sufficient control, traceability, and accountability for senior project oversight.
## Scope
Baselines, approvals, controls, records, governance forums, audit trails, and policy adherence.
## MUST
- Identify applicable governance requirements and decision forums at project initiation.
- Maintain authoritative records for approved baselines, material changes, decisions, risks, exceptions, and acceptance.
- Ensure governance artifacts have clear owners and version or change history.
- Escalate conflicts between project objectives and mandatory policy to authorized owners.
## MUST NOT
- Fabricate, backdate, or alter records to create the appearance of prior approval.
- Bypass mandatory controls solely for convenience or reporting optics.
## SHOULD
- Minimize duplicate governance artifacts by linking authoritative sources.
## Exceptions
Governance may be tailored when authorized and when equivalent control objectives remain satisfied.
## Verification
Audit representative decisions and changes from request through authority, evidence, approval, execution, and recorded outcome.
