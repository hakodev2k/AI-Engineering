# Project Closure Rules
## Purpose
Close projects only after obligations, ownership, evidence, and residual risks are explicitly resolved or transferred.
## Scope
Acceptance, handover, financial closure, contracts, residual work, lessons, records, and benefit ownership.
## MUST
- Confirm deliverable acceptance or documented disposition for incomplete items.
- Transfer operational ownership, residual risks, open actions, benefit tracking, and support responsibilities to named owners.
- Close or reconcile financial and vendor commitments as applicable.
- Capture material lessons with evidence and actionable recommendations.
- Preserve required project records according to applicable retention and security rules.
## MUST NOT
- Mark a project complete merely because the team disbands or funding ends.
- Lose unresolved obligations during handoff.
## SHOULD
- Compare final outcomes against original baselines and explain significant variance.
## Exceptions
Cancelled projects still require controlled closure proportional to outstanding risk and obligation.
## Verification
Review acceptance, handover, residual items, financial/vendor closure, lessons, archived records, and sponsor acknowledgement.
