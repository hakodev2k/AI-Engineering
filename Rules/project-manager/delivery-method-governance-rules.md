# Delivery Method Governance Rules
## Purpose
Use a delivery method that fits the work while preserving outcome, risk, and governance controls.
## Scope
Agile, iterative, predictive, hybrid, and program-specific delivery practices.
## MUST
- Define how work is planned, accepted, measured, changed, and escalated for the chosen delivery model.
- Preserve required governance, security, quality, financial, and compliance controls regardless of methodology.
- Adapt ceremonies and artifacts when they do not improve decisions, flow, evidence, or coordination.
- Make cross-team dependencies and commitments visible.
## MUST NOT
- Use methodology labels to avoid accountability or required approvals.
- Equate velocity, story points, or task completion with business value without supporting outcomes.
## SHOULD
- Favor the lightest governance that reliably controls material risk.
## Exceptions
Experimentation with delivery practices is acceptable when outcomes, risks, and review criteria are explicit.
## Verification
Review working agreements, planning cadence, acceptance flow, metrics, controls, and observed delivery bottlenecks.
