# Schedule Planning Rules
## Purpose
Create credible schedules that expose dependencies, uncertainty, and critical commitments.
## Scope
Milestones, sequencing, estimates, dependencies, critical path, buffers, and schedule baselines.
## MUST
- Build schedules from defined deliverables, dependencies, capacity assumptions, and estimate evidence.
- Identify critical or near-critical dependencies and milestone owners.
- Represent material uncertainty explicitly rather than embedding hidden optimism.
- Reforecast when evidence shows the approved schedule is no longer credible.
## MUST NOT
- Convert a target date into an evidence-free estimate.
- Compress schedules by silently removing validation, controls, or required approvals.
## SHOULD
- Use ranges or confidence levels when uncertainty materially affects commitments.
## Exceptions
Fixed external deadlines may constrain planning, but the resulting scope, resource, quality, and risk trade-offs must be documented and approved.
## Verification
Inspect dependency logic, assumptions, capacity, critical path, milestone evidence, and forecast variance.
