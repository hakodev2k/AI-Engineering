# Quality Management Rules
## Purpose
Make required quality explicit and prevent schedule pressure from silently degrading acceptance standards.
## Scope
Quality objectives, reviews, testing, acceptance evidence, defects, and corrective action.
## MUST
- Define quality and acceptance expectations before dependent delivery work is considered complete.
- Include required review, validation, testing, security, compliance, and operational readiness activities in the plan.
- Track material defects and quality risks with owners and disposition.
- Require authorized risk acceptance when known quality gaps remain at release.
## MUST NOT
- Declare deliverables complete solely because implementation activity ended.
- Remove mandatory validation to recover schedule without explicit approval.
## SHOULD
- Use leading quality indicators in addition to final defect counts.
## Exceptions
Risk-based reduction in validation requires evidence, residual-risk assessment, and accountable approval.
## Verification
Inspect acceptance criteria, test/review evidence, defect status, waivers, and release approvals.
