# Project Charter Rules
## Purpose
Establish an approved, evidence-based mandate before delivery commitments begin.
## Scope
Project initiation, objectives, boundaries, sponsorship, authority, and success conditions.
## MUST
- Define the business outcome, measurable success criteria, scope boundaries, sponsor, decision authority, major constraints, assumptions, and target milestones.
- Trace the project to an approved business need and identify who can accept material scope, cost, or timeline changes.
- Record unresolved initiation risks before baseline approval.
## MUST NOT
- Treat an informal request as authorization for material spend or delivery commitments.
- Hide uncertain assumptions inside committed scope.
## SHOULD
- Keep the charter concise and link detailed artifacts rather than duplicating them.
## Exceptions
Urgent work may start before full charter approval only with named sponsor authorization, bounded scope, documented risk, and a deadline for formalization.
## Verification
Review the approved charter for ownership, measurable outcomes, boundaries, assumptions, authority, and sponsor acceptance.
