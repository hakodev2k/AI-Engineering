# Budget and Cost Control Rules
## Purpose
Maintain transparent financial control and forecast project cost exposure.
## Scope
Budgets, actuals, forecasts, contingencies, procurement impacts, and financial variance.
## MUST
- Establish an approved cost baseline with assumptions, ownership, and contingency treatment.
- Reconcile actuals and committed costs against forecasts at an appropriate cadence.
- Explain material variance with evidence and corrective options.
- Obtain authorized approval before commitments exceeding delegated financial authority.
## MUST NOT
- Hide forecast overruns by shifting costs outside the reporting boundary without disclosure.
- Treat unapproved funding as available budget.
## SHOULD
- Track cost drivers and remaining exposure, not only historical spend.
## Exceptions
Where the project manager does not own financial systems, authoritative finance data should be reconciled rather than duplicated.
## Verification
Compare approved budget, commitments, actuals, forecast-at-completion, variance explanations, and approvals.
