# Decision and Approval Rules
## Purpose
Keep material project decisions attributable, evidence-based, and within delegated authority.
## Scope
Trade-offs, approvals, exceptions, baselines, release decisions, financial commitments, and risk acceptance.
## MUST
- Record material decisions with context, options considered, evidence, consequences, owner, date, and follow-up actions.
- Verify the decision maker has authority for the affected scope, risk, budget, contract, security, or production action.
- Distinguish analysis, recommendation, preparation, approval, and execution.
- Seek human approval before irreversible, destructive, security-weakening, production, or materially breaking actions.
## MUST NOT
- Interpret silence as approval for high-impact decisions.
- Allow an AI agent or project coordinator to exceed delegated authority.
## SHOULD
- Prefer reversible decisions when evidence is weak and reversal cost is acceptable.
## Exceptions
Emergency authority must be explicitly defined by applicable governance or incident procedures.
## Verification
Audit decision records, authority, evidence, approvals, execution logs, and resulting baseline changes.
