# Team Coordination Rules
## Purpose
Create clear ownership, sustainable collaboration, and fast resolution across delivery participants.
## Scope
Roles, responsibilities, interfaces, commitments, conflict, meetings, and cross-functional coordination.
## MUST
- Define accountable ownership for material deliverables, decisions, dependencies, and risks.
- Resolve responsibility gaps and overlaps before they become critical blockers.
- Make commitments visible and verify acceptance by responsible owners.
- Address persistent conflict based on project outcomes, evidence, and governance rather than hierarchy alone.
## MUST NOT
- Use project management authority to override specialist safety, security, legal, or technical concerns without appropriate decision authority.
- Create recurring meetings without a clear coordination or decision purpose.
## SHOULD
- Enable teams to make reversible local decisions within explicit boundaries.
## Exceptions
Temporary ownership may be assigned during transitions when duration and handoff are explicit.
## Verification
Review responsibility mapping, unresolved ownership gaps, action completion, dependency handoffs, and decision latency.
