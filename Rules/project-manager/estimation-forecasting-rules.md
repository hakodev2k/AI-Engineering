# Estimation and Forecasting Rules
## Purpose
Ensure estimates and forecasts communicate evidence and uncertainty rather than false precision.
## Scope
Effort, duration, completion forecasts, confidence, and re-estimation.
## MUST
- Record estimate basis, assumptions, dependencies, estimator ownership, and significant uncertainty.
- Distinguish estimates, forecasts, targets, and commitments in project communication.
- Update forecasts from actual progress and newly discovered work.
- Challenge estimates whose assumptions conflict with observed capacity or complexity.
## MUST NOT
- Present an unvalidated single-point estimate as guaranteed delivery.
- Manipulate estimates solely to match a desired date.
## SHOULD
- Use historical throughput, comparable work, decomposition, or expert evidence appropriate to the project.
## Exceptions
Early discovery may use coarse estimates when uncertainty is labeled and a refinement trigger is defined.
## Verification
Review forecast history, assumptions, actual variance, and evidence used for revisions.
