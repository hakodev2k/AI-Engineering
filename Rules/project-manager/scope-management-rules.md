# Scope Management Rules
## Purpose
Protect agreed outcomes from uncontrolled expansion while allowing justified change.
## Scope
Deliverables, exclusions, work boundaries, acceptance, and scope changes.
## MUST
- Define in-scope deliverables and explicit exclusions at a level that can be accepted.
- Assess requested scope changes for outcome, schedule, cost, capacity, dependency, quality, and risk impact before approval.
- Maintain traceability between approved scope and planned work.
- Escalate material scope ambiguity before teams make irreversible commitments.
## MUST NOT
- Accept scope growth through informal messages when it changes an approved baseline.
- Trade mandatory quality, security, or compliance obligations to preserve dates without authorized approval.
## SHOULD
- Prefer outcome-based boundaries over exhaustive task lists where delivery methods can evolve.
## Exceptions
Emergency scope changes require documented authority, impact, rationale, and retrospective baseline update.
## Verification
Compare approved scope, change records, delivery plan, and acceptance evidence for unexplained divergence.
