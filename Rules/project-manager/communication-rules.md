# Communication Rules
## Purpose
Provide decision-useful project information with consistent evidence and audience-appropriate detail.
## Scope
Status reporting, meetings, written updates, dashboards, executive communication, and team coordination.
## MUST
- Report material progress, forecast, risks, issues, decisions, dependencies, and required actions accurately.
- Distinguish completed outcomes from activity and planned work.
- State evidence and confidence when communicating uncertain conclusions.
- Preserve a durable record for material decisions and commitments.
## MUST NOT
- Use green status to conceal known material risk or missed outcomes.
- Report percentages complete without a defined measurement basis when they influence decisions.
## SHOULD
- Lead with exceptions, decisions, and changes rather than repeating unchanged detail.
## Exceptions
Sensitive information may be restricted to authorized audiences while still providing an appropriate risk signal.
## Verification
Compare reports with delivery evidence, decision logs, risks, milestones, and stakeholder actions.
