# Risk Management Rules
## Purpose
Identify, quantify, own, and actively reduce threats to project outcomes.
## Scope
Delivery, technical, operational, security, compliance, commercial, organizational, and external risks.
## MUST
- Record material risks with cause, event, impact, probability or exposure, owner, response, trigger, and review date.
- Prioritize risks by potential outcome impact, not visibility or stakeholder seniority.
- Track mitigation actions separately from statements of concern.
- Escalate risks exceeding agreed tolerance to the accountable decision owner.
## MUST NOT
- Close a risk because it has not happened yet.
- Treat an active issue as merely a future risk.
## SHOULD
- Use quantitative analysis for high-impact uncertainty when useful evidence exists.
## Exceptions
Minor risks may use lightweight tracking if aggregate exposure is still reviewed.
## Verification
Inspect risk aging, ownership, mitigation completion, triggers, residual exposure, and escalation records.
