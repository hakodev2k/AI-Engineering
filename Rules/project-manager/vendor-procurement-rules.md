# Vendor and Procurement Rules
## Purpose
Control external delivery, commercial dependencies, and acceptance risk.
## Scope
Vendors, statements of work, procurement milestones, third-party deliverables, contracts, and service dependencies.
## MUST
- Define vendor deliverables, acceptance evidence, responsibilities, dependencies, milestones, and escalation paths before relying on them.
- Track contractual and operational dependencies that can affect project outcomes.
- Validate deliverables against agreed acceptance criteria before recording completion.
- Escalate material commercial or performance deviations to authorized owners.
## MUST NOT
- Commit the organization to commercial terms outside delegated authority.
- Treat vendor-reported completion as accepted without required evidence.
## SHOULD
- Include transition, support, data return, and exit considerations where vendor dependency is material.
## Exceptions
Procurement-sensitive information may be access restricted but must remain available to authorized governance roles.
## Verification
Review agreements, acceptance records, milestone evidence, risks, invoices or commitments, and escalation history.
