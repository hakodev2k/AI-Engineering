# Change Control Rules
## Purpose
Ensure baseline changes are evaluated and authorized before becoming commitments.
## Scope
Scope, schedule, budget, quality, architecture-impacting commitments, contractual deliverables, and major assumptions.
## MUST
- Define which baselines require formal change control and who can approve changes.
- Assess each material change for benefits, alternatives, dependencies, cost, schedule, quality, risk, operational, security, and compliance impact as relevant.
- Record approval or rejection and update affected baselines after approval.
- Preserve traceability from request to decision to implementation.
## MUST NOT
- Backdate approval to legitimize an already-executed unauthorized change.
- Execute irreversible or high-impact changes solely because they were discussed informally.
## SHOULD
- Keep low-impact changes lightweight while preserving traceability.
## Exceptions
Emergency changes may execute under authorized emergency procedures with retrospective documentation and review.
## Verification
Audit change requests, impact assessments, approvals, baseline revisions, and implementation evidence.
