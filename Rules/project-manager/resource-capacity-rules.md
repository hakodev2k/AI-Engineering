# Resource and Capacity Rules
## Purpose
Align delivery commitments with real capability and sustainable capacity.
## Scope
People, specialist skills, environments, equipment, shared services, and capacity constraints.
## MUST
- Validate availability and required skills before baselining work dependent on them.
- Account for operational duties, leave, parallel commitments, onboarding, and realistic productivity constraints.
- Identify single points of dependency on scarce people or capabilities and plan mitigation.
- Reforecast when capacity materially changes.
## MUST NOT
- Assume nominal headcount equals available delivery capacity.
- Normalize sustained overtime as the primary plan for meeting an unrealistic baseline.
## SHOULD
- Sequence work to reduce context switching and scarce-resource contention.
## Exceptions
Short emergency surges require explicit duration, recovery plan, and accountable approval.
## Verification
Compare capacity assumptions with allocation data, calendars, throughput, skill needs, and actual utilization signals.
