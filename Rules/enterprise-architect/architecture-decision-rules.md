# Architecture Decision Rules

## Purpose
Ensure consequential enterprise decisions are traceable, evidence-based, and revisitable.

## Scope
Architecture decision records, strategic technology choices, standards, and exceptions.

## MUST
- Significant decisions MUST document context, constraints, options considered, trade-offs, decision, consequences, and owner.
- Evidence MUST be distinguished from assumptions and forecasts.
- Reversible and irreversible decisions MUST receive proportionate analysis and approval.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT rewrite historical decisions to hide changed assumptions; supersede them explicitly.
- MUST NOT treat consensus as evidence of technical or business validity.

## SHOULD
- Decisions SHOULD define triggers for reconsideration when assumptions or constraints may change.

## Exceptions
Time-critical decisions may use abbreviated records if completed retrospectively within an agreed period.

## Verification
Review decision records for completeness, linked evidence, approvals, supersession, and review triggers.
