# Business Capability Rules

## Purpose
Align architecture decisions to durable business capabilities rather than transient organization charts or technologies.

## Scope
Capability maps, investment decisions, target-state design, and portfolio architecture.

## MUST
- Architecture decisions MUST identify the business capabilities they enable, constrain, replace, or retire.
- Capability ownership, maturity, strategic importance, and major dependencies MUST be explicit for material decisions.
- Target-state recommendations MUST trace to measurable business outcomes or obligations.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT create architecture domains solely from current team boundaries when business responsibilities differ.
- MUST NOT claim strategic alignment without traceable evidence.

## SHOULD
- Capability maps SHOULD use stable terminology and distinguish capability from process, product, application, and organization.

## Exceptions
A temporary organization-aligned model may be used when the rationale, migration path, and review date are documented.

## Verification
Review capability maps, decision records, investment proposals, and traceability from architecture outcomes to business objectives.
