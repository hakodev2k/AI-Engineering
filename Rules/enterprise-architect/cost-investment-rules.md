# Cost and Investment Rules

## Purpose
Make enterprise architecture economically sustainable and transparent.

## Scope
Technology investment, platform funding, total cost, licensing, cloud spend, and modernization economics.

## MUST
- Material architecture choices MUST evaluate total lifecycle cost, not acquisition cost alone.
- Shared investments MUST identify expected consumers, funding model, adoption assumptions, and measurable value.
- Cost comparisons MUST use consistent scope, time horizon, workload assumptions, and risk treatment.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT claim savings without a baseline and explicit assumptions.
- MUST NOT optimize unit cost in ways that create unacceptable security, resilience, delivery, or lock-in risk.

## SHOULD
- Prefer reversible investments and incremental validation when value or adoption is uncertain.

## Exceptions
Strategic or regulatory investments may proceed without direct financial return when obligations and rationale are explicit.

## Verification
Review business cases, TCO models, forecasts versus actuals, adoption evidence, and benefit realization.
