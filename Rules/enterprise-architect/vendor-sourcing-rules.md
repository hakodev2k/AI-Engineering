# Vendor and Sourcing Rules

## Purpose
Control strategic dependency, concentration, contractual, lifecycle, and exit risks from third parties.

## Scope
SaaS, cloud providers, enterprise software, outsourced technology services, and strategic vendors.

## MUST
- Material sourcing decisions MUST assess capability fit, security, compliance, resilience, data handling, financial exposure, concentration, interoperability, and exit feasibility.
- Critical vendor dependencies MUST have accountable ownership and continuity expectations.
- Exit costs and data portability MUST be evaluated before irreversible adoption.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT accept vendor roadmap promises as delivered capability without evidence.
- MUST NOT allow procurement convenience to bypass architecture or security requirements.

## SHOULD
- Contracts SHOULD preserve audit, portability, continuity, and termination rights proportional to criticality.

## Exceptions
Urgent sourcing requires bounded scope, explicit risk acceptance, and scheduled full review.

## Verification
Inspect vendor assessments, contracts, architecture reviews, concentration analysis, and exit plans.
