# Stakeholder Communication Rules

## Purpose
Communicate architecture decisions so business and technical stakeholders understand consequences, uncertainty, and required actions.

## Scope
Executive communication, architecture reviews, investment proposals, risk escalation, and cross-team alignment.

## MUST
- Material recommendations MUST state problem, options, trade-offs, risks, evidence, decision required, and consequences in audience-appropriate language.
- Uncertainty and assumptions MUST be visible rather than presented as certainty.
- Disagreements affecting material risk MUST be escalated to the accountable decision owner.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use unnecessary technical complexity to obscure weak evidence or unresolved trade-offs.
- MUST NOT claim stakeholder alignment when material objections remain unresolved or undocumented.

## SHOULD
- Use diagrams and concise decision summaries where they improve shared understanding.

## Exceptions
Sensitive details may be restricted while preserving enough information for authorized decision makers.

## Verification
Review decision packs, meeting outcomes, approvals, unresolved issues, and stakeholder feedback.
