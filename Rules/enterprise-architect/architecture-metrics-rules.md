# Architecture Metrics Rules

## Purpose
Measure whether enterprise architecture improves outcomes rather than merely producing artifacts.

## Scope
Architecture KPIs, standards adoption, risk reduction, portfolio health, delivery enablement, and technical outcomes.

## MUST
- Metrics MUST define purpose, owner, calculation, source, cadence, and decision they inform.
- Architecture outcome claims MUST use baselines and trend evidence where practical.
- Metrics MUST distinguish activity, output, and outcome.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use number of reviews, diagrams, or standards as proof of architecture effectiveness by itself.
- MUST NOT optimize metrics in ways that encourage hidden risk or superficial compliance.

## SHOULD
- Prefer measures tied to reduced risk, faster safe delivery, lower duplication, improved resilience, or better portfolio health.

## Exceptions
Qualitative evidence may supplement sparse quantitative data when limitations are explicit.

## Verification
Inspect metric definitions, data sources, baselines, trends, decisions taken, and evidence of unintended incentives.
