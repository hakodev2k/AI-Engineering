# Roadmap and Transition Rules

## Purpose
Turn target architecture into sequenced, risk-aware, executable change.

## Scope
Architecture roadmaps, transition states, modernization, consolidation, and dependency planning.

## MUST
- Roadmaps MUST identify transition states, dependencies, sequencing constraints, value increments, risks, and accountable owners.
- Each material transition MUST preserve required security, continuity, data integrity, and contractual obligations.
- Roadmap assumptions MUST be revisited when major dependencies or strategy change.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT require big-bang migration when incremental paths materially reduce risk unless evidence justifies it.
- MUST NOT leave temporary transition architecture without exit criteria.

## SHOULD
- Sequence work to retire high-risk dependencies and validate uncertain assumptions early.

## Exceptions
Temporary dual-running may be accepted with reconciliation, cost, ownership, and exit controls.

## Verification
Review roadmap dependencies, milestones, transition architectures, risk register, and realized outcomes.
