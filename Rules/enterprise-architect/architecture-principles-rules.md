# Architecture Principles Rules

## Purpose
Establish enforceable principles that guide enterprise-wide technology decisions consistently.

## Scope
Architecture principles, standards, exceptions, and governance reviews.

## MUST
- Principles MUST state intent, rationale, implications, and observable decision consequences.
- Conflicts between principles MUST be resolved through documented trade-off decisions.
- Material exceptions MUST identify owner, risk, compensating controls, expiry or review date, and approval.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use principles as slogans without decision criteria.
- MUST NOT silently waive principles for schedule or vendor convenience.

## SHOULD
- Principles SHOULD remain technology-neutral unless a technology constraint is itself strategic and approved.

## Exceptions
Emergency deviations require retrospective review and a time-bounded remediation decision.

## Verification
Inspect architecture reviews, exception registers, and decision records for consistent principle application.
