# Cloud and Platform Strategy Rules

## Purpose
Guide cloud and platform choices according to enterprise outcomes, risk, and operating capability.

## Scope
Public cloud, private cloud, SaaS, shared platforms, landing zones, and hosting strategy.

## MUST
- Placement decisions MUST consider security, compliance, latency, resilience, data gravity, operating model, skills, and total cost.
- Shared platforms MUST define supported use cases, service boundaries, ownership, lifecycle, and operational responsibilities.
- Portability requirements MUST be justified by credible scenarios and costed trade-offs.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT mandate multi-cloud, cloud-native, or on-premises strategies without business and risk justification.
- MUST NOT hide provider lock-in where switching cost is material.

## SHOULD
- Prefer managed commodity capabilities when their risk and lifecycle characteristics are acceptable.

## Exceptions
Strategic constraints may override local optimization when explicitly approved and documented.

## Verification
Review placement assessments, platform catalogs, architecture decisions, cost models, and exit strategies.
