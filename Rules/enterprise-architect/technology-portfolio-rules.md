# Technology Portfolio Rules

## Purpose
Control technology sprawl, lifecycle risk, and strategic investment across the enterprise.

## Scope
Platforms, frameworks, runtimes, databases, infrastructure technologies, and managed services.

## MUST
- Material technologies MUST have lifecycle status, accountable owner, supported use cases, and risk classification.
- Adoption decisions MUST consider interoperability, skills, supportability, security, cost, portability, and exit risk.
- Deprecated or end-of-life technology MUST have an approved containment or retirement plan.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT introduce duplicate strategic technologies without documented need and comparison.
- MUST NOT treat vendor popularity as sufficient adoption evidence.

## SHOULD
- Standardize commodity capabilities while preserving justified differentiation where it creates measurable value.

## Exceptions
Experiments may bypass standardization only within bounded scope and without implicit production approval.

## Verification
Inspect technology catalog, lifecycle register, adoption records, exceptions, and retirement roadmaps.
