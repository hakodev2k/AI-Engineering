# Enterprise Architecture Risk Rules

## Purpose
Identify, communicate, and control systemic technology risks before they become enterprise failures.

## Scope
Strategic, operational, security, resilience, vendor, data, delivery, and obsolescence risks.

## MUST
- Material architecture risks MUST identify likelihood, impact, affected capabilities, owner, treatment, residual risk, and review trigger.
- Cross-system concentration and correlated-failure risks MUST be evaluated explicitly.
- Accepted high-impact risks MUST have authorized human approval.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT close risks merely because a project has completed.
- MUST NOT describe unsupported confidence as risk mitigation.

## SHOULD
- Risk treatment SHOULD prioritize reduction of blast radius, irreversibility, and hidden dependency.

## Exceptions
Emergency risk acceptance requires named authority and retrospective review.

## Verification
Review risk registers, architecture decisions, dependency maps, incidents, control evidence, and approval records.
