# Technology Operating Model Rules

## Purpose
Ensure architecture is operable by clear ownership, decision rights, skills, and support responsibilities.

## Scope
Platform ownership, product teams, centralized capabilities, federated governance, support, and service management.

## MUST
- Target architecture MUST define accountable ownership for build, run, security, data, lifecycle, and funding responsibilities where relevant.
- Shared capabilities MUST define service boundaries, consumers, support model, and escalation paths.
- Operating-model changes MUST assess required skills, capacity, incentives, and transition impacts.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT design enterprise capabilities that depend on nonexistent operational ownership.
- MUST NOT centralize decisions without evaluating bottlenecks and domain accountability.

## SHOULD
- Decision authority SHOULD be placed as close as practical to relevant knowledge while preserving enterprise constraints.

## Exceptions
Temporary ownership during transition requires named owner and handover criteria.

## Verification
Review RACI or equivalent ownership evidence, service catalogs, support models, staffing plans, and operational outcomes.
