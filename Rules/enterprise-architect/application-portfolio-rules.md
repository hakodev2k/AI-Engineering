# Application Portfolio Rules

## Purpose
Manage application duplication, lifecycle risk, ownership, and strategic fit.

## Scope
Enterprise applications, services, products, SaaS systems, and major legacy systems.

## MUST
- Applications MUST have accountable ownership, lifecycle state, criticality, business capability mapping, and major dependencies.
- Rationalization decisions MUST evaluate business fit, technical health, cost, security, data obligations, and migration impact.
- Retirement decisions MUST include dependency, data-retention, user-transition, and operational closure plans.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT retire systems based only on license or infrastructure cost.
- MUST NOT retain unsupported critical applications without explicit risk acceptance and mitigation.

## SHOULD
- Duplicate capabilities SHOULD be consolidated when benefits exceed migration and concentration risks.

## Exceptions
Temporary duplication is acceptable during controlled migration or resilience strategies with defined exit criteria.

## Verification
Review application inventory, ownership, lifecycle metadata, dependency maps, and rationalization evidence.
