# Standards and Exception Rules

## Purpose
Keep enterprise standards useful while controlling justified deviations.

## Scope
Reference architectures, approved technologies, mandatory controls, patterns, and waivers.

## MUST
- Standards MUST define applicability, mandatory versus recommended requirements, ownership, version, and review cadence.
- Exceptions MUST state scope, reason, evidence, risk, compensating controls, approver, and expiry or review date.
- Expired exceptions MUST be remediated, renewed with evidence, or escalated.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT create permanent exceptions through repeated automatic renewal.
- MUST NOT enforce obsolete standards after their assumptions no longer hold.

## SHOULD
- Standards SHOULD provide paved paths and verification mechanisms that reduce compliance effort.

## Exceptions
None for documenting deviations from mandatory standards.

## Verification
Inspect standards catalog, version history, automated checks, waiver register, and overdue exceptions.
