# Architecture Governance Rules

## Purpose
Make architecture governance risk-based, transparent, timely, and evidence-driven.

## Scope
Review boards, standards, waivers, assurance, decision rights, and compliance tracking.

## MUST
- Governance MUST define decision rights, thresholds, required evidence, escalation paths, and accountable approvers.
- Reviews MUST focus on material risk and cross-enterprise impact rather than stylistic preference.
- Decisions and exceptions MUST be recorded with rationale and follow-up obligations.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT approve high-risk deviations through undocumented informal consent.
- MUST NOT use governance to impose hidden requirements after teams have committed delivery plans.

## SHOULD
- Automate deterministic policy checks and reserve human review for judgment-heavy decisions.

## Exceptions
Emergency approval paths require named authority and retrospective governance review.

## Verification
Inspect governance charter, decision logs, exception register, review evidence, and overdue remediation.
