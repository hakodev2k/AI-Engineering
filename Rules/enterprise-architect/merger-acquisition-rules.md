# Merger and Acquisition Architecture Rules

## Purpose
Control technology, data, security, continuity, and integration risk during mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures.

## Scope
Technology due diligence, Day-1 readiness, integration, separation, transitional services, and portfolio rationalization.

## MUST
- Due diligence MUST identify critical systems, data, contracts, security exposures, dependencies, licensing, lifecycle, and separation constraints.
- Integration or separation plans MUST protect continuity, identity, data integrity, regulatory obligations, and access boundaries.
- Synergy or rationalization claims MUST include migration cost, timing, dependency, and risk evidence.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT connect enterprise trust zones before required security and identity controls are validated.
- MUST NOT destroy inherited systems or data before retention, dependency, and legal obligations are confirmed.

## SHOULD
- Prioritize reversible Day-1 controls before deep consolidation.

## Exceptions
Accelerated changes require executive risk approval and compensating controls.

## Verification
Review due-diligence findings, Day-1 controls, integration plans, access reviews, and retirement evidence.
