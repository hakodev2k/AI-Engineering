# Modernization and Legacy Rules

## Purpose
Modernize legacy estates based on business risk and value rather than technology fashion.

## Scope
Legacy applications, platforms, infrastructure, technical debt, and modernization programs.

## MUST
- Modernization proposals MUST assess business criticality, technical risk, operating cost, security, change frequency, dependencies, and migration risk.
- Replacement or decomposition MUST preserve required behavior, data obligations, and operational controls.
- Retirement MUST be verified before dependent infrastructure, licenses, or data are destroyed.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT rewrite systems solely because they are old.
- MUST NOT conceal modernization risk behind unsupported estimates or generic best practices.

## SHOULD
- Prefer incremental strangulation, rehosting, replatforming, refactoring, or replacement according to evidence rather than ideology.

## Exceptions
Urgent unsupported-platform remediation may prioritize risk reduction over ideal target architecture.

## Verification
Review modernization business cases, dependency maps, migration tests, cutover evidence, and retirement confirmation.
