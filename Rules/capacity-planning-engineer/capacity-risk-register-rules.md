# Capacity Risk Register Rules
## Purpose
Make material capacity risks visible, owned, and actionable.
## Scope
Known limits, forecast uncertainty, quota gaps, dependency constraints, and long-lead actions.
## MUST
- Material risks MUST record likelihood, impact, evidence, owner, mitigation, trigger, and due date.
- Risks threatening reliability objectives before mitigation completion MUST be escalated.
- Closed risks MUST include evidence that the condition is resolved or accepted.
## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT close a risk because demand has not yet reached the limit.
- MUST NOT hide uncertainty by assigning unsupported precise probabilities.
## SHOULD
- Risks SHOULD be ranked by time-to-impact and blast radius.
## Exceptions
Accepted risks require authorized human acceptance and review date.
## Verification
Review risk records against forecasts, incidents, action status, and approvals.
