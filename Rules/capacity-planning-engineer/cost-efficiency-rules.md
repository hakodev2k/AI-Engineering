# Cost Efficiency Rules
## Purpose
Balance reliability headroom with economically responsible capacity.
## Scope
Utilization, reservations, commitments, overprovisioning, rightsizing, and capacity alternatives.
## MUST
- Cost optimization proposals MUST quantify reliability and performance impact before reducing capacity.
- Capacity comparisons MUST use total relevant cost, including redundancy, data transfer, licenses, and operations.
- Large commitments MUST be supported by demand confidence and approved by authorized humans.
## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT optimize average utilization at the expense of required peak or failure headroom.
- MUST NOT present idle reserve as waste without identifying its reliability purpose.
## SHOULD
- Plans SHOULD compare scale-up, scale-out, architecture, and demand-shaping alternatives.
## Exceptions
Strategic overcapacity requires documented rationale and review date.
## Verification
Inspect cost models, utilization evidence, SLO impact analysis, and approvals.
