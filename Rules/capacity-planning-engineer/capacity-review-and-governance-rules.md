# Capacity Review and Governance Rules
## Purpose
Create repeatable Senior-level decision controls for capacity commitments.
## Scope
Periodic reviews, launch readiness, procurement, architectural capacity decisions, and executive commitments.
## MUST
- Critical capacity reviews MUST show demand forecast, usable capacity, headroom, failure scenario, lead time, dependencies, risks, and actions.
- Material architecture or procurement decisions MUST document alternatives, constraints, reversibility, and evidence.
- Owners and due dates MUST be assigned to every required capacity action.
- Review artifacts MUST state data freshness and unresolved uncertainty.
## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT report green status when a required action cannot complete before the risk window.
- MUST NOT treat agent confidence or stakeholder optimism as evidence.
## SHOULD
- Reviews SHOULD focus on decisions and exceptions rather than metric recitation.
## Exceptions
Reduced review scope requires documented low-risk rationale.
## Verification
Audit review records, action completion, decision evidence, approvals, and forecast outcomes.
