# Wireless Network Rules

## Purpose
Deliver secure, measurable, capacity-aware wireless service.

## Scope
Enterprise WLAN, RF design, SSIDs, authentication, roaming, guest access, and spectrum use.

## MUST
- Base RF design on measured coverage, interference, density, and client requirements.
- Separate guest/untrusted access from protected networks with enforceable policy.
- Use approved authentication and encryption appropriate to data sensitivity.
- Validate roaming and capacity for critical mobility use cases.

## MUST NOT
- Treat maximum signal strength as the sole wireless design objective.
- Deploy insecure legacy authentication without explicit risk acceptance and migration plan.

## SHOULD
- Minimize unnecessary SSIDs and channel contention.

## Exceptions
Legacy-client accommodations require isolated scope, compensating controls, expiry/review date, and approval.

## Verification
Review survey data, RF metrics, authentication configuration, segmentation, client telemetry, and capacity tests.
