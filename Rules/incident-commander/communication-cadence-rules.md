# Communication Cadence Rules

## Purpose
Keep responders and stakeholders synchronized with accurate, time-bounded incident information.

## Scope
Applies to internal incident channels, executive updates, status pages, customer communications, and responder handoffs.

## MUST
- Establish an update cadence appropriate to severity and impact.
- Every update MUST state current impact, known scope, actions in progress, unresolved risks, and next update time when applicable.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed facts from hypotheses and unknowns.
- Correct materially wrong prior statements explicitly.
- Ensure externally published claims are reviewed by the responsible communication or business owner when required.

## MUST NOT
- Invent an ETA without evidence.
- Publish speculative root cause as fact.
- Flood technical investigation channels with redundant stakeholder requests when a communication owner can absorb them.

## SHOULD
- Use consistent templates and timestamps.
- Reduce update frequency only after impact and operational risk materially decline.

## Exceptions
Immediate safety or regulatory notifications may bypass normal cadence but still require factual, minimal communication.

## Verification
Review incident updates for cadence, timestamps, factual separation, correction history, ownership, and alignment with observed system state.
