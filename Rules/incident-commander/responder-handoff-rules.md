# Responder Handoff Rules

## Purpose
Prevent loss of context, ownership, and risk awareness during shift or role transitions.

## Scope
Applies to Incident Commander, technical leads, communications owners, scribes, and specialist responders.

## MUST
- Every handoff MUST state current impact, active hypotheses, mitigations in progress, pending decisions, known risks, and next checkpoints.
- The receiving responder MUST acknowledge ownership before the outgoing responder disengages.
- Transfer links to dashboards, incident records, vendor cases, and change references needed to continue safely.
- Record handoff time and participants.
- Highlight assumptions that remain unverified.

## MUST NOT
- Hand off only through raw chat history without a concise state summary.
- Allow critical tasks to become ownerless during transition.
- Assume incoming responders know prior rejected hypotheses or failed mitigations.

## SHOULD
- Use a standard handoff template.
- Schedule overlap for high-severity or complex incidents when staffing permits.

## Exceptions
Emergency replacement may occur without overlap, but a written state summary remains required as soon as practical.

## Verification
Inspect handoff notes, acknowledgements, task ownership, and whether incoming responders could reconstruct current state without relying on memory.
