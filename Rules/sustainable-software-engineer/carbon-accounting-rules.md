# Carbon Accounting Rules

## Purpose
Ensure software carbon estimates are traceable, comparable, and not overstated.

## Scope
Applies when estimating or reporting emissions associated with software operation, cloud infrastructure, data processing, or delivery pipelines.

## MUST
- Carbon estimates MUST identify the accounting boundary, time period, activity data, emissions factors, allocation method, and uncertainty.
- Operational and embodied impacts MUST NOT be combined without labeling their different bases.
- Provider-reported values and internally calculated values MUST be distinguishable.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT present estimated emissions as exact measurements.
- MUST NOT selectively omit known material infrastructure components to improve reported results.

## SHOULD
- Prefer primary provider data when methodology is sufficiently transparent.
- Use consistent allocation rules across comparable services and periods.

## Exceptions
A proxy methodology may be used when direct data is unavailable, provided the proxy, uncertainty, and likely bias are documented.

## Verification
Inspect calculation inputs, factor sources, allocation formulas, methodology notes, and reconciliation against provider or billing telemetry where available.
