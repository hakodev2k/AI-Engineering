# Data Subject Rights Rules

## Purpose
Make privacy rights operational, accurate, timely, and auditable.

## Scope
Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, and related requests where applicable.

## MUST
- Rights workflows MUST authenticate requesters proportionately before disclosure or destructive action.
- Applicable systems and processors MUST be discoverable from the data inventory.
- Responses MUST meet defined legal or policy deadlines.
- Search and fulfillment MUST include relevant derived and replicated data where required.
- Denials or limitations MUST record the reason and approval basis.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT expose another person's data while fulfilling a request.
- MUST NOT rely on ad hoc manual searching as the sole mechanism for recurring rights obligations.

## SHOULD
- Workflows SHOULD support status tracking, evidence capture, and repeatable execution.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented applicability analysis, owner, reviewer, and retained evidence.

## Verification
Exercise representative requests end to end and inspect identity checks, search coverage, timestamps, outputs, deletions, and audit records.
