# Privacy Notice and Transparency Rules

## Purpose
Ensure disclosures accurately describe material personal-data processing.

## Scope
External notices, in-product disclosures, contextual notices, consent text, and material internal representations used to support them.

## MUST
- Notices MUST reflect actual data categories, purposes, recipients, retention, rights, and significant automated processing where applicable.
- Material processing changes MUST trigger notice review before release.
- Contextual disclosures MUST appear at points where unexpected collection or use occurs.
- Statements about deletion, anonymization, security, or sharing MUST be supportable by operational evidence.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT use broad language to conceal materially different uses.
- MUST NOT publish assurances that system behavior cannot satisfy.

## SHOULD
- Use layered, concise explanations with links to deeper detail.
- Maintain version history for notices tied to major processing changes.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented rationale and privacy/legal approval.

## Verification
Compare notices with data flows, schemas, vendor contracts, product behavior, consent records, and retention controls.
