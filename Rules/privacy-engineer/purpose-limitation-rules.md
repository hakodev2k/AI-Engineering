# Purpose Limitation Rules

## Purpose
Prevent personal data from being reused beyond approved, compatible purposes.

## Scope
Primary processing, secondary analytics, enrichment, experimentation, AI training, sharing, and internal reuse.

## MUST
- Processing purposes MUST be specific enough to evaluate compatibility and necessity.
- Secondary use MUST be assessed against the original purpose, user expectations, legal basis, sensitivity, and safeguards.
- Materially incompatible reuse MUST require a new approved basis or must not proceed.
- Purpose changes MUST propagate to notices, controls, retention, access rules, and downstream processors where applicable.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT rely on vague purposes such as "improve services" to justify unrelated processing.
- MUST NOT silently repurpose sensitive data for profiling, advertising, or model training.

## SHOULD
- Purpose metadata SHOULD travel with datasets and interfaces where practical.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented compatibility analysis, accountable approval, safeguards, and evidence.

## Verification
Review product requirements, data contracts, notices, processing records, analytics definitions, and downstream-use documentation.
