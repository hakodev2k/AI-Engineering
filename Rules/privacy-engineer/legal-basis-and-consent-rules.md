# Legal Basis and Consent Rules

## Purpose
Ensure personal-data processing has an appropriate documented basis and consent is used correctly when required.

## Scope
Collection, use, disclosure, tracking, profiling, marketing, analytics, and sensitive-data processing.

## MUST
- Each material processing purpose MUST map to an approved legal or policy basis where applicable.
- Consent-dependent processing MUST record what the individual agreed to, when, and under which notice/version.
- Consent withdrawal MUST stop dependent processing within the defined operational SLA.
- Consent interfaces MUST make acceptance and refusal meaningfully understandable.
- Changes that alter the basis or scope of processing MUST trigger reassessment.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT treat silence, inactivity, or unrelated acceptance as consent where affirmative consent is required.
- MUST NOT bundle optional processing into a mandatory action without a valid basis.

## SHOULD
- Consent records SHOULD be centrally auditable and propagated to downstream systems.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented legal/privacy review, rationale, owner, controls, and approval.

## Verification
Inspect processing records, consent logs, UI flows, withdrawal tests, downstream propagation, and audit evidence.
