# Privacy Incident Response Rules

## Purpose
Ensure suspected personal-data incidents are contained, assessed, evidenced, and escalated correctly.

## Scope
Unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, alteration, misuse, excessive collection, failed deletion, and privacy-control failures.

## MUST
- Suspected privacy incidents MUST be triaged promptly with security and legal stakeholders as applicable.
- Investigation MUST preserve evidence, affected data categories, subjects, systems, dates, recipients, and containment actions.
- Notification obligations and deadlines MUST be assessed using documented criteria.
- Containment MUST prioritize stopping further exposure without destroying evidence.
- Corrective actions MUST address root cause and recurrence risk.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT delay escalation while waiting for complete certainty.
- MUST NOT make unsupported statements about impact, exposure, or notification requirements.

## SHOULD
- Maintain tested playbooks and contact paths for likely privacy incident classes.

## Exceptions
Incident exceptions require designated authority and documented rationale.

## Verification
Review incident records, timelines, evidence, notification assessments, containment actions, post-incident findings, and remediation tracking.
