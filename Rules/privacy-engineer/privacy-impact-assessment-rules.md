# Privacy Impact Assessment Rules

## Purpose
Require structured assessment of processing that may create significant privacy risk.

## Scope
New products, sensitive-data processing, large-scale monitoring, profiling, novel technology, cross-context linkage, and material processing changes.

## MUST
- High-risk processing MUST undergo a privacy impact assessment before production release.
- Assessments MUST document purpose, necessity, proportionality, data flows, affected individuals, risks, safeguards, residual risk, and accountable owners.
- Assumptions about impact or likelihood MUST be supported by evidence where practical.
- Unresolved high residual risk MUST be escalated to the appropriate human authority.
- Material design changes MUST trigger reassessment.

## MUST NOT
- MUST NOT treat a completed template as proof that risk is acceptable.
- MUST NOT approve high-risk processing without clear mitigation ownership.

## SHOULD
- Reuse threat models, data inventories, security assessments, and user research as evidence inputs.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented applicability rationale and senior privacy/legal approval.

## Verification
Inspect assessment completeness, linked evidence, mitigation status, approvals, release gates, and reassessment history.
