# Psychological Safety Rules

## Purpose
Support candid inspection, respectful challenge, and early risk disclosure.

## MUST
- Protect respectful dissent and make retaliation for good-faith risk reporting unacceptable.
- Address repeated interruption, humiliation, intimidation, or exclusion through appropriate channels.
- Separate learning conversations from blame.

## MUST NOT
- Promise absolute confidentiality when legal, safety, or conduct obligations may require escalation.
- Force vulnerable disclosures in group ceremonies.

## SHOULD
Use anonymous or private feedback channels when power dynamics inhibit candor.

## Exceptions
Serious misconduct must follow applicable escalation and evidence-preservation procedures.

## Verification
Use behavioral observation, retrospective participation, escalation patterns, and confidential feedback where appropriate.
