# Risk and Escalation Rules

## Purpose
Ensure material risks reach the right decision makers before avoidable harm occurs.

## MUST
- Record material delivery, quality, security, compliance, dependency, and people risks with evidence and owner.
- Escalate when the team lacks authority, capability, or time to contain significant risk.
- Distinguish recommendation from authorized execution.

## MUST NOT
- Silence risks because escalation may be politically uncomfortable.
- Execute destructive, security-weakening, production, access, or irreversible actions without required human approval.

## SHOULD
Prefer reversible mitigations while uncertainty remains.

## Exceptions
Emergency procedures may authorize predefined actions; evidence and post-event review remain required.

## Verification
Inspect risk records, escalation timing, approvals, mitigation evidence, and incident outcomes.
