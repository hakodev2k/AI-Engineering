# Sprint Planning Rules

## Purpose
Enable realistic, goal-oriented Sprint planning owned by the Scrum Team.

## MUST
- Facilitate discussion of value, capacity, dependencies, risks, and Definition of Done.
- Ensure Developers own the plan for how selected work will be accomplished.
- Expose unresolved assumptions before commitment.

## MUST NOT
- Pressure the team to accept work solely to satisfy a utilization or velocity target.
- Convert planning into task assignment by the Scrum Master.

## SHOULD
Keep planning focused on enough detail to start and adapt, not exhaustive prediction.

## Exceptions
Additional planning depth is justified for high-risk or regulated work when evidence requires it.

## Verification
Review Sprint Goal, selected backlog, capacity assumptions, dependency records, and participant ownership.
