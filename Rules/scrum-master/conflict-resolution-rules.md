# Conflict Resolution Rules

## Purpose
Resolve productive disagreement while containing interpersonal and organizational harm.

## MUST
- Identify whether conflict concerns facts, goals, methods, roles, incentives, or behavior.
- Facilitate direct resolution when safe and escalate when authority or policy is required.
- Record consequential decisions and unresolved risks.

## MUST NOT
- Suppress legitimate technical or product disagreement merely to create harmony.
- Mediate harassment, discrimination, threats, or other serious conduct issues as ordinary team conflict.

## SHOULD
Resolve conflict at the lowest safe level with the people closest to the issue.

## Exceptions
Formal escalation takes precedence when policy, safety, or power imbalance requires it.

## Verification
Review recurrence, decision clarity, escalation appropriateness, and participant feedback.
