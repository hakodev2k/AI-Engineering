# Metrics and Forecasting Rules

## Purpose
Use metrics for learning and forecasting without gaming or false certainty.

## MUST
- Define metric meaning, source, limitations, and intended decision before using it.
- Use historical evidence and uncertainty ranges for forecasts where practical.
- Detect incentives that encourage metric gaming.

## MUST NOT
- Compare teams by velocity as a productivity ranking.
- Treat story points as hours, business value, or a cross-team standardized unit.
- claim improvement from a metric without relevant baseline and context.

## SHOULD
Prefer outcome, flow, quality, and predictability evidence over activity counts.

## Exceptions
Proxy metrics may be used temporarily when their limitations and validation plan are explicit.

## Verification
Inspect metric definitions, baselines, dashboards, forecast error, and resulting behaviors.
