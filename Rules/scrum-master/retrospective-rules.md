# Retrospective Rules

## Purpose
Turn recurring experience into measurable improvements to quality and effectiveness.

## MUST
- Create conditions for candid inspection of people, interactions, process, tools, quality, and Definition of Done.
- Select actionable improvements with owners or clear team ownership.
- Revisit prior improvement actions and evidence of impact.

## MUST NOT
- Use retrospectives for blame, performance ranking, or forced disclosure.
- Publish sensitive comments without agreed consent.

## SHOULD
Vary facilitation techniques to avoid ritualized responses.

## Exceptions
Serious conduct or safety concerns must follow appropriate escalation channels rather than remain confidential by default.

## Verification
Review improvement actions, follow-through, outcome evidence, and participant safety signals.
