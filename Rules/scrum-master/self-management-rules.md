# Self-Management Rules

## Purpose
Increase the team's ability to organize and make delivery decisions within clear boundaries.

## MUST
- Coach teams to own work selection details, coordination, adaptation, and technical execution.
- Make external constraints and decision boundaries explicit.
- Intervene when command-and-control behavior systematically removes team ownership.

## MUST NOT
- Assign individual tasks or dictate implementation sequencing as routine practice.
- Confuse self-management with absence of accountability.

## SHOULD
Use questions, facilitation, and experiments before prescribing solutions.

## Exceptions
Direct instruction may be appropriate for immediate safety, legal, or incident response needs with explicit hand-back.

## Verification
Observe who makes routine delivery decisions and whether ownership persists without Scrum Master presence.
