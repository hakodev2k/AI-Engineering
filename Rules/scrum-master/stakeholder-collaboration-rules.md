# Stakeholder Collaboration Rules

## Purpose
Create effective stakeholder engagement without bypassing product or team accountabilities.

## MUST
- Identify stakeholders whose decisions, knowledge, or impact materially affect outcomes.
- Make expectations, dependencies, risks, and feedback channels explicit.
- Facilitate direct collaboration between stakeholders and the appropriate Scrum Team members.

## MUST NOT
- Act as a permanent communication relay when direct collaboration is feasible.
- Promise scope, date, or priority without the accountable decision makers.

## SHOULD
Reduce handoffs and increase timely access to decision makers.

## Exceptions
Regulated or sensitive communication may require controlled channels.

## Verification
Inspect decision latency, stakeholder participation, handoffs, and unresolved expectation gaps.
