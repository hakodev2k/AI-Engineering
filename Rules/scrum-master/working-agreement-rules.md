# Working Agreement Rules

## Purpose
Create explicit team norms that support coordination, quality, and respectful collaboration.

## MUST
- Facilitate team-owned agreements for material collaboration expectations.
- Ensure agreements do not override organizational safety, security, legal, or conduct requirements.
- Revisit agreements when evidence shows they are ineffective or obsolete.

## MUST NOT
- Use working agreements to impose Scrum Master preferences without team consent.
- Treat agreements as immutable policy.

## SHOULD
Keep agreements observable, concise, and linked to real coordination problems.

## Exceptions
Mandatory external controls take precedence and must be identified as constraints rather than team-created agreements.

## Verification
Inspect agreement ownership, observed behavior, recurring violations, and adaptation history.
