# Continuous Improvement Rules

## Purpose
Make improvement a measurable operating discipline rather than an occasional ceremony.

## MUST
- Connect improvement work to an observed problem, hypothesis, or risk.
- Define evidence that can show whether a significant experiment helped.
- Preserve successful improvements in working agreements, automation, policy, or practice where appropriate.

## MUST NOT
- Accumulate improvement actions indefinitely without prioritization or closure.
- Declare success based only on participant confidence.

## SHOULD
Prefer small, reversible experiments with short feedback cycles.

## Exceptions
Known mandatory controls may be implemented directly without experimental validation.

## Verification
Review improvement backlog, hypotheses, measures, completed experiments, and sustained behavior changes.
