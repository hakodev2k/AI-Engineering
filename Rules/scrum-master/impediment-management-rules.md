# Impediment Management Rules

## Purpose
Remove or reduce impediments without creating dependency on the Scrum Master.

## MUST
- Distinguish blockers the team can solve from systemic impediments requiring escalation.
- Track material impediments to resolution, ownership, or explicit acceptance.
- Escalate persistent cross-team or organizational constraints with evidence of impact.

## MUST NOT
- Hide impediments to protect delivery optics.
- Personally absorb every coordination problem that the team can learn to resolve.

## SHOULD
Optimize for removal of root causes rather than repeated workarounds.

## Exceptions
Temporary workarounds are acceptable when risk and follow-up are explicit.

## Verification
Review impediment age, recurrence, escalation evidence, and resolution outcomes.
