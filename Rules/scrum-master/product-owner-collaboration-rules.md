# Product Owner Collaboration Rules

## Purpose
Help the Product Owner maximize value while preserving accountability boundaries.

## MUST
- Facilitate clarity of Product Goal, ordering decisions, stakeholder input, and backlog transparency.
- Surface delivery evidence, dependencies, and risks relevant to product decisions.
- Protect the Product Owner's accountability for Product Backlog management decisions.

## MUST NOT
- Reorder the Product Backlog on personal authority.
- Become a proxy Product Owner merely because stakeholder access is difficult.

## SHOULD
Coach toward outcome-based goals and evidence-informed prioritization.

## Exceptions
Delegated administrative backlog work is acceptable when decision authority remains explicit.

## Verification
Review decision ownership, backlog changes, Product Goal clarity, and stakeholder feedback loops.
