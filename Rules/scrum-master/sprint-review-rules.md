# Sprint Review Rules

## Purpose
Use Sprint Reviews to inspect product outcomes with stakeholders and adapt future direction.

## MUST
- Center discussion on usable outcomes, changed conditions, stakeholder feedback, and next decisions.
- Make incomplete work and material limitations transparent.
- Capture resulting adaptations in the Product Backlog or relevant decision records.

## MUST NOT
- Reduce the review to a ceremonial demo or approval gate.
- Misrepresent unfinished work as Done.

## SHOULD
Invite stakeholders who can provide relevant evidence or decisions.

## Exceptions
Sensitive material may be restricted to authorized participants while preserving necessary transparency.

## Verification
Inspect review evidence, stakeholder participation, feedback, and resulting backlog adaptations.
