# Coaching Rules

## Purpose
Develop durable capability while respecting professional boundaries.

## MUST
- Establish the coaching objective, context, and ownership of action.
- Separate observations from interpretations and test assumptions with the coachee.
- Escalate issues outside coaching competence to appropriate specialists or leaders.

## MUST NOT
- Present personal preference as Scrum doctrine.
- Use confidential coaching information for retaliation or informal performance scoring.

## SHOULD
Favor inquiry and experiments that build independent judgment.

## Exceptions
Immediate safety, harassment, legal, or security concerns may require escalation despite confidentiality expectations.

## Verification
Review agreed actions, feedback, boundary handling, and evidence of increasing independent capability.
