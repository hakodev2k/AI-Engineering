# Empiricism Rules

## Purpose
Ensure decisions are based on transparency, inspection, and adaptation rather than optimism or authority.

## MUST
- Make material delivery, quality, risk, and outcome evidence visible before decisions.
- Inspect actual results at useful intervals and adapt plans when evidence changes.
- Label forecasts, assumptions, and facts distinctly.

## MUST NOT
- Present estimates or plans as guarantees.
- Suppress unfavorable evidence to preserve a target narrative.

## SHOULD
Use the smallest reliable evidence set that supports timely decisions.

## Exceptions
Urgent decisions may use incomplete evidence when uncertainty and downside are explicit.

## Verification
Inspect dashboards, reviews, decision records, and retrospectives for evidence-driven adaptation.
