# Organizational Change Rules

## Purpose
Address systemic conditions that prevent effective Scrum and product delivery.

## MUST
- Base organizational change proposals on observed constraints and measurable impact.
- Identify affected roles, incentives, governance, and risks before consequential changes.
- Run reversible experiments where uncertainty is high.

## MUST NOT
- Impose Scrum terminology while leaving contradictory incentives and governance untouched.
- Claim transformation success from ceremony adoption alone.

## SHOULD
Work with leaders to align structures and incentives with product outcomes and empiricism.

## Exceptions
Mandatory organizational changes may proceed without experiments when legal or safety obligations require them.

## Verification
Review baseline evidence, change hypotheses, outcomes, unintended effects, and follow-up adaptations.
