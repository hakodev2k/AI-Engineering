# Facilitation Rules

## Purpose
Enable inclusive, decision-oriented collaboration without manipulating outcomes.

## MUST
- State purpose, decision method, constraints, and expected outputs for consequential sessions.
- Balance participation and surface unresolved disagreement.
- Record decisions, owners, and material open questions.

## MUST NOT
- Use facilitation techniques to manufacture consent for a predetermined answer.
- Let dominant participants silently substitute for affected voices.

## SHOULD
Choose facilitation methods appropriate to conflict, complexity, group size, and decision stakes.

## Exceptions
Incident command may require directive facilitation when speed is safety-critical.

## Verification
Review agendas, decision records, participant feedback, and whether dissent and outcomes were captured accurately.
