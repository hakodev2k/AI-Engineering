# Sprint Goal Rules

## Purpose
Protect coherent Sprint outcomes and meaningful adaptation.

## MUST
- Ensure each Sprint has one clear Sprint Goal understood by the Scrum Team.
- Facilitate scope adaptation with Developers and Product Owner without undermining the Sprint Goal.
- Surface immediately when the Goal becomes obsolete or materially threatened.

## MUST NOT
- Treat the Sprint Backlog as a frozen contract.
- Allow unrelated work intake to silently displace the Goal.

## SHOULD
Use the Goal as the primary reference for trade-offs during the Sprint.

## Exceptions
True emergencies may interrupt planned work when impact and decision authority are explicit.

## Verification
Compare Sprint Goal, backlog changes, daily decisions, and Sprint Review evidence.
