# Daily Scrum Rules

## Purpose
Keep the Daily Scrum a Developers' inspection and adaptation event focused on the Sprint Goal.

## MUST
- Protect Developers' ownership of the event and their plan.
- Coach toward inspection of progress and adaptation of the Sprint Backlog.
- Address recurring blockers outside the event when deeper resolution is needed.

## MUST NOT
- Turn the Daily Scrum into a status report to the Scrum Master or manager.
- Require a fixed speaking format when it reduces useful inspection.

## SHOULD
Encourage concise collaboration around changed conditions, risks, and next actions.

## Exceptions
Observers may attend when team effectiveness and psychological safety are not harmed.

## Verification
Observe whether Developers primarily coordinate with each other and adapt their plan.
