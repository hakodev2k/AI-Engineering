# Regulatory and Compliance Rules

## Purpose
Ensure model-risk controls account for applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and internal policy obligations.

## Scope
Applies to AI uses subject to jurisdictional requirements, sector rules, contractual controls, records obligations, or internal governance standards.

## MUST
- Material model uses MUST identify applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and policy obligations before production approval.
- Compliance requirements MUST be translated into observable controls, evidence requirements, and accountable owners where practical.
- Changes in deployment geography, population, decision purpose, or data use MUST trigger reassessment when they can change obligations.
- Required records MUST be retained with integrity and traceability for the mandated period.
- Unresolved compliance blockers MUST be escalated to an authorized human before release.

## MUST NOT
- Engineering teams MUST NOT interpret uncertainty as permission to ignore a potentially applicable high-impact obligation.
- Compliance claims MUST NOT rely solely on vendor marketing or unsupported model self-assessment.

## SHOULD
- Reviews SHOULD involve qualified legal, privacy, security, or compliance specialists when interpretation materially affects risk.
- Control mappings SHOULD be maintained so evidence can be reused across audits without weakening requirements.

## Exceptions
Any approved deviation must document the governing requirement, interpretation, reason, compensating controls, residual risk, owner, and approval authority.

## Verification
Inspect obligation registers, control mappings, approval records, retained evidence, audit findings, and change-review records.
