# Skill: Visual, Motion & Media Accessibility

**Purpose:** verify perceivability under low vision, color-vision differences, zoom/reflow, motion sensitivity, and non-audio/non-visual media use.

**Procedure:** check text/non-text contrast; test 200%+ zoom and narrow reflow; verify information is not encoded by color alone; inspect focus/hover states; test text spacing resilience; identify flashing/motion/auto-play; verify reduced-motion behavior; validate captions, transcripts, audio description or equivalent alternatives when required.

**Decision rules:** preserve meaning and operation before visual fidelity; exceptions require documented human approval and evidence.

**Output:** findings tied to component/state/viewport plus measurable expected result.

**Quality gate:** critical content remains readable, distinguishable, operable and understandable under supported adaptations.

**Common mistakes:** clipped zoom content, low-contrast disabled-but-actionable states, placeholder-only labels, motion without pause/reduction, captions that omit meaningful speech or sounds.
