# Process Analysis

## Purpose
Understand an as-is process and design a defensible to-be process with clear actors, states, handoffs, controls, and failure paths.

## Trigger
Workflow redesign, automation request, repeated operational delay, inconsistent handling, or unclear ownership.

## Inputs
Process objective, actors, current steps, systems, artifacts, timings, exceptions, controls, pain points.

## Procedure
1. Define process start/end and customer outcome.
2. Enumerate actors and system boundaries.
3. Model as-is steps, inputs, outputs, decisions, waits, queues, rework, and exceptions.
4. Mark evidence for each observed step versus assumed step.
5. Quantify delay, handoff, error, duplication, and control gaps where data exists.
6. Identify root causes rather than treating every symptom as a requirement.
7. Propose to-be options with trade-offs, dependencies, controls, and rollback considerations.
8. Validate exception handling and ownership at every handoff.
9. Produce agreed process deltas and implementation implications.

## Outputs
As-is map, to-be map, pain-point register, gap list, handoff matrix, control list, assumptions.

## Quality criteria
No orphan step, no undefined actor, no hidden decision, and every exception has an owner or escalation path.

## Stop conditions
Stop when process boundaries, ownership, major exceptions, and proposed deltas are reviewable by stakeholders.
