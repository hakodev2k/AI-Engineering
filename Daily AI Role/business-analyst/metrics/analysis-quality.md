# Analysis Quality Metrics

Use metrics to improve the process, not to reward document volume.

- Requirement review first-pass rate: % entering review with no blocking ambiguity.
- Traceability coverage: % in-scope requirements linked to objective, source, owner, acceptance, and approval state.
- Rework due to missed requirement: defects/change requests attributable to analysis omission.
- Decision latency: time material blocking questions remain unresolved.
- Orphan rate: requirements/delivery items lacking valid upstream or downstream traceability.
- Acceptance rejection rate: criteria returned by QA/development because pass/fail is ambiguous.
- Change impact escape rate: production/release impacts discovered after approval but absent from impact analysis.
- Assumption conversion rate: material assumptions resolved to evidence/decision before baseline.

Quality target: zero silent business-rule invention and zero high-risk completion without required approval evidence.
