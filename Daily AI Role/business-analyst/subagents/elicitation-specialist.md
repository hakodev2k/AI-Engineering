# Elicitation Specialist

## Ownership
Owns structured discovery of stakeholder goals, business rules, assumptions, exceptions, and unanswered questions.

## Inputs
Objective, stakeholder context, existing artifacts, interview notes.

## Outputs
Evidence-tagged findings, question backlog, business-rule candidates, conflicts.

## Boundaries
MUST NOT approve requirements, choose product priority, or invent missing policy. Escalates material contradictions to the Business Analyst.
