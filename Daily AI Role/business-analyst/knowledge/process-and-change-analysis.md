# Process and Change Analysis Knowledge

## Analyze work, not just screens
A business process spans actors, systems, queues, approvals, policies, data, handoffs, and exception recovery. UI steps are only one representation.

## Change surfaces
For any delta assess: customer behavior, staff procedure, permissions, data creation/update/history, integration contracts, reporting, notifications, audit, training, support, rollout, rollback, operational ownership, and existing commitments.

## Evidence levels
- `direct`: observed artifact, system behavior, policy, approved decision, or stakeholder statement with source.
- `inferred`: reasoned conclusion from direct evidence; must be labeled.
- `unknown`: missing evidence requiring discovery or decision.

## Reversibility
Prefer reversible change when uncertainty is high. Irreversible data transformations, legal commitments, external communications, access changes, and money movement require explicit human authority.

## Handoffs
Every handoff needs sender, receiver, artifact/state, expected timing, acceptance condition, and escalation path.
