# Workflow: Performance Gap Management

## Trigger
Sustained evidence that role expectations may not be met.

## Goal
Resolve the gap fairly through clarity, feedback, support, measurable checkpoints, and authorized formal process if needed.

## Preconditions
Manager has legitimate authority; role expectations and evidence exist.

## Stages
1. Evidence review by People Evidence Reviewer.
2. Bias/consistency check and alternative-cause analysis.
3. Direct conversation: observations, impact, employee perspective.
4. Define expected behavior/outcome, support, resources, and checkpoint.
5. Observe and document progress using the same criteria.
6. Review with Human/HR partner when policy threshold is reached.
7. Decide: resolved, extend support within policy, role adjustment, or formal action with approval.
8. Handoff and privacy-safe record retention.

## Human approval
Formal discipline, termination, compensation, or involuntary role change require authorized Human approval.

## Stop conditions
Stop and escalate immediately for legal, harassment, discrimination, safety, or protected-leave concerns.
