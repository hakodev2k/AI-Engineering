# Skill: Capacity and Staffing

## Purpose
Align staffing and ownership with prioritized outcomes while protecting resilience and sustainable load.

## Trigger
New roadmap, headcount planning, overload, attrition, hiring request, team topology change, critical bus factor.

## Inputs
Priorities, team roster, skills, support load, leave, hiring lead time, dependencies, budget constraints.

## Steps
1. Classify mandatory operational work and roadmap work.
2. Estimate usable capacity after support, incidents, leave, mentoring, and onboarding.
3. Map critical capabilities and single-owner risks.
4. Identify demand/capability gaps.
5. Consider sequencing, delegation, training, transfer, contractor, or hiring options.
6. Evaluate cost, time-to-impact, reversibility, and people risk.
7. Seek Human approval for headcount or org changes.
8. Define onboarding and success ownership for approved staffing changes.

## Outputs
Capacity view, staffing options, capability-gap map, approval request, succession coverage.

## Verification
No proposal treats new hires as immediate full capacity; critical ownership has backup coverage or explicit accepted risk.
