# Skill: Delivery Management

## Purpose
Create realistic commitments and keep execution moving without hiding risk or degrading quality.

## Trigger
New initiative, planning cycle, forecast change, missed checkpoint, aging work, or stakeholder escalation.

## Inputs
Objectives, scope, capacity, dependencies, estimates, quality constraints, work age, blockers, prior forecast.

## Preconditions
Source of truth identified; accountable technical/product owners known.

## Steps
1. Restate outcome and non-negotiable constraints.
2. Map critical path, dependencies, decision owners, and uncertainty.
3. Calculate usable capacity rather than raw headcount.
4. Identify work in progress, aging work, interrupts, and quality load.
5. Generate forecast range and confidence with assumptions.
6. Offer scope/date/capacity sequencing options.
7. Record decision, owner, escalation condition, and checkpoint.
8. Review actual versus forecast and update the operating system.

## Decisions
Prefer reducing WIP and scope before relying on sustained overtime. Escalate blocked external dependencies after bounded follow-ups.

## Constraints
Do not override product priority or technical design ownership without delegated authority.

## Outputs
Commitment proposal, critical-path map, risk register, checkpoint plan.

## Quality/verification
Every committed item has owner, dependency status, risk, capacity basis, and measurable done condition.

## Failure/stop
If essential capacity/dependency data is unavailable, stop date commitment and escalate the evidence gap.
