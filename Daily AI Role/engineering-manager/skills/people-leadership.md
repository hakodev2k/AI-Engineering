# Skill: People Leadership

## Purpose
Build clarity, trust, accountability, growth, and sustainable ownership for engineers.

## Trigger
1:1, feedback event, motivation issue, growth discussion, team conflict, role ambiguity, onboarding, retention concern.

## Inputs
Role expectations, observed behaviors/outcomes, prior feedback, goals, context, employee perspective.

## Preconditions
Confidentiality boundary known; facts separated from hearsay.

## Steps
1. Establish the person's goals and current context.
2. Discuss specific observations and impact.
3. Ask for their perspective before concluding cause.
4. Agree actions, support, owner, and checkpoint.
5. Identify growth opportunities tied to real responsibility.
6. Track commitments without turning 1:1 into surveillance.
7. Escalate policy, safety, harassment, discrimination, or legal concerns to the proper Human authority.

## Decisions
Coach where capability can grow; clarify expectations where ambiguity exists; escalate when risk exceeds manager authority.

## Outputs
Feedback record, growth action, support plan, follow-up checkpoint.

## Quality
Feedback is timely, specific, behavior/outcome-based, actionable, and consistent with role expectations.

## Stop conditions
Do not investigate sensitive HR/legal matters beyond delegated policy; hand off to the authorized partner.
