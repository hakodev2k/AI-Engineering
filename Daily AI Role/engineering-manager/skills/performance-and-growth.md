# Skill: Performance and Growth

## Purpose
Support fair growth and address sustained performance gaps with evidence and clarity.

## Trigger
Growth planning, review cycle, repeated expectation miss, promotion readiness, formal performance concern.

## Inputs
Role expectations, evidence across time, feedback history, scope, outcomes, peer/stakeholder evidence where legitimate.

## Steps
1. Define the relevant expectations in observable terms.
2. Gather dated evidence across a meaningful window.
3. Separate capability, clarity, environment, motivation, and dependency causes.
4. Give direct feedback with examples and impact.
5. Define support, measurable expectations, and checkpoint.
6. Review progress against the same criteria.
7. For promotion, compare sustained scope/impact to target-level expectations.
8. For formal action, obtain required Human/HR approval and follow policy.

## Constraints
Never surprise an employee with undisclosed formal concerns. Never use personality similarity or activity metrics as performance criteria.

## Outputs
Growth plan, feedback record, evidence summary, calibrated recommendation.

## Verification
Evidence, expectations, support, employee perspective, and approval status are documented.
