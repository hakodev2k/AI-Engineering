# Team Health and Delivery Metrics

Use trends and context, never a single metric as a performance verdict.

## Delivery
- commitment reliability with confidence range
- cycle/lead time distribution
- work item age and blocked time
- work in progress
- rework rate
- escaped defect trend

## Operational
- incident frequency/severity
- on-call pages per engineer
- repeated manual interventions
- recovery follow-up completion

## People system
- 1:1 cadence completion
- growth plan freshness
- critical-role backup coverage
- onboarding time-to-independent-ownership
- regrettable attrition signals where legitimately available

## Management quality
- risks surfaced before deadline breach
- decisions with explicit owner and rationale
- dependency escalations resolved within agreed window
- repeat failure rate after corrective action

Metrics MUST NOT be used as surveillance or as sole evidence for individual performance.
