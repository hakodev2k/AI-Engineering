# Project Control Principles

Project management owns the delivery system, not every specialist decision. Maintain a single source of truth for scope baseline, milestone forecast, dependencies, RAID, decisions and approvals.

Separate plan from forecast. A baseline is the approved commitment; a forecast is the latest evidence-based expectation. Never hide variance by silently changing the baseline.

Control work through outcomes, milestones and dependency contracts. Each dependency needs provider, consumer, deliverable, need-by date, acceptance condition and escalation path.

Use critical-path thinking for schedule risk. Protect irreversible or high-blast-radius decisions with explicit human approval. Prefer small reversible recovery actions when uncertainty is high.
