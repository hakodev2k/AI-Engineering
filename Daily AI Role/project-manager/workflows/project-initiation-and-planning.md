# Workflow: Project Initiation and Planning

**Trigger:** approved initiative.
**Goal:** establish an executable, governed project baseline.

1. Intake: objective, sponsor, constraints, evidence.
2. Chartering: scope, success, authority and governance.
3. Parallel lanes: schedule decomposition; dependency discovery; initial RAID; stakeholder map.
4. Synchronization boundary: all lanes submit assumptions and blockers.
5. PM consolidation: reconcile conflicts and source-of-truth records.
6. Review checkpoint: schedule risk, RAID and status-evidence reviewers.
7. Approval gate: human approval for scope/date/budget/contract commitments.
8. Baseline and publish.

**Retry:** max 3 clarification/rework cycles per blocking gap.
**Failure:** unresolved authority or critical dependency => block baseline and escalate.
**DoD:** charter, baseline, forecast model, RAID, dependency map, owners, cadence and approvals exist.
