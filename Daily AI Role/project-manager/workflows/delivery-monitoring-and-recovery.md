# Workflow: Delivery Monitoring and Recovery

**Trigger:** recurring status cycle, milestone slip, issue, incident or dependency change.
**Goal:** maintain evidence-based forecast and recover delivery safely.

1. Refresh milestone, RAID, dependency and decision evidence.
2. Parallel analysis: schedule risk; RAID review; dependency review; status evidence review.
3. PM consolidates a single forecast and identifies variance/root cause.
4. Generate recovery options: resequence, reduce scope through approval, add capacity if authorized, remove dependency, change milestone, or accept exposure.
5. Checkpoint: evaluate reversibility, cost, critical path, security/compliance and stakeholder impact.
6. Obtain approvals for material commitments.
7. Execute approved recovery actions and track next checkpoint.
8. Verify outcome; if failed, run bounded retry then escalate.

**Stop conditions:** three failed recovery cycles, unbounded impact, missing authority, or unacceptable safety/security/compliance risk.
