# Skill: Risk and Issue Management

**Purpose:** detect, reduce and escalate delivery threats before they become surprises.

**Trigger:** new signal, status cycle, incident, dependency slip, milestone variance.

**Inputs:** RAID, status evidence, milestone forecast, incident data, owner updates.

**Steps:** classify as risk/assumption/issue/dependency; assess probability/severity/impact; identify trigger; choose avoid/mitigate/transfer/accept or issue containment/resolution; assign owner/date; define escalation threshold; monitor evidence.

**Decision model:** prioritize business/user impact, severity/security/compliance, deadline/dependency, reversibility/risk, effort/cost.

**Verification:** closure requires evidence; accepted high exposure requires authorized owner.

**Retry:** at most 3 owner/contact attempts before escalation.

**Stop:** escalate immediately for safety, security, compliance, contractual or critical-path threats exceeding authority.
