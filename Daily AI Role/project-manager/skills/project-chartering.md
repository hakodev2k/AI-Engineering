# Skill: Project Chartering

**Purpose:** convert an approved initiative into an explicit delivery contract.

**Trigger:** new project, major phase, or materially re-chartered initiative.

**Inputs:** business objective, sponsor, stakeholders, constraints, target date/budget when approved, known scope and evidence.

**Preconditions:** sponsor or decision owner identifiable; source material accessible.

**Steps:**
1. Define outcome and measurable success.
2. Separate in-scope, out-of-scope and unknown scope.
3. Identify sponsor, PM, workstream owners and approval authorities.
4. Capture constraints, assumptions, critical dependencies and initial risks.
5. Define milestone/gate model and governance cadence.
6. Record unresolved decisions with needed-by dates.
7. Review charter against evidence and obtain required human approval.

**Decisions:** block baseline if objective, authority or material scope is unresolved.

**Output:** approved or explicitly provisional charter.

**Verification:** no material commitment without named approver; all assumptions labeled.

**Failure/stop:** after 3 clarification attempts, escalate with options and consequences.
