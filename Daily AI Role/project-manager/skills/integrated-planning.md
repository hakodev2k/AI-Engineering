# Skill: Integrated Planning

**Purpose:** create one executable plan across workstreams.

**Trigger:** charter approved, major replan, phase transition.

**Inputs:** charter, work breakdown, estimates, capacity assumptions, dependency contracts, risk register.

**Steps:**
1. Decompose outcomes into milestones and acceptance events.
2. Map predecessors, decision gates, external dependencies and handoffs.
3. Separate committed dates from estimates and hypotheses.
4. Identify critical/near-critical paths and float.
5. Reconcile resource conflicts and sequencing constraints.
6. Run independent schedule, dependency and risk reviews in parallel.
7. Consolidate into baseline proposal and forecast model.
8. Obtain approval for material commitment.

**Quality:** every milestone has owner, evidence condition and dependency context.

**Output:** baseline plan, forecast, dependency map, RAID and governance cadence.

**Stop:** do not baseline a plan with unowned critical dependencies or fabricated capacity.
