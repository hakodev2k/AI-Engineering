# Workflow: Cost Anomaly Response

## Trigger
Budget/anomaly alert or unexpected invoice/usage movement.

## Flow
1. Validate signal and materiality.
2. Classify severity by active burn, forecast exposure, and business risk.
3. Decompose cost dimensions.
4. Correlate usage/deployment/business events.
5. If active leak, identify reversible containment.
6. Obtain approval before risky production action.
7. Contain and monitor service health.
8. Determine root cause.
9. Quantify actual impact and residual exposure.
10. Add prevention control and learning record.
11. Verify billing normalization before close.

## Escalation
Critical runaway spend, unknown owner, security event, or potential service-impacting containment escalates immediately.
