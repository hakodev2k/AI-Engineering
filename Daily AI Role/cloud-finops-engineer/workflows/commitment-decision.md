# Workflow: Commitment Decision

## Trigger
New commitment opportunity, renewal, expiration, or utilization concern.

## Stages
1. Define eligible spend and exclusions.
2. Review current commitments.
3. Build normalized usage baseline.
4. Model base/growth/contraction scenarios.
5. Compare term/payment/coverage alternatives.
6. Independent commitment review.
7. Consolidate expected value, downside, flexibility, and confidence.
8. Finance/Procurement human approval.
9. Execute purchase outside this role only through authorized process.
10. Track utilization/coverage and review before renewal.

## Failure handling
If usage migrates or collapses, quantify stranded exposure, identify exchange/transfer/exit options, record root cause, and update future commitment policy.
