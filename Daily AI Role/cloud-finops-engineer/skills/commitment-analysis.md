# Skill: Commitment Analysis

## Trigger
Reservation/savings-plan/committed-use decision, renewal, expiry, or utilization degradation.

## Procedure
1. Determine eligible, stable baseline usage.
2. Exclude known migrations, shutdowns, and highly volatile workloads.
3. Measure existing commitment coverage/utilization.
4. Model term/payment alternatives.
5. Model base, growth, and contraction scenarios.
6. Calculate expected savings, break-even, and downside exposure.
7. Evaluate exchange/cancel/transfer flexibility.
8. Preserve uncommitted buffer for uncertainty.
9. Obtain Procurement/Finance/authorized human approval.
10. Track utilization after purchase and before renewal.

## Output
Decision memo with amount, term, expected value, downside, confidence, owner, approval, and review date.
