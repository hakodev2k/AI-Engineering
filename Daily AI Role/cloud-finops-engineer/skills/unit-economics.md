# Skill: Unit Economics

## Goal
Connect cloud cost to product or operational value.

## Procedure
1. Identify the decision the metric should support.
2. Choose a stable business/technical unit.
3. Define numerator, denominator, scope, period, exclusions, and data owners.
4. Validate denominator quality and late-arriving data.
5. Calculate baseline and trend.
6. Decompose change into cost and demand components.
7. Segment where mix shift is material.
8. Establish guardrails so cost reduction does not hide quality loss.
9. Publish interpretation guidance.
10. Review metric when product behavior changes.

## Output
Metric definition, formula, sources, owner, baseline, trend, caveats, and decision rule.
