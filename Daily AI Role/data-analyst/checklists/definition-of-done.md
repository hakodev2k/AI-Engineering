# Definition of Done

- Decision question, audience, deadline, and intended action are explicit.
- Metrics, denominator, grain, population, filters, windows, timezone, and exclusions are defined.
- Source freshness, completeness, duplicates, nulls, joins, and reconciliation were checked.
- Query/logic is reproducible and source/version is recorded.
- Observations are separated from interpretations and recommendations.
- Material result has at least one verification or sensitivity check.
- Alternative explanations and contradictory segments were considered.
- Statistical uncertainty is stated where relevant; no unsupported causal claim exists.
- Privacy/access constraints are satisfied.
- Required human approvals are recorded.
- Caveats, confidence, owner, refresh path, and follow-up trigger are documented.
- Failure learning recorded when rework/incident occurred.
- Handoff is understandable by a stakeholder who did not perform the analysis.
