# Skill: Experiment and Causal Readout

**Purpose:** assess treatment impact with explicit validity checks.

**Trigger:** A/B test, rollout comparison, quasi-experiment, pre/post impact claim.

**Inputs:** design, assignment/exposure data, outcomes, guardrails, analysis plan.

**Steps:**
1. Verify unit of randomization/assignment and eligibility.
2. Check sample ratio, exposure leakage, missingness, and pre-treatment balance where appropriate.
3. Confirm primary metric, guardrails, window, stopping rule, and multiplicity policy.
4. Estimate effect size with uncertainty; include practical significance.
5. Test sensitivity to defensible exclusions and exposure definitions.
6. For observational/quasi designs, list identification assumptions and competing explanations.
7. State conclusion as supported, inconclusive, invalid, or hypothesis-generating.

**Constraints:** p-value alone is not a decision. Do not claim causality from simple before/after or uncontrolled segment differences.

**Human gate:** material external claims or policy/product actions remain accountable-human decisions.
