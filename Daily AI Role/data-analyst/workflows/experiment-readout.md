# Workflow: Experiment Readout

**Trigger:** experiment reaches planned analysis point or accountable human requests a bounded interim read.

1. Load pre-registered/design context and decision rule.
2. Validate assignment, exposure, sample ratio, missingness, guardrails, and window.
3. Parallelize data-quality review and statistical review.
4. If integrity fails, classify invalid/inconclusive before outcome storytelling.
5. Estimate primary effect and uncertainty; review practical significance.
6. Run pre-specified segments and sensitivity checks; mark exploratory slices.
7. Synthesize with strongest alternative explanation.
8. Get human approval for material external/executive claim or irreversible action.
9. Store readout and follow-up learning plan.

No repeated automated peeking loop; follow the defined stopping policy.
