# Growth Quality Metrics
Track a compact stack of outcome and process metrics:
- Qualified acquisition volume and marginal CAC by channel/segment.
- Activation rate and median time-to-value.
- D7/D30 or domain-appropriate retained-value rate by cohort.
- Revenue/value per activated and retained user; payback where meaningful.
- Experiment velocity only with validity rate and decision usefulness.
- Instrumentation health: missing/duplicate event rate, freshness, schema drift.
- Lifecycle guardrails: unsubscribe, complaint, suppression failure, support load.
- Learning reuse: percentage of new experiments citing prior evidence.
Never reward experiment count or click-through rate in isolation.
