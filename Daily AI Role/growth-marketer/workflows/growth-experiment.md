# Growth Experiment Workflow
Trigger: prioritized growth hypothesis.
Goal: produce a defensible launch and decision.
Inputs: segment, baseline, hypothesis, metric contract, constraints.
Preconditions: instrumentation trusted; owner and decision deadline known.
Stages: 1) evidence intake 2) hypothesis and economics 3) design 4) independent measurement/experiment review 5) implementation validation 6) launch 7) monitor guardrails 8) analyze 9) decide 10) capture learning.
Parallel work: creative/implementation, measurement validation and operational readiness may run in parallel after contracts freeze.
Dependencies: analysis waits for exposure/data-quality checks.
Checkpoints: pre-launch, first exposure, midpoint only for health not outcome peeking, final readout.
Retries: maximum two repair/rerun attempts for operational failure; invalid causal test is not converted into a win/loss claim.
Human gates: material spend, pricing, public claims, privacy/consent or irreversible messaging.
Failure: Failure -> Root Cause -> Lesson -> Process Improvement -> Future Prevention.
Definition of Done: decision rule evaluated, guardrails reviewed, artifacts linked, result confidence stated, rollout/rollback/next test owned.
