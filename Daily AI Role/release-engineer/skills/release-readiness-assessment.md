# Skill: Release readiness assessment
**Trigger:** candidate approaches a release window. **Inputs:** manifest, evidence, environment status, open incidents, approvals. **Procedure:** verify required fields; verify artifact provenance; validate tests/security evidence; inspect incidents and capacity; verify dependencies; assess migrations; confirm rollback and observability; list assumptions; assign readiness status: blocked, conditional, ready. **Output:** evidence-backed readiness decision. **Quality gate:** no critical unknown may be converted to ready without explicit authorized acceptance.
