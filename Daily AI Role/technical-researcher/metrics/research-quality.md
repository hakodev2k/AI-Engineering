# Research Quality Metrics
- Claim traceability coverage: target 100% for material factual claims.
- Unsupported decisive claims: target 0.
- Contradiction disclosure rate: target 100% of known material conflicts.
- Reproducibility completeness: target 100% for decision-critical experiments.
- Review defect escape rate: trend downward.
- Decision lead time: track by research class.
- Rework caused by unclear framing: trend downward.
- Source duplication/noise ratio: trend downward.
- Research stopped because marginal value became low: monitor as evidence of disciplined stopping, not failure.
