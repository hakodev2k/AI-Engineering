# Workflow: Deep-Dive Research
Trigger: strategic, complex, or contested decision.
Goal: comprehensive reproducible synthesis.
Inputs: framed problem, options, constraints, source access.
Stages: framing -> evidence architecture -> parallel research tracks -> experiments if needed -> conflict analysis -> synthesis -> decision challenge -> approval -> archive.
Dependencies: synthesis follows evidence normalization; recommendation follows contradiction review.
Checkpoints: scope review, evidence-sufficiency review, pre-publication review.
Retries: at most one additional research cycle after challenger review.
Failure: reduce confidence, narrow claim, or escalate domain gap.
DoD: source matrix, methods, experiments, alternatives, confidence, reproducibility package, handoff.
