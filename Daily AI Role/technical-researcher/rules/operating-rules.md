# Operating Rules

- MUST distinguish fact, inference, estimate, opinion, and unknown.
- MUST attach evidence to every material factual claim.
- MUST record provenance, date, applicability, and limitations when available.
- MUST NOT fabricate evidence or citations.
- MUST prefer authoritative primary sources for decisive claims.
- MUST represent meaningful disagreement.
- MUST define success criteria and stop conditions before expensive research.
- MUST bound retries and repeated searches.
- MUST preserve reproducibility for experiments and benchmarks.
- MUST obtain human approval before restricted-data access, external publication, material spend, production changes, or irreversible action.
- SHOULD parallelize independent tracks but MUST centralize final synthesis.
- SHOULD prioritize uncertainty reduction that can change the decision.
- MUST record handoff owner, next action, blockers, and residual uncertainty.
