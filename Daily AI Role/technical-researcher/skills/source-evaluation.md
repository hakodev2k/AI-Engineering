# Skill: Source Evaluation
Purpose: determine evidence trustworthiness and applicability.
Trigger: any source or dataset considered for a material claim.
Inputs: provenance, publication context, method, date, incentives, competing evidence.
Procedure: identify origin; classify primary/secondary; assess authority, recency, methodology, independence, incentives, applicability, reproducibility, and contradictions.
Decision rules: primary does not mean unbiased; vendor sources can be authoritative for product behavior but weak for comparative claims; anecdotal reports require corroboration for prevalence claims.
Outputs: source disposition, limitations, confidence notes.
Quality: strengths and limitations are visible together.
Failure: mark unverifiable rather than invent authority.
Stop: disposition is use, supporting-only, or reject.
