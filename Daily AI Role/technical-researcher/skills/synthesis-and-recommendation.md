# Skill: Synthesis and Recommendation
Purpose: convert evidence into a decision-ready conclusion.
Trigger: evidence collection reaches stop condition.
Inputs: claim matrix, source assessments, experiments, contradictions, constraints.
Procedure: normalize claims; separate fact from interpretation; compare options; identify convergence/conflict; quantify uncertainty; test against constraints; document alternatives and reversal path; assign confidence.
Decision rules: recommendation strength cannot exceed evidence strength; equivalent options should be reported as equivalent; decision-critical uncertainty triggers targeted research rather than false certainty.
Outputs: findings, recommendation, alternatives, risks, confidence, unknowns.
Quality: decisive statements map to evidence.
Retry: at most one targeted evidence cycle.
Stop: actionable recommendation or explicit block by unresolved uncertainty.
