# Skill: Evidence Planning
Purpose: map claims to minimum sufficient evidence.
Trigger: framed research question.
Inputs: claims, decision risk, source access, time/cost limits.
Procedure: classify claim; choose primary/secondary/experimental evidence; rank sources by authority, independence, recency, method, applicability; define corroboration; assign tracks; identify gaps.
Decision rules: irreversible/high-impact decisions require stronger corroboration; contested claims require independent sources; operational claims prefer measurement.
Outputs: evidence matrix and collection plan.
Quality: every claim has an evidence path or explicit cannot-verify path.
Failure: reduce scope or escalate access gap.
Stop: marginal evidence no longer changes confidence materially.
