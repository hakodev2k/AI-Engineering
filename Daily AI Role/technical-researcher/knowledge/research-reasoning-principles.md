# Research Reasoning Principles

1. Decision value matters more than source volume.
2. Evidence quality is multidimensional: authority, independence, recency, method, and applicability.
3. Correlation, causation, mechanism, and anecdote are different evidence classes.
4. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence unless the observation process supports that inference.
5. Contradictory evidence is information, not noise to delete.
6. Confidence should track evidence strength, not writing fluency.
7. Reproducibility is both a quality feature and future-maintenance mechanism.
8. Benchmark validity depends on representative workload and controlled variables.
9. Search saturation occurs when new sources stop changing coverage or confidence.
10. Stop when additional research has low expected decision value.
