# Workflow: Backlog Reprioritization
**Trigger:** incident, deadline change, new evidence, dependency movement, strategic change or capacity constraint.
**Inputs:** ordered backlog and changed facts.
**Stages:** Detect change -> Identify affected items -> Re-score qualitatively -> Review opportunity cost -> Dependency synchronization -> Product Owner merge -> Communicate ordering.
**Parallel work:** evidence refresh and dependency review may run concurrently; no two agents write final order.
**Conflict rule:** Product Owner owns final ordering; contractual/legal authority remains with humans.
**Checkpoint:** all top-order changes have explicit cause and affected stakeholders notified.
**Retry:** maximum two rounds for disputed ordering.
**DoD:** one source of truth reflects new order, rationale and next revisit trigger.
