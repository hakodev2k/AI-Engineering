# Product Delivery Health
Track trends, not isolated numbers:
- % backlog items with explicit outcome and target user.
- % Ready items with testable acceptance criteria and dependency review.
- Decision latency for blocked/high-impact items.
- Scope-change rate after Ready and after implementation starts.
- Rework caused by requirement ambiguity.
- Release acceptance pass rate.
- % material releases with defined success metric and review date.
- Time from release to validated learning.
- Outcome attainment vs planned output completion.
- Escaped product-rule defects and customer-impacting misunderstandings; target trend downward.
Metrics are diagnostic; do not game them as individual performance targets.
