# Skill: Release Scope and Outcome Review
**Trigger:** release planning, scope cut, phased rollout or post-release review.
**Inputs:** accepted items, dependencies, risk, rollout capability, telemetry, support readiness, target outcome.
**Procedure:** 1) define minimum coherent release outcome; 2) identify must-have vs deferrable scope; 3) review dependency closure; 4) choose rollout/flag/rollback strategy; 5) define success and guardrail metrics; 6) name measurement owner and review date; 7) approve scope; 8) after release compare actuals with hypothesis and decide ramp, hold, rollback or iterate.
**Output:** release decision and measurement plan.
**Quality:** scope reduction must preserve user coherence and contractual obligations.
**Human approval:** irreversible, contractual, pricing or regulatory changes.
**Stop:** if critical dependency/evidence is missing, do not release.
