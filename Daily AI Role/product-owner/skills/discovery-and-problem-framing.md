# Skill: Discovery and Problem Framing
**Trigger:** request, complaint, opportunity, strategic goal or weakly defined feature idea.
**Inputs:** user signals, business context, telemetry, research, constraints, stakeholder claims.
**Preconditions:** identify target user and decision owner.
**Procedure:** 1) restate observed problem; 2) separate facts from assumptions; 3) define desired user/business outcome; 4) map current behavior; 5) list solution-independent constraints; 6) rank risky assumptions; 7) choose cheapest evidence for the riskiest assumption; 8) timebox discovery; 9) update recommendation.
**Parallelism:** user evidence, analytics and operational constraints may be investigated in parallel; converge at evidence checkpoint.
**Output:** problem brief, assumptions, evidence, options, recommendation and confidence.
**Quality:** no solution is labeled required unless the requirement truly constrains implementation.
**Failure:** insufficient evidence -> propose reversible experiment or escalate decision cost.
**Stop:** when evidence supports the next reversible decision.
