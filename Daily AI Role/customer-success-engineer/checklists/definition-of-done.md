# Definition of Done Checklist

A Customer Success Engineer task is complete only when applicable items are satisfied.

## Objective and scope
- [ ] Original customer objective is explicit.
- [ ] Scope and non-scope are clear.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, hypotheses, decisions, and unknowns are separated.

## Delivery
- [ ] Required deliverable exists.
- [ ] Owners and dates are assigned for remaining non-blocking follow-up.
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are resolved or explicitly handed off.

## Quality and evidence
- [ ] Evidence supports the claimed status.
- [ ] Success criteria were tested or measured.
- [ ] Risks and residual risks are documented.
- [ ] Customer-facing statements contain no unsupported commitments.
- [ ] Sensitive information was handled according to policy.

## Review
- [ ] Role-specific review completed.
- [ ] Required specialist review completed for security, production, product, or commercial concerns.
- [ ] Required human approvals are recorded.

## Handoff and closure
- [ ] Receiving owner accepted any handoff.
- [ ] Next checkpoint is defined when follow-up remains.
- [ ] No blocking ambiguity remains.
- [ ] Work performed and work verified are both recorded.

Do not mark complete because a meeting occurred, a ticket closed, or an artifact was produced without outcome evidence.
