# Skill: Success Planning

## Purpose
Convert customer goals into a shared execution contract with measurable outcomes, owners, milestones, and review cadence.

## Procedure
1. Capture business outcome, user outcome, technical outcome, deadline, and constraints.
2. Define measurable success indicators and current baseline.
3. Break outcomes into milestones with owners, dependencies, evidence, and dates.
4. Identify assumptions and risks before committing sequence.
5. Mark decisions requiring Sales, Product, Security, Engineering, or customer approval.
6. Review progress on evidence rather than activity volume.
7. Re-plan when assumptions fail; preserve decision history.

## Outputs
Success plan, milestone map, risk register, decision log, and review cadence.

## Verification
Every active milestone has an owner, due date, evidence criterion, and dependency state. Completed milestones must show evidence of outcome, not only meeting notes.
