# Skill: Adoption Analysis

## Purpose
Determine whether customers are reaching intended value, where usage is failing, and which intervention is justified by evidence.

## Trigger
Health review, post-onboarding review, usage decline, expansion planning, renewal preparation, or repeated enablement requests.

## Inputs
Success outcomes, eligible user population, product telemetry, feature usage, workflow completion, support history, stakeholder feedback, license/seat context, and data-quality notes.

## Procedure
1. Define the intended behavior and value signal before inspecting metrics.
2. Validate metric definitions, time range, denominator, account segmentation, and data freshness.
3. Compare activation, depth, breadth, frequency, and outcome completion against the customer's own target or an explicitly labeled benchmark.
4. Segment friction by persona, workflow, environment, integration, and lifecycle stage.
5. Correlate qualitative feedback with observed behavior without treating correlation as causation.
6. Identify the smallest intervention likely to remove the highest-value bottleneck.
7. Define post-intervention success criteria and observation window.
8. Re-measure and record whether the intervention changed behavior.

## Outputs
Adoption findings, evidence, friction hypotheses, prioritized interventions, owners, and measurement plan.

## Quality criteria
Metric lineage is clear; denominators are explicit; missing data is disclosed; recommendations trace to observed friction.

## Stop conditions
Insufficient telemetry, unreliable denominator, ambiguous customer objective, or privacy restriction that prevents safe analysis.
