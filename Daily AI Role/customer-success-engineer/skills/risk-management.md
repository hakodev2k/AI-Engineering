# Skill: Customer Risk Management

## Purpose
Detect, qualify, prioritize, and reduce risks that threaten customer value, implementation, adoption, trust, or continuity.

## Inputs
Health signals, milestones, unresolved incidents, stakeholder engagement, adoption trends, support history, technical debt, dependencies, deadlines, and explicit customer concerns.

## Procedure
1. Identify risk signal and supporting evidence.
2. Classify category: value, adoption, technical, stakeholder, security, delivery, dependency, commercial-support, or unknown.
3. Score impact, likelihood, urgency, reversibility, confidence, and dependency blocking.
4. State the risk as cause → event → consequence.
5. Choose mitigation, contingency, owner, due date, and verification signal.
6. Escalate high-impact low-confidence risks early rather than hiding uncertainty.
7. Reassess after intervention; close only when evidence shows exposure materially reduced.

## Outputs
Risk record, priority, mitigation, contingency, owner, checkpoint, and closure evidence.

## Failure handling
If mitigation depends on unavailable authority or another team, create a handoff with cost of delay and explicit decision request.
