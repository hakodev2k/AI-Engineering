# Customer Health Metrics

Use metrics as evidence, not as a substitute for customer context. Record source, freshness, denominator, and confidence.

## Value
- Outcome milestone completion rate.
- Time to first verified value.
- Customer-defined KPI progress where accessible and appropriate.

## Adoption
- Activation rate = activated eligible users / eligible users.
- Breadth = active users or teams / eligible users or teams.
- Depth = use of target workflows/features required for the success outcome.
- Frequency = relevant workflow executions over an agreed period.
- Workflow success rate = successful target workflow completions / attempts.

## Technical
- Open severity-weighted blockers.
- Integration success/error rate.
- Incident recurrence.
- Mean age of unresolved customer-blocking issues.

## Stakeholder
- Named champion present.
- Accountable technical owner present.
- Decision-maker access when required.
- Critical stakeholder change/risk.

## Delivery
- Milestones on time / due milestones.
- Blocking dependencies by age and owner.
- Approval latency for required decisions.

## Health interpretation
Do not collapse dimensions into a single score without retaining component evidence. A green adoption score does not override a critical security or production blocker.

## Review cadence
Review high-risk signals immediately; implementation metrics at milestone checkpoints; adoption/value metrics at a cadence long enough for behavior to change.
