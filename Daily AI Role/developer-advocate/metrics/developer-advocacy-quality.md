# Developer Advocacy Metrics
Measure outcomes rather than vanity reach alone.

Core measures:
- tutorial/sample clean-run success rate
- time to first successful outcome
- activation or feature adoption after enablement
- recurring setup/support failures per journey
- stale/broken asset rate
- issue-to-actionable-handoff rate
- feedback closure rate
- launch asset readiness before deadline
- demo failure rate
- percentage of high-risk claims independently reviewed

Views, followers, and attendance may be supporting metrics but must not substitute for developer success.
