# Demos, Samples, and Trust
A public demo is a product promise in practice. Keep dependencies pinned or explicitly ranged, remove secrets, document permissions, avoid hidden setup, and provide deterministic expected output.

Samples should favor clarity and safe defaults over cleverness. Never use production credentials, destructive defaults, broad permissions without explanation, or undocumented behavior. When a workaround is necessary, label it as a workaround and state its boundary.
