# Community Feedback and Signal
Not all feedback has equal evidentiary value. Classify by reproducibility, affected audience, frequency, severity, business/user impact, and confidence.

Strong signal combines a concrete journey, environment/version, expected vs actual behavior, reproduction evidence, and repeated occurrence. Weak signal should be recorded without being promoted to a product conclusion.

Route feedback as: defect, documentation gap, onboarding friction, missing capability, confusing contract, performance issue, policy limitation, or feature request. Avoid converting individual preference into roadmap priority without broader evidence.
