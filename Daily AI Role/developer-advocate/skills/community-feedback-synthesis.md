# Skill: Community Feedback Synthesis
**Trigger:** recurring issues, community discussions, event feedback, support themes.
**Inputs:** messages/issues, versions, reproduction details, user segment, frequency.
**Procedure:** deduplicate; classify; separate fact from sentiment; score severity, reach, reproducibility, and cost of delay; find representative evidence; identify owner; propose action; track closure.
**Outputs:** signal report and prioritized handoffs.
**Quality:** preserves minority severe issues without letting noisy popularity dominate.
**Verification:** owner acknowledges handoff and evidence is sufficient to act.
**Stop:** privacy-sensitive or confidential data must be sanitized/escalated.
