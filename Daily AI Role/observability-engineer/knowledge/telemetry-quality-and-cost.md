# Telemetry Quality and Cost
Unbounded identifiers such as user_id, request_id or raw URL in metric labels can create cardinality explosions. High-cardinality context belongs in traces/logs when necessary and policy-compliant. Sampling must preserve investigation value and be documented. Retention should follow operational and regulatory needs rather than habit. Estimate telemetry cost from event rate, payload size, dimensions, retention and query patterns. Prefer aggregation, filtering, tail/head sampling or tiered retention only when the decision value remains intact. Never reduce cost by silently removing critical evidence.
