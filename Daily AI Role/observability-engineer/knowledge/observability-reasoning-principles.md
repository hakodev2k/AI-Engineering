# Observability Reasoning Principles
Observability exists to support decisions, not maximize telemetry volume. Start from user impact and the question an operator must answer. Prefer a small set of semantically stable signals over many ambiguous fields. Correlation across request, service, deployment and business context is more valuable than isolated logs. Instrument boundaries and failure modes, not every line of code. Missing telemetry is itself a signal. Treat dashboards as decision interfaces and alerts as requests for human or automated action. Separate symptoms from causes and use traces/logs for explanation after metrics or user-impact signals identify a problem.
