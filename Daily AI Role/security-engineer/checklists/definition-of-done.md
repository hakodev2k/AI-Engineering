# Security Definition of Done

- [ ] Scope, owner, deadline, assets, entry points, and trust boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and decisions are separated.
- [ ] Threats/findings are linked to evidence and affected assets.
- [ ] Authentication and authorization are reviewed independently where relevant.
- [ ] Sensitive-data, secrets, dependency, abuse, logging, and recovery risks are considered.
- [ ] Critical/high findings have owner, remediation, verification method, and deadline.
- [ ] Residual risk is documented after mitigation.
- [ ] Critical/high fixes receive independent verification.
- [ ] Human approval exists for exceptions or privileged/destructive actions.
- [ ] No unsupported compromise, exploitability, or compliance claim remains.
- [ ] Retry/review loops are bounded.
- [ ] Handoff states next action, owner, and unresolved unknowns.
