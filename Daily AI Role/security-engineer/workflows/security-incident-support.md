# Workflow: Security Incident Support

## Trigger
Suspected or confirmed security incident.

## Goal
Support fast containment and trustworthy recovery while preserving evidence.

## Stages
1. Join incident command; confirm roles and severity.
2. Freeze facts vs hypotheses and start evidence inventory.
3. Parallel evidence collection and identity/cloud analysis.
4. Model likely attack paths and blast radius.
5. Propose containment options with reversibility and business impact.
6. Obtain human approval for privileged/destructive containment.
7. Track eradication and recovery evidence.
8. Independent security verification.
9. Handoff residual risks and follow-up actions.

## Checkpoints
Before containment, before credential/identity changes, before recovery declaration.

## Retry
No infinite hunting; investigation hypotheses are time-boxed and re-prioritized every cycle.

## Done
Incident commander accepts security exit criteria, evidence is preserved, and residual unknowns are explicit.
