# Product Management Quality Metrics

Track process quality and product outcomes separately.

Process indicators:
- percentage of material bets with explicit problem/evidence/outcome/stop condition
- decision traceability rate
- discovery-to-decision cycle time
- roadmap churn caused by unvalidated requests
- proportion of bets reviewed on schedule
- experiment inconclusive rate with documented cause
- assumption retirement rate
- percentage of launched bets with instrumentation ready

Outcome indicators depend on product context: activation, adoption, retention, task success, conversion, revenue, margin, cost-to-serve, reliability guardrails, satisfaction, or time saved.

Do not reward shipping volume as a primary Product Manager success metric.
