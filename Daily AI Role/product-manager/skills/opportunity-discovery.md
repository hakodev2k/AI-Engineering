# Skill: Opportunity Discovery

## Purpose
Turn weak signals into an evidence-backed problem statement and ranked uncertainty list.

## Trigger
New customer pain, KPI deterioration, strategic theme, repeated support/sales signal, market change, or stakeholder request.

## Inputs
Raw signals, user segments, analytics, strategy, prior research, constraints.

## Preconditions
Identify requester, decision deadline, affected users, and what is already known.

## Procedure
1. Separate observation from interpretation.
2. Define target segment and problem context.
3. Gather existing qualitative and quantitative evidence in parallel when independent.
4. Map pain frequency, severity, alternatives, current workaround, and business consequence.
5. List desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, and compliance uncertainties.
6. Rank assumptions by impact x uncertainty.
7. Design the cheapest credible test for the highest-risk assumption.
8. Consolidate conflicting evidence and classify confidence.

## Decisions
Proceed to sizing, run more discovery, park, or reject.

## Constraints
Do not manufacture demand or generalize a small anecdote to the whole market.

## Output
Canonical opportunity record with evidence, assumptions, confidence, and next test.

## Verification
A reviewer can trace every material claim to evidence or an explicit assumption.

## Failure handling
Retry evidence collection at most twice after fixing a specific failure. Stop if sources remain unavailable or the question cannot be answered credibly.
