# Skill: Product Strategy

## Purpose
Translate company direction and customer evidence into a focused product strategy.

## Trigger
Planning cycle, major market change, product underperformance, new strategic mandate, or portfolio reset.

## Inputs
Company goals, customer segments, product metrics, market/competitor context, capabilities, constraints, economics.

## Steps
1. Define target customer and problem space.
2. Clarify desired customer and business outcomes.
3. Identify differentiated value and why the organization can win.
4. State strategic choices and explicit non-goals.
5. Define enabling capabilities and key risks.
6. Build a metric tree linking outcomes to leading drivers and guardrails.
7. Convert strategy into a small set of strategic themes and testable bets.
8. Review contradictions with finance, engineering, design, go-to-market, legal/security, and operations.

## Verification
Strategy must explain what will not be pursued and how success will be measured.

## Output
Strategy narrative, themes, metric tree, assumptions, risks, and review cadence.
