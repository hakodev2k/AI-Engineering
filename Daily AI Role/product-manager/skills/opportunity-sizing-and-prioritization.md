# Skill: Opportunity Sizing and Prioritization

## Purpose
Compare product opportunities using common evidence and trade-off dimensions.

## Inputs
Validated opportunity records, capacity envelope, strategy, dependencies, cost ranges, risk constraints.

## Steps
1. Estimate affected population, frequency, severity, expected outcome range, strategic leverage, and cost range.
2. State uncertainty rather than forcing precise numbers.
3. Score customer impact, business impact, strategic fit, urgency, confidence, dependency leverage, risk reduction, cost, reversibility, and learning value.
4. Identify portfolio conflicts and displaced work.
5. Separate high-impact/low-confidence items into discovery candidates rather than implementation commitments.
6. Compare alternatives, including do-nothing.
7. Produce recommendation with rationale and dissenting evidence.

## Quality
No hidden weighting, no unsupported market-size claims, and no priority decision without displaced-work visibility.

## Output
Ranked opportunity set with recommendation, uncertainty, and decision owner.

## Stop conditions
Stop when missing evidence would materially change ranking and route back to discovery.
