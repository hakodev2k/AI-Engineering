# Skill: Roadmap and Bet Management

## Purpose
Manage a portfolio of product bets against outcomes, capacity, dependencies, and uncertainty.

## Procedure
1. Maintain roadmap items as outcome-oriented bets with owner, evidence, expected impact, confidence, cost range, dependencies, review date, and stop condition.
2. Distinguish committed external obligations from internal targets and exploratory bets.
3. Re-score when material evidence, constraints, market conditions, or capacity changes.
4. Parallelize independent discovery; serialize conflicting capacity decisions.
5. Surface displaced work before accepting urgent requests.
6. Escalate unresolved cross-team dependency or irreversible commitment conflicts.
7. Publish changes with rationale and affected stakeholders.

## Quality gates
No silent scope inflation, no date promise without delivery-owner input, no stale bet without review checkpoint.

## Output
Current roadmap, change log, dependencies, risks, and decision rationale.
