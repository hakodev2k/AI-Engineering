# Workflow: Roadmap Reprioritization

## Trigger
New evidence, urgent request, capacity change, dependency change, incident, market shift, or strategy update.

## Stages
1. Identify trigger and deadline.
2. Freeze one canonical roadmap snapshot for comparison.
3. Re-score affected bets using common dimensions.
4. Analyze dependencies and displaced work in parallel where possible.
5. Separate externally committed obligations from internal targets.
6. Produce options: keep, swap, defer, reduce scope, accelerate discovery, or stop.
7. Resolve conflicts with accountable stakeholders.
8. Obtain approval for material contractual, financial, public, or irreversible changes.
9. Publish updated roadmap and decision record.

## Stop conditions
If critical dependency or authority conflict remains unresolved, do not silently reprioritize; escalate.

## Verification
Every changed item has a reason, affected stakeholder, and impact on displaced work.
