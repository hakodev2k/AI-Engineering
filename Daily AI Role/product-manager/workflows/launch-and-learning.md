# Workflow: Launch and Learning

## Trigger
A product bet reaches launch readiness.

## Stages
1. Verify outcome, target segment, baseline, metrics, guardrails, and instrumentation.
2. Confirm technical, support, security/legal, marketing, and operational readiness as applicable.
3. Check required human approvals.
4. Launch through accountable delivery owners.
5. Monitor primary metric and guardrails; preserve raw evidence.
6. Metrics Analyst prepares readout; Customer Signal Analyst reviews qualitative response if useful.
7. Risk and Assumption Reviewer challenges confounders and unsupported causal claims.
8. Product Manager decides scale, continue, iterate, pivot, rollback, or stop.
9. Update strategy, roadmap, assumptions, and post-launch review.

## Failure handling
For failed instrumentation or contaminated data, correct once identified and rerun only within bounded retry policy. If evidence remains invalid, mark result inconclusive.

## DoD
Decision, evidence, confidence, next owner, and learning are recorded.
