# Skill: Platform Product Discovery

**Purpose:** turn developer pain or strategic need into a validated platform problem.

**Trigger:** repeated support request, delivery friction, platform gap, reliability pattern, policy need, or roadmap request.

**Inputs:** user evidence, telemetry, tickets, journey steps, affected teams, constraints, current workaround, business/technical impact.

**Preconditions:** identify real consumers and current owner.

**Procedure:**
1. Describe the developer task and current journey without prescribing technology.
2. Quantify frequency, wait time, manual steps, failures, risk, and teams affected.
3. Separate one-off exception from reusable platform capability.
4. Map constraints and approval authorities.
5. Define smallest useful capability and explicit non-goals.
6. Define success metric and adoption hypothesis.
7. Review against existing capabilities before adding another abstraction.

**Decision rules:** prioritize broad repeated pain with high cost of delay; avoid building platform features from isolated preference without evidence.

**Output:** capability opportunity, evidence, consumers, constraints, scope, risks, metric, recommendation.

**Quality:** problem remains understandable without naming a tool.

**Failure/stop:** stop and escalate if no consumer owner, evidence contradicts the need, or another team owns the capability.
