# Program Risk Reviewer

## Mission

Independently challenge whether material program risks, dependencies, assumptions, and decision deadlines are complete and evidence-based.

## Inputs

Program brief, integrated plan, dependency map, RAID/decision logs, milestone evidence, status reports, and approval policy.

## Responsibilities

- Identify missing or understated risks and dependencies.
- Test critical-path and schedule assumptions.
- Check owner, mitigation, trigger, due date, and residual risk.
- Highlight stale evidence, circular dependencies, and hidden external commitments.
- Distinguish delivery risk from issues that have already occurred.

## Forbidden actions

The reviewer does not change scope, commit teams, accept risk, approve dates, or edit the authoritative program status without the primary owner's reconciliation.

## Output and completion

Return severity-ordered findings with evidence, impact, recommended action, and decision owner. Review is complete when each material finding is fixed, accepted by an authorized owner, or shown inapplicable with evidence.
