# Technical Program Delivery Workflow

## Trigger

An authorized initiative spans multiple teams, systems, or milestones and needs integrated technical coordination.

## Stages

1. **Charter:** confirm sponsor, outcome, scope, exclusions, success measures, authority, deadline, and constraints.
2. **Plan:** define workstreams, milestones, interfaces, owners, estimates, evidence, and completion criteria.
3. **Map dependencies and risks:** identify critical path, decision deadlines, external dependencies, mitigations, and escalation owners.
4. **Baseline:** review feasibility with accountable teams and obtain approval for commitments.
5. **Execute:** teams deliver within their ownership while the TPM integrates status, dependencies, decisions, and risks.
6. **Review:** verify milestone evidence, quality, architecture/security/operational gates, and change impact.
7. **Recover or rebaseline:** when outcomes slip, record cause/impact, options, ownership, and authorized baseline change.
8. **Verify outcomes:** demonstrate success measures and operational readiness using current evidence.
9. **Handoff and close:** transfer residual work, publish final status, archive decisions, and record improvements.

## Concurrency and synchronization

Independent workstreams may execute in parallel after interfaces and owners are agreed. Synchronize at scope baseline, architecture/interface decisions, integration milestones, readiness review, and outcome acceptance. Only one integrated plan and status are authoritative.

## Failure and escalation

Escalate when a critical dependency has no owner/date, a required decision misses its deadline, evidence contradicts reported status, scope changes without authority, or residual risk exceeds tolerance. Include evidence, impact, options, recommendation, and decision deadline.

## Definition of Done

The checklist passes, intended outcomes are demonstrated, operational ownership is accepted, approvals and decisions are recorded, and every residual action has an owner and date.
