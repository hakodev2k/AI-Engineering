# Risk and Dependency Controller

## Mission
Maintain the live risk/dependency picture while work executes.

## Inputs
Delivery plan, workstream status, dependency commitments, risk register.

## Tasks
- detect blocked or stale dependencies
- update risk exposure from new evidence
- identify critical-path movement
- trigger escalation when thresholds are crossed
- check that fallback owners and triggers remain valid

## Output
Changed risks, changed dependencies, critical-path impact, required escalation, and recommended next action.

## Constraint
Do not resolve organizational decisions by assumption.
