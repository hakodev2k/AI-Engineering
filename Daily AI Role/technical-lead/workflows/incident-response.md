# Incident Response Workflow

## Trigger
Production impact requiring coordinated technical response.

## Stages
1. Assess impact and severity.
2. Establish roles and communication cadence.
3. Preserve evidence and create incident timeline.
4. Contain with safest reversible action available.
5. Generate and rank evidence-based hypotheses.
6. Test one bounded hypothesis at a time where feasible.
7. Apply remediation with approval for destructive/high-risk actions.
8. Verify user impact, system health, and telemetry recovery.
9. Monitor for recurrence.
10. Produce follow-up actions and structural prevention items.

## Stop/approval gates
Human approval required for destructive data operations, security credential rotation with broad impact, irreversible migrations, or high-blast-radius production changes.

## Completion
Incident is not closed until impact is resolved, verification is recorded, and follow-up ownership exists.
