# Delivery Risk Management

## Purpose
Continuously surface and reduce risks that can break delivery, production, quality, or team throughput.

## Trigger
Planning, scope change, dependency delay, repeated defect, incident, release readiness review, or slipping milestone.

## Risk model
Score each risk using probability 1-5 and impact 1-5. Exposure = probability x impact.

## Procedure
1. Capture risk as a concrete event and consequence.
2. Record evidence, owner, exposure, mitigation, fallback, and review date.
3. Prefer eliminating risk over monitoring it.
4. Create an explicit trigger for fallback execution.
5. Re-score after mitigation.
6. Escalate high-exposure risks whose mitigation needs authority outside the team.

## Categories
Requirement, architecture, dependency, security, data, performance, reliability, release, people/ownership, vendor, and schedule.

## Rules
- A risk without an owner is unowned work.
- A mitigation without a trigger is only an idea.
- Repeated incidents must become structural risk items, not isolated tickets.
- Do not hide schedule risk by reducing verification.

## Output
A prioritized risk register linked to delivery decisions and checkpoints.
