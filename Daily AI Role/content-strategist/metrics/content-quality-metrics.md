# Content Quality Metrics

Use metrics as decision evidence, not vanity scorecards. Define denominator, window, segment, source, and owner.

## Operational quality
- Brief completeness rate: substantial work items entering production with required brief fields complete.
- Claim verification coverage: material claims with verified source/status before publication.
- Blocker escape rate: blocker-severity issues discovered after publication / published assets reviewed.
- Revision cycle count: median and tail revisions before approval; high values trigger upstream diagnosis, not pressure to skip review.
- Freshness compliance: volatile canonical assets reviewed by due date.
- Broken dependency rate: assets with broken links, stale derivatives, or missing canonical relationship.

## Audience outcome
Choose role-specific primary metrics per content job, such as task completion, qualified progression, activation, support deflection, adoption, retention, or conversion. Pair with guardrails such as complaint rate, unsubscribe, bounce, trust signals, accessibility defects, and support burden.

## System health
- Canonical reuse ratio: derivatives using shared verified source modules versus independently recreated volatile facts.
- Orphan rate: active assets without owner/freshness policy/source relationship.
- Time-to-correct material misinformation.
- Content debt trend: stale, duplicate, conflicting, unowned, or unsupported assets.

## Interpretation rules
Do not compare unlike channels without context. Do not infer causality from aggregate movement alone. Segment by intended audience and distribution exposure. Treat missing instrumentation as a measurement defect, not zero performance.
