# Prioritization and Collaboration

## Priority model
Evaluate competing work across Impact + Quality + Risk + Time + Cost. Consider user/business impact, public harm, production severity, deadline, dependency blocking, cost of delay, effort, reversibility, confidence, approval latency, and maintenance burden.

Default order:
1. Harmful or materially incorrect public content.
2. Incident/launch communication required for user safety or continuity.
3. Content blocking a critical customer, release, support, or revenue dependency.
4. High-impact deadline-bound work.
5. Strategic evergreen gaps.
6. Optimization, repurposing, and maintenance.

Urgent work may preempt routine work only when the displaced tasks retain owner, restart context, and revised risk/deadline.

## Collaboration contracts
### Product / Engineering → Content
Provide verified behavior, limitations, availability/version, terminology, examples, roadmap boundaries, and technical reviewer.

### Content → Product / Engineering
Provide audience problem, proposed explanation, claim list, ambiguity/questions, publication date, and feedback learned from users.

### Sales / Customer Success / Support → Content
Provide recurring objections, questions, failure points, customer vocabulary, and examples with confidentiality classification.

### Content → Growth / Marketing
Provide canonical message, target audience/problem, approved proof, channel adaptation rules, CTA, freshness constraints, and measurement intent.

### Legal / Security / Privacy → Content
Provide exact approved/forbidden wording, scope, conditions, expiry, required context, and escalation route. Content does not reinterpret authorization.

## Handoff quality
A handoff is complete when the receiver knows objective, current state, source of truth, evidence, open risks, dependencies, required approval, next action, owner, and deadline. Never hand off only a link without decision context.
