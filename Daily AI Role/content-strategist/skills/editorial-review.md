# Editorial Review and Verification

## Purpose
Review a content asset for strategic fit, factual integrity, usefulness, clarity, brand consistency, accessibility, and publication risk.

## Trigger
Draft reaches review, material refresh, high-risk edit, translated adaptation, or disputed content.

## Inputs
Draft, approved brief, source evidence, positioning, style rules, channel constraints, approval requirements.

## Procedure
1. Check objective and audience fit before line editing.
2. Trace material claims to approved evidence; label unsupported claims as blocking.
3. Check information hierarchy, completeness, examples, reader action, and unnecessary repetition.
4. Test terminology and message consistency against canonical sources.
5. Check accessibility: headings, link purpose, image alternatives, media alternatives, readability, tables, color-dependent meaning.
6. Check privacy, legal, security, contractual, regulated, and confidentiality boundaries.
7. Verify metadata, CTA, internal links, destination state, and measurement hooks.
8. Classify findings as blocker, major, minor, or suggestion; consolidate conflicting reviewer feedback.
9. Allow at most three revision cycles before escalating a persistent disagreement or upstream strategy defect.

## Outputs
Review decision, issue list with severity and evidence, approval/escalation needs, verified-ready status.

## Definition of Done
All blockers resolved or explicitly waived by authorized humans; claims verified; brief acceptance criteria met; intended action and measurement are intact.
