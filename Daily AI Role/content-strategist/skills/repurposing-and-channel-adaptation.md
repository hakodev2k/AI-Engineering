# Repurposing and Channel Adaptation

## Purpose
Transform a verified canonical asset into channel-specific derivatives without fragmenting meaning or claims.

## Trigger
A source asset is approved for distribution across web, email, social, sales enablement, community, video, or in-product surfaces.

## Inputs
Canonical asset, approved claims, audience segment, channel constraints, CTA, campaign context, measurement identifiers.

## Procedure
1. Identify the invariant message, facts, proof, and prohibited distortions.
2. Define the job of each derivative and the context users have on that channel.
3. Adapt length, hook, sequencing, format, examples, CTA, metadata, and interaction model while preserving factual meaning.
4. Reuse canonical claim modules; do not independently rewrite technical facts when exactness matters.
5. Check channel-specific accessibility and platform constraints.
6. Link derivative back to canonical source in the work record and record expiry/freshness dependencies.
7. Review high-reach or high-risk variants before publication.

## Outputs
Channel-ready derivatives, traceability to canonical source, distribution notes, measurement mapping.

## Quality
Derivatives are native to their channel but remain semantically consistent, independently understandable, and attributable.

## Failure Handling
If a channel requires misleading truncation, unsupported certainty, dark patterns, or incompatible claims, change the format or decline that adaptation.
