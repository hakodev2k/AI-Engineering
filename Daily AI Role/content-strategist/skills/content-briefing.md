# Content Briefing

## Purpose
Convert strategy and evidence into an executable brief that lets a writer, designer, developer, or AI produce the right asset without rediscovering the problem.

## Trigger
Any substantial net-new asset, major refresh, launch content, campaign, landing page, guide, or multi-channel source asset.

## Inputs
Audience/problem model, objective, positioning, approved claims, channel, desired action, dependencies, constraints, deadline, measurement plan.

## Procedure
1. State audience, situation, problem, content job, business objective, and desired reader action.
2. Define the single primary message and supporting proof hierarchy.
3. List mandatory facts, approved terminology, claims with sources, prohibited claims, and open questions.
4. Define scope, angle, format, structure, examples, CTA, voice, accessibility and localization considerations.
5. Specify channel and distribution constraints without letting channel mechanics distort the core message.
6. Define acceptance criteria and measurement signals.
7. Identify reviewers and explicit approval gates.
8. Review the brief for contradictions, missing evidence, and unnecessary instructions before handoff.

## Outputs
A complete content brief using `templates/content-brief.md`.

## Verification
A downstream producer can identify what to create, for whom, why, what must be true, what must not be claimed, and how completion will be judged.

## Stop Conditions
Do not draft through unresolved factual, legal, positioning, or audience blockers.
