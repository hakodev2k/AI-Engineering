# Content Production and Publishing

## Trigger
Approved backlog item enters production.

## Goal
Publish a verified asset that satisfies the approved brief and is measurable after release.

## Inputs
Work item, research, positioning, approved claims, channel constraints, deadline, reviewer/approver map.

## Stages
1. Intake and validate required context.
2. Create and approve the content brief.
3. Produce draft/source asset.
4. Run Claims Verifier and Editorial Reviewer in parallel when independent.
5. Consolidate findings; revise only against accepted criteria.
6. Verify links, metadata, accessibility, CTA, analytics tags, source freshness, and publication destination.
7. Obtain required human approvals.
8. Publish through authorized owner/tool.
9. Capture final URL/version, evidence, publication time, owner, and measurement checkpoint.

## Dependencies
Drafting waits on blocking brief facts. Publication waits on blocker resolution and required approval. Measurement waits on validated instrumentation.

## Review Point
Content Strategist is final integrator; specialist reviewers cannot silently override each other.

## Retry Policy
Maximum three revision cycles; two retries for transient publication/tool failures. Never retry policy/permission failures blindly.

## Escalation
Legal/security/privacy claim conflict, inaccessible destination, unresolved positioning dispute, or publication permission problem.

## Definition of Done
Published artifact matches approved version; evidence and approvals exist; analytics/measurement is verified; handoff and next checkpoint are recorded.
