# Urgent Content Response

## Trigger
Production incident, breaking product change, high-impact misinformation, launch blocker, crisis/support surge, or time-critical stakeholder request.

## Goal
Publish the minimum accurate, approved, useful communication quickly without bypassing safety or authority boundaries.

## Inputs
Incident/change facts, owner, affected audience, severity, known/unknown status, approved channels, legal/security/privacy constraints, update cadence.

## Stages
1. Confirm authoritative incident/change owner and current source of truth.
2. Define affected audience, immediate user need, prohibited speculation, and next update point.
3. Draft concise factual communication with explicit uncertainty and workaround only if verified.
4. Claims Verifier checks facts while Editorial Reviewer checks clarity in parallel.
5. Obtain mandatory incident/legal/security/communications approval as applicable.
6. Publish through authorized channel and capture version/time.
7. Monitor corrections and changed facts; supersede rather than silently rewriting material history where transparency matters.
8. After stabilization, perform failure → root cause → lesson → process improvement → future prevention.

## Priority
This workflow can preempt routine work when severity/user harm/cost of delay justifies it; paused tasks retain owners and restart notes.

## Retry Policy
One rapid correction cycle for known drafting errors; two transient tool retries. Permission or factual conflict escalates immediately.

## Definition of Done
Current public/internal message is accurate, approved, timestamped, actionable, consistent with incident truth, and has a next-update/closure state.
