# Definition of Done
- Decision/problem and business outcome are explicit.
- Scope, stakeholders and authority are known.
- Current-state evidence and assumptions are recorded.
- Affected capabilities/domains/systems/data are identified.
- At least one viable alternative was considered for material decisions.
- Trade-offs include cost, risk, reversibility, dependencies and time horizon.
- Security, privacy, regulatory and operational impacts are checked.
- Cross-domain conflicts are reconciled or escalated.
- Human approvals are captured where required.
- Decision/exception has owner, date and review/expiry date where applicable.
- Transition steps, dependencies and accountable owners are assigned.
- Source-of-truth artifacts are updated.
- Handoff recipient acknowledges actionable guidance.
- No TODO, silent assumption, secret or unsupported certainty remains.
