# Capability and Portfolio Analysis
Purpose: identify capability gaps, duplication, ownership issues and rationalization opportunities.
Trigger: strategy change, acquisition, portfolio review, modernization or repeated cross-system friction.
Inputs: capability map, app inventory, costs, owners, lifecycle, dependencies, incidents, constraints.
Preconditions: inventory freshness and owner confidence known.
Steps: frame business outcome; map systems to capabilities; identify overlap/gaps; assess strategic fit, health, cost, risk and dependency; classify retain/invest/consolidate/replace/retire; challenge hidden migration cost.
Decisions: prefer consolidation only when capability and boundary semantics are compatible.
Constraints: do not infer usage/cost absent evidence.
Outputs: portfolio findings, options, evidence gaps and recommended next decisions.
Quality: every classification traceable to criteria.
Verification: owner review and dependency sampling.
Failure: mark unknowns; retry evidence collection twice then escalate.
Stop: regulated or ownership ambiguity invalidates conclusion.
