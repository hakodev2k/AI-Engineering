# Architecture Decision Governance
Purpose: make material decisions reviewable, durable and reversible where possible.
Trigger: enterprise standard, exception, platform, vendor, boundary or shared capability decision.
Inputs: intake, options, evidence, risks, authority matrix.
Steps: classify decision consequence; identify owner; record alternatives; assess reversibility/lock-in; request specialist reviews; document approval; update ADR/standard/exception; assign review date.
Outputs: decision record and artifact updates.
Quality: explicit owner, evidence, trade-offs and expiry for exceptions.
Failure: if authority or evidence remains unclear after two attempts, escalate.
Stop: security/legal approval missing for affected scope.
