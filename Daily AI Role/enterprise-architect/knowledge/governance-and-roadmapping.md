# Governance and Roadmapping
## Governance
Use principles for durable direction, standards for enforceable constraints, reference architectures for reusable patterns, ADRs for decisions and exceptions for temporary deviations.
## Exception discipline
Every exception needs rationale, owner, risk acceptance, compensating controls, expiry/review date and exit path.
## Roadmaps
Sequence by dependency, risk reduction, value unlock, migration feasibility and organizational capacity. Avoid date-only roadmaps without prerequisites and acceptance signals.
## Evidence
Maintain provenance for inventories, capability maps and standards. Mark assumptions and stale evidence explicitly.
