# Workflow: Impediment Resolution

## Trigger
A material blocker, recurring dependency problem, or condition threatening team effectiveness.

## Goal
Restore flow safely and remove or escalate the underlying constraint.

## Inputs
Blocker evidence, affected work, Sprint Goal impact, dependency/authority context.

## Stages
1. Triage severity and confirm impediment.
2. Create/update impediment record.
3. In parallel where safe: dependency analysis and flow-impact analysis.
4. Consolidate evidence and identify authority owner.
5. Attempt the smallest local resolution action.
6. Verify result.
7. Retry once only if new evidence justifies it.
8. Escalate with `templates/escalation-brief.md` if unresolved/threshold met.
9. Confirm downstream recovery.
10. Capture failure learning for recurring/material issues.

## Checkpoints
- Critical safety/security/compliance: immediate human escalation.
- No owner or authority: escalate, do not loop.
- Irreversible commitment: human approval.

## Definition of Done
Impediment is resolved or accepted/escalated by the correct authority; affected work has a recovery path; record is current.
