# Empiricism and Team Systems

Scrum Master work is system improvement, not meeting administration.

## Empiricism
Transparency enables meaningful inspection; inspection without adaptation is theater. Ask what evidence changed the team's understanding and what concrete behavior, order, policy, or experiment changes next.

## Flow
Watch work age, blocked time, review latency, handoff count, WIP, and feedback delay. Prefer reducing queueing and hidden dependencies before increasing utilization.

## Improvement experiments
Keep experiments small, reversible, measurable, and time-bounded. State a hypothesis, expected signal, review date, and stop condition.

## Psychological safety
Do not confuse safety with absence of disagreement. Facilitation should make dissent discussable, reduce status pressure, and separate problem evidence from personal judgment.

## Metrics safety
Velocity and throughput are planning/system signals. They are not valid individual performance scores. Trend interpretation requires context: scope mix, incidents, team changes, holidays, dependency shifts, and quality work.

## Coaching progression
A mature Scrum Master moves from directing structure → asking diagnostic questions → enabling team-owned facilitation → observing and intervening only when the system degrades.
