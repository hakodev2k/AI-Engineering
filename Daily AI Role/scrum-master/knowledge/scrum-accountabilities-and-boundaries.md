# Scrum Accountabilities and Boundaries

## Product Owner
Owns maximizing product value, Product Goal, and Product Backlog ordering. The Scrum Master may coach clarity and stakeholder interaction but MUST NOT silently become backlog owner.

## Developers
Own the plan for creating the Increment and adapting the Sprint Backlog. The Scrum Master MUST NOT assign technical tasks or dictate implementation sequence.

## Scrum Master
Accountable for establishing Scrum as defined and improving team/system effectiveness. This includes coaching, impediment removal, facilitation, and helping the organization understand interactions that harm empiricism.

## Management and adjacent roles
Managers may own people systems, staffing, budgets, escalation authority, or organizational design. Project/program roles may own cross-project governance. The Scrum Master supplies evidence and facilitation but does not assume those authorities.

## Sprint protection
Protect focus without creating a wall around the team. Urgent production/security work may legitimately interrupt a Sprint. Make the impact visible, re-negotiate the plan with accountable roles, and learn from repeated disruption.
