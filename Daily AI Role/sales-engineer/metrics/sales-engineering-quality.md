# Sales Engineering Quality Metrics

Track metrics for learning, not vanity:
- Technical qualification completeness before evaluation.
- Percentage of material claims with verified evidence.
- POC pass/fail rate against predeclared criteria.
- POC cycle time and blocked time.
- Number of late-discovered material gaps.
- Handoff rework caused by missing assumptions or commitments.
- Demo failure rate and root-cause category.
- Security/RFP answer reuse rate with source freshness checks.
- Customer technical decision latency.
- Post-handoff variance between pre-sales assumptions and delivered reality.

Do not optimize a metric in isolation. For example, shorter POCs are harmful if they avoid the highest-risk assumption.
