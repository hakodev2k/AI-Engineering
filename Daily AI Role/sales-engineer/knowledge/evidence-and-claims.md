# Evidence and Claims

A claim is only as strong as its source and scope. Prefer current first-party product documentation, tested behavior, signed security/compliance evidence, approved architecture guidance, or accountable-owner confirmation.

For each material claim record: claim, source, source date/version, environment, confidence, limitations, and owner if unresolved.

Use language deliberately:
- **Verified:** demonstrated or directly supported by authoritative evidence.
- **Conditional:** true only when prerequisites hold.
- **Planned:** not currently available; requires approved roadmap wording.
- **Unknown:** investigation required.

Never transform 'possible with customization' into 'supported out of the box'. Never transform an internal idea into a roadmap commitment.
