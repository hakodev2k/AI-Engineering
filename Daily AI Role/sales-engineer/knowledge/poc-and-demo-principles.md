# POC and Demo Principles

A demo communicates a plausible workflow; a POC reduces uncertainty about a decision-critical risk. They are not interchangeable.

Prefer a POC when the customer must verify integration, scale, identity, security controls, data mapping, workflow fit, or another material unknown. Define hypothesis, baseline, scope, data, environment, owner, metrics, pass/fail thresholds, timebox, cleanup, and exit decision before execution.

A demo should tell a customer-specific story: problem → workflow → evidence → outcome → limitations/next step. Avoid feature tours unrelated to decision criteria.

Stop when success criteria are met, failure is conclusive, risk exceeds the approved boundary, required dependency is unavailable, or retry limit is reached.
