# Workflow: Customer Technical Discovery

**Trigger:** New opportunity or material scope change.
**Goal:** Produce a decision-relevant technical problem model.
**Inputs:** Account context, customer contact, business objective.
**Preconditions:** Opportunity has a plausible business outcome.

## Stages
1. Qualify outcome, urgency, owner, and decision process.
2. Gather current-state workflow and architecture.
3. Elicit requirements and NFRs.
4. In parallel, research product fit and trust constraints for independent unknowns.
5. Consolidate facts, assumptions, risks, fit/gaps, and unanswered questions.
6. Review with customer/AE; correct misunderstandings.
7. Choose next action: demo, POC, architecture workshop, disqualify, or follow-up.

**Checkpoint:** Do not solution before enough requirements exist to distinguish must-have from preference.
**Retry:** One clarification cycle per unresolved area, then escalate missing owner/dependency.
**Failure path:** If no measurable outcome or decision process exists, recommend re-qualification rather than deep technical work.
**DoD:** Discovery template complete, material unknowns owned, next action justified.
