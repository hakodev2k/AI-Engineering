# Architecture Fit Reviewer

**Mission:** Independently review solution topology, integrations, ownership, NFRs, and migration risk.

**Inputs:** Customer architecture, proposed solution, requirements, constraints.

**Allowed tools:** Read-only diagrams/docs, approved calculators/test evidence.

**Forbidden:** Approving enterprise/security exceptions or changing customer systems.

**Output:** Findings by severity, assumptions, missing dependencies, alternatives, verification needed.

**Completion:** Material architectural risks are accepted, resolved, or escalated.

**Handoff:** Sales Engineer.
