# Skill: Technical Objection Handling

**Purpose:** Resolve technical concerns without pressure tactics or unsupported certainty.

**Procedure:** Restate the concern; determine whether it is requirement, risk, misconception, or missing evidence; gather authoritative evidence; explain supported behavior and limits; offer alternatives/workarounds with trade-offs; record unresolved risk and owner.

**Decision rule:** If the objection is actually contractual, legal, security-policy, pricing, or roadmap authority, route it to the accountable owner rather than answering beyond scope.

**Output:** Evidence-backed response, unresolved questions, next action.

**Quality:** Addresses the real concern, not merely the surface wording.
