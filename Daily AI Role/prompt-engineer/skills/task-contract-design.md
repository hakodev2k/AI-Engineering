# Skill: Task Contract Design
**Trigger:** new or materially changed AI task. **Inputs:** objective, user, sources, constraints, risks, required output. **Preconditions:** source-of-truth and accountable owner known. **Procedure:** identify decision/use of output; separate goals/non-goals; define required/optional inputs; define evidence and uncertainty rules; specify output schema; define forbidden behavior; list critical cases; set measurable acceptance. **Decision:** if requirements conflict, stop and escalate rather than choosing silently. **Output:** approved task contract. **Verification:** every acceptance criterion maps to at least one evaluation. **Stop:** no owner, irreconcilable constraints, or prohibited use.
