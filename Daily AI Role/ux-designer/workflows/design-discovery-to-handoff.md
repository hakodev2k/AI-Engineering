# Workflow: Design Discovery to Handoff

**Trigger:** new or materially changed user experience.
**Goal:** produce evidence-based, reviewed, implementation-ready interaction design.
**Inputs:** request, user/business context, evidence, constraints, current product.
**Preconditions:** decision owner and target outcome exist.

## Stages
1. Intake: validate work item and authority.
2. Problem frame: user/task/friction/outcome, evidence vs assumptions, risk register.
3. Discovery: synthesize existing evidence; plan targeted validation for highest-risk unknowns.
4. Interaction design: task flow, state matrix, content/feedback/recovery behavior.
5. Parallel review lanes: research evidence, accessibility, interaction consistency, usability risk.
6. Checkpoint: UX Designer consolidates findings, resolves conflicts, records decisions.
7. Feasibility sync: Engineering/QA review implementation constraints and testability.
8. Validation: usability evaluation proportional to risk.
9. Approval gates: sensitive research, critical residual risk, policy/privacy issues, major scope trade-offs.
10. Handoff: behavior spec, states, risks, decisions, verification criteria.

## Dependencies/source of truth
One versioned work item and approved design candidate are authoritative. Reviewers return findings only.

## Parallel work
Stages 5 lanes can run concurrently; conflicting edits are prohibited.

## Retry/failure
Maximum two materially different iterations per blocking design hypothesis, then escalate with evidence/options.

## Definition of Done
Problem and evidence are traceable; critical states/accessibility/usability risks are covered; approvals recorded; handoff and validation criteria are actionable.
