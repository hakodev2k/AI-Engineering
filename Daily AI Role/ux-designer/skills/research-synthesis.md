# Skill: Research Synthesis

**Purpose:** turn supplied qualitative/quantitative evidence into defensible UX findings without inventing data.
**Trigger:** interviews, observations, tickets, survey responses, analytics, or prior studies require synthesis.
**Inputs:** raw evidence, research questions, participant/source metadata, known sampling limits.
**Preconditions:** provenance is available enough to distinguish sources.
**Context/tools:** notes, transcripts, tagged evidence, spreadsheets, analytics exports.

## Steps
1. Inventory sources, dates, populations, missing data, and quality limits.
2. Extract observations separately from interpretations.
3. Cluster repeated patterns while retaining contradictory evidence.
4. Quantify only where source data permits; do not infer prevalence from anecdote.
5. State finding, supporting evidence, counterevidence, confidence, and consequence.
6. Separate findings from hypotheses and recommendations.
7. Identify unanswered questions and cheapest next validation method.

## Decisions
Promote a pattern to a finding only when evidence supports it; otherwise label hypothesis.

## Constraints
No invented quotes, participants, sample sizes, analytics, or statistical claims.

## Output
Structured synthesis with findings, evidence, confidence, limitations, and next questions.

## Quality/verification
Independent evidence reviewer checks traceability and overclaiming.

## Failure/stop
If evidence provenance is insufficient after two clarification passes, stop and escalate instead of manufacturing certainty.
