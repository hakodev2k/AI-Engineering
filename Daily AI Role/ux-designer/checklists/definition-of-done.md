# Definition of Done

- Problem, users, scenario, desired outcome, constraints, and non-goals are explicit.
- Evidence and assumptions are clearly separated.
- Priority follows impact, severity, dependency/deadline, reversibility, then effort/cost.
- Main happy path plus empty, loading, error, permission, interruption, retry, and recovery states are considered where relevant.
- Accessibility and inclusive-design risks are reviewed.
- Research or usability findings include evidence and confidence, not fabricated certainty.
- Dependencies and owners are named.
- Design decisions include alternatives and trade-offs.
- Engineering feasibility questions are resolved or recorded.
- Critical review findings are resolved, accepted by an authorized human, or escalated.
- Validation metrics or observable acceptance criteria exist.
- Handoff states behavior, content, edge cases, unresolved questions, and verification steps.
- Failure learning is recorded for material rework/incidents.
- No TODO placeholders, omitted referenced artifacts, hardcoded secrets, or destructive default actions exist.
