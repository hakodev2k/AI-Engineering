# Cross-Functional Technical Alignment

## Purpose
Align engineering, product, docs, support, marketing, and DevRel around technically accurate developer-facing decisions.

## When to use
Use for launches, roadmap feedback, contentious developer experience issues, migrations, or cross-team programs.

## Inputs
Decision to make, developer evidence, technical constraints, roadmap, commitments, owners, deadlines.

## Context to inspect
Source-of-truth artifacts, stakeholder incentives, decision rights, unresolved technical questions, dependencies, and external commitments.

## Core knowledge
Senior DevRel acts as a bidirectional technical interface, not merely a messenger. Alignment requires shared facts, explicit trade-offs, decision ownership, and durable records.

## Procedure
1. Frame the developer problem and required decision.
2. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and open questions.
3. Bring reproducible developer evidence rather than generalized sentiment.
4. Identify decision owner and required contributors.
5. Surface technical, operational, support, trust, and timing trade-offs.
6. Resolve terminology and source-of-truth conflicts.
7. Record decision, rationale, scope, owner, and follow-up.
8. Translate the decision into consistent external artifacts.
9. Verify execution across docs, code samples, support, and messaging.
10. Reopen only when new evidence changes assumptions materially.

## Decision points
Use synchronous review for high-ambiguity/high-impact issues; asynchronous ADR/brief for durable decisions. Escalate conflicting commitments to accountable leadership rather than smoothing over contradictions.

## Common failure patterns
Meeting without decision rights, representing anecdotes as consensus, hiding trade-offs, inconsistent external claims, and undocumented verbal agreements.

## Verification
Confirm all affected artifacts use the same technical facts and terminology, owners accept follow-ups, and unresolved risks are explicit.

## Expected output
A durable cross-functional decision record and consistent developer-facing execution plan.

## Stop conditions
Stop when no accountable decision owner exists, required engineering facts are unavailable, or requested messaging contradicts verified behavior or policy.
