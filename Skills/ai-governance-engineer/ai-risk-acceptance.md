# AI Risk Acceptance

## Purpose
Make residual AI risk acceptance explicit, evidence-based, time-bounded where appropriate, and owned at the correct authority level.

## When to use
Use when material residual risk remains after feasible controls or when release decisions require formal acceptance.

## Inputs
Risk assessment, impact assessment, evaluations, controls, exceptions, alternatives, business benefit, risk appetite, approval matrix.

## Procedure
1. State the residual risk in concrete consequence terms.
2. Summarize evidence and uncertainty.
3. Confirm implemented controls and remaining gaps.
4. Compare alternatives: mitigate, avoid, transfer, restrict, or accept.
5. Determine acceptance authority from risk tier.
6. Define conditions, scope, monitoring, and expiry/review triggers.
7. Obtain explicit accountable approval.
8. Record dissent or independent challenge where required.
9. Link acceptance to the deployed system/version.
10. Reopen acceptance when assumptions change.

## Decision points
Do not use acceptance to bypass mandatory law or policy. High uncertainty may justify temporary restricted deployment rather than permanent acceptance.

## Common failure patterns
Implicit acceptance through launch, approver below authority, vague risk language, no expiry, acceptance before controls are verified.

## Verification
Decision record identifies exact residual risk, evidence, authority, conditions, and reassessment trigger.

## Expected output
Auditable risk acceptance decision.

## Stop conditions
Stop when risk exceeds delegated appetite or mandatory requirements prohibit acceptance.
