# AI Governance Exception Management

## Purpose
Handle justified deviations from AI policy or controls without normalizing unmanaged risk.

## When to use
Use when a required control cannot be met temporarily or an alternative control may achieve the objective.

## Inputs
Requested exception, control objective, business rationale, duration, risk assessment, compensating controls, accountable owner.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the requirement and control objective.
2. Determine why normal compliance is infeasible.
3. Assess incremental risk from the deviation.
4. Evaluate compensating controls.
5. Define narrow scope and expiration.
6. Assign remediation owner and milestones.
7. Route to risk-appropriate approver independent of requester where required.
8. Record decision and rationale.
9. Monitor active exceptions and approaching expiry.
10. Close, renew with fresh evidence, or escalate overdue exceptions.

## Decision points
Reject exceptions that remove a non-negotiable legal/safety boundary. Permanent design choices should update policy rather than remain perpetual exceptions.

## Common failure patterns
Open-ended waivers, self-approval, repeated renewal, vague scope, no remediation plan, exception register disconnected from deployment controls.

## Verification
All active exceptions are unexpired, scoped, approved, monitored, and linked to compensating controls.

## Expected output
Auditable exception record and remediation plan.

## Stop conditions
Escalate non-negotiable requirement conflicts or residual risk beyond approver authority.
