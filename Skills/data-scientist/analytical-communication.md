# Analytical Communication

## Purpose
Turn complex evidence into accurate, decision-ready communication without hiding uncertainty or overstating conclusions.

## When to use
Use for stakeholder reviews, experiment readouts, model recommendations, executive summaries, and analytical handoffs.

## Inputs
Question, evidence, uncertainty, audience, decision context, alternatives, and recommended action.

## Context to inspect
Audience technical depth, incentives, prior assumptions, decision authority, time constraints, and likely misinterpretations.

## Core knowledge
Communication should lead with the decision and evidence, not the analysis chronology. Visuals should expose comparisons honestly. Uncertainty, limitations, and causal boundaries are part of the result.

## Procedure
1. Identify the audience's decision and required confidence.
2. State the question and recommendation in plain language.
3. Present the minimum evidence needed to support it.
4. Quantify effect size and uncertainty, not only significance.
5. Use visual encodings appropriate to the comparison.
6. Separate facts, assumptions, interpretations, and forecasts.
7. Explain material limitations and failure modes.
8. Present alternatives and trade-offs when the decision is non-obvious.
9. Make next actions and ownership explicit.
10. Preserve technical detail in appendices or reproducible artifacts.

## Decision points
Use an executive summary for decision makers and deeper diagnostics for technical reviewers; do not force one artifact to serve every audience.

## Common failure patterns
Dashboard dumping, misleading axes, excessive jargon, causal wording from correlational evidence, burying caveats, and reporting metrics without decision implications.

## Verification
Ask an independent reviewer to state the conclusion, uncertainty, and recommended action after reading the artifact.

## Expected output
A concise, accurate narrative connecting evidence to a decision with transparent limitations.

## Stop conditions
Stop when evidence does not support the requested claim or confidentiality rules prevent appropriate disclosure.
