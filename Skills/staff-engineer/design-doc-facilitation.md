# Design Doc Facilitation

## Purpose
Guide complex design discussions toward clear decisions by improving problem framing, surfacing assumptions, and ensuring alternatives and trade-offs are evaluated constructively.

## When to use
Use for contentious or high-impact technical proposals involving multiple teams or substantial uncertainty.

## Inputs
Design document, requirements, alternatives, comments, relevant metrics, architecture constraints, stakeholder map.

## Preconditions
There is a concrete decision or set of unresolved questions to address.

## Context to inspect
Prior decisions, affected systems, organizational ownership, technical evidence, deadlines, dissenting viewpoints, and decision authority.

## Core knowledge
Staff-level facilitation is not about winning an argument. It separates facts, assumptions, preferences, constraints, and values; it creates decision clarity while preserving productive dissent.

## Procedure
1. State the decision to be made and decision owner.
2. Separate requirements from proposed solutions.
3. List assumptions that materially affect the choice.
4. Ensure credible alternatives are represented fairly.
5. Identify areas of agreement and true disagreement.
6. Request evidence for disputed factual claims.
7. Compare alternatives using shared criteria.
8. Time-box unresolved exploration where appropriate.
9. Record the decision, dissent, and follow-up evidence needed.
10. Ensure affected owners understand consequences.

## Decision points
Seek consensus when inexpensive, but do not require unanimity. Escalate value or priority conflicts to the accountable decision owner rather than disguising them as technical disputes.

## Common failure patterns
Dominating discussion, bikeshedding, endless review cycles, hidden decision ownership, premature convergence, and suppressing dissent without resolving evidence.

## Verification
Confirm the decision is explicit, rationale is recorded, unresolved risks have owners, and participants understand next steps.

## Expected output
A decision-ready design record with clarified assumptions, alternatives, trade-offs, decision, and follow-ups.

## Stop conditions
Stop when the decision requires missing business requirements or authority outside the engineering group.
