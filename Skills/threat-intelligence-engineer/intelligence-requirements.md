# Intelligence Requirements

## Purpose
Translate stakeholder risk questions into prioritized intelligence requirements (PIRs) that direct collection and analysis.

## When to use
Use when starting or reprioritizing a threat-intelligence program, campaign investigation, or executive request. Do not collect broadly without a decision need.

## Inputs
Stakeholder decisions, business assets, threat model, incident history, regulatory constraints, existing intelligence.

## Context to inspect
Identify critical assets, exposed technologies, geographic footprint, likely adversaries, current controls, and who will act on findings.

## Core knowledge
PIRs are decision-oriented and time bounded. Requirements should define the question, consumer, urgency, scope, and evidence threshold; collection is subordinate to requirements.

## Procedure
1. Interview consumers about decisions and uncertainties.
2. Inventory critical assets and plausible threat scenarios.
3. Convert uncertainties into answerable PIRs.
4. Rank PIRs by impact, likelihood, urgency, and actionability.
5. Define indicators that would satisfy or invalidate each PIR.
6. Map required sources and collection gaps.
7. Assign owners and review cadence.
8. Record assumptions and confidence expectations.
9. Validate priorities with consumers before collection.

## Decision points
Prefer narrow PIRs when action must be immediate; use broader strategic requirements for planning. Drop requirements whose answer would not change a decision.

## Common failure patterns
Collecting everything, technology-led requirements, vague questions, no consumer, no expiry date, and treating historical interest as current priority.

## Verification
A requirement is implemented when documented and mapped to sources; it is verified when a named consumer confirms the answer would alter or support a real decision.

## Expected output
Prioritized PIR register with consumers, rationale, sources, evidence thresholds, owners, and review dates.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when stakeholders cannot identify a decision, required access is unavailable, or collection would violate legal/privacy constraints.
