# Change Impact Assessment

## Purpose
Assess how an AI-enabled workflow changes roles, responsibilities, controls, skills, workload, and downstream processes before rollout.

## When to use
Use when a pilot changes who performs work, how decisions are made, or what evidence is required.

## Inputs
Current and target workflow, role descriptions, controls, service levels, training needs, support model, and implementation plan.

## Context to inspect
Inspect handoffs, approvals, staffing, incentives, exception paths, reporting, compliance controls, and dependencies on other teams.

## Core knowledge
AI adoption often shifts work rather than removing it. Drafting may decrease while review, exception handling, or escalation increases. Senior assessment captures both visible productivity gains and displaced cognitive or operational load.

## Procedure
1. Compare current and target workflows step by step.
2. Identify removed, added, accelerated, and transformed tasks.
3. Map changes to each role and decision owner.
4. Estimate workload shifts, new queues, and exception demand.
5. Identify controls that are removed or weakened.
6. Define new skills, training, and support needs.
7. Assess effects on incentives and performance metrics.
8. Identify downstream teams affected by changed volume or quality.
9. Rank impacts by severity and readiness.
10. Define mitigations and owners before rollout.

## Decision points
Automate only when the resulting control environment remains adequate. Phase rollout when role redesign or support capacity is a major dependency.

## Common failure patterns
Counting only time saved, ignoring reviewer workload, changing accountability implicitly, training after launch, and failing to assess downstream queues.

## Verification
Validate the impact map with affected practitioners and owners. Confirm every high-impact change has a mitigation or explicit acceptance.

## Expected output
A change-impact matrix covering roles, tasks, controls, workload, training, support, risks, and mitigations.

## Stop conditions
Stop when target operating responsibilities are unresolved or high-impact changes lack an accountable owner.
