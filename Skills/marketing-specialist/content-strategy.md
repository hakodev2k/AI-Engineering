# Content Strategy

## Purpose
Build a content system that serves customer needs and business outcomes across discovery, evaluation, conversion, adoption, and retention.

## When to use
Use when content production is fragmented, organic demand matters, buyers require education, sales needs reusable assets, or content performance is unclear.

## Inputs
Audience research, journey stages, positioning, search and community insights, product expertise, existing content inventory, distribution channels, and goals.

## Context to inspect
Audit content quality, traffic, engagement, conversion contribution, duplication, topical gaps, customer questions, sales objections, maintenance burden, and distribution performance.

## Core knowledge
Content strategy includes audience need, editorial focus, formats, distribution, governance, lifecycle, and measurement. Publishing volume is not a strategy. Content must earn attention or utility and support a measurable customer or business outcome.

## Procedure
1. Define content objectives and target audiences.
2. Map customer questions and decisions by journey stage.
3. Audit existing assets for keep, improve, consolidate, repurpose, or retire decisions.
4. Define priority themes and distinctive expertise.
5. Select formats based on user need and distribution context.
6. Create an editorial and production model with ownership.
7. Build distribution into each asset plan.
8. Define conversion paths without forcing every asset into direct response.
9. Establish quality, accessibility, factual, and brand standards.
10. Measure consumption, assisted outcomes, conversion, retention, and content decay.
11. Refresh or retire content based on evidence.

## Decision points
Create evergreen assets when demand persists; use timely content when speed and relevance matter. Build owned content for durable value and use rented channels for distribution, not dependency alone.

## Common failure patterns
Publishing without distribution, keyword-only content, duplicate topics, no subject-matter review, measuring pageviews alone, stale claims, and content that answers company questions rather than customer questions.

## Verification
Confirm priority customer questions are covered, content has owners and distribution plans, conversion paths work, and performance can be tied to defined outcomes.

## Expected output
A content strategy with audience needs, themes, formats, governance, distribution, measurement, and lifecycle rules.

## Stop conditions
Stop when subject-matter evidence is unavailable for high-stakes claims or required approvals cannot be obtained.
