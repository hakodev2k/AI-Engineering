# Paid Media Management

## Purpose
Plan, operate, and optimize paid acquisition while protecting incrementality, economics, brand safety, privacy, and downstream customer quality.

## When to use
Use for search, social, display, video, sponsorship, or other paid distribution where spend can be measured and controlled.

## Inputs
Business objective, audience, creative, landing experience, budget, historical performance, conversion data, unit economics, and platform constraints.

## Context to inspect
Review tracking, consent, attribution windows, account structure, audience overlap, frequency, creative fatigue, auction dynamics, conversion quality, fraud risk, and downstream value.

## Core knowledge
Platform-reported conversions are not automatically incremental. Bid systems optimize toward supplied signals; poor events produce poor optimization. Evaluate marginal returns, not average ROAS alone.

## Procedure
1. Define the business outcome and economic guardrails.
2. Validate conversion and value signals.
3. Choose channels based on intent, audience, creative fit, and economics.
4. Structure campaigns for sufficient learning without needless fragmentation.
5. Define audiences, exclusions, budgets, bids, and frequency controls.
6. Prepare message and creative variants.
7. QA destinations, tracking, consent, and naming conventions.
8. Launch with controlled budgets.
9. Monitor delivery, signal quality, spend, conversion quality, and anomalies.
10. Test incrementality and creative systematically.
11. Reallocate budget by marginal expected value.
12. Document decisions and platform changes.

## Decision points
Use automated bidding when signal volume and quality are sufficient; retain tighter controls when data is sparse or risk is high. Scale only while marginal economics remain acceptable.

## Common failure patterns
Blind trust in platform attribution, optimizing cheap leads instead of valuable customers, fragmented campaigns, excessive retargeting, uncontrolled broad matching, creative neglect, and budget scaling faster than signal quality.

## Verification
Reconcile spend, validate conversion events, compare platform and first-party outcomes, inspect downstream quality, and use holdouts or geo tests where practical.

## Expected output
A controlled paid-media program with budgets, targeting, creative plan, economic guardrails, experiments, and performance decisions.

## Stop conditions
Pause when tracking breaks, spend behaves unexpectedly, brand safety is compromised, policy violations occur, or economics breach approved guardrails.
