# Stakeholder Alignment and Reporting

## Purpose
Turn marketing plans and performance into clear decisions, trade-offs, risks, and commitments across leadership and partner teams.

## When to use
Use for planning reviews, campaign decisions, launch readiness, performance reporting, budget changes, cross-functional dependencies, and executive communication.

## Inputs
Business objectives, marketing plan, metrics, forecasts, risks, dependencies, experiment results, budget status, and stakeholder needs.

## Context to inspect
Inspect decision rights, stakeholder incentives, metric definitions, prior commitments, unresolved dependencies, reporting cadence, and the level of detail appropriate to each audience.

## Core knowledge
Senior communication is decision-oriented. Reports should distinguish facts, interpretation, uncertainty, and recommendations. More metrics do not create more clarity. Escalation should happen early enough to preserve options.

## Procedure
1. Identify the decision or understanding required from the audience.
2. Select the minimum evidence needed to support it.
3. State outcome versus target and relevant baseline.
4. Explain material drivers, not every fluctuation.
5. Separate verified facts from hypotheses.
6. Surface risks, dependencies, and uncertainty explicitly.
7. Present alternatives with trade-offs when a decision is needed.
8. Recommend a course of action and owner.
9. Record decisions and follow-up commitments.
10. Adjust future reporting based on which information actually changed decisions.

## Decision points
Use detailed operational reporting for teams managing execution and concise exception-based reporting for executives. Escalate when risk exceeds team authority or delay materially reduces options.

## Common failure patterns
Vanity dashboards, burying bad news, unexplained metric changes, reporting activity instead of outcomes, excessive detail, recommendations without evidence, and meetings that end without decisions or owners.

## Verification
Confirm figures reconcile to trusted sources, decisions are captured, owners acknowledge commitments, and stakeholders can explain the key implication consistently.

## Expected output
A concise decision-ready report or review with outcomes, drivers, uncertainty, risks, options, recommendation, and actions.

## Stop conditions
Stop publication when material figures cannot be reconciled, confidential information would reach an unauthorized audience, or unresolved discrepancies could mislead decisions.
