# Social Media Strategy

## Purpose
Use social platforms deliberately for audience development, brand relevance, community interaction, demand creation, distribution, and learning.

## When to use
Use when target audiences actively consume or discuss relevant topics on social platforms and the organization can sustain useful participation.

## Inputs
Audience research, brand positioning, platform behavior, content resources, business objectives, moderation capacity, and historical analytics.

## Context to inspect
Inspect audience-platform fit, organic reach, paid support, competitor behavior, community norms, platform risks, moderation history, content performance, and conversion paths.

## Core knowledge
Each platform has distinct consumption mechanics and social norms. Follower count is not a business outcome. Social strategy should balance reach, engagement quality, distribution, community trust, and measurable downstream effects.

## Procedure
1. Define the role social should play in the customer journey.
2. Select platforms based on audience behavior and strategic fit.
3. Define audience value and content territories.
4. Establish channel-specific formats and cadence.
5. Create participation, response, and moderation standards.
6. Connect social content to owned destinations when appropriate.
7. Build a repeatable production and approval workflow.
8. Track reach quality, meaningful engagement, traffic, leads, conversions, and sentiment.
9. Test formats and messages while preserving brand consistency.
10. Review platform dependence and diversify strategically.

## Decision points
Prioritize depth on fewer platforms when resources are constrained. Use paid amplification when organic distribution cannot reach the intended audience efficiently. Engage in trends only when relevance is authentic.

## Common failure patterns
Posting everywhere, copying identical content across platforms, chasing vanity engagement, slow community response, trend hijacking, inconsistent voice, and depending on a single platform for customer access.

## Verification
Confirm platform audiences match target segments, content delivers intended value, moderation works, traffic and conversion paths are valid, and metrics align with the strategic role.

## Expected output
A platform strategy with objectives, audience, content territories, formats, cadence, engagement rules, distribution, risks, and measurement.

## Stop conditions
Pause content when misinformation, safety incidents, legal concerns, or moderation capacity create unacceptable risk.
