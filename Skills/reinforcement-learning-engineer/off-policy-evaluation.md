# Off-Policy Evaluation

## Purpose
Estimate candidate-policy performance from data generated by other policies while exposing overlap, variance, and identifiability limitations before online deployment.

## When to use
Use for offline RL, risky domains, policy selection from logs, or pre-deployment analysis where direct online evaluation is limited.

## Inputs
- Logged trajectories
- Behavior-policy probabilities or approximations
- Candidate policy
- Reward/outcome data
- Evaluation horizon

## Preconditions
Logging must preserve action context and episode structure. Behavior-policy support for candidate actions must be analyzed.

## Context to inspect
Inspect propensity quality, state-action overlap, weight distribution, effective sample size, censoring, policy changes over time, and reward delay.

## Core knowledge
Importance sampling can be unbiased under strong assumptions but suffers high variance. Weighted estimators trade bias and variance. Direct-method and doubly robust estimators depend on model quality. No estimator can recover reliable value when support is missing.

## Procedure
1. Validate logged action probabilities or reconstruct behavior policy cautiously.
2. Measure candidate/behavior overlap before computing value estimates.
3. Inspect importance-weight tails and effective sample size.
4. Compute simple importance, weighted, direct, and doubly robust estimates when assumptions permit.
5. Use temporal or policy-version slices to detect drift.
6. Bootstrap or otherwise quantify estimator uncertainty.
7. Compare estimators and investigate disagreement.
8. Run synthetic or simulator calibration tests when ground truth is available.
9. Reject estimates dominated by unsupported actions.
10. Document assumptions and deployment margin explicitly.

## Decision points
Prefer doubly robust methods when both propensity and outcome models are credible. Prefer conservative conclusions when estimators disagree materially or overlap is weak.

## Common failure patterns
- Missing propensities are treated as harmless.
- Huge importance weights create fake gains.
- Random splitting hides policy drift.
- OPE point estimates are reported without uncertainty.

## Verification
Require acceptable overlap, bounded weight diagnostics, estimator agreement within justified uncertainty, and calibration against known-value scenarios where possible.

## Expected output
An OPE report with support diagnostics, multiple estimates, uncertainty, assumptions, and a clear statement of what cannot be inferred.

## Stop conditions
Stop when candidate actions lack support, behavior propensities are unusable, or estimator uncertainty is too large for a decision.
