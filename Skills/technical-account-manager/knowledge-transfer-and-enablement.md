# Knowledge Transfer and Enablement

## Purpose
Build customer self-sufficiency by transferring operational, architectural, troubleshooting, and product knowledge to the right roles using evidence-based enablement rather than generic training.

## When to use
Use during onboarding, team turnover, new feature adoption, post-incident follow-up, or when repeated support cases reveal knowledge gaps.

## Inputs
Customer roles, current skills, architecture, recurring support themes, runbooks, product documentation, success goals, and operating procedures.

## Context to inspect
Who operates the system, common failure modes, critical tasks, escalation patterns, documentation gaps, access boundaries, and differences between implementation and operations teams.

## Core knowledge
Enablement is successful when people can perform critical tasks correctly without unnecessary dependency. Senior TAMs target observable capability and avoid overwhelming users with broad product tours.

## Procedure
1. Identify the operational or technical capability that must improve.
2. Define the target audience and prerequisite knowledge.
3. Assess current gaps using incidents, support cases, interviews, or task observation.
4. Select authoritative documentation and realistic examples.
5. Teach the workflow, decision points, failure modes, and escalation boundaries.
6. Require participants to perform representative tasks or explain decisions back.
7. Capture missing documentation or product friction discovered during enablement.
8. Publish reusable notes or runbooks through the customer's approved knowledge system.
9. Measure whether repeat cases or execution errors decrease.

## Decision points
Use workshops for complex cross-team workflows, focused sessions for specific gaps, and documentation-first enablement for repeatable operational procedures.

## Common failure patterns
Training without role targeting, presenting features instead of workflows, using outdated documentation, and treating attendance as proof of competency.

## Verification
Confirm target users can independently complete representative tasks, find authoritative references, and describe when to escalate.

## Expected output
A role-specific enablement package with objectives, materials, practice evidence, knowledge gaps, and follow-up actions.

## Stop conditions
Stop when required access or safe practice environments are unavailable, documentation is materially outdated, or the activity would require exposing restricted information.
