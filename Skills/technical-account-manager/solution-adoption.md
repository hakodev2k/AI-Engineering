# Solution Adoption

## Purpose
Increase durable customer adoption by diagnosing technical and organizational barriers, prioritizing high-value use cases, and proving usage through evidence rather than activity counts.

## When to use
Use when purchased capability is underused, expansion depends on adoption, or stakeholders report low realized value.

## Inputs
Usage telemetry, user groups, use cases, architecture, enablement history, support data, success metrics, and customer priorities.

## Context to inspect
Activation rates, feature usage, workflow fit, integration friction, permissions, performance, training gaps, change resistance, and operational constraints.

## Core knowledge
Adoption is a system outcome involving product fit, workflow integration, incentives, reliability, and user capability. A Senior TAM separates technical blockers from process and change-management issues.

## Procedure
1. Define the intended behavior and measurable adoption state.
2. Segment users and use cases by business value and readiness.
3. Compare expected usage with actual telemetry.
4. Interview representative users and operators to identify friction.
5. Classify blockers as technical, product, process, governance, or enablement.
6. Prioritize interventions by impact and controllability.
7. Run targeted pilots or enablement for high-value cohorts.
8. Measure behavior change after each intervention.
9. Retire low-value activities that do not change adoption.

## Decision points
Prefer targeted use-case activation over broad training when friction is localized. Escalate product gaps only after validating configuration and workflow assumptions.

## Common failure patterns
Counting meetings as adoption, focusing on feature breadth instead of valuable workflows, ignoring permissions, and training users before resolving technical friction.

## Verification
Use telemetry and customer-confirmed outcomes to show sustained improvement over the agreed baseline.

## Expected output
An adoption plan with prioritized blockers, interventions, owners, and measurable evidence of progress.

## Stop conditions
Stop when required telemetry is unavailable, the use case is not viable, or adoption depends on unapproved organizational change.
