# Support Case Governance

## Purpose
Improve the quality, prioritization, and closure of customer support cases by maintaining evidence, ownership, severity discipline, and recurring-problem visibility.

## When to use
Use for strategic accounts with active case volume, aging escalations, repeated defects, or unclear support ownership.

## Inputs
Open and historical cases, severity, timestamps, customer impact, diagnostics, ownership, SLAs, and related incidents.

## Context to inspect
Case age, reproduction quality, duplicate issues, pending customer actions, engineering handoffs, severity accuracy, recurrence, and communication gaps.

## Core knowledge
Support governance should accelerate resolution without bypassing support process. Senior TAMs identify systemic patterns and prevent executive escalation from becoming the normal routing mechanism.

## Procedure
1. Review active cases by impact, age, and dependency.
2. Confirm severity reflects actual business impact.
3. Check whether each case contains sufficient diagnostics and reproduction details.
4. Identify blockers and the party responsible for the next action.
5. Merge context across duplicate or related cases without losing evidence.
6. Escalate cases that meet formal criteria.
7. Track recurring themes for product, architecture, or enablement action.
8. Close governance items only after resolution and customer confirmation where appropriate.

## Decision points
Escalate based on impact and blocked progress, not account pressure alone. Create a problem-management workstream when multiple incidents share a likely systemic cause.

## Common failure patterns
Inflating severity, opening duplicate cases, executive escalation without diagnostics, and leaving resolved cases administratively open.

## Verification
Confirm active cases have correct owners, next actions, evidence, and severity; verify recurring themes have preventive follow-up.

## Expected output
A prioritized support portfolio with clear actions, escalation decisions, and systemic issue tracking.

## Stop conditions
Stop when access to case details is restricted, severity disputes require support leadership, or security-sensitive handling is required.
