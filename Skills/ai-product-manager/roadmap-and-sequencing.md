# AI Roadmap and Sequencing

## Purpose
Sequence AI product work so customer value, technical learning, platform dependencies, risk reduction, and operational readiness reinforce one another.

## When to use
Use for quarterly planning, major AI initiatives, model migrations, or when several capability investments compete for capacity.

## Inputs
Product strategy, prioritized use cases, dependencies, engineering estimates, evaluation readiness, data readiness, risk controls, commercial commitments.

## Context to inspect
Current roadmap, shared AI platform work, model/provider dependencies, infrastructure limits, launch calendars, team capacity, and unresolved discovery risks.

## Core knowledge
AI roadmaps should manage uncertainty explicitly. Early work should retire the highest-value unknowns before committing to expensive production architecture or broad rollout.

## Procedure
1. Translate strategy into measurable outcomes rather than feature lists.
2. Identify the assumptions that could invalidate each initiative.
3. Sequence discovery and evaluation work before irreversible implementation.
4. Map shared dependencies such as data, retrieval, observability, and safety controls.
5. Separate experiments, productization, and scale-hardening phases.
6. Define exit criteria for each phase.
7. Reserve capacity for production quality and regression work.
8. Identify commitments that depend on uncertain model capability.
9. Re-plan when evidence changes rather than preserving sunk-cost scope.

## Decision points
Sequence foundational work early only when several validated use cases depend on it. Avoid building generic AI platforms ahead of proven product needs.

## Common failure patterns
Feature-count roadmaps, committing before evals exist, hiding uncertainty behind dates, underfunding production hardening, and treating model capability as fixed.

## Verification
Confirm every major roadmap item maps to a strategic outcome, dependency, evidence threshold, owner, and measurable completion criterion.

## Expected output
An outcome-oriented AI roadmap with phases, dependencies, uncertainty, decision gates, and sequencing rationale.

## Stop conditions
Stop when roadmap commitments require capabilities that have not been validated and the uncertainty would materially affect scope or dates.
