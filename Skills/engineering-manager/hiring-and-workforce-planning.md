# Hiring and Workforce Planning

## Purpose
Plan and execute hiring based on capability gaps and sustainable team design rather than headcount targets alone.

## When to use
Use for growth planning, replacement hiring, new capability creation, or when delivery expectations exceed sustainable capacity.

## Inputs
Roadmap, team topology, skills inventory, attrition risk, budget, hiring constraints, workload evidence, and expected organizational changes.

## Context to inspect
Review actual bottlenecks, role distribution, seniority mix, contractor usage, management load, on-call burden, and future capability requirements.

## Core knowledge
Hiring is slow and uncertain. Capacity is not proportional to headcount during onboarding. Seniority mix, complementary skills, and role clarity often matter more than raw team size.

## Procedure
1. Translate future outcomes into required capabilities.
2. Inventory current capability and capacity.
3. Separate temporary workload from persistent capability gaps.
4. Consider simplification, automation, prioritization, internal mobility, or contracting before hiring.
5. Define role outcomes and seniority expectations.
6. Build an evidence-based hiring plan and sequence.
7. Design structured interviews tied to job competencies.
8. Calibrate interviewers and scoring criteria.
9. Plan onboarding capacity before offers are accepted.
10. Review hiring quality and time-to-productivity after onboarding.

## Decision points
Hire for durable capability gaps; use contractors for bounded specialist or temporary work when knowledge transfer is planned. Prefer fewer well-defined roles over speculative hiring.

## Common failure patterns
Hiring to solve prioritization problems, vague job descriptions, inconsistent interviews, cloning existing team profiles, ignoring onboarding load, and treating senior titles as interchangeable.

## Verification
Verify each opening has a documented capability gap, approved budget, measurable role outcomes, calibrated interview loop, and onboarding owner.

## Expected output
A workforce plan with capability gaps, alternatives considered, prioritized roles, interview criteria, and onboarding implications.

## Stop conditions
Stop when budget or employment constraints are unresolved, the role cannot be defined clearly, or hiring would mask a fundamental organizational or product-priority problem.
