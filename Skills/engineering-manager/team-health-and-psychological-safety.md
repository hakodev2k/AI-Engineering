# Team Health and Psychological Safety

## Purpose
Create conditions where engineers can raise risks, admit mistakes, challenge ideas, ask for help, and sustain performance without normalizing burnout.

## When to use
Use continuously and especially after reorganizations, incidents, attrition, conflict, sustained overtime, or signs that important concerns are not surfacing.

## Inputs
Team feedback, workload, incident patterns, attrition signals, meeting dynamics, delivery pressure, on-call burden, and organizational changes.

## Context to inspect
Observe who speaks, who gets interrupted, whether bad news travels upward, workload distribution, recurring heroics, PTO patterns, and whether disagreement has consequences.

## Core knowledge
Psychological safety means interpersonal risk can be taken without fear of punishment; it does not remove accountability or performance standards. Sustainable pace is an engineering-quality concern because exhaustion increases errors and attrition.

## Procedure
1. Gather qualitative and quantitative health signals without invasive surveillance.
2. Model admitting uncertainty and mistakes.
3. Invite dissent before decisions close.
4. Respond constructively when people surface bad news.
5. Make behavioral norms explicit and enforce them consistently.
6. Identify unsustainable workload, on-call, or dependency patterns.
7. Redistribute work and reduce commitments when capacity is persistently exceeded.
8. Address exclusion, interruption, or information asymmetry.
9. Protect recovery after intense incidents or deadlines.
10. Recheck whether interventions changed observable behavior.

## Decision points
Use anonymous mechanisms when power dynamics inhibit candor, but do not replace direct management relationships with surveys. Reduce scope when workload is structurally unsustainable rather than relying on resilience messaging.

## Common failure patterns
Equating safety with comfort, tolerating harmful behavior from high performers, mandatory vulnerability, wellness initiatives without workload changes, and punishing people who raise risk.

## Verification
Verify concerns surface earlier, behavioral norms are enforced, workload is sustainable, and team members can disagree without retaliation while remaining accountable.

## Expected output
A team-health improvement plan with specific systemic and behavioral actions.

## Stop conditions
Escalate credible harassment, discrimination, retaliation, health or safety risks, or other matters requiring formal organizational support.
