# Decision Making Under Uncertainty

## Purpose
Make timely engineering and organizational decisions when evidence is incomplete while preserving reversibility, learning, and accountability.

## When to use
Use for architecture bets, ambiguous incidents, staffing choices, sequencing, vendor selection, migration strategy, or any decision where waiting for certainty has material cost.

## Inputs
Decision statement, options, evidence, assumptions, constraints, risk tolerance, deadlines, stakeholders, and reversibility.

## Context to inspect
Inspect what is known, unknown, knowable within the decision window, cost of delay, downside exposure, and who owns the consequences.

## Core knowledge
Uncertainty cannot always be removed economically. Good decisions separate confidence from consequence, use reversible experiments where possible, and define what new evidence would change the choice.

## Procedure
1. State the decision and deadline clearly.
2. Identify decision owner and affected stakeholders.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, estimates, and unknowns.
4. Generate viable alternatives including delay or no action.
5. Compare expected upside, downside, reversibility, and opportunity cost.
6. Seek information only when its expected value exceeds delay cost.
7. Prefer small reversible probes for high uncertainty.
8. Make the decision with rationale and confidence level.
9. Define leading signals and reversal triggers.
10. Review outcomes without judging solely by whether uncertainty happened to resolve favorably.

## Decision points
Move quickly on reversible decisions; require stronger evidence and broader review for irreversible high-blast-radius choices.

## Common failure patterns
Analysis paralysis, false certainty, consensus as a substitute for ownership, hindsight bias, collecting data with no decision impact, and refusing to revisit assumptions.

## Verification
Verify the decision record contains options, assumptions, trade-offs, owner, triggers, and evidence appropriate to its reversibility and impact.

## Expected output
A timely, evidence-aware decision with explicit uncertainty and review conditions.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when potential consequences exceed delegated risk authority or when required legal, security, financial, or personnel expertise is missing.
