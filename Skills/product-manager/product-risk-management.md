# Product Risk Management

## Purpose
Identify and manage product risks across customer value, usability, feasibility, viability, security, compliance, operations, and reputation.

## When to use
Use for major initiatives, integrations, launches, migrations, regulated workflows, and decisions with high irreversibility.

## Inputs
Initiative scope, architecture, customer impact, business model, dependencies, legal constraints, operational plan, and known uncertainties.

## Context to inspect
Inspect failure modes, affected segments, data sensitivity, third parties, rollback options, support capacity, contractual commitments, and historical incidents.

## Core knowledge
Risk is a combination of uncertainty and consequence. Senior product management reduces risk early with evidence, design, controls, staged exposure, and explicit acceptance.

## Procedure
1. Enumerate credible risks by category.
2. Estimate likelihood, impact, detectability, and reversibility.
3. Identify assumptions and leading indicators.
4. Rank risks by decision relevance.
5. Choose avoid, reduce, transfer, monitor, or accept responses.
6. Assign owners and concrete mitigations.
7. Define launch guardrails, rollback triggers, and escalation paths.
8. Review residual risk with authorized owners.
9. Reassess as scope or evidence changes.

## Decision points
Prefer avoidance for catastrophic low-tolerance risks. Use staged rollout when exposure can validate uncertainty safely. Accept only when residual risk and owner are explicit.

## Common failure patterns
Risk registers without actions, focusing only on schedule, hiding uncertainty to protect commitments, and assuming rollback is always possible.

## Verification
Top risks have owners, mitigations, monitoring, and residual-risk decisions; launch criteria reflect material risks.

## Expected output
A prioritized risk register integrated into product and launch decisions.

## Stop conditions
Stop or escalate when risk exceeds organizational tolerance, required approvals are absent, or safe mitigation cannot be demonstrated.
