# Product Decision Records and Learning Reviews

## Purpose
Preserve important product decisions, assumptions, evidence, and outcomes so teams learn rather than repeatedly relitigating history.

## When to use
Use for strategic choices, irreversible commitments, controversial trade-offs, experiments, major launches, and decisions likely to be revisited.

## Inputs
Decision question, options, evidence, assumptions, owner, risks, expected outcomes, and review date or trigger.

## Context to inspect
Inspect prior decisions, related experiments, roadmap dependencies, metrics, and changes in market or technical conditions.

## Core knowledge
Decision quality should be evaluated separately from outcome luck. Good records are concise and capture what was known at the time.

## Procedure
1. State the decision and date.
2. Record the accountable decision owner.
3. Summarize context and evidence available at decision time.
4. List viable alternatives and why they were not selected.
5. Capture assumptions, uncertainty, and expected outcomes.
6. Define metrics or events that would invalidate the decision.
7. Link related research and delivery artifacts.
8. At the review point, compare actual outcomes with expectations.
9. Identify whether errors came from assumptions, execution, measurement, or external change.
10. Feed reusable learning into future product practice.

## Decision points
Record lightweight reversible decisions briefly; invest more detail in costly, irreversible, or organization-wide choices.

## Common failure patterns
Writing records after the fact to justify outcomes, no revisit trigger, documenting meetings instead of decisions, and judging a sound decision as bad solely because the outcome was unlucky.

## Verification
Records reflect contemporaneous evidence, assumptions are testable, and learning reviews result in concrete updates to models or process.

## Expected output
A durable decision record plus later learning review that improves future judgment.

## Stop conditions
Do not record sensitive legal, personnel, or customer information in inappropriate repositories; use approved systems instead.
