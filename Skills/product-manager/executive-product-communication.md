# Executive Product Communication

## Purpose
Communicate product decisions, performance, risks, and asks to executives with enough evidence for decisions and without drowning them in delivery detail.

## When to use
Use for strategy reviews, investment asks, roadmap changes, incidents, business reviews, and major product decisions.

## Inputs
Decision or update objective, metrics, customer evidence, economics, risks, alternatives, recommendation, and requested action.

## Context to inspect
Inspect audience priorities, previous decisions, company goals, financial context, known objections, and the level of detail required.

## Core knowledge
Executive communication should lead with the decision context and material implications. Distinguish facts, interpretations, forecasts, and asks.

## Procedure
1. Define what the audience must understand or decide.
2. State the headline outcome or recommendation first.
3. Connect it to company and customer objectives.
4. Present a small set of decisive evidence.
5. Explain alternatives and material trade-offs.
6. Quantify uncertainty and risks honestly.
7. Make the ask, owner, and timing explicit.
8. Prepare supporting detail for questions without putting all of it in the main narrative.
9. Record decisions and follow-up actions.

## Decision points
Use written pre-reads for complex or consequential decisions. Use concise live updates for operational alignment. Increase detail when assumptions or risk are disputed.

## Common failure patterns
Status dumps, vanity metrics, burying the ask, overstating certainty, hiding bad news, and presenting only one option for a genuine trade-off.

## Verification
The audience can state the decision, rationale, risk, and required action; follow-ups have owners and deadlines.

## Expected output
A concise decision-oriented product narrative with evidence, trade-offs, risks, and explicit asks.

## Stop conditions
Stop and validate data when material numbers conflict, or involve finance/legal/security owners when claims require their authority.
