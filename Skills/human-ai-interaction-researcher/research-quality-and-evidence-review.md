# Research Quality and Evidence Review

## Purpose
Review a human-AI study or research program for methodological fitness, evidence traceability, system-version integrity, inference limits, and decision usefulness before findings are treated as established.

## When to use
Use for peer review, launch-critical research, high-stakes claims, cross-team studies, external research adoption, and retrospective quality improvement.

## Inputs
Research brief, protocol, sample, system configuration, raw or summarized evidence, analysis, findings, recommendations, and decision context.

## Context to inspect
Inspect research questions, method choice, recruitment, moderator materials, instrumentation, model version, exclusions, analysis rules, contradictory evidence, and deviations from plan.

## Core knowledge
Quality is fit-for-purpose rather than a single hierarchy of methods. A small qualitative study can strongly identify a mechanism but not estimate prevalence; an experiment can estimate a treatment effect but may have limited ecological validity. AI-system drift creates an additional validity dimension.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the decision and claim each finding is intended to support.
2. Check whether method and sampling fit those claims.
3. Verify system configuration and study dates are documented.
4. Review task realism, measurement validity, and moderator or instrumentation effects.
5. Check exclusions, missing data, attrition, and analytic flexibility.
6. Trace major findings to underlying evidence.
7. Search for negative cases and contradictory results.
8. Evaluate whether prevalence, causality, or generalization claims exceed the design.
9. Assess whether recommendations follow from findings and consider trade-offs.
10. Rate confidence and identify what additional evidence would change it.
11. Record reusable lessons for future studies.

## Decision points
Require stronger review when findings gate a consequential launch or policy. Accept exploratory uncertainty when the decision is reversible and research is explicitly formative.

## Common failure patterns
Method prestige replacing fit, undocumented model versions, sample-size rituals, unsupported causal language, hiding null results, recommendations detached from evidence, and reviewing presentation polish instead of validity.

## Verification
A second reviewer should be able to reproduce the evidence chain for critical claims and identify the same principal limitations from the documented materials.

## Expected output
An evidence-quality review with claim-level confidence, validity risks, required corrections, unresolved uncertainty, and disposition for decision use.

## Stop conditions
Stop and withhold endorsement when critical evidence cannot be traced, the studied system cannot be identified, ethical violations are unresolved, or the claim materially exceeds what the design supports.
