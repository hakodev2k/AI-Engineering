# Requirements Workshop Facilitation

## Purpose
Facilitate structured stakeholder sessions that produce decisions, shared understanding, validated requirements, and owned follow-up actions.

## When to use
Use for cross-functional discovery, conflicting requirements, process redesign, prioritization, rule clarification, or scope alignment.

## Inputs
Workshop objective, participant list, known issues, pre-read material, decisions required, and relevant models or evidence.

## Preconditions
Participants include the people needed to supply facts and make or influence required decisions.

## Context to inspect
Stakeholder interests, unresolved conflicts, terminology differences, dependencies, prior decisions, and time constraints.

## Core knowledge
A Senior BA designs a workshop around outcomes, not presentations. Facilitation separates facts, assumptions, options, and decisions while preventing dominant voices from replacing evidence.

## Procedure
1. Define explicit workshop outcomes and decision questions.
2. Invite the smallest complete stakeholder group.
3. Prepare pre-read and working artifacts.
4. Establish scope, terminology, and decision rules at the start.
5. Facilitate discussion using scenarios, models, examples, or structured exercises.
6. Surface conflicting assumptions and unresolved evidence.
7. Time-box low-value debate and park unrelated issues.
8. Confirm decisions verbally before moving on.
9. Record actions with owners and dates.
10. Circulate outcomes and obtain confirmation promptly.

## Decision points
Use collaborative workshops when shared understanding matters; use separate interviews first when hierarchy or conflict would suppress honest input.

## Common failure patterns
Inviting too many observers, allowing solution debate before problem alignment, failing to record decisions, and leaving actions ownerless.

## Verification
Confirm required outcomes were achieved, decisions are attributable, and participants agree with the documented results.

## Expected output
A concise workshop record containing decisions, validated requirements, unresolved questions, and owned actions.

## Stop conditions
Stop or reschedule when required decision-makers are absent or critical evidence is unavailable.
