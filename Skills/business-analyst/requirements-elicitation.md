# Requirements Elicitation

## Purpose
Discover real business needs, constraints, assumptions, and stakeholder expectations before solution design begins.

## When to use
Use for new initiatives, major changes, unclear requests, conflicting stakeholder expectations, or when scope is unstable.

## Inputs
Business goals, stakeholder list, current processes, existing documentation, constraints, known pain points, and prior decisions.

## Preconditions
Access to relevant stakeholders and enough context to distinguish business need from proposed solution.

## Context to inspect
Current workflows, systems, policies, metrics, customer feedback, operational issues, dependencies, and regulatory constraints.

## Core knowledge
Elicitation is not note taking. A Senior BA separates stated wants from underlying needs, validates terminology, exposes hidden rules, and captures uncertainty explicitly.

## Procedure
1. Define the business objective and decision to support.
2. Identify stakeholder groups and decision owners.
3. Review existing process and system evidence.
4. Prepare targeted questions around goals, actors, triggers, rules, exceptions, data, and constraints.
5. Facilitate interviews, workshops, observation, or document analysis as appropriate.
6. Capture facts, assumptions, decisions, conflicts, and open questions separately.
7. Probe edge cases and exception paths.
8. Reframe solution requests into business outcomes where needed.
9. Validate findings with stakeholders.
10. Maintain a requirements trace from objective to detailed need.

## Decision points
Choose interviews for depth, workshops for alignment, observation for real behavior, and document analysis for established rules. Combine methods when evidence is weak.

## Common failure patterns
Accepting proposed solutions as requirements, interviewing only managers, missing operational exceptions, recording ambiguous terms, and treating silence as agreement.

## Verification
Confirm stakeholders can restate the agreed objective, unresolved questions are visible, contradictions are resolved or owned, and important exceptions are documented.

## Expected output
A validated elicitation record containing business needs, assumptions, constraints, open questions, stakeholders, and traceable requirements.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when decision ownership is unclear, critical stakeholders are unavailable, or conflicting requirements cannot be resolved at BA level.
