# ML Governance and Auditability

## Purpose
Create traceable decision and evidence paths for model ownership, intended use, risk classification, approvals, changes, performance, and retirement.

## When to use
Use for regulated, high-impact, enterprise, or broadly reused ML systems where accountability and review matter.

## Inputs
Model purpose, owners, risk tier, datasets, evaluations, approvals, deployment history, monitoring, incidents, retention obligations.

## Preconditions
An organizational risk/approval framework exists or responsible decision owners are identifiable.

## Context to inspect
Registry metadata, data catalog, model cards, change management, access logs, incident records, compliance controls, and retention policy.

## Core knowledge
Governance should be evidence generated by normal engineering workflows, not a disconnected paperwork exercise. Controls should be proportional to risk and preserve who decided what, using which evidence.

## Procedure
1. Record intended use and prohibited use.
2. Assign accountable technical and business owners.
3. Classify risk and required controls.
4. Link training data, code, artifacts, evaluations, and approvals.
5. Capture deployment and rollback history.
6. Record monitoring and incident evidence.
7. Define periodic review triggers.
8. Track exceptions with expiry and owner.
9. Define retention and deletion obligations.
10. Test reconstruction of a historical production decision.

## Decision points
Automate evidence collection where reliable; require human approval for high-impact exceptions or ambiguous risk.

## Common failure patterns
Documents drifting from deployed reality, approvals without immutable version references, permanent exceptions, unknown ownership, and audit logs that omit manual changes.

## Verification
Reconstruct why a specific model version entered production and who approved it, using stored evidence without relying on memory.

## Expected output
Governance record, risk tier, evidence links, approval history, exception register, and review cadence.

## Stop conditions
Escalate missing accountable ownership, mandatory evidence gaps, or prohibited-use conflicts.
