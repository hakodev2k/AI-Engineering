# Data Ownership and Stewardship

## Purpose
Establish accountable ownership and executable stewardship for important data assets.

## When to use
Use when ownership is missing, disputed, nominal, or ineffective; when controls require accountable decisions; or when quality issues persist across teams.

## Inputs
Domain map, asset inventory, business processes, policies, issue history, organization roles, authority model.

## Context to inspect
Inspect who creates, changes, funds, consumes, and bears risk for the data; distinguish business accountability from technical custody.

## Core knowledge
Owners are accountable for material decisions and risk acceptance; stewards operationalize definitions, quality, access, and issue resolution; custodians operate technology. Titles matter less than authority, capacity, and measurable duties.

## Procedure
1. Identify critical domains/assets lacking effective accountability.
2. Map business decision authority and technical custody.
3. Define owner and steward responsibilities explicitly.
4. Set appointment criteria and required authority.
5. Assign one accountable owner per governed decision scope.
6. Define stewardship workflows and service expectations.
7. Connect roles to catalog, quality, access, change, and incident processes.
8. Establish delegation and backup coverage.
9. Train role holders using realistic scenarios.
10. Measure unresolved issues, decision latency, and stewardship workload.

## Decision points
Use domain-level ownership by default; add asset-level ownership only when risk or complexity justifies it. Delegate execution, not accountability. Avoid assigning ownership solely to IT when business semantics and risk belong elsewhere.

## Common failure patterns
Honorary owners, RACI matrices without workflows, multiple accountable owners, no steward capacity, technical custodians forced to make business decisions, and ownership records that go stale.

## Verification
Sample governed decisions and confirm the named owner can make them, stewards know procedures, escalation works, and metadata reflects current assignments.

## Expected output
A role model, assignments, responsibility matrix, stewardship workflow, and operating metrics.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when no stakeholder has legitimate authority, conflicts cannot be resolved, or staffing is insufficient to perform mandatory stewardship.
