# Data Policy Lifecycle

## Purpose
Create maintainable data policies that translate obligations and principles into clear, testable organizational requirements.

## When to use
Use when drafting, revising, consolidating, or retiring data-governance policy.

## Inputs
Laws, contracts, risk requirements, architecture standards, incidents, existing policies, control capabilities.

## Context to inspect
Inspect overlapping policies, accountable authorities, affected workflows, enforcement mechanisms, exceptions, and evidence collection.

## Core knowledge
Policy states mandatory outcomes; standards define required implementation; procedures explain execution. Requirements should be testable and traceable to rationale or obligation.

## Procedure
1. Define policy objective, authority, and scope.
2. Map source obligations and risks.
3. Review existing policy for conflicts or duplication.
4. Write concise mandatory requirements.
5. Assign accountable policy owner.
6. Map requirements to standards and controls.
7. Define exceptions, evidence, enforcement, and consequences.
8. Conduct legal/security/privacy/engineering review as relevant.
9. Publish with effective date and transition plan.
10. Measure adoption and exceptions.
11. Review periodically and after material change.
12. Retire superseded policy explicitly.

## Decision points
Use policy only for durable mandatory requirements; place implementation detail in standards. Prefer fewer coherent policies over fragmented documents.

## Common failure patterns
Policy as aspiration, conflicting requirements, untestable language, no owner, no enforcement, stale references, and undocumented exceptions.

## Verification
Trace each material requirement to an obligation/risk, a control or accountable workflow, evidence, and an exception path.

## Expected output
Approved policy with scope, requirements, accountability, control mappings, lifecycle, and exception process.

## Stop conditions
Escalate unresolved legal interpretation, conflicting executive mandates, or requirements that cannot be enforced with available authority.
