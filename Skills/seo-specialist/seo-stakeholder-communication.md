# SEO Stakeholder Communication

## Purpose
Translate search evidence into clear decisions for engineering, product, content, executives, legal, and marketing teams.

## When to use
Use for recommendations, incidents, roadmap negotiations, migrations, and performance reporting.

## Inputs
Findings, evidence, business impact, implementation options, risks, audience context, and decision deadline.

## Context to inspect
Stakeholder goals, technical literacy, ownership, constraints, prior decisions, and unresolved assumptions.

## Core knowledge
Senior SEO communication separates fact, hypothesis, forecast, and recommendation. The goal is a sound decision, not demonstrating SEO vocabulary.

## Procedure
1. Define the decision required from the audience.
2. Lead with business/user impact.
3. Present evidence and affected scope.
4. Explain root cause in audience-appropriate language.
5. Offer viable options with trade-offs.
6. State recommendation, confidence, and risks.
7. Define owner, next action, and verification.
8. Record material decisions and assumptions.

## Decision points
Use concise executive framing for investment decisions and detailed reproducible evidence for implementers. Escalate only material unresolved risk.

## Common failure patterns
Vanity metrics, jargon, presenting forecasts as guarantees, overwhelming stakeholders with crawler output, and omitting implementation cost.

## Verification
Confirm the decision, owner, acceptance criteria, and measurement plan are understood and documented.

## Expected output
Decision-ready communication with evidence, options, recommendation, risks, and next steps.

## Stop conditions
Stop when evidence is insufficient to make the requested claim confidently.
