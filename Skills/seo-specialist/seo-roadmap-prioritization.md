# SEO Roadmap Prioritization

## Purpose
Convert SEO opportunities into an executable roadmap based on impact, confidence, effort, dependencies, risk, and strategic fit.

## When to use
Use after audits, quarterly planning, resource constraints, or when stakeholders compete for SEO capacity.

## Inputs
Opportunity backlog, business goals, engineering estimates, traffic/revenue data, dependencies, risks, and deadlines.

## Context to inspect
Current bottlenecks, platform roadmap, release windows, ownership, technical debt, seasonality, and measurement readiness.

## Core knowledge
SEO opportunity is constrained by implementation capacity and uncertainty. Prioritization should expose assumptions rather than disguise them in a single score.

## Procedure
1. Translate findings into discrete outcomes and deliverables.
2. Estimate affected scope and business value.
3. Assess confidence and evidence quality.
4. Identify effort, dependencies, reversibility, and risk.
5. Group enabling work before dependent optimizations.
6. Sequence quick wins, strategic bets, and mandatory risk reduction.
7. Assign owners and success metrics.
8. Review roadmap as evidence changes.

## Decision points
Prioritize systemic fixes over isolated pages when expected aggregate value is higher. Reserve capacity for diagnostics and regressions.

## Common failure patterns
Ranking only by traffic potential, ignoring engineering effort, vague tickets, and roadmaps without owners or verification.

## Verification
Ensure each roadmap item has rationale, scope, dependency, owner, acceptance criteria, and measurable outcome.

## Expected output
A sequenced, evidence-backed SEO roadmap suitable for cross-functional planning.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when business priorities or required capacity cannot be resolved by the working team.
