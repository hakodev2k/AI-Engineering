# Organic Traffic Drop Investigation

## Purpose
Diagnose organic-search losses methodically and separate technical, content, demand, tracking, competitive, and algorithmic causes.

## When to use
Use after material declines in impressions, clicks, sessions, conversions, or rankings.

## Inputs
Search data, analytics, releases, crawl/indexation evidence, server health, rank history, and market trends.

## Context to inspect
Exact onset, affected segments, tracking changes, site releases, status codes, robots, canonicals, demand, SERP changes, and competitors.

## Core knowledge
Start with segmentation and timing. Sitewide assumptions are dangerous when losses are concentrated by template, query, device, or country.

## Procedure
1. Verify the drop is real and not instrumentation noise.
2. Determine onset and magnitude.
3. Segment by page, query, market, device, brand, and template.
4. Correlate with releases and technical changes.
5. Inspect crawl/indexation and server evidence.
6. Compare demand and SERP composition.
7. Review content quality and competitive displacement.
8. Rank hypotheses by evidence.
9. Fix confirmed causes and monitor recovery.

## Decision points
Rollback when a recent change has strong causal evidence and rollback risk is low; otherwise gather evidence before disruptive action.

## Common failure patterns
Blaming an algorithm update immediately, changing many things at once, and comparing unadjusted seasonal periods.

## Verification
Confirm root-cause evidence explains affected segments and remediation reverses the relevant signals.

## Expected output
Timeline, segmented impact, ranked hypotheses, evidence, actions, and monitoring.

## Stop conditions
Escalate production incidents or analytics failures to their owners immediately.
