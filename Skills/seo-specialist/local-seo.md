# Local SEO

## Purpose
Improve discoverability for location-dependent searches through accurate entities, location pages, reputation, and local relevance.

## When to use
Use for businesses serving physical locations or defined service areas.

## Inputs
Locations, business details, categories, local pages, profiles, reviews, citations, and local query data.

## Context to inspect
Name/address/phone consistency, opening hours, categories, service areas, duplicate profiles, local landing pages, and review patterns.

## Core knowledge
Local visibility depends on relevance, distance, prominence, entity accuracy, and genuine local usefulness. Location pages must offer more than swapped place names.

## Procedure
1. Verify authoritative business data per location.
2. Audit major business profiles and duplicates.
3. Map local intent to useful landing pages.
4. Improve location-specific content, services, directions, and contact details.
5. Implement appropriate local structured data.
6. Establish compliant review acquisition and response processes.
7. Correct material citation inconsistencies.
8. Track local rankings, calls, directions, and conversions.

## Decision points
Create separate pages only for real locations/service distinctions with useful unique information; avoid doorway pages.

## Common failure patterns
Fake locations, keyword-stuffed names, duplicate profiles, fabricated reviews, and thin city pages.

## Verification
Validate business facts across owned surfaces, test local pages, and monitor profile/search performance.

## Expected output
Location-level remediation, governance rules, and measurable local-search outcomes.

## Stop conditions
Stop when requested tactics violate platform policies or misrepresent business presence.
