# Project Closure and Lessons Learned

## Purpose
Close projects deliberately by confirming acceptance, transferring ownership, resolving obligations, measuring outcomes, and converting experience into reusable organizational learning.

## When to use
Use when deliverables are complete, a project is terminated, or responsibility is formally transferred.

## Inputs
Acceptance evidence, open actions, contracts, budget, operational handover, risks, benefits measures, project records, and stakeholder feedback.

## Context to inspect
Inspect unresolved defects, support commitments, financial accruals, vendor obligations, data/document retention, benefits ownership, and previous retrospectives.

## Core knowledge
Closure is more than administrative completion. A project can finish delivery while benefits continue later. Lessons should identify causal patterns and concrete changes, not generic observations.

## Procedure
1. Confirm deliverable acceptance against agreed criteria.
2. Verify operational/business ownership and residual action owners.
3. Close or transfer risks, issues, dependencies, and support commitments.
4. Reconcile budget, contracts, assets, and access.
5. Archive required records and decision evidence.
6. Compare final outcomes with charter success criteria and baselines.
7. Facilitate a blameless lessons review using evidence.
8. Convert lessons into specific process, governance, technical, or planning improvements.
9. Assign owners for benefits realization that continues after closure.
10. Obtain formal closure authorization where required.

## Decision points
Close with residual actions only when ownership and deadlines are explicit and risk is accepted. Keep the project open when critical acceptance or transition obligations remain genuinely project-owned.

## Common failure patterns
Closing because the team disbands, lessons without owners, ignoring abandoned vendor commitments, losing decision history, and claiming benefits before measurement.

## Verification
Acceptance, handover, financial closure, residual ownership, archival, and closure authorization are evidenced; lessons have actionable follow-through.

## Expected output
A closure record, final performance assessment, transferred obligations, and actionable lessons.

## Stop conditions
Do not declare closure while critical deliverables lack acceptance, mandatory obligations remain unowned, or material financial/compliance reconciliation is incomplete.
