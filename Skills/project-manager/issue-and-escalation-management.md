# Issue and Escalation Management

## Purpose
Drive active problems to resolution with clear ownership, urgency, evidence, and timely escalation.

## When to use
Use when an event already threatens scope, schedule, cost, quality, compliance, or stakeholder outcomes.

## Inputs
Issue description, impact evidence, timeline, owners, attempted actions, dependencies, decisions required, and escalation paths.

## Context to inspect
Review incident records, project plan, decision rights, contractual obligations, related risks, and affected stakeholders.

## Core knowledge
Escalation is a management control, not failure. Effective escalation states impact, urgency, options, recommendation, and the decision required. Project managers coordinate resolution without obscuring technical ownership.

## Procedure
1. Verify facts and distinguish symptom from underlying problem.
2. Quantify current and potential impact.
3. Assign a resolution owner and response deadline.
4. Identify containment actions that limit further impact.
5. Gather options, trade-offs, and specialist recommendations.
6. Escalate at the threshold defined by governance or risk tolerance.
7. Record decisions and update dependent plans.
8. Track actions until objective resolution criteria are met.
9. Communicate closure and residual consequences.
10. Feed systemic causes into lessons and risk management.

## Decision points
Resolve locally when authority and capability are sufficient. Escalate when additional authority, budget, cross-team prioritization, risk acceptance, or executive intervention is required.

## Common failure patterns
Escalating without a decision request, waiting until deadlines fail, status-only issue logs, blaming individuals, premature closure, and hiding impact behind vague language.

## Verification
Confirm containment worked, the decision owner acted, resolution evidence exists, and affected baselines and stakeholders were updated.

## Expected output
A current issue record with impact, owner, actions, decisions, escalation status, and closure evidence.

## Stop conditions
Escalate immediately for safety, security, legal, regulatory, or severe business impact outside delegated authority.
