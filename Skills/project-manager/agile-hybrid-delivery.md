# Agile and Hybrid Delivery

## Purpose
Choose and operate a delivery model that preserves adaptability while maintaining the governance and coordination the project actually requires.

## When to use
Use when teams employ Scrum, Kanban, iterative delivery, stage gates, or mixed predictive/agile practices.

## Inputs
Work uncertainty, product ownership, team structure, release constraints, governance needs, dependencies, and compliance requirements.

## Context to inspect
Inspect team practices, decision rights, backlog ownership, release cadence, portfolio reporting, dependencies, and mandatory gates.

## Core knowledge
Agile does not remove project risks, dependencies, budgets, or commitments. Hybrid delivery should integrate controls at meaningful boundaries rather than duplicate agile artifacts with traditional paperwork.

## Procedure
1. Assess uncertainty, coupling, compliance, and stakeholder decision needs.
2. Identify work suited to iterative discovery versus predictive coordination.
3. Preserve clear product ownership and team-level flow.
4. Map project milestones to demonstrable outcomes, not arbitrary sprint counts.
5. Use throughput, cycle time, burn-up, and release evidence where appropriate.
6. Integrate dependencies, risks, budget, and governance without micromanaging the backlog.
7. Forecast using empirical delivery data.
8. Align change control with legitimate discovery.
9. Remove ceremonies and reports that add no decision value.
10. Adapt the operating model based on evidence.

## Decision points
Use flow-based methods for interrupt-driven work, iterations for cohesive increments, and predictive planning for fixed external constraints with lower uncertainty.

## Common failure patterns
Calling waterfall phases sprints, project managers owning product priority, story points used as executive productivity metrics, and duplicate reporting systems.

## Verification
Team delivery metrics and project forecasts reconcile, governance does not contradict workflow, and milestones correspond to accepted outcomes.

## Expected output
A coherent delivery operating model connecting team execution to project governance.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when governance forces misleading metrics or no accountable owner can prioritize outcomes.
