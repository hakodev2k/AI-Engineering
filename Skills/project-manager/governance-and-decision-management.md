# Governance and Decision Management

## Purpose
Establish proportionate governance that makes ownership, approvals, trade-offs, and escalations explicit without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

## When to use
Use when a project spans teams, has significant risk/cost, requires formal approvals, or suffers from slow or contradictory decisions.

## Inputs
Organization policies, sponsor structure, project risk, decision categories, budget authority, compliance needs, and delivery model.

## Context to inspect
Inspect existing steering forums, product/technical authority, financial approvals, change control, regulatory gates, and escalation routes.

## Core knowledge
Governance should place decisions with the lowest competent authority while preserving accountability for material risk. Decision latency itself is a project risk.

## Procedure
1. Identify recurring decision categories and required authority.
2. Define accountable decision owners and delegated thresholds.
3. Establish lightweight forums and cadence for unresolved cross-cutting decisions.
4. Define required evidence for major decisions.
5. Maintain a decision log with rationale and consequences.
6. Time-box decisions whose delay threatens delivery.
7. Escalate unresolved conflicts through predefined paths.
8. Revisit decisions when assumptions materially change, not merely because stakeholders change preference.
9. Remove governance steps that add no control value.
10. Audit whether decisions are actually followed in delivery plans.

## Decision points
Increase governance for irreversible, regulated, high-cost, or high-risk decisions. Delegate reversible low-impact decisions to delivery teams.

## Common failure patterns
Committees without authority, duplicate approvals, undocumented verbal decisions, revisiting settled decisions without new evidence, and escalation only after schedule failure.

## Verification
Decision rights are understood, major decisions are traceable, approval latency is monitored, and project plans reflect approved choices.

## Expected output
A governance model and decision record that accelerate accountable execution.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when no authorized owner can decide a critical matter or governance requirements conflict with mandatory policy.
