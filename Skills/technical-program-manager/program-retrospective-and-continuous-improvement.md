# Program Retrospective and Continuous Improvement

## Purpose
Convert program execution evidence into concrete improvements to planning, governance, technical coordination, risk management, and organizational operating mechanisms.

## When to use
Use after major milestones, launches, migrations, incidents, or program completion. Also use periodically on long-running initiatives.

## Inputs
Outcome metrics, milestone history, decision logs, risk register, incident reports, dependency records, stakeholder feedback, delivery data.

## Context to inspect
Original charter and assumptions, scope changes, forecast accuracy, quality results, operational outcomes, and unresolved follow-up actions.

## Core knowledge
A Senior TPM avoids blame-oriented retrospectives and focuses on system conditions, decision quality, information flow, incentives, and repeatable mechanisms. Improvements must have owners and observable effects.

## Procedure
1. Compare achieved outcomes with the original charter and baselines.
2. Identify significant surprises, delays, defects, recoveries, and successful interventions.
3. Reconstruct why key decisions were made using evidence available at the time.
4. Separate local mistakes from systemic conditions.
5. Identify a small number of high-leverage process or technical coordination improvements.
6. Assign owners, due dates, and success measures.
7. Update templates, governance, risk heuristics, or readiness criteria where justified.
8. Share reusable lessons with affected teams.
9. Review follow-through at a later checkpoint.

## Decision points
Prefer changes that remove recurring failure modes over adding meetings or documentation. Preserve practices that worked even if final outcomes were imperfect.

## Common failure patterns
Blame, vague lessons, excessive action items, hindsight bias, and retrospectives whose actions are never tracked.

## Verification
Confirm agreed improvements are implemented and later evidence shows whether they changed the targeted behavior or outcome.

## Expected output
A concise evidence-based retrospective with owned, measurable improvements that strengthen future programs.

## Stop conditions
Stop and route sensitive personnel, legal, security, or compliance matters through authorized processes rather than documenting them broadly.
