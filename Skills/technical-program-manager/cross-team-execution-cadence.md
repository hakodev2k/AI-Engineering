# Cross-Team Execution Cadence

## Purpose
Establish an operating cadence that keeps multi-team execution synchronized while minimizing coordination overhead.

## When to use
Use when several engineering teams share milestones, dependencies, risks, or release constraints.

## Inputs
Program plan, owner map, dependencies, decisions, risks, milestone dates.

## Context to inspect
Existing team ceremonies, reporting channels, time zones, escalation paths, and decision forums.

## Core knowledge
Cadence should exist to resolve uncertainty and drive decisions, not to generate status. Senior TPMs separate async reporting from synchronous problem solving.

## Procedure
1. Identify information that can remain asynchronous.
2. Define recurring forums only for dependency, risk, decision, and readiness needs.
3. Assign clear inputs and outputs for each forum.
4. Require owners to update evidence before meetings.
5. Use meeting time for exceptions and decisions.
6. Record actions, owners, and deadlines.
7. Eliminate forums that do not change outcomes.
8. Recalibrate cadence as the program moves from design to integration to launch.

## Decision points
Increase cadence temporarily near critical cutovers; reduce it when execution stabilizes. Prefer direct owner working sessions over large status meetings.

## Common failure patterns
Meeting inflation, duplicate forums, passive status reading, unresolved actions, and no link between cadence and decisions.

## Verification
Measure decision latency, overdue actions, dependency aging, and participant value. Confirm each forum produces explicit outcomes.

## Expected output
A lean operating rhythm that improves execution visibility and decision speed.

## Stop conditions
Escalate when required owners repeatedly cannot participate or governance conflicts with mandated organizational processes.
