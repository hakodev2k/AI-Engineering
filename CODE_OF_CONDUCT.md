# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of experience, identity, background, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Welcome questions and make space for different levels of experience.
- Give credit for ideas, research, and contributions.
- Protect private information and respect security disclosure boundaries.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, or knowingly misleading technical claims.
- Encouraging unsafe actions that could expose credentials, data, systems, or people.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an appropriate non-public GitHub channel. If the concern involves a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy. Responses may range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as a violation.
