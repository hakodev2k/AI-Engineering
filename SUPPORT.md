# Support

This repository is community-maintained and support is provided on a best-effort basis.

## Where to ask

- Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hakodev2k/AI-Engineering/issues) for reproducible defects, documentation problems, and scoped feature requests.
- Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hakodev2k/AI-Engineering/discussions), when enabled, for usage questions, design ideas, and broader conversation.
- Use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities.

Do not use public support channels for credentials, personal data, proprietary prompts, internal logs, or security-sensitive details.

## Before opening an issue

1. Search existing issues and the relevant collection for an answer.
2. Confirm the problem still exists on the latest `main` branch.
3. Reduce the report to the smallest reproducible example.
4. Remove secrets and sensitive data from logs, screenshots, and sample configuration.

## What to include

- the affected path or package;
- what you were trying to accomplish;
- expected and actual behavior;
- exact validation steps or error output;
- relevant runtime, platform, and dependency versions;
- a minimal sanitized example;
- any workaround already attempted.

Clear, reproducible reports are easier to triage. Maintainers may close requests that are out of scope, cannot be reproduced, or do not include enough information after follow-up.

## Support boundaries

Maintainers cannot provide guarantees for production readiness, organization-specific compliance, third-party service availability, or locally modified copies. Adopters remain responsible for security review, testing, permissions, costs, deployment, and operational monitoring in their own environment.
